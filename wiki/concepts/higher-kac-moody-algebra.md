## Definition

The **higher Kac–Moody algebra** is the holomorphic-twist enhancement of a flavor symmetry: the
(derived) algebra
$$\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\mathbb C^2\setminus\{0\})\otimes\mathfrak f$$
of $\mathfrak f$-valued holomorphic functions on punctured $\mathbb C^2$, the two-complex-dimensional
analogue of the loop/affine algebra $\mathbb C[z,z^{-1}]\otimes\mathfrak f$ of a 2d CFT. It is
generated by the modes of the semi-chiral current superfield $\mathbf J$ (with
$\bar\partial\mathbf J=0$): a global flavor rotation is implemented by integrating $\mathbf J$
around an operator, and weighting that integral by a holomorphic $(0,\bullet)$-form promotes the
global symmetry to a **local, holomorphic** one.

Like the [[two-virasoro-algebra|2-Witt algebra]], it has **no ordinary central extension** but a
**one-dimensional space of $L_\infty$ central extensions** (a 3-cocycle), whose coefficient is
the flavor level / cubic flavor 't Hooft anomaly $k_{FFF}$.

## Motivation

Faonte–Hennion–Kapranov constructed these higher current algebras purely mathematically; the
holomorphic twist realizes them physically as the enhancement of ordinary flavor symmetry. The
appearance of the flavor anomaly as a **ternary** bracket (rather than the 2d affine level, a
2-cocycle) is again forced by the derived, homotopy-Lie nature of holomorphic symmetry in
complex dimension $>1$.

## Key formulas

- Pure flavor 't Hooft anomaly as a ternary bracket:
  $\{\!\{\mathbf J_{F_a}\,\mathbf J_{F_b}\,\mathbf J_{F_c}\}\!\}
  =\tfrac{k_{FFF}}{2\pi^2}\,\mathrm{tr}_F(T_a\{T_b,T_c\})\,[\lambda_1\lambda_2]$.
- ABJ (broken) case — non-conservation of the current:
  $\mathbf Q\,\mathbf J=\bar\partial\mathbf J-\tfrac{\hbar k}{2\pi^2}\partial\mathbf A\,\partial\mathbf A$
  in a background $\mathbf A$; see [[ac-anomalies-as-central-extensions]].

## Relations

- Spacetime analogue: [[two-virasoro-algebra]]. Both are organized by the
  [[holomorphic-lambda-bracket|$\lambda$-brackets]] and generated by
  [[semi-chiral-superfield|semi-chiral superfields]].
- Under the [[superconformal-deformation-to-voa|superconformal deformation]] it becomes an
  affine Kac–Moody subalgebra of the associated VOA at level $k_{2d}=-\tfrac12 k_F$
  (matching [[scft-voa-correspondence|Beem et al.]]).
