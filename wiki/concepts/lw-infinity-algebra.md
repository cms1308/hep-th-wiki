## Definition

The infinite-dimensional symmetry algebra of gravity in 4d asymptotically flat space
generated by the tower of soft positive-helicity graviton currents, discovered in the
celestial OPEs of gravity amplitudes (Guevara–Himwich–Pate–Strominger 2021) and identified
by Strominger (2021) as (a loop/wedge version of) the classical $w_{1+\infty}$ algebra of
area-preserving diffeomorphisms:

$$[w^p_{\bar m,m},\,w^q_{\bar n,n}]=\bigl(\bar m(q-1)-\bar n(p-1)\bigr)\,w^{p+q-2}_{\bar m+\bar n,\,m+n},$$

with a loop index (denoted $m$ in [[2603.26459|Strominger–Wei 2026]] conventions) making it
$\mathcal Lw_{1+\infty}$, the loop algebra. The flat-space soft-theorem realization lives on
a wedge $p=1,\tfrac32,2,\dots$, $\bar m=1-p,\dots,p-1$.

## Motivation

The gravitational counterpart of the gauge-theory [[s-algebra]]: the symmetry backbone that
any celestial dual of 4d gravity must carry. It is transparent on twistor space and
perturbatively exact in self-dual gravity (Ball–Narayanan–Salzer–Strominger 2021).

## Key formulas

- Flat-space wedge: $p=1,\tfrac32,2,\dots$ with $\bar m=1-p,\dots,p-1$; the $p=2$ level
  contains the supertranslation current and $p=\tfrac52$ the superrotations, embedding the
  [[bms-symmetry|BMS]] tower.
- Turning on a cosmological constant deforms the algebra to the one-parameter
  [[lambda-deformed-w-algebra|$\mathcal L_\Lambda w_{1+\infty}$ algebra]] (Taylor–Zhu 2023;
  Bittleston–Bogna–Heuveline–Kmec–Mason–Skinner 2024), recovering $\mathcal Lw_{1+\infty}$
  at $\Lambda\to0$.

## Relations

- [[s-algebra]] — gauge-theory sibling; minimal coupling to gravity mixes the two towers.
- [[lambda-deformed-w-algebra]] — the $\Lambda\neq0$ deformation, realized in every CFT$_3$
