## Definition

Strongly coupled 4d $\mathcal{N}=2$ SCFTs with Coulomb branch operators of *fractional*
scaling dimension, originally found at special points of Coulomb branches where mutually
non-local dyons become simultaneously massless (Argyres-Douglas 1995, on the $SU(3)$
pure Coulomb branch; generalized by Argyres-Plesser-Seiberg-Witten and Eguchi-Hori-Ito-
Yang). Standard labels: $H_0=(A_1,A_2)$, $H_1=(A_1,A_3)$, $H_2=(A_1,D_4)$, and the
$(G,G')$ series of Cecotti-Neitzke-Vafa. They admit no known $\mathcal{N}=2$ Lagrangian.

## Motivation

The simplest genuinely non-Lagrangian 4d SCFTs — the testing ground for every method
that does not rely on a Lagrangian (Coulomb-branch geometry, indices via RG flows,
bootstrap). $H_0$ is the interacting $\mathcal{N}=2$ SCFT with the smallest known
central charges ($a=\tfrac{43}{120}$, $c=\tfrac{11}{30}$, saturating the bound
$c \ge \tfrac{11}{30}$ of [1509.00033](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.00033)).

## Key formulas

Central charges of the theories appearing as fixed points in
[[2408.02953|Cho-Maruyoshi-Nardoni-Song 2024]]:
$H_0$: $(a,c) = (\tfrac{43}{120}, \tfrac{11}{30})$;
$H_1$: $(\tfrac{11}{24}, \tfrac12)$;
$H_2$: $(\tfrac{7}{12}, \tfrac23)$;
$(A_1,A_4)$: $(\tfrac{67}{84}, \tfrac{17}{21})$;
$(A_1,A_5)$: $(\tfrac{11}{12}, \tfrac{23}{24})$.

## Relations

- Reachable from Lagrangian $\mathcal{N}=1$ gauge theories via
  [[flip-deformation|flip]]-driven supersymmetry-enhancing RG flows (Maruyoshi-Song
  [1606.05632](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.05632),
  [1607.04281](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.04281); Agarwal-Maruyoshi-Song
  [1610.05311](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.05311)) — this gives access to their
  [[superconformal-index|superconformal indices]].
- Their $\mathcal{N}=1$ *deformations* (by Coulomb branch operators) appear throughout
  the [[4d-n1-scft-landscape|landscape]]: the deformed $(A_1,A_4)$ is the
  [[minimal-n1-scft|minimal-known $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFT]], now with two dual Lagrangians;
  the deformed $(A_2,A_3)$ also acquires a Lagrangian
  ([[2408.02953|Cho-Maruyoshi-Nardoni-Song 2024]]).
- Rank-1 $\mathcal{N}=2$ SCFTs are classified by Coulomb-branch geometry — the frame
  against which the [[candidate-rank-1-cb-dimension-5-4|$\Delta_{\rm CB}=5/4$ candidate]] clashes.
