## Definition

Null infinity $\mathscr I$ is the conformal boundary of an asymptotically flat spacetime,
reached along null directions. It is formalized by the Penrose conformal compactification
([[2602.02644|Ruzziconi 2026]] §4.5): Weyl-rescale $ds^2 \to \Omega^2 ds^2$ with
$\Omega \sim r^{-1}$, so the metric extends to a compactified spacetime whose boundary
$\{\Omega = 0,\ \nabla_\mu\Omega \neq 0\}$ consists of future and past null infinity,
$\mathscr I^+$ and $\mathscr I^-$ — null hypersurfaces where outgoing/incoming massless
radiation is registered. In $4d$ Minkowski space, $\mathscr I^+ = \mathbb R \times S^2 =
\{r_\circ \to +\infty\}$ in retarded Bondi coordinates $(u_\circ, r_\circ, z_\circ, \bar
z_\circ)$; each constant-$u_\circ$ cut is a **celestial sphere**.

*Conventions: rectangular Bondi coordinates $(u, r, z, \bar z)$, the topic-canonical choice
of [[carrollian-physics]] ([[2602.02644|Ruzziconi 2026]]), in which cuts of $\mathscr I$ are
flat complex planes rather than round spheres.*

## Motivation

Asymptotically flat spacetimes model isolated gravitating systems; $\mathscr I$ is where
their gravitational-wave emission is read off (Bondi mass loss, news tensor), and where the
asymptotic symmetry analysis yields the [[bms-symmetry|BMS group]] instead of Poincaré. For
holography it is the natural home of the dual theory: $\mathscr I$ is a null hypersurface,
hence a [[carrollian-geometry|Carrollian manifold]], and the [[carrollian-cft|Carrollian CFT]] of [[flat-space-holography]] lives on it in one lower dimension.

## Key formulas

- Minkowski in rectangular Bondi coordinates: $ds^2 = -2\,du\,dr + 2r^2 dz\,d\bar z$, with
  $\mathscr I^+$ at $r \to +\infty$ and $\mathscr I^-$ at $r \to -\infty$. The null geodesics
  generated by $\partial_r$ at fixed $(u,z,\bar z)$ run from $\mathscr I^-$ to $\mathscr I^+$,
  giving an immediate geometric matching between the two. (Round-sphere version:
  $ds^2 = -du_\circ^2 - 2\,du_\circ dr_\circ + \frac{4 r_\circ^2}{(1+z_\circ\bar z_\circ)^2}
  dz_\circ d\bar z_\circ$.)
- Induced geometry at $\mathscr I^+$ (canonical $\Omega = r^{-1}$):
  $$q_{ab}\,dx^a dx^b = 0\,du^2 + 2\,dz\,d\bar z, \qquad
  n^a\partial_a = \lim_{\bar r\to\infty}\, g^{\mu\nu}\nabla_\nu\Omega\,\partial_\mu\big|_{r=\bar r} = \partial_u ,$$
  with $q_{ab}n^b = 0$: a flat [[carrollian-geometry|Carrollian structure]], with Ehresmann
  connection $k_a dx^a = -du$ (so $k_a n^a = -1$).
- Residual freedom $\Omega \to \omega\Omega$ acts as $q_{ab} \to \omega^2 q_{ab}$,
  $n^a \to \omega^{-1} n^a$ — a **conformal Carrollian geometry**, coinciding with the
  "universal structure" of Geroch and Ashtekar (the geometric data common to all
  asymptotically flat spacetimes).
- In Bondi gauge ($g_{rr} = g_{rA} = 0$, $\det g_{AB} = -r^4$), asymptotically flat metrics
  expand near $\mathscr I^+$ as
  $$ds^2 = \Big(\tfrac{2M}{r} + \mathcal O(r^{-2})\Big)du^2 - 2\big(1+\mathcal O(r^{-2})\big)du\,dr
  + \big(r^2 q_{AB} + r\,C_{AB} + \mathcal O(r^0)\big)dx^A dx^B + \ldots$$
  with Bondi mass aspect $M(u,z,\bar z)$, asymptotic shear $C_{AB}$ (trace-free), angular
  momentum aspect $N_A$, and news tensor $N_{AB} = \partial_u C_{AB}$ encoding outgoing
  radiation.
- Bondi mass loss: with $m(u) = \int d^2z\, M$ and matter obeying the averaged null energy
  condition,
  $$m(u_2) - m(u_1) = -\int_{u_1}^{u_2} du \int d^2z\,
  \Big(\tfrac18 N_{AB}N^{AB} + 4\pi G\, T^{m(2)}_{uu}\Big) \le 0 .$$
- Asymptotic symmetries: the extended BMS algebra restricted to $\mathscr I^+$ coincides with
  the conformal Killing vectors of its Carrollian structure,
  $\mathfrak{ebms}_4 \simeq \mathfrak{eCCarr}_3$, and in general dimension
  $\mathfrak{bms}_{d+1} \simeq \mathfrak{CCarr}_d$ — the starting point of Carrollian
  holography.

## Relations

- $\mathscr I$ carries a flat [[carrollian-geometry|Carrollian structure]]; it is however not
  the most general Carrollian manifold — compatibility with the Einstein equations and
  asymptotic flatness forces the intrinsic Carrollian shear to vanish (technically, a weakly
  isolated horizon) ([[2602.02644|Ruzziconi 2026]] §4.5.1).
- Its symmetries realize the [[bms-symmetry|BMS algebra]] $\simeq$
  [[conformal-carrollian-algebra]] ([[bms-conformal-carroll-isomorphism]]).
- The Bondi mass and angular momentum aspects assemble into a Carrollian stress tensor at
  $\mathscr I$ (the [[null-brown-york-stress-tensor]] construction extended to infinity),
  with radiation acting as a source in the flux-balance laws.
- Constant-$u$ cuts are the celestial sphere/plane on which [[celestial-holography]] is
  formulated; [[carrollian-amplitude|Carrollian amplitudes]] are $\mathcal S$-matrix elements
  written in position space $(u, z, \bar z)$ on $\mathscr I$.
- Bulk massless fields induce boundary operators on $\mathscr I$ through their large-$r$
  expansion — the field-theoretic side of [[flat-space-holography]] and
  [[flat-carrollian-limit-correspondence]].
