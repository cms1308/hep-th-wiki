## Definition

In a 6d $\mathcal N=(1,0)$ supergravity with $T$ tensor multiplets, the charges of BPS
strings live in a **unimodular Lorentzian lattice** $\Gamma$ of signature $(1,T)$, equipped
with the **Dirac pairing**

$$v\cdot w=\Omega_{\alpha\beta}\,v^\alpha w^\beta,$$

where $\Omega_{\alpha\beta}$ is the (integral, unimodular) string-charge intersection form
of Lorentzian signature $(+,(-)^T)$. This is the lattice-theoretic backbone of the
[[6d-supergravity-swampland|6d landscape]]: it was formulated by Seiberg-Taylor (2011,
`Seiberg:2011dr`), with quantization refinements by Monnier-Moore (2018), and geometrically
it is the divisor lattice of the F-theory base surface.

## Motivation

Tensor moduli space is $SO(1,T)/SO(T)$, parametrized by a unit vector $J$ with $J\cdot J=1$,
$J^0>0$. A BPS string of charge $Q$ has tension $\propto J\cdot Q$, so physical (positive-
tension) strings occupy the **tensor cone** $\{Q:J\cdot Q\ge 0\}$, dual to the **BPS cone**
generated by the primitive charges $\mathcal C_i$ (each with $\mathcal C_i^2\le 0$). String
tensionless-ness at a moduli-space boundary $J_\partial\cdot Q=0$ is the non-perturbative
data that constrains the landscape. The [[h-string|$H$-string]] $f$ ($f^2=0$, $b_0\cdot f=2$)
and the anomaly vectors $b_0,b_i$ are specific lattice vectors.

## Key formulas

- Pairing: $v\cdot w=\Omega_{\alpha\beta}v^\alpha w^\beta$; $\Gamma$ unimodular, signature
  $(1,T)$.
- Moduli space $SO(1,T)/SO(T)$, $J\cdot J=1$; tension $\propto J\cdot Q\ge 0$.
- Gravitational-anomaly relation $b_0\cdot b_0=9-T$ (so $b_0^2$ turns negative for $T>9$).
- Anomaly sublattice $\mathfrak L=\mathrm{span}(b_0,b_i)$ with Gram matrix
  $\mathcal G_{IJ}=b_I\cdot b_J$, constrained to $n_+^{\mathfrak L}\le 1$,
  $n_-^{\mathfrak L}\le T$.
- Exceptional case $T=9$, $b_0=0$: $\Gamma^{1,9}\simeq U\oplus E_8(-1)$ (even, unimodular).

## Relations

- The pairing signature $n_+\le 1$ is exactly the [[supergravity-block]] "one positive
  eigenvalue" criterion.
- Vectors $b_0,b_i$ fixed by the [[green-schwarz-sagnotti-mechanism]].
- Hosts the [[h-string]] and the generators of a [[non-higgsable-gravity-block]].
- Blowdown $Q\to Q+(\mathcal C\cdot Q)\mathcal C$ reduces the base to a Hirzebruch surface
  $\mathbb F_n$ or $\mathbb P^2$.
