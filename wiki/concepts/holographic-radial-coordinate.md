## Definition

In AdS/CFT the bulk radial coordinate $U$ of [[anti-de-sitter-space]] (Poincaré patch) is
identified with the energy scale of the dual field theory: large $U$ = UV, small $U$ = IR.
Motion in the extra dimension is renormalization-group flow.

## Motivation

In [[hep-th-9711200|Maldacena 1997]], $U = r/\alpha'$ is the Higgs vev / stretched-string mass
held fixed in the [[brane-decoupling-limit]], and the redshift relation
$E_{proper} = \frac{\omega(4\pi gN)^{1/4}}{\sqrt{\alpha'}\,U}$ ties gauge-theory energy
$\omega$ to bulk position. Polyakov independently argued the YM string needs an extra
(Liouville) dimension — this is that dimension.

## Key formulas

$$U \equiv \frac{r}{\alpha'} \sim \text{energy scale},\qquad
E_{proper} = \frac{\omega\,(4\pi gN)^{1/4}}{\sqrt{\alpha'}\,U}$$
