## Definition

The four-point functions of all Kaluza–Klein $\tfrac12$-BPS operators $\mathcal{O}_k$ in $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM (dual to supergravitons on $AdS_5\times S^5$) are, at tree level in $1/N$, generated by a **single** function via a hidden $SO(10,2)$ conformal symmetry that mixes the $AdS_5$ and $S^5$ directions. Promoting each insertion to a twelve-component vector $Z_i=(P_i,t_i)$ combining the spacetime null vector $P_i$ and the R-symmetry polarization $t_i$, the lowest correlator ($k_i=2$) becomes a generating function for all others: individual weights are recovered by Taylor-extracting powers of the R-symmetry variables. Discovered for supergraviton correlators by Caron-Huot–Trinh 2018, with a weak-coupling / Lagrangian-insertion (loop-integrand) version shortly after.

## Motivation

Without it, each $\langle\mathcal{O}_{k_1}\mathcal{O}_{k_2}\mathcal{O}_{k_3}\mathcal{O}_{k_4}\rangle$ must be bootstrapped separately; the hidden symmetry reduces infinitely many correlators to one seed and makes the $\log$-structure (anomalous dimensions of double-trace operators) computable by a single differential operator.

## Key formulas

- Uplift: $Z_i=(P_i,t_i)$, with $P_i\cdot P_j\to Z_i\cdot Z_j$ repackaging spacetime and R-symmetry cross ratios into a single ten-dimensional distance.
- **Defect generalization** ([[giant-graviton-defect|giant graviton]] case): the symmetry is *partially broken* by the 4d defect. One promotes the defect projector rather than the individual heavy points,
$$P_a\!\cdot\!\mathbb{N}\!\cdot\!P_b\ \to\ Z_a\!\cdot\!(\mathbb{N}+\mathbb{M})\!\cdot\!Z_b\quad(a,b=1,2),\qquad H_{k_1k_2}=\frac{\sqrt{k_1k_2}}{2}\,\mathbf{H}\big|_{\lambda_1^{k_1-2}\lambda_2^{k_2-2}},$$
where $\mathbb{N}+\mathbb{M}$ is the 10d projector onto the D3-brane worldvolume $\mathbb{R}\times S^3$; only the light points 1,2 are uplifted.
- In the four-point (bulk) case the symmetry underlies an eighth-order differential operator generating all-loop leading logs; the defect case has a [[giant-graviton-defect-anomalous-dimensions|fourth-order operator]] $\widehat{\mathbf{\Delta}}^{(4)}$ instead.
