## Definition

The **extended vertex algebra** is the enlargement of the Schur
[[scft-voa-correspondence|vertex operator algebra]] of a 4d $\mathcal N=2$ SCFT by the
**extended** (line, surface, domain-wall) operators produced by [[twisted-schur-descent]]. Where
the ordinary VOA is generated by local [[schur-operators]], the extended VA also contains
nonlocal operators whose world volumes extend transverse to a fixed spatial 2-plane.

The **VOA plane** is that plane, $\{x^\pm = 0\}$ in the lightcone coordinates
$x^\pm \doteq \pm x^1 + x^2$, carrying a complex coordinate $z \doteq x^3 - i x^4$. The defining
feature of the extended VA is that correlators of its operators depend meromorphically **only on
$z$** — the location(s) where the extended world volumes cross this plane — exactly as VOA
correlators of local operators do. This is why the extended operators form a *vertex algebra* and
not merely a collection of BPS defects.

## Motivation

Extended operators (Wilson–'t Hooft lines, surface operators) in strongly coupled
$\mathcal N=2$ SCFTs are hard to control. Placing them in $\mathbb T_\pm$-cohomology inherits the
analytic power of the local VOA: their correlators reduce to meromorphic functions of a single
coordinate. The extended VA is the algebraic structure organizing all of them together with the
Schur operators.

## Key formulas

- **Meromorphy.** Descent operators commute with twist translations $\partial_z$ and preserve
  chiral weight $h_{\mathcal O}$, so their correlators are (locally) meromorphic in $z$.
- **Virasoro without a stress tensor.** In the free hypermultiplet the weight-2 operator $T$
  satisfies the Virasoro OPE with central charge $-1$,
  $$T(z)\,T(0)\sim \frac{-1/2}{z^4} + \frac{2T(0)}{z^2} + \frac{\partial_z T(0)}{z},$$
  but fails the primary condition on descent operators (e.g. $T(z)\,\mathbf S[q](0)$ is not the
  standard primary OPE). So $T$ is **not** the stress-energy operator of the extended VA, and the
  extended VA need not be a *conformal* vertex algebra — see
  [[extended-va-conformal-vertex-algebra]].
- **Hatted / boundary-like operators.** The $\widehat{\mathbf S},\widehat{\mathbf W}^\pm$ classes
  carry sign ("cut") discontinuities and behave as boundary/line operators rather than genuine
  vertex operators.
