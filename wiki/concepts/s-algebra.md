## Definition

The infinite-dimensional symmetry algebra of 4d nonabelian gauge theory generated by the
tower of soft positive-helicity gluon currents at celestial dimensions
$\Delta=1,0,-1,\dots$, labeled $p=\frac{3-\Delta}{2}=1,\frac32,2,\dots$ with modes
$S^{p,a}_{\bar m,m}$ in the wedge $|\bar m|<p$. The commutation relations are

$$[S^{p,a}_{\bar m,m},\,S^{q,b}_{\bar n,n}] = -i f^{abc}\,S^{p+q-1,c}_{\bar m+\bar n,\,m+n}.$$

The $p=1$ level is a level-zero Kac–Moody-type current algebra on the celestial sphere,
the current algebra of the leading soft gluon theorem; the higher-$p$ generators come from
subleading soft theorems. Discovered from celestial OPEs
(Guevara–Himwich–Pate–Strominger 2021), it is the gauge-theory sibling of the gravitational
[[lw-infinity-algebra|$w_{1+\infty}$ soft algebra]].

## Motivation

Any holographic dual pair must share its symmetries; the $S$-algebra (and its gravity
counterpart) is the symmetry backbone of [[celestial-holography]], playing the structural
role Brown–Henneaux Virasoro plays for AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$.

## Key formulas

- Leading soft generator as a null-line integral on $\mathscr I^+$:
  $S^{1,a}(z)=-4\pi\int_{\mathscr I^+}du\,F^a_{uz}$, with
  $[S^{1,a}_{0,m},S^{1,b}_{0,n}]=-if^{abc}S^{1,c}_{0,m+n}$
  ([[2601.00096|Sheta–Strominger–Tropper–Wei 2025]] conventions, $g_{YM}=1$).
- Mellin form of the tower: $X^{p,a}_z=N_p\int_{\mathscr I^+}du\,u^{2p-2}F^a_{uz}$,
  $N_p=4\pi i^{2p}/\Gamma(2p-1)$; equivalently soft generators are residues of
  Mellin-transformed amplitudes at $\Delta=3-2p$.
- Conformal-descendant construction ([[2601.00096|Sheta–Strominger–Tropper–Wei 2025]]):
  $SO(4,2)$ invariance plus the *leading* soft theorem alone imply the full algebra — the
  subleading tower is defined iteratively by
  $\bigl[K_{\bar r,r},S^{p,a}_{\bar m,m}\bigr]=(-)^{r-\bar r}\bigl((p-1)+2rm\bigr)S^{p+\frac12,a}_{\bar m+\bar r,m+r}$
  and the commutators follow from Jacobi identities.

## Quantum status

Tree-level exact in classical gauge theory; in generic quantum theories loop corrections
deform the subleading soft theorems (IR-divergent one-loop corrections of Bern–Davies–Nohle
type), and it had been speculated that an uncorrected quantum $S$-algebra could exist only
in twistorial theories without IR divergences. Current evidence:

- **Self-dual Yang–Mills**: verified in the quantum theory by analysis of splitting
  functions ([[2601.00096|Sheta et al. 2025]] citing Ball–Narayanan–Salzer–Strominger
  2021).
- **AdS$_4$/CFT$_3$**: realized by [[light-ray-operators|light transforms]] of the
  boundary nonabelian conserved current and their $SO(3,2)$ descendants
  ([[ads4-s-algebra-from-cft3-light-transforms]], [[2601.00096|Sheta et al. 2025]]); the
  $p=1$ commutator is nonperturbative (Córdova–Shao), the full tower flagged by the
  authors as "potentially well-defined at the quantum level".
- **Planar $\mathcal N=4$ SYM**: the IR-finite hard amplitudes obey the uncorrected
  tree-level soft theorem and tree-exact half-collinear poles at finite coupling, so soft
  insertions represent the undeformed $S$-algebra
  ([[undeformed-s-algebra-planar-n4-sym]], [[2606.08582|Alday–Strominger 2026]]).

## Relations

- Gravity counterpart: [[lw-infinity-algebra]]; its cosmological-constant deformation is the
  [[lambda-deformed-w-algebra|$\mathcal L_\Lambda w_{1+\infty}$ algebra]]. Minimal coupling
  to gravity mixes the $S$- and $w$-towers.
- CFT$_3$ realization: [[light-ray-operators]] (light transform of the global symmetry
  current), seed of the AdS$_4$ statement.
- The wedge and mode conventions vary by presentation; the wiki quotes each paper's
  normalization explicitly.
