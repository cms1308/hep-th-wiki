## Definition

TsT ("T-duality – shift – T-duality") is a solution-generating technique for string backgrounds
with two commuting $U(1)$ isometries (Lunin–Maldacena 2005): T-dualize on one circle, shift the
dual coordinate against the other, and T-dualize back. Applied to string theory on AdS$_3$, TsT
produces the string dual of a **single-trace** [[ttbar-deformation|$T\bar T$]] (or
[[jt-bar-deformation|$J\bar T$]]) deformation of the boundary symmetric-orbifold CFT — the
"TsT/$T\bar T$" and "TsT/$J\bar T$" correspondences.

Single-trace $T\bar T$ differs from the ordinary (double-trace) deformation: the deforming
operator is a single-trace operator of the symmetric-product CFT, so the deformation acts within
each block rather than on the full theory, and the bulk retains an asymptotically-linear-dilaton /
long-string region instead of merely a finite radial cutoff.

## Key formulas

The TsT-deformed background is fixed by the single dimensionless combination of the deformation
parameter and the AdS$_3$ radius; the deformed boundary two-point functions computed on the
worldsheet are the string-theory benchmarks that the field-theory operator constructions must
reproduce — see [[deformed-cft-two-point-function]].

## Relations

The holographic face of the [[ttbar-deformation|$T\bar T$]] / [[jt-bar-deformation|$J\bar T$]]
deformations, connecting them to [[ads-cft-correspondence]]. For $J\bar T$ the natural bulk is a
warped-AdS$_3$ geometry, aligning with the Kerr/CFT motivation. The worldsheet two-point function
via TsT (Cui–Du–Song 2023 for $T\bar T$; the TsT/$J\bar T$ result) is what
[[2507.08588|Chen–Du–Liu–Song 2025]] and [[2511.10557|Chen–Du–Song 2025]] match with their
[[physical-operator-deformed-cft|physical-operator]] correlators.
