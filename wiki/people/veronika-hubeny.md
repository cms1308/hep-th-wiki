## Contributions

The "H" of the [[hrt-formula|HRT formula]]: with Rangamani and Takayanagi she gave the
covariant, time-dependent generalization of holographic entanglement entropy via bulk
extremal surfaces ([[0705.0016|HRT 2007]]). Related work develops the causal-wedge / causal
holographic information, entanglement wedge, and holographic entropy inequalities.

## Key papers

- [[0705.0016|Hubeny-Rangamani-Takayanagi 2007]] — *A Covariant Holographic Entanglement
  Entropy Proposal* (the HRT formula).

## Context

At UC Davis. Long-running collaboration with Mukund Rangamani on holography, black-hole
dynamics, and the geometry of entanglement.
