## Contributions

Theoretical physicist (Tsinghua University, Yau Mathematical Sciences Center) working on
holography beyond AdS: Kerr/CFT, warped-AdS$_3$, and the [[jt-bar-deformation|$J\bar T$]] /
[[ttbar-deformation|$T\bar T$]] deformations and their single-trace
[[tst-holography|TsT holographic]] duals. Co-author of the Kerr/CFT correspondence and of the
single-trace $J\bar T$ / TsT constructions (Apolo–Song). Senior author of the 2025 program that
builds the symmetry-organized [[dressed-operator-deformed-cft|dressed]] and
[[physical-operator-deformed-cft|physical]] operators of deformed CFTs and computes their
correlators.

- [[2507.08588|Chen–Du–Liu–Song 2025]] — Symmetries and operators in $T\bar T$-deformed CFTs.

  CFTs.

  holography (Apolo–Song 2018) — not yet ingested.

## Context

Her deformed-CFT operator work sits at the intersection of integrable irrelevant deformations and
non-AdS holography, and builds technically on the Hamiltonian flow formalism and the nonlocal
conformal symmetry developed with and by [[monica-guica]].
