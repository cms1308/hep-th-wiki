## Contributions

Theoretical physicist; long career spanning 2d gravity, string theory, and quantum chaos. Co-author
of the double-scaling limit of matrix models of 2d gravity (1990) and the analysis showing D-brane
$e^{-1/g_s}$ effects control the $(2g)!$ large-genus growth of the string genus expansion — both
directly reused in the [[jt-gravity-matrix-integral-duality|JT/matrix duality]]. With Maldacena and
Stanford, established the maximal chaos bound and the role of out-of-time-order correlators; with
Stanford, the "double cone" origin of the [[spectral-form-factor|ramp]].

## Key papers

- [[1903.11115|Saad–Shenker–Stanford 2019]] — JT gravity as a matrix integral.

## Context

Richard Herschel Weiland Professor at Stanford (SITP). His 1990 work on nonperturbative
$e^{-1/g_s}$ effects is the historical template for the
[[nonperturbative-completion-jt|D-brane effects]] that produce the JT plateau. Frequent collaborator of [[douglas-stanford]] and
[[phil-saad]].
