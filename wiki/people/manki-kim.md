## Contributions

- Introduced the [[seam-graded-expansion|seam-graded expansion]] for the classical
  [[closed-string-tachyon-vacuum|closed string tachyon vacuum]] equation, showing that grading the
  string field by the number of integrated internal geodesics makes the
  [[fvm-quadratic-integral-equation|FVM quadratic integral equation]] purely algebraic at every order.
- Identified the grade-independent linearized operator $M=2JB$ with tachyon eigenvalue $2$, and showed
  the seam iteration reproduces the genus-0 [[mirzakhani-topological-recursion|Mirzakhani/FVM recursion]].
- Gave numerical evidence (up to level $(0{+}2{+}4)$) that the linearized recursion is unobstructed but
  the tachyon-only expansion diverges, re-confirming
  [[ghost-dilaton-condensation-tachyon-vacuum|ghost-dilaton condensation]].
