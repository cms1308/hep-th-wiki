## Contributions

- Refined the tensor-intersection structure of 6d $\mathcal N=(1,0)$ supergravity and
  identified the distinguished [[h-string|$H$-string]], with a proof that the massless
  spectrum is finite ($T\le 193$, $r(V)\le 480$) — the load-bearing precursor (Kim-Vafa-Xu
  2024, `Kim:2024eoa`) to the supergravity-block program.
- Central-charge bounds on 6d supergravity strings (Kim-Tarazi-Vafa 2019, `Kim:2019vuc`),
  giving $\sum_i c_{\mathfrak g_i}\le 20$ (and $16$ for $T>1$).
- Co-author of the [[supergravity-block|supergravity-block]] framework and the complete
  [[classification-non-higgsable-gravity-blocks|classification of non-Higgsable gravity blocks]]
  ([[2607.05496|Hamada-Jeon-Kim 2026]]).
- Broader work on 6d SCFTs, little string theories, and their partition functions/elliptic
  genera.
