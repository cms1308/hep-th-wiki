## Contributions

Theoretical physicist working at the interface of **theoretical physics and machine learning**. Lead
developer of the [[neural-network-field-theory|NN-FT / NN-QFT correspondence]] — defining Euclidean
(and, via the Osterwalder–Schrader axioms, quantum) field theories directly from neural-network
ensembles, engineering their symmetries, and realizing free-scalar and $\phi^4$ theories inside
ensembles of infinite-width networks (the Gauss-net / Cos-net / Scalar-net examples). Also a longtime
contributor to [[machine-learning-in-string-theory|machine learning for string theory]] — reinforcement
learning on the flux/brane landscape, conjecture generation in the F-theory ensemble, and the NN-metric-
flow / Ricci-flow correspondence.

  from correlators (not yet ingested).
