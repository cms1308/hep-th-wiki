## Contributions

Mathematical physicist specializing in twists of supersymmetric field theories. Co-classified the
space of square-zero supercharges (the [[nilpotence-variety|nilpotence variety]]) and, with Brian
Williams, developed the higher symmetries of [[holomorphic-twist|holomorphically twisted]]
theories — showing in particular that the Beem-et-al
[[scft-voa-correspondence|SCFT/VOA correspondence]] arises as a
[[superconformal-deformation-to-voa|superconformal deformation]] of
the holomorphic twist (Saberi–Williams 2019), the construction systematized in the "Unravelling
the Holomorphic Twist" series.

## Key papers

- Saberi–Williams 2019 — higher symmetries of holomorphic twists; superconformal deformation to
  the VOA (not separately ingested).
- Eager–Saberi–Walcher 2018 — the nilpotence variety of twisting supercharges (not ingested).
- Foundational input to ~[[2509.16737|Bomans-Garner-Williams-Wu 2025]].

## Context

Works with factorization-algebra and derived-geometry methods (Costello–Gwilliam school).
Frequent collaborator of Brian Williams ([[brian-williams]]), Richard Eager, and Johannes Walcher.
