## Contributions

Theoretical physicist and mathematician working across string theory, topological quantum field
theory, and low-dimensional topology, and a leading figure in **rigorous machine learning for
mathematics**. Best known in the [[machine-learning-in-string-theory|ML-for-strings]] context for the
reinforcement-learning approach to the **UNKNOT** problem — recasting it as braid-word sequence
classification and training agents that produce human-checkable unknotting certificates — and for
applying RL / Bayesian optimization to the **smooth 4d Poincaré conjecture (SPC4)**, eliminating all
but two of 3375 candidate counterexample pairs. His "search hard, verify trivially" certificate
pattern is the template for ML results mathematicians accept at full rigor.
