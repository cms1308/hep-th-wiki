## Contributions

Cornell theorist working on 2d CFT, quantum gravity, and holography. In the line of work
relevant to this wiki he develops the topological and CFT machinery for the exact path
integral of pure AdS$_3$ gravity. He defines [[conformal-turaev-viro-theory|Conformal Turaev-Viro theory]] — a triangulation-based dual of [[virasoro-tqft|Virasoro TQFT]] — and
proves that its partition function is the modular $S$-transform of $|Z_{\rm Vir}|^2$
in [[2507.11652|Hartman 2025]], adapting Roberts' [[chain-mail-formalism|chain-mail formalism]] to the continuous Virasoro spectrum.

## Key papers

- [[2507.11652|Hartman 2025]] — *Conformal Turaev-Viro Theory* (defines CTV; the
  chain-mail/Fourier-transform identity relating triangulations to $|Z_{\rm Vir}|^2$, and
  new $S$-transforms of squared [[virasoro-6j-symbol|Virasoro crossing kernels]]).

## Context

Earlier work spans holographic entanglement, the CFT derivation of the modular bootstrap and
Cardy formula, chaos and the Regge limit, and the eigenstate thermalization / ensemble picture
of gravity (the Chandra-Collier-Hartman-Maloney "gravity = ensemble of CFTs" program), which
supplies the physical motivation for the CTV construction.
