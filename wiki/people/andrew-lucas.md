## Contributions

Condensed-matter and quantum-dynamics theorist at the University of Colorado Boulder. In this wiki, co-author of the [[entropic-order]] program, contributing the lattice-model, Monte Carlo, and quantum-information / computational-complexity analyses — the explicit local models (the bosonic checkerboard solid, the [[arithmetic-ising-model|Arithmetic Ising Model]]), the [[entropic-topological-order]] construction, and the spin-glass complexity corollary.

## Context

Brings the lattice / condensed-matter and quantum-information side to a program whose other half is relativistic QFT (see [[zohar-komargodski]]). The collaboration's premise — that a physical mechanism visible in $^3$He (the [[pomeranchuk-effect|Pomeranchuk effect]]) and in large-$N$ CFTs can be realized in simple local lattice models — is exactly the bridge between those communities.
