## Contributions

Central figure of the Carrollian route to [[flat-space-holography]]: co-developed the
Carrollian holography dictionary at [[null-infinity]], the notion of
[[carrollian-amplitude|Carrollian amplitudes]], the invertible transform establishing
[[carrollian-celestial-equivalence]], the position-space
[[flat-space-limit-of-witten-diagrams|flat limit of Witten diagrams]] realizing the
[[flat-carrollian-limit-correspondence]], the reformulation of
[[soft-graviton-theorems|soft theorems]] as Carrollian stress-tensor Ward identities, and
Carrollian OPEs. Author of the comprehensive review
[[2602.02644|Carrollian Physics and Holography (2026)]]. Earlier work on asymptotic symmetries
and charges in gauge theories and
gravity ([[bms-symmetry|BMS]] flux-balance laws).
