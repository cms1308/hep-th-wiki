## Contributions

Theoretical physicist at Seikei University working on supersymmetric field theories.
Co-initiator of two threads central to the
[[4d-n1-scft-landscape|$\mathcal{N}=1$ landscape program]]:

- **Supersymmetry-enhancing RG flows**: with Jaewon Song, discovered that
  $\mathcal{N}=1$ [[flip-deformation|flip]] deformations of gauge theories can flow to
  $\mathcal{N}=2$ [[argyres-douglas-theories|Argyres-Douglas theories]]
  ([1606.05632](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.05632),
  [1607.04281](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.04281); with Agarwal
  [1610.05311](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.05311)) — giving Lagrangian access to
  non-Lagrangian indices.
- **The landscape program**: co-author of the pilot $SU(2)$ landscape
  (Maruyoshi-Nardoni-Song, [1806.08353](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08353)) and of the
  7,346-entry landscape [[2408.02953|Cho-Maruyoshi-Nardoni-Song 2024]].

## Key papers

- [[2408.02953|Cho-Maruyoshi-Nardoni-Song 2024]] — Large Landscape of 4d SCFTs from
  Small Gauge Theories.
- [1606.05632](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.05632) — Enhancement of supersymmetry via
  renormalization group flow (with Song).

## Context

Long-term collaborator of [[jaewon-song]] and Emily Nardoni on the classification of
4d $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFTs by deformation.
