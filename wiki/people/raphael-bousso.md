## Contributions

Formulated the [[bousso-bound|covariant entropy bound]] and the light-sheet construction
(1999), the sharpest general statement of the holographic principle. This bound supplied the
conceptual engine for the covariant [[hrt-formula|HRT]] holographic entanglement entropy
proposal. Also works on the holographic principle in cosmology, the multiverse measure
problem, and (later) the quantum focusing conjecture and quantum null energy condition.

## Key papers

- Bousso 1999 — *A Covariant Entropy Conjecture* (the Bousso bound / light-sheets) — not yet
  a wiki page.

## Context

At UC Berkeley. His entropy bound underpins much of the modern entropy-and-geometry program,
including [[holographic-entanglement-entropy|holographic entanglement entropy]].
