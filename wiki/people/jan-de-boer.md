## Contributions

Worked out the finite-$N$ structure of the chiral-primary spectrum of the D1–D5 CFT, extracting it from the grand-canonical (residue) structure of the [[symmetric-orbifold-cft|symmetric-orbifold]] partition function (1998). This sharpened the [[stringy-exclusion-principle]] into a precise finite-$N$ statement and is a direct antecedent of the [[grand-canonical-residue-expansion]] used in the AdS$_3$ giant-graviton expansion.

## Key papers

- de Boer (1998), "Six-dimensional supergravity on $S^3\times \mathrm{AdS}_3$ and 2d conformal field theory" — grand-canonical structure of finite-$N$ chiral primaries (not yet ingested).
