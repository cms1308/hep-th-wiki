## Contributions

Theoretical physicist at the Institute for Advanced Study, one of the central figures in supersymmetric field theory, dualities, and — more recently — the modern theory of generalized global symmetries. Best known for Seiberg duality of $\mathcal N=1$ gauge theories and (with Witten) the exact low-energy solution of $\mathcal N=2$ gauge theory. In the $2+1$d / topological-order context relevant to this wiki, he co-developed [[one-form-global-symmetry|generalized (one-form) global symmetries]] and their gauging, the anomaly classification of one-form symmetries and the minimal TQFTs $\mathcal A^{n,p}$, and the web of $2+1$d boson/fermion dualities.
