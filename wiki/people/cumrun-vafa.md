## Contributions

- Originated the [[swampland-program]] (hep-th/0509212, 2005): the idea that most
  consistent-looking effective field theories admit no quantum-gravity completion, with
  finiteness criteria as the first proposed tests.
- Co-author of the [[weak-gravity-conjecture|Weak Gravity Conjecture]]
  ([[hep-th-0601001|Arkani-Hamed-Motl-Nicolis-Vafa 2006]]).
- Microscopic counting of black hole entropy with Strominger
  ([hep-th/9601029](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9601029)) — the first string-theoretic
  derivation of the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy for BPS black holes.
- F-theory ([hep-th/9602022](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9602022)); topological string
  theory and Gopakumar-Vafa invariants.
- Co-author (Kim-Vafa-Xu 2024) of the non-perturbative [[h-string|$H$-string]] constraints
  and finiteness proof for 6d $\mathcal N=(1,0)$ supergravity, which underpin the
  [[supergravity-block|supergravity-block]] construction of the
  [[6d-supergravity-swampland|6d landscape]] ([[2607.05496|Hamada-Jeon-Kim 2026]]).
