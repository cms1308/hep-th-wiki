## Contributions

Theoretical physicist working on low-dimensional gravity and wormholes. First author of the
[[jt-gravity-matrix-integral-duality|JT-gravity/matrix-integral duality]] and co-author of the
earlier "double cone" analysis relating the [[spectral-form-factor|spectral form factor ramp]] to a
wormhole geometry, and of the "half-wormhole" attempts to understand
[[factorization-vs-wormholes|factorization]] in a single realization.

## Key papers

- [[1903.11115|Saad–Shenker–Stanford 2019]] — JT gravity as a matrix integral.

## Context

PhD at Stanford (SITP) with [[stephen-shenker]] and [[douglas-stanford]], subsequently at the
Institute for Advanced Study. Central to the JT-gravity / ensemble-averaging program.
