## Contributions

Dutch theoretical physicist (Utrecht), Nobel laureate 1999 (with Veltman, for renormalizability
of gauge theories). For this wiki two threads are central: (1) the
[[large-n-thooft-limit|large-$N$ expansion]] (1974) — gauge theory diagrams organize into a
string-like genus expansion, whose dual string [[hep-th-9711200|Maldacena 1997]] finally
identified; (2) the [[holographic-principle]] (1993, with Susskind's refinement) — the idea
that quantum gravity in a region is encoded on its boundary, realized concretely by
[[ads-cft-correspondence]].

## Key papers

- A Planar Diagram Theory for Strong Interactions (1974) — the large-$N$ limit.
- Dimensional Reduction in Quantum Gravity (gr-qc/9310026) — the holographic principle.

## Context

His large-$N$ program and holography conjecture are the two conceptual ancestors of
[[ads-cft-correspondence]].
