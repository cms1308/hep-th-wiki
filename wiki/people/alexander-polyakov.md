## Contributions

One of the founders of modern quantum and conformal field theory. Conjectured in 1970 that critical points of statistical systems have emergent **conformal** symmetry — the idea underlying the whole CFT program and specifically the [[emergent-conformal-symmetry-3d-ising|3D Ising conformal symmetry]] tested by [[fuzzy-sphere-regularization]]. Also foundational to 2D CFT, the conformal bootstrap idea, Polyakov loops, the Polyakov action and path integral for strings, and instanton/confinement physics.

## Key papers

- "Conformal Symmetry of Critical Fluctuations" (1970) — the emergent-conformal-symmetry conjecture.
- (Elsewhere) Belavin–Polyakov–Zamolodchikov 1984 — infinite-dimensional conformal symmetry in 2D QFT.

## Context

His conjecture on emergent conformal symmetry went untested *directly* in $d\ge3$ for half a century, until the fuzzy-sphere realization of the [[3d-ising-cft]] (see [[fuzzy-sphere-ising-spectrum]]).
