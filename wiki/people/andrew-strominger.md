## Contributions

- **Celestial holography / IR structure of gravity**: architect of the "IR triangle"
  connecting soft theorems, asymptotic ([[bms-symmetry|BMS]]) symmetries, and memory effects
  (2013–17); with Pasterski–Shao, celestial amplitudes; with Guevara–Himwich–Pate, the soft
  symmetry towers — the [[s-algebra]] of gauge theory and the
  [[lw-infinity-algebra|$w_{1+\infty}$ algebra]] of gravity (2021). Currently extending soft
  algebras to AdS$_4$/CFT$_3$ via [[light-ray-operators]]
  ([[2601.00096|Sheta–Strominger–Tropper–Wei 2025]],
  [[2603.26459|Strominger–Wei 2026]]) and to quantum-exact status in planar
  $\mathcal N=4$ SYM ([[2606.08582|Alday–Strominger 2026]]).
- **Black holes in string theory**: with [[cumrun-vafa|Vafa]], the first microscopic
  counting of black hole entropy (1996) via the D1–D5 system — a key precursor of
  [[ads-cft-correspondence|AdS/CFT]]; black $p$-brane solutions with Horowitz (1991).
- **Kerr/CFT, dS/CFT** and other boundary-symmetry approaches to holography beyond AdS.
