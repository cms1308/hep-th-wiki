## Contributions

Mathematical physicist working on the algebraic and complex-geometric structure of
[[holomorphic-twist|holomorphically twisted]] field theories. Central to the program that
realizes the infinite-dimensional symmetry algebras of higher-dimensional holomorphic QFT — the
higher [[two-virasoro-algebra|Virasoro]] and [[higher-kac-moody-algebra|Kac–Moody]] algebras —
as derived ($L_\infty$) algebras, and that identifies the 4d conformal anomalies $a,c$ with their
central extensions ([[ac-anomalies-as-central-extensions]]). Also worked out the six-dimensional
analogue (four central extensions matching the type-A and three type-B Weyl anomalies).

## Context

Works at the interface of factorization algebras (Costello–Gwilliam), vertex algebras, and
supersymmetric QFT. Frequent collaborator of Ingmar Saberi ([[ingmar-saberi]]), Kevin Costello,
and Owen Gwilliam.
