## Contributions

Statistical and conformal field theorist. Established the finite-size [[state-operator-correspondence]] on the cylinder — the relation $\delta E_n=(v/R)\Delta_n$ between the energy spectrum of a critical quantum system on a sphere/cylinder and CFT scaling dimensions (1984–85) — which is the theoretical backbone of both 2D critical-chain studies and the 3D [[fuzzy-sphere-regularization]] program. Also known for Cardy's formula for the density of states, boundary CFT (Cardy states), and the conjectured $c$-theorem in four dimensions.

## Key papers

- "Conformal Invariance and Universality in Finite-Size Scaling" (1984) — the finite-size state-operator relation.
- Cardy (1985) — operator content from finite-size spectra.

## Context

His finite-size scaling is exactly the tool the fuzzy-sphere construction lifts from $d=2$ (chains on $S^1$) to $d=3$ (the [[3d-ising-cft]] on $S^2$), turning energies into scaling dimensions (see [[fuzzy-sphere-ising-spectrum]]).
