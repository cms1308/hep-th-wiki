## Contributions

Theoretical physicist working on **machine learning for string theory**, particularly the string
landscape and numerical geometry. An early contributor to search over string vacua (genetic
algorithms and reinforcement learning), and a lead author of **`cymetric`** — the Kähler-potential-
correction PINN package that computes numerical Ricci-flat Calabi–Yau metrics on complete-intersection
and Kreuzer–Skarke manifolds anywhere in moduli space, modeling $J_{\rm CY}=J_{\rm ref}+\partial\bar\partial\phi_\theta$
against a Monge–Ampère surrogate loss. Also authored a broadly cited review of ML applied to the
landscape.
