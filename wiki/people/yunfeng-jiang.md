## Contributions

Integrability and exact correlators in $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM. Computed exact three-point functions of determinant ([[giant-graviton|giant graviton]]) operators and their weak-coupling four-point functions via integrable boundary states / $g$-functions, the data against which the strong-coupling [[giant-graviton-correlators|defect approach]] is checked. Co-author of the reformulation of giant graviton correlators as [[giant-graviton-defect|defect]] systems.

## Context

At Southeast University / the Peng Huanwu Center for Fundamental Theory. Bridges integrability (TBA, $g$-functions, boundary states) and the holographic bootstrap; collaborator of [[xinan-zhou]] and [[junding-chen]].
