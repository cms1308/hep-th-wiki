## Contributions

Holographic and defect correlators in $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM. First author of the program recasting [[giant-graviton|giant graviton]] correlators as [[giant-graviton-defect|zero-dimensional defect]] systems — deriving the defect fluctuation spectrum from the D3-brane action, running the [[analytic-defect-bootstrap]], and establishing the partially broken [[ten-dimensional-hidden-conformal-symmetry|10d hidden symmetry]] that generates all correlators from one seed.
