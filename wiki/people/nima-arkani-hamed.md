## Contributions

- Co-author of the [[weak-gravity-conjecture|Weak Gravity Conjecture]]
  ([[hep-th-0601001|Arkani-Hamed-Motl-Nicolis-Vafa 2006]]).
- Large extra dimensions with Dimopoulos and Dvali
  ([hep-ph/9803315](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9803315),
  [hep-ph/9807344](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9807344)) — the framework that makes
  naturally tiny gauge couplings ($g \sim 10^{-15}$, sub-millimeter forces) a live
  phenomenological target for the WGC's cutoff bound.
- [[extranatural-inflation]] with Cheng, Creminelli, Randall
  ([hep-th/0301218](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0301218)) — the 5d Wilson-line inflaton
  model his own WGC paper then parametrically excluded.
- Positivity/causality bounds on higher-dimension operators with Adams, Dubovsky, Nicolis,
  Rattazzi ([hep-th/0602178](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0602178)), connected to the WGC
  through the $F^4$-corrected extremality bound
  ([[f4-corrected-extremality-bound]]).
- Scattering-amplitude geometry (the amplituhedron, with Trnka).
