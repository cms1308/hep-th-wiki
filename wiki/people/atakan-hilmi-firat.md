## Contributions

- Gave the first explicit closed-form local coordinates / mapping radii for the hyperbolic cubic
  [[hyperbolic-string-vertices|string vertex]] (2021), solving the Fuchsian equation on the
  three-punctured sphere in terms of hypergeometric functions.
- With Valdes-Meller, formulated the [[mirzakhani-topological-recursion|topological recursion]] for
  hyperbolic string vertices — generating all $V_{0,n}$ from $V_{0,3}$ — and reduced the classical
  [[closed-string-field-theory|closed SFT]] equation of motion to the
  [[fvm-quadratic-integral-equation|FVM quadratic integral equation]], solving the analogous stubbed
  scalar QFT exactly.

Works on [[closed-string-field-theory|closed string field theory]] and the geometry of hyperbolic
