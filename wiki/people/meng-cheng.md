## Contributions

Condensed-matter theorist (Yale) working on topological order, symmetry-enriched topological phases, symmetry fractionalization, and the interplay of generalized symmetries with lattice models. His earlier work recasting the [[abelian-hierarchy-construction|Abelian hierarchy construction]] as a stack-and-condense (gauging/enrichment) sequence, and on bosonic/fermionic/electronic topological theories, is the technical backbone of the proliferation-transition framework.

## Context

Bridges the categorical anyon-condensation literature and the field-theoretic generalized-symmetry viewpoint; contributes the topological-order / [[symmetry-fractionalization-enrichment|symmetry-fractionalization]] side of the [[topological-phase-transitions-2plus1d]] program.
