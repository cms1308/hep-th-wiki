## Contributions

Field theorist at the Simons Center for Geometry and Physics (Stony Brook). In the context of this wiki, the central figure behind **high-temperature symmetry breaking in QFT**: the multicritical large-$N$ constructions in which a symmetry stays broken up to $T\to\infty$ (see [[high-temperature-order-in-qft]]), which motivated and became the field-theory face of the [[entropic-order]] program. Co-author of both entropic-order papers, contributing the QFT and large-$N$ free-energy analyses.

## Key papers

- [[2503.22789|Han–Huang–Komargodski–Lucas–Popov 2025]] — Entropic Order (mechanism, lattice models, large-$N$ QFT, superconductivity).
- [[2512.07980|Huang–Komargodski–Lucas–Popov–Sulejmanpasic 2025]] — Minimal Models of Entropic Order (the [[arithmetic-ising-model|Arithmetic Ising Model]]).

## Context

His earlier work on persistent / inverse symmetry breaking in CFTs (the Chai–Komargodski line, and a local unitary $d=2$ model with Hawashin) established that high-$T$ order exists in UV-complete relativistic theories. The entropic-order program grew out of the question of whether the same physics is available to simple local lattice models, and identified the shared ingredient — an unbounded local Hilbert space that carries the ordering entropy. Also widely known for the proof of the 4d $a$-theorem (with Schwimmer) — see [[hofman-maldacena-bound]] and the $3c-2a$ line for adjacent appearances in the wiki.
