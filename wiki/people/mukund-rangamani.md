## Contributions

The "R" of the [[hrt-formula|HRT formula]]: with Hubeny and Takayanagi he gave the covariant
extremal-surface prescription for holographic entanglement entropy ([[0705.0016|HRT 2007]]).
Also works on fluid/gravity correspondence, holographic hydrodynamics, and entanglement-wedge
reconstruction.

## Key papers

- [[0705.0016|Hubeny-Rangamani-Takayanagi 2007]] — *A Covariant Holographic Entanglement
  Entropy Proposal* (the HRT formula).
