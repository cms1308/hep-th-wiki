## Contributions

Works on string theory and holography, including the microscopic structure of AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$. Co-author of the bulk prescription reproducing the finite-$N$ chiral-primary spectrum of symmetric orbifolds from a sum over $(\mathrm{AdS}_3\times\mathrm{S}^3)/\mathbb{Z}_k$ orbifold geometries ([[ads3-giant-graviton-expansion]]).
