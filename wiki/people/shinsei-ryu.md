## Contributions

Co-proposed the [[ryu-takayanagi-formula|Ryu-Takayanagi formula]] for holographic
entanglement entropy with Tadashi Takayanagi ([[hep-th-0603001|Ryu-Takayanagi 2006]]). Also
known for the classification of topological insulators and superconductors (the "tenfold way")
and for entanglement diagnostics of topological phases.

## Key papers

- [[hep-th-0603001|Ryu-Takayanagi 2006]] — *Holographic Derivation of Entanglement Entropy
  from AdS/CFT* (the RT formula).

## Context

Works at the interface of condensed matter, quantum information, and holography.
