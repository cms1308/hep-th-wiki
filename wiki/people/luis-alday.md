## Contributions

- **Amplitude/Wilson-loop duality**: with Maldacena (2007), computed planar
  $\mathcal N=4$ gluon amplitudes at strong coupling via minimal surfaces, revealing the
  duality with null polygonal Wilson loops — the origin of
  [[amplitude-wilson-loop-duality]] and, with Gaiotto–Maldacena–Sever–Vieira (2010), of the
  [[wilson-loop-ope|Wilson-loop OPE]] in flux-tube eigenstates.
- **Flat-space limits of AdS/CFT**: with Nocchi, Ruzziconi and Yelleshpur Srikant (2024),
  showed flat limits of Witten diagrams reproduce Carrollian amplitudes diagram by diagram
  ([[flat-space-limit-of-witten-diagrams]], [[flat-carrollian-limit-correspondence]]).
- **Soft algebras**: with [[andrew-strominger|Strominger]] (2026), the soft/hard
  factorization of planar $\mathcal N=4$ amplitudes and the argument that the
  [[s-algebra]] represented on the IR-finite hard amplitudes is undeformed at finite
  coupling ([[undeformed-s-algebra-planar-n4-sym]]).
- Analytic conformal bootstrap and large-spin expansions; AdS virtual corrections to
  holographic correlators.
