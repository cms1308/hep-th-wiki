## Contributions

Mathematician (1977–2017), Fields Medalist (2014), known for work on the geometry and dynamics of
Riemann surfaces and their moduli spaces. Her recursion for
[[weil-petersson-volumes|Weil–Petersson volumes]] $V_{g,n}(b_1,\dots,b_n)$ of moduli spaces of
bordered hyperbolic surfaces (2007) is the mathematical engine behind
the [[jt-gravity-matrix-integral-duality|JT-gravity/matrix-integral duality]]: after a Laplace
transform it coincides with the
[[eynard-orantin-topological-recursion|Eynard–Orantin topological recursion]] for the
[[jt-spectral-curve|JT spectral curve]].

## Key papers

- Mirzakhani (2007), *Simple geodesics and Weil–Petersson volumes of moduli spaces of bordered
  Riemann surfaces* — the volume recursion (not separately ingested).

## Context

First woman and first Iranian to receive the Fields Medal. Professor at Stanford. Her volume
recursion, proved equivalent to topological recursion by Eynard–Orantin, is what let
[[1903.11115|Saad–Shenker–Stanford]] identify JT gravity with a matrix model.
