## Contributions

Mathematical physicist specializing in random matrix theory and integrable systems. Streamlined the
matrix-model loop equations into a residue recursion (2004) and, with Nicolas Orantin, generalized
it to the [[eynard-orantin-topological-recursion|topological recursion]] on an arbitrary spectral
curve (2007) — a framework that computes the all-genus correlators of a matrix model from a single
curve. Eynard and Orantin also proved that Mirzakhani's
[[weil-petersson-volumes|Weil–Petersson volume]] recursion is exactly topological recursion for the
curve $y=\frac{\sin 2\pi z}{4\pi}$, the identity that underlies the
[[jt-gravity-matrix-integral-duality|JT/matrix duality]].
