## Contributions

Theoretical physicist at KAIST working on superconformal field theories: the
[[superconformal-index|superconformal index]] and its Cardy-like limit, dense-spectrum
SCFTs, and swampland-type constraints on CFTs.

- Co-derived the [[supersymmetric-cardy-formula-4d|4d supersymmetric Cardy formula]]
  (Kim-Kim-Song, [1904.03455](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.03455)), connecting the index
  asymptotics to BPS black hole entropy in $AdS_5$.
- Tested the [[nakayama-nomura-wgc-bound|AdS WGC bound]] in dense-spectrum SCFTs
  (Agarwal-Song [1912.12881](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.12881), Agarwal-Lee-Song
  [2007.16165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.16165)).
- Senior author of the [[modified-ads-wgc-3c-2a|modified AdS WGC]] proposal
  ([[2308.01717|Cho-Choi-Lee-Song 2023]]).
- Senior author of the [[4d-n1-scft-landscape|$\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFT landscape program]]:
  7,346 fixed points from ten small seed gauge theories, including a Lagrangian for the
  [[minimal-n1-scft|minimal-known $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFT]]
  ([[2408.02953|Cho-Maruyoshi-Nardoni-Song 2024]]); co-discoverer of the
  supersymmetry-enhancing RG flows to [[argyres-douglas-theories|Argyres-Douglas theories]]
  behind it (with [[kazunobu-maruyoshi]],
  [1606.05632](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.05632)).
- Senior author of the complete simple-gauge-group large-$N$ classification and its
  [[large-n-universality-classes|Type I/II/III universality]]
  ([[2510.19136|Cho-Lee-Song 2025]]), continuing the
  [[dense-spectrum-scft|dense-spectrum]] program (Agarwal-Lee-Song
  [2007.16165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.16165)).

## Context

The through-line of the work ingested so far: use exactly computable protected quantities
(index, anomalies) to make quantum-gravity statements about CFTs that need not have
weakly coupled duals.
