## Contributions

Theoretical condensed-matter physicist at Perimeter Institute; a conceptual lead and corresponding author of the [[fuzzy-sphere-regularization]] program. Co-designed the scheme that realizes the [[3d-ising-cft|3D Ising transition]] on $S^2\times\mathbb R$ via [[lowest-landau-level-projection]] and demonstrated the [[state-operator-correspondence]] directly, opening a general microscopic route to 3D conformal field theories.

## Key papers

- [[2210.13482|Zhu-Han-Huffman-Hofmann-He 2022]] — founding fuzzy-sphere Ising paper (see [[fuzzy-sphere-ising-spectrum]]).

## Context

Works on quantum criticality, deconfined criticality, and topological phases. The fuzzy-sphere program he co-leads has since been extended (in the broader literature) to $O(N)$ Wilson–Fisher, defect CFT, and OPE-coefficient extraction.
