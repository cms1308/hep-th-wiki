## Contributions

Theorist of 4d $\mathcal N=2$ superconformal field theories and their Coulomb-branch geometry.
Best known for the discovery of the Argyres–Douglas theories — non-Lagrangian $\mathcal N=2$
SCFTs with mutually non-local massless states — and for the systematic classification of
$\mathcal N=2$ Coulomb-branch geometries. In the [[scft-voa-correspondence]] program he,
with Matteo Lotito and Mitch Weaver, extended the vertex operator algebra of local
[[schur-operators]] to an [[extended-vertex-algebra]] of line, surface, and domain-wall operators
via [[twisted-schur-descent]].
