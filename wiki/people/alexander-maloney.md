## Contributions

Theorist working on quantum gravity, holography, and the gravitational path integral,
particularly 3d gravity and its relation to 2d CFT / ensemble averaging. Co-author of the
[[imaginary-distance-bound|imaginary distance bound]], which reads Euclidean scalar wormholes as
a limit on imaginary continuation of couplings and unifies the
[[weak-gravity-conjecture|WGC]] and the [[kontsevich-segal-witten-criterion|KSW criterion]].

## Key papers

- [[2605.05336|Maldacena-Maloney-McPeak 2026]] — Wormholes and the imaginary distance bound.

## Context

Based at Syracuse University. Known for work on the AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$ partition function and
averaging over CFTs; the wormhole/ensemble themes there connect to the
[[factorization-vs-wormholes|factorization puzzle]].
