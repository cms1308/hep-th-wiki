## Contributions

Theoretical/computational condensed-matter physicist at Westlake University; corresponding author and numerics lead of the founding [[fuzzy-sphere-regularization]] paper. Built the fuzzy-sphere Ising model and carried out the [[exact-diagonalization]] and DMRG computations that resolved the [[3d-ising-cft|3D Ising]] spectrum.

## Key papers

- [[2210.13482|Zhu-Han-Huffman-Hofmann-He 2022]] — fuzzy-sphere Ising state-operator correspondence (see [[fuzzy-sphere-ising-spectrum]]).

## Context

Works on strongly-correlated systems, fractional quantum Hall physics, and tensor-network / exact-diagonalization methods — the numerical background that made the fuzzy-sphere construction feasible.
