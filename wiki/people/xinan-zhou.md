## Contributions

Holographic correlators and the analytic bootstrap. Co-developer (with Rastelli) of the Mellin-amplitude program for $AdS_5\times S^5$ supergraviton four-point functions, and of the defect bootstrap — including the $p=-1$ ($\mathcal{N}=4$ on $\mathbb{RP}^4$) case that is the immediate methodological predecessor of the [[giant-graviton-correlators|giant graviton defect approach]]. Senior author of the program recasting [[giant-graviton|giant graviton]] correlators as [[giant-graviton-defect|$p=0$ defect]] systems.

## Context

At KITS/UCAS and the Peng Huanwu Center. Works on the [[analytic-defect-bootstrap]] and the [[ten-dimensional-hidden-conformal-symmetry]] as tools for AdS/CFT correlators; frequent collaborator of [[yunfeng-jiang]] and [[junding-chen]].
