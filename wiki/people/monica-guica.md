## Contributions

Theoretical physicist (IPhT Saclay) who introduced the
[[jt-bar-deformation|$J\bar T$ deformation]] (2017) as the solvable $(1,2)$ deformation
preserving left-moving conformal
symmetry, and who developed much of the understanding of the surviving *nonlocal* conformal
symmetry of [[ttbar-deformation|$T\bar T$]] / $J\bar T$-deformed CFTs — via mixed boundary
conditions in AdS$_3$ gravity and flowed/field-dependent Virasoro generators
(Guica–Monten; Georgescu–Guica). This nonlocal symmetry ([[nonlocal-conformal-symmetry-deformed-cft]])
and the Hamiltonian flow it lives in are the foundation the 2025 operator constructions build on.

## Context

Also a contributor to Kerr/CFT and warped-AdS$_3$ holography, the setting that motivates the
$J\bar T$ deformation as a boundary dual with reduced $SL(2,\mathbb R)_L\times U(1)_R$ symmetry.
Frequent collaborator-adjacent to [[wei-song]]'s deformed-CFT program.
