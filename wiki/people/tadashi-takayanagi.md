## Contributions

Co-originator of holographic entanglement entropy. With Shinsei Ryu he proposed the
[[ryu-takayanagi-formula|Ryu-Takayanagi formula]] $S_A=\mathrm{Area}(\gamma_A)/4G_N$
([[hep-th-0603001|Ryu-Takayanagi 2006]]), and with Hubeny and Rangamani its covariant
generalization, the [[hrt-formula|HRT formula]] ([[0705.0016|HRT 2007]]). His broader program
develops entanglement as a probe of holographic geometry (AdS/BCFT, holographic complexity,
"gravity from entanglement").

## Key papers

- [[hep-th-0603001|Ryu-Takayanagi 2006]] — *Holographic Derivation of Entanglement Entropy
  from AdS/CFT* (the RT formula).
- [[0705.0016|Hubeny-Rangamani-Takayanagi 2007]] — *A Covariant Holographic Entanglement
  Entropy Proposal* (the HRT formula).

## Context

Based at the Yukawa Institute (Kyoto). The RT/HRT papers are among the most cited results in
holography and seeded the [[holographic-entanglement-entropy|entanglement/geometry]] program.
