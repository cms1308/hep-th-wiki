## Contributions

Theorist working on the gravitational path integral, JT gravity, and black-hole microstate
counting. Lead work on casting path-integral consistency as
[[gravitational-path-integral-positivity|positivity of universe-state inner products]], used to
derive the axionic [[weak-gravity-conjecture|weak gravity conjecture]] with a precise
coefficient.

## Key papers

- [[2605.05305|Di Ubaldo-Iliesiu-Lin-Yan 2026]] — Positivity of the gravitational path integral
  implies the axionic weak gravity conjecture.

## Context

At UC Berkeley. Prior work connects to the [[jt-gravity|JT gravity]] / matrix-integral program
([[1903.11115|Saad-Shenker-Stanford 2019]]) and the counting of black-hole states from the
Euclidean path integral.
