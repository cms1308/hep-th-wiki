## Contributions

Theoretical physicist working on quantum chaos, black holes, and low-dimensional gravity. Central
figure in the modern understanding of [[jt-gravity|JT gravity]] and the SYK model: the maximal
chaos bound and out-of-time-order correlators (with Maldacena and Shenker), the Schwarzian dual of
near-$AdS_2$ (Maldacena–Stanford–Yang), the "double cone" wormhole behind the
[[spectral-form-factor|ramp]], and the [[jt-gravity-matrix-integral-duality|matrix-integral duality]]
of JT gravity. Later extended the duality to all Altland–Zirnbauer symmetry classes (Stanford–Witten).
