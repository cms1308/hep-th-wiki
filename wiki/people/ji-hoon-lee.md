## Contributions

Works on supersymmetric partition functions and the finite-$N$ structure of holographic theories. Developed the negative-mode / contour-rotation derivation of the [[giant-graviton-expansion]] in AdS$_5$/CFT$_4$, showing how giant-graviton branes contribute to the superconformal index with alternating signs and encode finite-$N$ trace relations. Transplanted this program to AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$: co-author of the bulk sum-over-geometries prescription that reproduces the finite-$N$ chiral-primary spectrum and gives a bulk [[stringy-exclusion-principle]] ([[ads3-giant-graviton-expansion]]).

## Context

At ETH Zurich. His giant-graviton / negative-mode methodology is the direct template for the AdS$_3$ construction; connects to the [[grand-canonical-residue-expansion]] approach to finite-$N$ indices.
