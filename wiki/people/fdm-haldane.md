## Contributions

Condensed-matter theorist (2016 Nobel laureate) whose spherical Landau-level construction and pseudopotential formalism (1983) are the technical foundation of [[lowest-landau-level-projection]] and hence of [[fuzzy-sphere-regularization]]. Placing charged particles on a monopole sphere and classifying rotationally invariant interactions by relative angular momentum — the Haldane pseudopotentials — is precisely the machinery used to build the fuzzy-sphere Ising Hamiltonian.

## Key papers

- Haldane (1983) — fractional quantization on the sphere; Landau levels with a monopole and pseudopotentials.

## Context

Best known for the Haldane gap, the Haldane model (Chern insulator), and topological classification of matter. His quantum-Hall-on-a-sphere technology, developed for fractional quantum Hall physics, is reused decades later to regularize 3D CFTs (see [[2210.13482|Zhu-Han-Huffman-Hofmann-He 2022]]).
