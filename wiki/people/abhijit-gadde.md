## Contributions

Theoretical physicist at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR), Mumbai. Known
for work on superconformal field theories (the superconformal index and 4d/2d correspondences)
and, more recently, for a program on **multipartite entanglement** built from **local-unitary
invariants** and their diagrammatic (edge-colored graph) description.

In the entanglement program, multipartite states are probed by **multi-invariants** — homogeneous
polynomials in the state coefficients and their conjugates, encoded by edge-colored graphs or
tuples of permutations — from which entanglement quantities such as multi-entropy are built. The
capstone of this line is a general construction of **genuine multipartite entanglement signals**
from compatible families of lower-partite symmetric LU-invariants, with [[mobius-inversion]] on
the [[partition-lattice]] as the organizing tool.

## Context

At TIFR; supported by the Department of Atomic Energy and the Infosys Endowment for the study of
the Quantum Structure of Spacetime. The entanglement work sits at the interface of quantum
information and high-energy theory, with applications to holographic states and to identifying the
low-energy topological quantum field theory from a gapped ground-state wavefunction.
