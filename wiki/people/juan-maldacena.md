## Contributions

Argentine-American theoretical physicist (then Harvard, later IAS). Author of the
[[maldacena-conjecture]] founding the [[ads-cft-correspondence]] — the most-cited paper in
high-energy physics. Prior work load-bearing for it: D-brane black hole entropy (with Callan;
with Horowitz–Strominger), greybody factors (with Strominger), NS5-brane thermodynamics (with
Strominger — the template for the [[brane-decoupling-limit]]), the MSW (0,4) CFT (with
Strominger and Witten). Later: eternal black holes and thermofield doubles, ER=EPR (with
Susskind), SYK/near-$AdS_2$ gravity (with Stanford).

## Key papers

- [[hep-th-9711200|Maldacena 1997]] — The Large N Limit of Superconformal Field Theories and
  Supergravity.

## Context

Works with/alongside [[andrew-strominger]], Witten, Susskind; fulfilled the program implied by
[[gerard-t-hooft]]'s large-$N$ expansion.
