## Statement

The [[entanglement-signal|signal]] construction requires a **compatible family** of
[[local-unitary-invariant|LU-invariants]], defined by
$f_\pi(|\psi\rangle_\kappa)=f_{\pi\wedge\kappa}(|\psi\rangle_\kappa)$ on every $\kappa$-separable
state. Restrictions of additive symmetric invariants furnish examples, but the condition's explicit
form is cumbersome. **What is the full class of compatible families, characterized structurally?**
Are there compatible families not obtained from additive invariants?

## Why it matters

Compatibility is the exact hypothesis that turns each separability constraint into a linear
[[mobius-inversion|meet-vanishing]] condition, hence what makes the whole Möbius-vector
classification work. A clean structural characterization would delimit precisely which LU-invariant
quantities can seed GME signals, and might reveal new signals beyond the additive-seed examples.

## Main attempts

- Additive symmetric LU-invariants and any function $g$ of a compatible family are known to be
  compatible (Lemma 2 and the extension remark of the source) — an infinite but not obviously
  exhaustive supply.
