## Statement

The [[supergravity-block|supergravity-block]] framework of
[[2607.05496|Hamada-Jeon-Kim 2026]] produces candidate 6d $\mathcal N=(1,0)$ theories that
satisfy **all known local and non-perturbative consistency constraints** (anomaly
cancellation, [[h-string|$H$-string]] / tensionless-string constraints, blowdown to a
Kähler-surface base) yet have **no known geometric (F-theory) realization** — they contain
*frozen-singularity* [[non-higgsable-cluster|non-Higgsable clusters]] such as
$\overset{\mathfrak{su}_8}{\hat 1}$ (explicit $T=10$ and $T=11$ examples). Do these
non-geometric candidates admit genuine string-theory / quantum-gravity UV completions, or
are they in the [[swampland-program|swampland]]?

## Why it matters

It is the sharp test of whether the currently known constraints are *complete*. If the
frozen-NHC candidates UV-complete, the 6d landscape is strictly larger than its geometric
(F-theory) part and a non-geometric construction is needed. If they cannot, then some
additional swampland constraint — invisible to anomalies and to the $H$-string data — must
exist and exclude them. Either answer sharpens the boundary between landscape and swampland
in the most controlled higher-dimensional setting.

## Main attempts

- The block classification itself isolates these candidates and shows the middle frozen
  cluster ($\mathfrak{su}_{16}$ on $\hat 1$) cannot embed in *any* supergravity, while the
  $\overset{\mathfrak{su}_8}{\hat 1}$ variants do pass all block-level tests.
- Frozen-singularity backgrounds (Bhardwaj et al. 2019; related to the CHL / type-I$'$
  frozen O-plane constructions) are the natural candidate UV framework, but no explicit
  compactification realizing these particular $T=10,11$ theories is known.

## Current status

**Open.** The candidates are explicitly constructed at the EFT level in
[[2607.05496|Hamada-Jeon-Kim 2026]]; their global UV completion is left to future work. More
broadly: can *every* intrinsically non-geometric 6d supergravity satisfying all known
constraints be UV-completed?
