## Statement

The [[dressed-operator-deformed-cft|dressed]] and [[physical-operator-deformed-cft|physical]]
operators of [[ttbar-deformation|$T\bar T$]] / [[jt-bar-deformation|$J\bar T$]]-deformed CFTs are
constructed *classically*, from Poisson brackets. A fully quantum definition is missing: it is
*proposed* that the quantum physical operator obeys the same relation to the dressed operator with
normal ordering assumed, and that dressed-operator correlators are exactly the undeformed-CFT
ones — but this is not proven, and only the leading-log (most UV-sensitive) tower of the
correlator has been resummed.

## Why it matters

The whole point of the operator dictionary is to make the deformed theory's local correlators
computable by leveraging its (nonlocal) conformal symmetry. Without a controlled quantum
definition — including the normal-ordering prescription, the subleading-log contributions, and
the role of the quantum central charge — the striking agreement with the string-theory
([[tst-holography|TsT]]) two-point functions rests on a classical construction plus a
leading-log resummation, and the full correlator (and its finite-size / cylinder version) is out
of reach.

## Main attempts

- Classical closed-form dressed and physical operators, with a quantum uplift assumed via normal
  ordering and CFT Ward identities ([[2507.08588|Chen–Du–Liu–Song 2025]]).
- Leading-log resummation reproducing the nonperturbative
  [[deformed-cft-two-point-function|two-point function]], cross-checked against TsT and
  large-momentum results.
- For $J\bar T$, a Borel/trans-series analysis exposing an instanton contribution in position
  space, hinting that the quantum completion has genuinely nonperturbative content
  ([[2511.10557|Chen–Du–Song 2025]]).

## Current status

Open. The classical construction and leading-log correlators are solid and agree with holography;
a rigorous quantum operator definition, the subleading-log completion, the interplay with the UV
regulator (given the dynamical cutoff $\sim\lambda\sqrt{k}$), and the finite-size case remain to
be worked out. A companion worldsheet derivation from strings on the TsT-transformed AdS$_3$
background is anticipated.
