## Statement

The [[proliferation-transition|proliferation-transition]] framework of [[2603.00245|Cheng–Seiberg 2026]] is built for a **single Abelian anyon** creating a single complex scalar $\Phi$ in the fundamental representation. What is the corresponding continuum theory when (i) $\Phi$ transforms in a higher-dimensional representation, or (ii) the light anyon is **non-Abelian**? Both are expected to involve gauging a **non-invertible** one-form symmetry, e.g. $\mathrm{SU}(2)_{10}\to\mathrm{Spin}(5)_1$.

## Why it matters

Many physical transitions between topological orders are driven by non-Abelian anyons, which lie outside the Abelian/invertible-symmetry setting. A continuum description would extend the single-integer classification to the non-invertible case and connect proliferation transitions to the broader program of gauging non-invertible symmetries. Related open directions raised in the same work: fermionic and electronic (spin$^c$) versions, and multicritical points where several mutually non-local anyons become light simultaneously.

## Main attempts

None yet within this framework — flagged as future work in [[2603.00245|Cheng–Seiberg 2026]]. The Abelian case is fully solved there and is the natural starting point.

## Current status

Open. The Abelian, single-anyon case is completely characterized; the non-Abelian / higher-representation / non-invertible extension is unaddressed.
