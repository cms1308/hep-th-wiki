## Statement

Can the [[giant-graviton-defect|defect approach]] be extended from two to four [[giant-graviton|giant gravitons]] — computing $\langle\mathcal{D}\mathcal{D}\mathcal{D}\mathcal{D}\rangle$-type correlators at strong coupling — and does the [[ten-dimensional-hidden-conformal-symmetry|hidden symmetry]] survive for non-maximal and dual giant gravitons?

## Why it matters

A four-giant correlator describes two D3-branes exchanging supergravity in the bulk; it is expected to exhibit phase transitions as the operator locations vary (a brane-scattering analogue of the connected/disconnected crossover). It would be the first genuinely defect-defect observable in this program, and a test of whether the partially broken 10d hidden symmetry is a general feature of heavy-operator dynamics or an artifact of the maximal-giant, two-defect case.

## Main attempts

The two-giant, arbitrary-KK-level correlators are solved and packaged by the hidden symmetry ([[2503.22987|Chen-Jiang-Zhou 2025]], [[2602.13570|Chen-Jiang-Zhou 2026]]); five-point functions (two giants, three supergravitons — "AdS scattering form factors") and the four-giant case are flagged there as natural next steps, along with non-maximal/dual giants and other backgrounds ($AdS_7\times S^4$, $AdS_4\times S^7$). No computation has yet been carried out.

## Current status

Open. The generic [[llhh-correlator|LLHH]] reduction guarantees a defect description for a heavy *pair*; extending to two interacting defects and to giant gravitons of variable size is the open frontier.
