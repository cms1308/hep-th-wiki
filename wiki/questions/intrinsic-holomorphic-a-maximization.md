## Statement

The [[holomorphic-a-maximization|holomorphic $a$-maximization]] procedure currently imports two
ingredients from the untwisted physical theory: (i) that the superconformal $\langle SSJ\rangle$
bracket has a **single** invariant tensor structure (giving the extremization condition
$k_f=9k_{RRf}$), and (ii) a **unitarity** input $k_{Rfg}<0$ that selects a maximum rather than a
mere extremum. Can both be derived from first principles **within** the holomorphic twist, without
reference to the parent SCFT?

## Why it matters

An intrinsically holomorphic proof would complete $a$-extremization as a statement purely about the
higher-current algebra, making the twist a self-contained tool for extracting exact IR central
charges and diagnosing (super)symmetry enhancement along RG flows. It would also clarify the role
of the exact superconformal $R$-symmetry, which is "somewhat opaque from a purely holomorphic
perspective."

## Main attempts

- The uniqueness of the tensor structure is currently justified by the Osborn three-point
  analysis in the untwisted theory.
- A candidate for the missing positivity is **graded unitarity** — a VOA positivity criterion
  corresponding to 4d unitarity in the parent SCFT (ArabiArdehali et al. 2025) — but whether it,
  or a generalization, extends to the holomorphic twist is open.

## Current status

Open, and explicitly flagged as a future direction in
~[[2509.16737|Bomans-Garner-Williams-Wu 2025]].
