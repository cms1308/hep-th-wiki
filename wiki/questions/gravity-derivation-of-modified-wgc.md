## Statement

Can the [[modified-ads-wgc-3c-2a|modified AdS WGC bound]]
$\Delta^2/q^2 \le 12(3c-2a)/k_F$ be *derived* from the bulk — i.e. from the extremality
condition of charged black holes in higher-derivative-corrected 5d gauged supergravity
with vector multiplets? ([[2308.01717|Cho-Choi-Lee-Song 2023]])

## Why it matters

The bound is currently only conjectured and tested. Since $a \ne c$ corresponds to
higher-derivative corrections in the bulk, the replacement $c \to 3c-2a$ should be visible
as a correction to the extremal charge-to-mass ratio — a flat-space analog is the known
higher-derivative shift of extremality behind the WGC
([hep-th/0606100](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0606100),
[1801.08546](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.08546),
[1810.03637](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03637)). A derivation would turn the modified
bound from a pattern into a theorem about AdS gravity.

## Main attempts

None yet. The obstruction named in [[2308.01717|Cho-Choi-Lee-Song 2023]]: four-derivative
corrections to 5d gauged supergravity and the corrected black hole entropy are available
only for *minimal* gauged supergravity (R-charged black holes,
[2112.06961](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.06961),
[2207.10671](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.10671),
[2208.01007](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.01007)) — the WGC bound concerns flavor charges,
which require vector multiplets.

## Current status

Open. The complementary CFT-side question is [[higher-dimensional-modularity]].
