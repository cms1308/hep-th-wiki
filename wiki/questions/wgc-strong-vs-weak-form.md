## Statement

Which sharp form of the [[weak-gravity-conjecture|Weak Gravity Conjecture]] is true? In
units where large extremal black holes have $M/Q = 1$
([[hep-th-0601001|Arkani-Hamed-Motl-Nicolis-Vafa 2006]]):

- **(II) strong form**: the *lightest* charged particle satisfies $M/q \le 1$;
- **(III) weak form**: *some* state satisfies $M/q \le 1$, possibly at large charge $Q_*$.

The founding paper: "Even though we have no counterexamples for conjecture (II) most of
our evidence only supports the weaker conjecture (III)." (Form (I), the minimal-charge
state, is already refuted there by the stable $SO(32)$ heterotic spinors and the
Wen-Witten fractionally charged winding states.)

## Why it matters

The two forms give "two very different pictures" for trans-Planckian physics
([[hep-th-0601001|Arkani-Hamed-Motl-Nicolis-Vafa 2006]]): if (II) holds, there would be a
sharp obstacle to inflation models relying on super-Planckian axion decay constants (e.g.
[[extranatural-inflation]]); if — "as is more likely" per the paper — only (III) holds,
such models are non-generic but could have sporadic exceptions. A discovery of primordial
gravitational waves might experimentally force trans-Planckian inflaton excursions,
making the question acute.
The strong form also controls whether the light charged spectrum is constrained near
minimal charge, where it interfaces with phenomenology
($\Lambda \lesssim g M_{\rm Pl}$ at observed couplings).

## Main attempts

- [[hep-th-0601001|Arkani-Hamed-Motl-Nicolis-Vafa 2006]]: heterotic spectra always
  contain a sub-extremal state in each non-BPS charge direction (the $N_L = 0$ state via
  spectral flow) — evidence the paper itself counts as supporting only the weaker (III);
  (III) is argued from finiteness of exactly stable states per charge-space direction,
  with the $Q_*$ distribution speculated to be "peaked for charges of order 1, but perhaps
  with sporadic exceptions."
- Later refinements propose intermediate forms — e.g. sublattice/tower versions requiring
  super-extremal states on a sublattice of the charge lattice
  ([1506.03447](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.03447),
  [1606.08437](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08437)) — not yet ingested here.

## Current status

Open. No counterexample to (II) is known to this wiki; the systematically tested statement
in the AdS/CFT setting is the weak-form spectral bound
([[modified-ads-wgc-3c-2a]], checked over the chiral ring in
[[2308.01717|Cho-Choi-Lee-Song 2023]] and [[2510.19136|Cho-Lee-Song 2025]]).
