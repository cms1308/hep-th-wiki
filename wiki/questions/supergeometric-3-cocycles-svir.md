## Statement

The [[higher-super-virasoro-algebra|super-Virasoro algebras]] $\mathfrak{svir}(2|\mathcal N-1)$
can be written invariantly as (derived) holomorphic super vector fields on a punctured superspace,
but their **central extensions** — the 3-cocycles encoding the anomalies $a,c$ — are not known as
invariant supergeometric expressions in all cases. For $\mathcal N=2$ the space of extensions is
known to be two-dimensional, corresponding to the degree-four Chern-class combinations
$(c_1^2,c_2)$, yet no explicit supergeometric formulas for the two 3-cocycles are available; only
component-level expressions at the level of the twist are known.

## Why it matters

For $\mathcal N=1$ the central extensions of the higher Witt algebra have a clean geometric
description via the classes $(c_1^3,c_1c_2)$, from which the 3-cocycles defining
[[two-virasoro-algebra|$\mathfrak{vir}(2)_{a,c}$]] follow. A matching supergeometric description in
the extended-SUSY case would make the anomaly structure of $\mathfrak{svir}(2|1)$,
$\mathfrak{svir}(2|2)$, $\mathfrak{svir}(2|3)$ manifest and coordinate-free, and would strengthen
the algebraic derivation of results like $a=c$ for $\mathcal N\geq3$ (see
[[ac-anomalies-as-central-extensions]]).

## Main attempts

- The relevant cohomology dimensions are established (Fuks 1986; pimenov): one-dimensional for
  $\mathcal N\geq3$ (hence $a=c$), two-dimensional for $\mathcal N=2$.
- Component-level 3-cocycles are given, but invariant superspace representatives are missing.
