## Statement

Do the Carrollian correlators that encode the flat-space $\mathcal S$-matrix — obtained
today as limits of AdS/CFT observables ([[flat-carrollian-limit-correspondence]]) or by
transforming amplitudes ([[carrollian-amplitude]]) — arise from a *standalone quantum
theory* with its own Hilbert space, operator algebra, and axioms? Or is flat space
holography irreducibly a limiting procedure applied to AdS/CFT?

## Why it matters

Named the central challenge of the program in [[2602.02644|Ruzziconi 2026]]. Without an
intrinsic dual, [[flat-space-holography]] cannot make predictions beyond what the parent
AdS/CFT computation already contains — no analogue of "solve the boundary theory to learn
about the bulk", no nonperturbative definition of quantum gravity in flat space.

## Main attempts

- **Lagrangian quantization of Carrollian theories**: blocked —
  [[carrollian-quantization-obstruction|lattice-regularized electric theories are generalized free fields]]
  (UV/IR mixing, flat-band dispersion $E^2=m^2$;
  [[2407.11971|Cotler et al. 2024]]), and magnetic theories are secretly Euclidean CFTs one
  dimension down with time-independent correlators. Neither gives interacting,
  time-dependent correlators.
- **Symmetry bootstrap**: define the theory by its Ward identities, branch structure, and
  OPEs ([[carrollian-cft]]) — works kinematically but lacks a state-operator
  correspondence, conformal blocks, and an associative operator algebra to bootstrap with.
- **$c$ as regulator**: define the Carrollian theory as the $c\to0$ limit of a preferred
  relativistic uplift, making the limit itself part of the definition
  ([[2602.02644|Ruzziconi 2026]]'s suggested direction).
- Cotler et al. 2025 raise the possibility that the true flat-space dual is intrinsically
  *non-local* on $\mathscr I$.

## Current status

Open. The working definition of the flat-space dual remains "the Carrollian limit of a
holographic CFT", with [[carrollian-abjm-correspondence|Carrollian ABJM]] as the concrete
top-down laboratory.
