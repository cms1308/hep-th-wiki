## Statement

The Type II and Type III families of the
[[large-n-universality-classes|large-$N$ classification]] have every diagnostic of a
holographic CFT — $a \simeq c$ at large $N$ ($\tfrac{27}{128}\dim G$ and
$\tfrac14 \dim G$ respectively), sparse BPS spectra, conformal manifolds after
(flip-assisted) relevant deformations, and a Hawking-Page-like transition of the
large-$N$ [[superconformal-index|index]] saddle. What are their supergravity duals?
([[2510.19136|Cho-Lee-Song 2025]])

## Why it matters

Beyond $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM and its Leigh-Strassler-type deformations, few 4d
$\mathcal{N}=1$ gauge theories are *derived* to have weakly coupled Einstein duals. A
systematically classified list of holographic candidates — with universal, gauge-group-
independent large-$N$ data — is a concrete target list for constructing new
$AdS_5$ backgrounds, and a laboratory for the question of when gravity emerges from a
gauge theory. The contrast class is sharp: Type I
([[dense-spectrum-scft|dense-spectrum]]) theories cannot be holographic, yet have
$a, c = O(N)$ — what bulk description, if any, do *they* have?

## Main attempts

None yet for the new families; the known anchors are $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM (Type III
prototype, $a = c = \tfrac14(N^2-1)$ for the three-adjoint theory) and the
$\mathcal{N}=2\to\mathcal{N}=1$ adjoint-mass flows whose $\tfrac{27}{32}$
central-charge ratio (Tachikawa-Wecht,
[0906.0965](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.0965)) reappears in the Type II value
$\tfrac{27}{128}\dim G$ — suggesting Klebanov-Witten-like duals for Type II.

## Current status

Open; posed in [[2510.19136|Cho-Lee-Song 2025]] as the main future direction of the
classification program.
