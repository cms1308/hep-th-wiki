## Statement

Realize the gravitational soft symmetry algebra — the
[[lambda-deformed-w-algebra|$\mathcal L_\Lambda w_{1+\infty}$ algebra]] — in AdS$_4$/CFT$_3$
at the quantum level, as done for the gauge-theory [[s-algebra]]
([[ads4-s-algebra-from-cft3-light-transforms]]), and understand its bulk and de Sitter
avatars.

## Why it matters

Soft algebras are the symmetry backbone shared by any holographic dual pair; establishing
the gravitational one at $\Lambda\neq0$ ties [[celestial-holography]] to the
best-understood quantum gravity we have ([[ads-cft-correspondence]]) and constrains every
CFT$_3$ with a gravity dual.

## Main attempts

- Tree-level bulk: the deformation was found by perturbing celestial OPEs with a
  cosmological constant (Taylor–Zhu 2023) and by twistor methods in self-dual gravity
  (Bittleston et al. 2024).
- Conjectured from the gauge case in [[2601.00096|Sheta et al. 2025]]: an $SO(3,2)$
  multiplet of light-ray operators containing the [[anec-operator|ANEC operator]] should
  generate the deformed algebra.
- **Largely realized** by [[2603.26459|Strominger–Wei 2026]]: every CFT$_3$ carries a wedge
  of $\mathcal L_\Lambda w_{1+\infty}$ at $\Lambda=-1$ from ANEC descendants
  ([[every-cft3-has-l-lambda-w-symmetry]]).

## Current status

The CFT$_3$ side is established. Remaining open ([[2603.26459|Strominger–Wei 2026]]):

- A careful **bulk AdS$_4$ derivation incorporating Dirichlet boundary conditions**, which
  reflect helicity and are incompatible with self-duality; perhaps involving combinations
  of the form $w^p_{\bar m,m}\pm\bar w^{3-p}_{\bar m,m}$.
- A **de Sitter interpretation** of $\mathcal L_\Lambda w_{1+\infty}$ at $\Lambda>0$.
- Generators **outside the wedge** $\bar m+p\ge1$, $m-p\ge-2$: may exist in particular
  CFT$_3$s but are not produced by the light-ray construction.

A further question the paper does not itself pose: what the universal symmetry constrains
in CFT$_3$ dynamics.
