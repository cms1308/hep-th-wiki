## Statement

Is there a 4d analog of 2d modular invariance — a structural property connecting the
high-temperature (Cardy-like) asymptotics of a 4d (S)CFT partition function to its
low-lying charged spectrum?

## Why it matters

In 2d CFT, modular invariance both yields Cardy's formula and *proves* WGC-type bounds on
the charged spectrum ([1603.09745](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.09745),
[1606.08438](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08438),
[1811.00976](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.00976)). In 4d the
[[supersymmetric-cardy-formula-4d|supersymmetric Cardy formula]] exists — universal
asymptotics of the [[superconformal-index|superconformal index]] fixed by $3c-2a$ — but
no known symmetry forces a bound on light charged states. If such a structure exists, the
[[modified-ads-wgc-3c-2a|modified AdS WGC]] would be its consequence, and its empirical
success is a clue to what the structure is ([[2308.01717|Cho-Choi-Lee-Song 2023]]).

## Main attempts

- The modified WGC of [[2308.01717|Cho-Choi-Lee-Song 2023]] is itself the evidence-driven
  guess at what such modularity would imply; possible connections are suggested to the
  Charge Convexity Conjecture ([2108.04594](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.04594)) and the CFT
  Distance Conjecture ([2011.10040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.10040)). Its comprehensive
  validation — every theory of the simple-gauge-group large-$N$ classification, at every
  finite $N$, including non-holographic [[dense-spectrum-scft|dense-spectrum]] theories
  ([[2510.19136|Cho-Lee-Song 2025]]) — strengthens the case that some such structure
  exists.
- Extensions to other dimensions would be a test: in 6d the Cardy formula is controlled by
  the anomaly combination $\alpha - 4\beta + 16\gamma$, suggesting an analogous WGC bound
  there.
