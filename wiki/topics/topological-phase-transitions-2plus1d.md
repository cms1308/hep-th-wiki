## Overview

A gapped phase of matter in $2+1$ dimensions with **topological order** — anyons, ground-state degeneracy on the torus, long-range entanglement — is described in the deep IR by a topological quantum field theory (TQFT) $\mathcal T$, typically a Chern–Simons theory. This topic collects the continuum field-theory description of **transitions out of such a phase**: what happens when the system is tuned to a critical point where the topological order changes or disappears.

The organizing idea is that a transition is triggered by a single **Abelian anyon** $a$ of the TQFT becoming light. In the condensed-matter literature this is universally called **"anyon condensation."** A continuum description couples the TQFT to a complex scalar $\Phi$ that creates $a$ and drives the transition by tuning a mass $\mu^2|\Phi|^2$: one sign of $\mu^2$ gives back $\mathcal T$, the other gives the new phase $\mathcal T'$. The framework treats gapped-to-gapped and gapped-to-gapless transitions on the same footing, and connects three neighboring structures — the [[abelian-hierarchy-construction|Abelian hierarchy construction]] of fractional quantum Hall states, the [[gauging-one-form-symmetry|gauging of one-form global symmetries]], and $2+1$d particle/vortex duality.

## Historical development

- **1983–1992** — the [[abelian-hierarchy-construction|Abelian hierarchy]] of fractional quantum Hall states (Haldane 1983; Halperin 1984), formulated in $K$-matrix / Chern–Simons language (Blok–Wen 1990; Read 1990; Wen–Zee 1992). Later recast as a TQFT-to-TQFT map (Lan–Wang–Wen 2017; Lan 2019).
- **1988–1990** — Chern–Simons TQFT and its line operators (Witten 1988; Elitzur–Moore–Schwimmer–Seiberg 1989; Moore–Seiberg 1988/1989); the gauging-of-lines / twisted-sector machinery that underlies "condensation."
- **2008** — categorical "anyon condensation" (Bais–Slingerland), the standard formulation this topic reframes.
- **2014** — [[one-form-global-symmetry|generalized (one-form) global symmetries]] and their gauging (Gaiotto–Kapustin–Seiberg–Willett), giving "condensation" a symmetry meaning: condensing $a$ = gauging the $\mathbb Z_n^{(1)}$ it generates.
- **2016** — $2+1$d boson/fermion and particle/vortex dualities (Karch–Tong; Seiberg–Senthil–Wang–Witten), the tool relating a transition and its inverse.
- **2018** — anomaly of one-form symmetries and minimal TQFTs $\mathcal A^{n,p}$ (Hsin–Lam–Seiberg).
- **2026** — [[2603.00245|Cheng–Seiberg 2026]]: the most general relativistic transition theory for a single light Abelian anyon depends on **only one integer** $p$ beyond the pair $(\mathcal T,a)$; introduces the [[proliferation-transition|proliferation transition]] reframing (worldlines proliferate, nothing condenses).

## Key concepts

- [[proliferation-transition]] — the transition as proliferation of anyon worldlines, not condensation of a particle.
- [[anyon-condensation]] — the standard term and its categorical meaning; disambiguated from proliferation.
- [[one-form-global-symmetry]] — $\mathbb Z_n^{(1)}$ generated by an Abelian anyon; its anomaly $p$.
- [[abelian-hierarchy-construction]] — the TQFT-to-TQFT map producing the far-side phase $\mathcal T'$.
- [[chern-simons-matter-transition-theory]] — TQFT $+$ one complex scalar; the continuum transition Lagrangian.

## Key results

- [[proliferation-transition-single-integer-p]] — for fixed $(\mathcal T,a)$ the transition theory is labelled by one integer $p$ (the integer lift of the $\mathbb Z_n^{(1)}$ anomaly).
- [[p-zero-transition-implements-gauging]] — at $p=0$ a Higgs transition realizes gauging an anomaly-free one-form symmetry, $\mathcal T\to\mathcal T/\mathbb Z_n$.

## Current status

The single-light-Abelian-anyon case is completely characterized in [[2603.00245|Cheng–Seiberg 2026]]: phases, global symmetries (including an emergent magnetic $\mathrm U(1)_{\mathcal B}$), Hall-conductivity jumps, the particle/vortex dual, and enrichment by an ordinary $\mathrm U(1)_A$ symmetry (with an incompatibility when $a$ carries fractional $\mathrm U(1)_A$ charge). Non-Abelian and multi-anyon transitions are open.

## Open questions

- [[gauging-noninvertible-one-form-symmetry-transitions]] — transitions from higher-dimensional $\Phi$ representations / non-Abelian anyons, gauging non-invertible one-form symmetries.
- Fermionic and electronic (spin$^c$) versions.
- Multicritical points with several mutually non-local light anyons.
