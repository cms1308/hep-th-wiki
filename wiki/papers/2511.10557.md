## TL;DR

The $J\bar T$ companion to [[2507.08588|Chen–Du–Liu–Song 2025]]. Working in the
[[hamiltonian-flow-deformed-cft|Hamiltonian formalism]] on the plane, it shows a
[[jt-bar-deformation|$J\bar T$-deformed CFT]] carries two commuting Virasoro–Kac–Moody algebras
— local in the left-moving sector, nonlocal in the right — and builds the corresponding
[[dressed-operator-deformed-cft|dressed]] (nonlocal primary) and
[[physical-operator-deformed-cft|physical]] (local) operators. Conformal perturbation theory
resums the leading-log two- and $N$-point functions to all orders in $\lambda$, matching the
[[tst-holography|TsT/single-trace $J\bar T$]] string prediction in momentum space, while a
Borel analysis of the position-space correlator reveals a nonperturbative *instanton*
contribution setting an effective UV cutoff $\sim\lambda\sqrt{k}$.

## Motivation

AdS/CFT needs an isolated AdS region that generic and astrophysical black holes lack, motivating
holography beyond AdS. Kerr/CFT and warped-AdS$_3$ carry a reduced
$SL(2,\mathbb R)_L\times U(1)_R$ symmetry, pointing to a dual that keeps the left-moving
conformal symmetry but breaks the right — exactly the structure of the $(1,2)$ $J\bar T$
deformation. Concretely: under $J\bar T$ the left sector stays Virasoro while the right-moving
stress tensor becomes nonlocal and field-dependent; the paper asks how these symmetries organize
operators and turns them into a tool for computing correlators.

## Historical context

Continues the $J\bar T$ program: the deformation introduced by Guica (2017) as a solvable
$(1,2)$ deformation preserving left conformal symmetry, its spectrum and charge flow
(Chakraborty–Giveon–Kutasov 2018), modular covariance (Aharony et al. 2018), and single-trace
$J\bar T$ / [[tst-holography|TsT]] holography (Apolo–Song 2018). The nonlocal field-dependent
right-moving symmetry is from Guica–Monten (2020) and Georgescu–Guica (2024); the Hamiltonian
flow formalism from Kruthoff–Parrikar (2020). It generalizes the authors' own $T\bar T$ paper
([[2507.08588|Chen–Du–Liu–Song 2025]]) and imports the Borel/instanton analysis of $T\bar T$
correlators (Hirano 2025). Roots trace to AdS/CFT ([[hep-th-9711200|Maldacena 1997]]) and
Kerr/CFT (Guica–Hartman–Song–Strominger 2008).

## Main results

1. **Symmetry = two commuting Virasoro–Kac–Moody algebras.** Solving the flow equation for the
   dressed currents gives, under Poisson brackets,
   $$i\{\tilde L_n,\tilde L_m\}=(n-m)\tilde L_{n+m},\quad
   i\{\tilde{\mathcal J}_n,\tilde{\mathcal J}_m\}=mk\,\delta_{m,-n},\quad
   i\{\tilde L_n,\tilde{\mathcal J}_m\}=-m\,\tilde{\mathcal J}_{m+n},$$
   and a barred copy, all cross brackets vanishing. Left generators are built from *local*
   currents; the right ones are *nonlocal*, built from a field-dependent coordinate $\hat v$.
2. **Solvable dressing.** The right energy density solves
   $\mathcal H_R=\tfrac{2\pi}{\lambda^2 k}\big(1-\lambda\mathcal J^{(0)}_+-\sqrt{(1-\lambda\mathcal J^{(0)}_+)^2-\tfrac{\lambda^2 k}{\pi}\mathcal H^{(0)}_R}\big)$,
   and $\tilde{\mathcal J}_+=\mathcal J^{(0)}_++\tfrac{\lambda k}{2\pi}\mathcal H_R$ is chiral and
   flow-invariant, with the Sugawara-type invariant $\partial_\lambda(\mathcal H_L-\tfrac{\pi}{k}\tilde{\mathcal J}_+^2)=0$.
3. **Charge flow.** The left $U(1)$ charge flows, $Q_L=\tilde{\mathcal J}_0-\tfrac{\lambda k}{2\pi}H_R$;
   the right charge does not.
4. **Physical (local) operator.** For an undeformed primary of weights $(h,\bar h)$ and chiral
   charge $q$,
   $$\mathcal O_{h,\bar h,q}=\frac{\mathcal O^{(0)}_{h,\bar h,q}}{(1-\lambda\tilde{\mathcal J}_+)\big(1-\lambda\tilde{\mathcal J}_++\tfrac{\lambda^2 k}{4\pi}\mathcal H_R\big)^{\bar h-1}},$$
   a left-moving primary with shifted charge $\tilde q=q+\tfrac{\lambda k}{2\pi}p_R$.
5. **Dressed (nonlocal) operator.**
   $\tilde{\mathcal O}_{h,\bar h,q}=\sum_n\tfrac{1}{n!}\partial_v^n\big((-\delta\hat v)^n e^{i\lambda q\bar\eta_0}\mathcal O_{h,\bar h,q}\big)$,
   local only in the left null direction; physical operators are recovered by localizing dressed
   operators onto the field-dependent coordinates.
6. **Momentum-space two-point function.** Resumming the leading logs,
   $$\langle\mathcal O_{h,\bar h,q}(p,\bar p)\,\mathcal O_{h,\bar h,-q}(-p,-\bar p)\rangle\sim
   \frac{\pi\,\Gamma(1-2h_\lambda)}{\Gamma(2h_\lambda)}\Big(\tfrac{|p|}{2}\Big)^{4h_\lambda-2},\quad
   h_\lambda=h-\frac{i\lambda q\bar p}{2\pi}-\frac{\lambda^2 k\,\bar p^2}{8\pi^2},$$
   matching the TsT/$J\bar T$ string result; an analogous $N$-point formula holds.
7. **Instanton in position space.** The leading-log series organizes into
   $K\equiv-\tfrac{\lambda^2 k}{4\pi^2}\,\log|x|^2/(\bar x-\tfrac{\lambda q}{\pi}\log|x|^2)^2$,
   which is factorially divergent; Borel resummation hits a branch point at $\zeta=\tfrac14$, and
   the principal value adds an instanton term $\propto e^{-1/4K}$ setting an effective UV cutoff
   $\sim\lambda\sqrt{k}$.
8. **Scaling regimes.** IR: standard CFT with unshifted weights. UV: the left power law survives
   while the right sector softens to logarithmic, $x^{-2h}(\log|x|^2)^{-\bar h}$ (zero charge) or
   $(\log|x|^2)^{-2\bar h}$ (nonzero charge) — a divergence milder than the CFT but more divergent
   than $T\bar T$, with oscillations/cusps in the crossover.

## Methods

Same framework as the $T\bar T$ paper ([[hamiltonian-flow-deformed-cft]]): $J\bar T$ as a
canonical transformation generated by the bilocal
$\mathcal X_{J\bar T}=\int dy\,dy'\,G(y-y')\,\tilde{\mathcal J}_+(y)\mathcal H_R(y')$; operators
classified by the flow (covariant-derivative) equation; a nonlocal field-dependent coordinate
$\hat v$; Ward identities from the dressed generators; conformal perturbation theory with a
Wilson-line dressing and point-splitting for the correlators; and, for the position-space
result, Borel resummation / trans-series to extract the instanton and Stokes discontinuity from
a hypergeometric branch point.

## Conventions

Lightcone $u=y+t$, $v=y-t$; $\mathcal H_L\equiv T_{ut}$, $\mathcal H_R\equiv-T_{vt}$,
$\mathcal P=T_{yt}$. Deformation $\partial_\lambda\mathcal H=-\mathcal O_{J\bar T}=\epsilon^{\alpha\beta}\mathcal J_{+\alpha}T_{v\beta}$,
fixing the sign/normalization of $\lambda$. $U(1)$ level $k$: $\langle j(w_1)j(w_2)\rangle=k/(w_1-w_2)^2$,
$i\{\tilde{\mathcal J}_n,\tilde{\mathcal J}_m\}=mk\delta_{m,-n}$, $\tilde{\mathcal J}_+=\mathcal J^{(0)}_++\tfrac{\lambda k}{2\pi}\mathcal H_R$.
The stress tensor is non-symmetric (Lorentz broken by $J\bar T$), taken in Noether form, valid
for theories with no classical central charge (Liouville-type excluded). Wick rotation $u\to-ix$,
$v\to i\bar x$, $p_L\to-ip$, $p_R\to-i\bar p$, with $\tilde{\mathcal H}_L\to T/2\pi$,
$\tilde{\mathcal H}_R\to\bar T/2\pi$, $\mathcal J_+\to j/2\pi$. Fourier convention
$\mathcal O(p_L,p_R)=\int du\,dv\,e^{-ip_L u+ip_R v}\mathcal O(u,v)$.

## Key equations

- $\mathcal X_{J\bar T}=\int dy\,dy'\,G(y-y')\tilde{\mathcal J}_+(y)\mathcal H_R(y')$ — bilocal generating the plane flow.
- $\mathcal H_R=\tfrac{2\pi}{\lambda^2 k}\big(1-\lambda\mathcal J^{(0)}_+-\sqrt{(1-\lambda\mathcal J^{(0)}_+)^2-\tfrac{\lambda^2 k}{\pi}\mathcal H^{(0)}_R}\big)$ — solvable stress-tensor dressing.
- $\tilde{\mathcal J}_+=\mathcal J^{(0)}_++\tfrac{\lambda k}{2\pi}\mathcal H_R$, $\ \partial_\lambda(\mathcal H_L-\tfrac{\pi}{k}\tilde{\mathcal J}_+^2)=0$ — chiral flow invariant.
- $\mathcal O_{h,\bar h,q}=\big[(1-\lambda\tilde{\mathcal J}_+)(1-\lambda\tilde{\mathcal J}_++\tfrac{\lambda^2 k}{4\pi}\mathcal H_R)^{\bar h-1}\big]^{-1}\mathcal O^{(0)}_{h,\bar h,q}$ — physical operator.
- $h_\lambda=h-\tfrac{i\lambda q\bar p}{2\pi}-\tfrac{\lambda^2 k\bar p^2}{8\pi^2}$ — effective weight; $K=-\tfrac{\lambda^2 k}{4\pi^2}\log|x|^2/(\bar x-\tfrac{\lambda q}{\pi}\log|x|^2)^2$ — Borel variable, instanton $\propto e^{-1/4K}$.

## Open questions raised

- Momentum- and position-space results are not related by a simple Fourier transform (they
  differ by a tower of subleading logs); whether analogous nonperturbative effects exist in
  momentum space is unclear.
- The interplay between the deformation and the (point-splitting) UV regulator, given the
  dynamical cutoff $\sim\lambda\sqrt{k}$.
- Extension to Lorentzian signature (light-cone limit), the physical meaning of the instanton and
  of the crossover oscillations/cusps, and cases with a classical central charge.
- See [[quantum-operators-deformed-cft]] for the shared quantum-definition question.
