## TL;DR

The gravitational path integral computes inner products of open- and closed-universe states,
and a well-defined Hilbert space requires them to be positive semi-definite. This letter shows
that a low-energy gravity theory with an axion of *exact* shift symmetry violates that
positivity: axion wormhole amplitudes diverge at a critical imaginary axion separation, so the
two-boundary (variance) amplitude blows up while the one-boundary mean stays finite. Curing
this requires shift-symmetry-breaking instantons to proliferate before the divergence, which
reproduces the axionic [[weak-gravity-conjecture|weak gravity conjecture]]
$S_{\rm inst}\lesssim\mathcal O(1)\,M_{\rm Pl}/f_a$ — with its precise $\mathcal O(1)$ constant.

## Motivation

Quantum gravity is believed to forbid exact global symmetries, so an axion's continuous shift
symmetry must be broken non-perturbatively below the Planck scale; the axionic WGC sharpens
this but its $\mathcal O(1)$ coefficient had never been derived from first principles. The
tension the paper exploits: axion wormholes are real, perturbatively stable Euclidean saddles
that *should* contribute to the path integral, yet their contribution to statistical moments of
the partition function can become divergent and negative — incompatible with a positive-norm
Hilbert space. This turns a consistency requirement (positivity) into a sharp EFT constraint.

## Main results

1. **Positivity $\Rightarrow$ Stieltjes moment constraints.** Gluing open/closed-universe
   boundaries gives inner products $\langle\mathcal J_1|\mathcal J_2\rangle=Z(\mathcal J_1^\Theta\cup\mathcal J_2)$;
   positive semi-definiteness of all Gram matrices is equivalent to a positive ensemble measure
   $P(\alpha)\ge0$ with non-negative densities. For non-negative partition functions the moments
   $m_n=\overline{Z^n}$ must solve the **Stieltjes moment problem** (Hankel and shifted-Hankel
   matrices PSD). See [[gravitational-path-integral-positivity]].

2. **Imaginary Distance Conjecture.** Axion wormhole amplitudes diverge when the imaginary part
   of the axion boundary-value difference reaches a *universal* critical value, independent of
   AdS radius and wormhole slicing:
   $$\mathrm{AdS:}\ |\mathrm{Im}\,\Delta a|\le\frac{1}{f_a}\sqrt{\frac{\pi(d-1)}{8d\,G_N}},\qquad
     \mathrm{Flat:}\ |\mathrm{Im}\,\Delta a|\le\frac{1}{f_a}\sqrt{\frac{\pi d}{8(d-1)\,G_N}}.$$
   A finite-distance, "timelike" excursion bound, distinct from the (real-direction,
   infinite-distance) distance conjecture. See [[imaginary-distance-bound]].

3. **Positivity violation from wormholes.** As $|\mathrm{Im}\,\Delta a|\to\mathrm{Im}\,\Delta a_{\rm crit}$,
   the connected two-boundary amplitude $\kappa_2$ diverges while the mean $\kappa_1$ stays finite
   (by the exact shift symmetry). A Gaussian variable with variance $\gg$ mean is negative with
   probability $\to50\%$, contradicting $Z_\alpha\ge0$; higher-genus corrections (suppressed by
   $e^{-\#/G_N}$) cannot restore the third Stieltjes inequality
   $\kappa_1\kappa_3\ge\kappa_2(\kappa_2-\kappa_1^2)$.

4. **Sharp axionic WGC.** Positivity is rescued only if shift-symmetry-breaking instantons
   proliferate before the divergence — their effective action $\mathrm{Re}\,S_{\rm eff}=S_{\rm inst}\mp\mathrm{Im}\,a$
   must turn negative for $|\mathrm{Im}\,\theta|<|\mathrm{Im}\,\Delta a_{\rm crit}|/2$, requiring
   $S_{\rm inst}\le\tfrac12|\mathrm{Im}\,\Delta a_{\rm crit}|$ — precisely the axionic WGC with a
   fixed coefficient.

5. **Axio-dilaton / string realization.** The moduli metric
   $ds^2_{\rm mod}=d\varphi^2-e^{-\beta\varphi}dA^2$ ($A=ia$) is Lorentzian AdS$_2$ of radius
   $2/\beta$. Requiring the $D(-1)$-instanton instability (which breaks $SL(2,\mathbb R)\to SL(2,\mathbb Z)$)
   to set in before the wormhole diverges gives $\beta>\beta_{\rm crit}=\sqrt{2d/(d-1)}$; type IIB
   on AdS$_5\times S^5$ ($d=4$) has $\beta_{\rm crit}=\sqrt{8/3}<\beta_{\rm IIB}=2$, so it passes.
   The critical imaginary axion coincides with the Bogomolny bound in $\mathcal N=4$ SYM.

6. **Ordinary WGC by uplift.** Uplifting the axion to a higher-dimensional $U(1)$ holonomy
   reproduces the standard WGC $\tfrac{m}{|q|}\le\sqrt{\tfrac{e_{d+2}^2 d}{8\pi G_{d+2}(d-1)}}$
   with its precise coefficient (with a caveat about cigar/holonomy saddles breaking the
   one-boundary shift symmetry).

7. **Bound on $f_a$.** For a $d=3$ bulk axion coupled to Yang-Mills, requiring instanton
   destabilization before divergence gives $f_a\lesssim3.9\times10^{17}$ GeV for $SU(3)$
   ($S_{\rm 1\text{-}inst}\simeq12$); via AdS$_3\times S^1$ compactification of the Standard
   Model, $f_a^{D=4}<2.3\times10^{17}$ GeV.

## Methods

- **GPI inner products / baby-universe Hilbert space:** open-universe state $|\mathcal J\rangle$
  from one asymptotic boundary; inner products from gluing with CPT reflection $\Theta$.
- **Moment-problem reasoning:** inner products as Laplace transforms of a density of states;
  Bernstein-Widder (complete monotonicity $\Leftrightarrow$ $\rho\ge0$) and the Stieltjes
  positivity conditions on moments/cumulants.
- **Fixed-flux (dual $(d-1)$-form) wormhole computation:** dualize the axion to a $(D-2)$-form
  with fixed integer flux; real saddles exist for imaginary axion. On-shell action is exactly
  linear in $|m|$.
- **Flux sum $\to$ Dirichlet amplitude:** Fourier-weight the fixed-flux saddles with
  $e^{im\Delta a}$, yielding $Z_{\rm WH}\sim\sinh/(\cosh-\cos)$ with an explicit divergence.
- **FRW/minisuperspace:** scale factor $b(r)$ with $b'^2=k+b^2/\ell^2-\tilde C^2/b^{2d-2}$;
  $\mathrm{Im}\,\Delta a=|C|\Delta\tau$ for $k=-1,0,+1$ slicings.
- **Moduli-space geodesic method** (axio-dilaton): axion-dilaton profile = timelike geodesic in
  Lorentzian AdS$_2$; geodesics exceeding the single Poincaré-patch length remain valid in the
  universal cover.

## Conventions

$D=d+1$ = bulk spacetime dimension; $d$ = boundary dimension. Axion normalization $a\sim a+2\pi$,
decay constant $f_a$ in the EFT action
$S_{\rm EFT}=\int d^Dx\sqrt g\big(-\tfrac{R-\Lambda}{16\pi G_N}+\tfrac{f_a^2}{2}\partial_\mu a\,\partial^\mu a\big)$.
Wormholes are real for imaginary axion $a\in i\mathbb R$, equivalently a real dualized
$(d-1)$-form flux. The critical bound is written for $\mathrm{Im}\,\Delta a$ (the imaginary axion
excursion). This is the same physical bound as the [[2605.05336|Maldacena-Maloney-McPeak]]
$\tau_{\rm IDB}$, phrased for a single axion with decay constant $f_a$ rather than a canonically
normalized modulus — see [[imaginary-distance-bound]] for the dictionary.

## Key equations

- Open/closed inner products: $\langle\mathcal J_1|\mathcal J_2\rangle=Z(\mathcal J_1^\Theta\cup\mathcal J_2)$.
- Positive ensemble decomposition:
  $\overline{\rho_J(E_1)\cdots\rho_J(E_n)}=\int d\alpha\,P(\alpha)\prod_i\rho^\alpha_J(E_i)$,
  $P(\alpha)\ge0$, $\rho^\alpha_J\ge0$.
- Stieltjes cumulant inequalities: $\kappa_1\ge0$, $\kappa_2\ge0$,
  $\kappa_1\kappa_3\ge\kappa_2(\kappa_2-\kappa_1^2)$.
- Dirichlet wormhole amplitude:
  $Z_{\rm WH}(\Delta a)=\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z}Z^{\rm 1\text{-}loop}_m e^{-|m|\,\mathrm{Im}\,\Delta a_{\rm crit}+im\Delta a}
  \sim\frac{\sinh(\mathrm{Im}\,\Delta a_{\rm crit})}{\cosh(\mathrm{Im}\,\Delta a_{\rm crit})-\cos(\Delta a)}$.
- WGC from positivity: $S_{\rm inst}\le\tfrac12|\mathrm{Im}\,\Delta a_{\rm crit}|$.
- Axio-dilaton moduli metric: $ds^2_{\rm mod}=d\varphi^2-e^{-\beta\varphi}dA^2$ (Lorentzian
  AdS$_2$, radius $2/\beta$); $\beta_{\rm crit}=\sqrt{2d/(d-1)}$.
- $D(-1)$ instanton action: $S^\pm_{D(-1)}=2\pi e^{-\varphi}\pm2\pi A$.

## Open questions raised

- Is positivity actually *restored* once instantons proliferate? The modified amplitudes are not
  shown to satisfy all Stieltjes constraints — an explicit **wormhole bootstrap** is left open.
- Are multi-boundary wormholes truly subleading in the $1/G_N$ expansion for $d\ge3$?
- Extension to non-compact scalars (continuous flux): does the imaginary-distance bound translate
  to a real-direction constraint like the standard distance conjecture?
- Can the $f_a$ bound be sharpened toward the GUT scale?
- Reproduce the divergence of one-boundary $\mathcal N=4$ SYM integrated correlators at the
  Bogomolny bound from a bulk instanton-backreaction computation.
- The $d=1$ (JT) case has no divergence — its status is deferred to the companion paper.
