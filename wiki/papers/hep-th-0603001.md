## TL;DR

Proposes that the [[entanglement-entropy|entanglement entropy]] of a spatial region $A$ in a
$(d+1)$-dimensional holographic CFT equals the area of a static
[[extremal-surface|minimal surface]] $\gamma_A$ in the dual AdS$_{d+2}$, divided by $4G_N$ —
the [[ryu-takayanagi-formula|Ryu-Takayanagi formula]], structurally identical to
[[bekenstein-hawking-entropy|Bekenstein-Hawking]] black-hole entropy. The proposal is verified
exactly in AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$, where the bulk geodesic length reproduces the 2d CFT $\frac{c}{3}\log$
entanglement entropy, and is applied to higher dimensions and finite temperature.

## Motivation

Entanglement entropy $S_A=-\mathrm{tr}_A\rho_A\log\rho_A$ diagnoses quantum correlations and
critical/topological order, but is hard to compute in interacting or higher-dimensional QFTs.
A long-noted analogy — the traced-out region $B$ is like a black-hole interior for an observer
in $A$ — suggests $S_A$ should have a geometric, gravitational description mirroring the
area law, but a comprehensive holographic formula was lacking.

## Historical context

Builds on Bekenstein-Hawking entropy and its microscopic BPS derivation (Strominger-Vafa),
[[hep-th-9711200|AdS/CFT]] and the GKP-Witten dictionary, the black-hole-entropy-as-entanglement
idea (Bombelli-Koul-Lee-Sorkin, Srednicki), and 2d CFT entanglement entropy
(Holzhey-Larsen-Wilczek; Calabrese-Cardy). The AdS$_3$ check uses Brown-Henneaux
$c=3R/2G_N^{(3)}$, the BTZ black hole, and the Susskind-Witten holographic UV/IR cutoff.

## Main results

1. **RT formula:** $S_A=\mathrm{Area}(\gamma_A)/4G_N^{(d+2)}$ with $\gamma_A$ the static
   minimal surface anchored on $\partial A$; manifestly gives $S_A=S_B$ (pure state) and
   subadditivity.
2. **AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$ check:** the geodesic reproduces
   $S_A=\frac{c}{3}\log\!\big(\frac{L}{\pi a}\sin\frac{\pi l}{L}\big)$ exactly.
3. **Multiple intervals:** a signed sum of geodesic lengths identifies the correct minimal
   surface for disjoint regions.
4. **Finite temperature (2d):** $S_A(\beta)=\frac{c}{3}\log\!\big(\frac{\beta}{\pi a}\sinh\frac{\pi l}{\beta}\big)$
   from the Euclidean BTZ dual; $S_A=S_B$ fails at $T>0$.
5. **Higher-$d$ area law + universal term:** leading UV divergence $\propto\mathrm{Area}(\partial A)/a^{d-1}$,
   plus a cutoff-independent finite constant (even $d$) or $\log$ coefficient (odd $d$).
6. **AdS$_5\times S^5$ vs free $\mathcal N=4$ SYM:** explicit belt/disk results; the finite
   coefficient agrees with the free-field estimate up to an $O(1)$ factor $\sim\tfrac32$
   (the familiar strong/weak thermal-entropy mismatch).
7. **Horizon wrapping:** at finite $T$ and large $A$, $\gamma_A$ wraps the horizon, giving a
   thermal (extensive) contribution and $S_A\to S_{\rm BH}$ as $A\to$ whole system — fixing
   the $1/4G_N$ normalization.

## Methods

Recast entanglement entropy as a classical geometric extremization (minimal-surface / area
functional, analogous to holographic Wilson loops); use the GKP-Witten / Brown-Henneaux
dictionary to translate bulk geometry into CFT data; regulate divergent areas with the
holographic UV cutoff $e^{\rho_0}\sim L/a$ (and $\sim\beta/a$ at finite $T$ via BTZ / thermal-AdS
modular equivalence); benchmark higher-$d$ finite terms against free-field
(Casini-Huerta / Fursaev) estimates.

## Conventions

- **Entropy:** von Neumann $S_A=-\mathrm{tr}_A\rho_A\log\rho_A$; normalization
  $S=\mathrm{Area}/4G_N^{(d+2)}$, the $\tfrac14$ taken from Bekenstein-Hawking and confirmed by
  the horizon-wrapping limit.
- **Surface:** *static* minimal surface on a constant-time slice — no covariant/Lorentzian
  prescription (supplied later by [[0705.0016|HRT]]).
- **Signature:** $(-,+,+,\dots)$; e.g. Poincaré $ds^2=R^2z^{-2}(dz^2-dx_0^2+\sum dx_i^2)$;
  finite-$T$ computations are Euclidean (BTZ / thermal AdS). Central charge $c=3R/2G_N^{(3)}$;
  cutoff dictionary $e^{\rho_0}\sim L/a$ stated up to a numerical factor.

## Key equations

- $S_A=\dfrac{\mathrm{Area}(\gamma_A)}{4G_N^{(d+2)}}$ — RT formula; cf.
  $S_{\rm BH}=\mathrm{Area}/4G_N$.
- $S_A=\dfrac{c}{3}\log\!\big(\tfrac{L}{\pi a}\sin\tfrac{\pi l}{L}\big)$, $c=\dfrac{3R}{2G_N^{(3)}}$
  — AdS$_3$ check = Calabrese-Cardy.
- $S_A(\beta)=\dfrac{c}{3}\log\!\big(\tfrac{\beta}{\pi a}\sinh\tfrac{\pi l}{\beta}\big)$ —
  finite temperature.
- Higher-$d$ belt area: leading $\propto (L/a)^{d-1}$ term $+$ universal $(L/l)^{d-1}$ term.

## Open questions raised

- Covariant / Lorentzian generalization (a static surface is used) — answered by
  [[hrt-formula|HRT]].
- A first-principles derivation of the formula from AdS/CFT (later Lewkowycz-Maldacena).
- Quantitative strong-coupling field-theory checks in $d>1$; quantum ($1/N$) corrections;
  disjoint-region minimal surfaces; two-boundary (eternal) black-hole entanglement.
