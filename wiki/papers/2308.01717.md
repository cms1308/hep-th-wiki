## TL;DR

Proposes a modified [[weak-gravity-conjecture|Weak Gravity Conjecture]] for $AdS_5$/CFT$_4$:
every 4d $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFT with a $U(1)$ non-R flavor symmetry contains an operator with
$\Delta^2/q^2 \leq 12(3c-2a)/k_F$, replacing the central charge $c$ in the
[[nakayama-nomura-wgc-bound|Nakayama-Nomura bound]] by the combination $3c-2a$ that controls
the Cardy-like limit of the [[superconformal-index|4d superconformal index]]
([[supersymmetric-cardy-formula-4d]]) and the entropy of large BPS black holes in $AdS_5$.
Known counterexamples to the original bound — notably SQCD in the
[[conformal-window|conformal window]] — satisfy the modified bound, which is verified against
all superconformal $SU(N)$ gauge theories admitting a large-$N$ limit with vanishing
superpotential, and conjectured to hold for any 4d SCFT
([[modified-ads-wgc-3c-2a]]).

## Motivation

AdS/CFT implies any CFT is dual to *some* quantum gravity in AdS, not necessarily weakly
coupled Einstein gravity — so one can ask which gravitational statements are universal across
all CFTs. The Nakayama-Nomura translation of the WGC, $\Delta^2/q^2 \le 12c/k_F$, was derived
from semi-classical Einstein-Maxwell theory in $AdS_5$ and fails for 4d $\mathcal{N}=1$ SQCD
in the conformal window when $N_f/N \gtrsim 2.1$ (no chiral-ring operator satisfies it). In
2d, modular invariance — the same property behind Cardy's formula — *proves* the WGC; the
authors seek the 4d analog, and note that the universal 4d supersymmetric Cardy formula is
controlled by $3c-2a$, not $c$, suggesting the bound should be modified accordingly.

## Historical context

- **WGC**: proposed for flat space in 2006 (Arkani-Hamed–Motl–Nicolis–Vafa,
  [hep-th/0601001](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0601001)); the
  [[convex-hull-condition|convex hull condition]] extends it to multiple $U(1)$'s
  ([1402.2287](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.2287)).
- **WGC in AdS/CFT**: the CFT bound $\Delta^2/q^2 \le 12c/k_F$ and its SQCD counterexample
  ([[nakayama-nomura-wgc-bound|Nakayama-Nomura 2015]],
  [1509.01647](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.01647)); tests against dense-spectrum SCFTs
  (Agarwal-Song [1912.12881](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.12881), Agarwal-Lee-Song
  [2007.16165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.16165)).
- **2d modularity proves the WGC**: [1603.09745](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.09745),
  [1606.08438](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08438), [1811.00976](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.00976).

  [1810.12067](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.12067), Kim-Kim-Song

- **SCFT technology**: [[a-maximization]] (Intriligator-Wecht,
  [hep-th/0304128](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0304128)); the

  [[hofman-maldacena-bound|Hofman-Maldacena bound]]
  ([0803.1467](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.1467)).

## Main results

1. **Modified WGC** ([[modified-ads-wgc-3c-2a]], the central conjecture): any 4d
   $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFT with a $U(1)$ non-R flavor symmetry $F$ contains an operator
   $\mathcal{O}$ with
   $$\left.\frac{\Delta^2}{q^2}\right|_{\mathcal{O}} \leq \frac{12(3c-2a)}{k_F}\,, \qquad
   k_F \equiv -3\,\mathrm{tr}\, R F F\,,$$
   replacing $c \to 3c-2a$ in the Nakayama-Nomura bound. For multiple $U(1)$'s, the
   [[convex-hull-condition|convex hull condition]] holds with charge-to-dimension ratios
   measured in units of $\sqrt{k_F/[12(3c-2a)]}$. For holographic theories
   $3c-2a \simeq c \sim N^2$, so the two bounds agree at large $N$; they differ drastically
   away from that regime. Conjectured to hold for *any* 4d SCFT, regardless of a weakly
   coupled gravity dual or a large-$N$ limit. It applies only to non-R symmetries: chiral
   operators saturate the original bound for the R-symmetry via $\Delta = \tfrac{3}{2}R$,
   and would violate the new one when $c<a$.
2. **Rescue of the known counterexamples**: four families violate the Nakayama-Nomura bound
   in the chiral ring but satisfy the modified bound even at small finite $N$:
   - $SU(N)$ SQCD in the conformal window $\tfrac32 N < N_f < 3N$: the baryon
     $\epsilon Q^N$ has $\Delta^2/q_B^2 = \tfrac94(1-N/N_f)^2$, strictly below the modified
     bound $12(3c-2a)/k_B = \tfrac98(1-N^2/N_f^2)$ throughout the window (the original bound
     fails for $N_f/N\gtrsim 2.1$). Analogous statements hold for $SO(N)$ and $Sp(N)$ SQCD.
   - $SU(N)$ with $1\,\mathbf{S}+1\,\overline{\mathbf{S}}+2\,\mathbf{A}+2\,\overline{\mathbf{A}}
     +1(\Box+\overline{\Box})$ (weakly coupled fixed point, $g^2\sim 1/N$): violates the
     original bound for all $N$, satisfies the modified one for all $N$.
   - $SU(N)$ with $2\,\mathbf{S}+2\,\overline{\mathbf{S}}+N_f(\Box+\overline{\Box})$: at
     fixed $\alpha=N_f/N$ the original bound fails for all $N$ when $\alpha\gtrsim 0.6$ (no
     chiral-ring operator can ever satisfy it, shown via the convex hull of elementary
     fields); the modified bound holds for all $\alpha,N$.
   - $SO(10)$ with $1\,\mathbf{S}+1\,\mathbf{A}+4\,\Box$ (vanishing one-loop beta function):
     the modified-bound convex-hull distance is **exactly 1** — precise saturation
     (with $R_Q=R_S=R_A=\tfrac23$: $a=\tfrac{34}{3}$, $c=\tfrac{137}{12}$, $k_{F_1}=414$,
     $k_{F_2}=313605$; the original-bound distance is $\sqrt{137/139}<1$).
3. **Comprehensive large-$N$ test**: for all asymptotically free superconformal gauge
   theories with simple gauge group admitting a large-$N$ limit and no superpotential
   (asymptotic freedom restricts matter to rank $\le 2$ tensors, making the list finite),
   the modified bound is *always* satisfied, while the original holds only conditionally
   (SQCD: $\alpha\lesssim 2.1$; $2\,\mathbf{S}+2\,\overline{\mathbf{S}}$:
   $\alpha\lesssim 0.6$; $1\,\mathbf{A}+1\,\overline{\mathbf{A}}$: $\alpha\lesssim 0.3$;
   several adjoint-matter families: always). All super-extremal states found are BPS.
   Classification details deferred to a companion paper.
4. **Scope caveat**: for non-supersymmetric 4d CFTs the collider bound is
   $\tfrac13 \le a/c \le \tfrac{31}{18}$, so $3c-2a$ can in principle be negative; but no
   known nontrivial 4d CFT has $\tfrac32 \le a/c \le \tfrac{31}{18}$ (largest known
   $\mathcal{N}=1$ value $a/c\simeq 1.158$ — since superseded by
   $a/c = \tfrac{507}{419} \simeq 1.21$,
   [[2408.02953|Cho-Maruyoshi-Nardoni-Song 2024]]).

## Methods

- Cardy-like limit of the [[superconformal-index|superconformal index]] (anomaly-based
  asymptotics at the vanishing-holonomy saddle) and Legendre transform to microcanonical
  entropy at large $J$ — the source of the $3c-2a$ combination
  ([[supersymmetric-cardy-formula-4d]]).
- Extremal black hole decay kinematics in $AdS_5$: the strongest bound arises in the
  $Q\to 0$ (flat-space) limit, $m^2/q^2 \le 3g^2/2G_N$, translated to CFT via the
  $C_T$, $C_F$ dictionary.
- [[a-maximization]] to fix IR R-charges, hence $a$, $c$, and $k_F$ for each candidate
  theory (imposing $\mathrm{Tr}\,RGG=0$).
- [[convex-hull-condition|Convex hull condition]] with Gram-Schmidt-orthonormalized flavor
  $U(1)$'s ($\mathrm{Tr}\,RF_iF_j=\delta_{ij}$): the WGC holds iff the convex hull of
  normalized charge-to-dimension vectors of chiral-ring generators contains the unit ball.
- Chiral-ring reduction: composites built from the same field ratios have identical
  charge-to-dimension ratios, so only a small generating set (mesons, baryons
  $\epsilon Q^N$, $\epsilon\epsilon A^{2n}S^{N-2n-m}Q^{2m}$, …) is needed.
- Classification of large-$N$ superconformal gauge theories via asymptotic freedom,
  $b_0 = 3h^\vee(G) - \sum_\chi T(\mathbf{R}_\chi) > 0$.

## Conventions

No wiki-canonical convention exists yet for this area; the paper's conventions are adopted
as-is on all pages created from it.

- **Index**: $\mathcal{I} = \mathrm{Tr}_{\mathcal{H}}\big[(-1)^F e^{-\omega_1(J_1+R/2)}
  e^{-\omega_2(J_2+R/2)} \prod_I z_I^{f_I}\big]$, trace over radially quantized states on
  $S^3$; $J_{1,2}$ are Cartans of $SO(4)$, $R$ the $U(1)_R$ charge. The Cardy analysis uses
  the shifted form with $(-1)^F \to e^{\pi i R}$ via $\omega_1 \to \omega_1 - 2\pi i$ (the
  index is not $2\pi i$-periodic in $\omega_{1,2}$ since $\mathcal{N}=1$ R-charges are not
  integer-quantized). Shorthands: $\omega \equiv \tfrac{\omega_1+\omega_2}{2}$,
  $J \equiv \tfrac{J_1+J_2}{2}$, $\delta \equiv \tfrac{\omega_1+\omega_2-2\pi i}{2}$.
- **BPS condition**: contributing states satisfy $\Delta - J_1 - J_2 - \tfrac32 R = 0$;
  chiral operators obey $\Delta = \tfrac32 R$.
- **Central charges**: $a = \tfrac{3}{32}(3\,\mathrm{Tr}R^3 - \mathrm{Tr}R)$,
  $c = \tfrac{1}{32}(9\,\mathrm{Tr}R^3 - 5\,\mathrm{Tr}R)$;
  [[flavor-central-charge|flavor central charge]] $k_F \equiv -3\,\mathrm{tr}\,RFF$.
- **Bulk-boundary dictionary**: $C_T = \frac{40}{\pi^4}c = \frac{40 L^3}{\pi^2}G_N^{-1}$,
  $C_F = \frac{3}{4\pi^4}k_F = \frac{6L}{\pi^2}g^{-2}$, $\Delta \simeq Lm$.
- **"Violation of WGC"** throughout means no *chiral-ring* operator satisfies the bound;
  non-BPS super-extremal operators are not excluded but deemed highly unlikely.

## Key equations

- Cardy limit of the index (flavor fugacities off):
  $$\log\mathcal{I} \sim \mathrm{Tr}R^3\,\frac{\delta^3}{6\omega_1\omega_2}
  + \mathrm{Tr}R\,\frac{\pi^2\delta}{6\omega_1\omega_2}
  = \frac{8(5a-3c)}{27\,\omega_1\omega_2}\delta^3
  + \frac{8\pi^2(a-c)}{3\,\omega_1\omega_2}\delta\,.$$
- Equivalently $\mathrm{Re}\log\mathcal{I} \sim (3c-2a)\,
  \frac{16\pi^3\,\mathrm{Im}(\omega_1\omega_2)}{27|\omega_1|^2|\omega_2|^2}$.
- Microcanonical entropy at large $J$ (unrefined, $\omega_1=\omega_2$):
  $$\mathrm{Re}(S) = 2^{1/3}\,3^{1/2}\,(3c-2a)^{1/3}\,\pi\, J^{2/3} + O(J^{1/3})\,,$$
  positive by the [[hofman-maldacena-bound|Hofman-Maldacena bound]]
  ($\tfrac12 < a/c < \tfrac32$), matching large BPS black holes in $AdS_5$.
- $AdS_5$ WGC from extremality: $\frac{m^2}{q^2} \le \frac{3g^2}{2G_N}$ (strongest at
  $Q\to0$).
- Nakayama-Nomura bound: $\frac{\Delta^2}{q^2} \le \frac{9}{40}\frac{C_T}{C_F}
  = \frac{12c}{k_F}$.
- Modified bound (main proposal): $\left.\frac{\Delta^2}{q^2}\right|_{\mathcal{O}}
  \le \frac{12(3c-2a)}{k_F}$.
- SQCD check: bound $\frac{12(3c-2a)}{k_B} = \frac{9}{8}\big(1-\frac{N^2}{N_f^2}\big)$ vs.
  baryon $\left.\frac{\Delta^2}{q_B^2}\right|_{\epsilon Q^N}
  = \frac{9}{4}\big(1-\frac{N}{N_f}\big)^2$, satisfied throughout
  $\tfrac13 < N/N_f < \tfrac23$.

## Open questions raised

- No derivation: the modified bound is only tested. A gravity-side derivation would require
  higher-derivative corrections to 5d gauged supergravity *with vector multiplets*
  ([[gravity-derivation-of-modified-wgc]]).
- Is there a 4d analog of 2d modular invariance connecting Cardy asymptotics to the
  low-lying charged spectrum ([[higher-dimensional-modularity]])? Possible connections to
  the Charge Convexity Conjecture ([2108.04594](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.04594)) and the
  CFT Distance Conjecture ([2011.10040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.10040)).
- Do nontrivial 4d CFTs with $\tfrac32 \le a/c \le \tfrac{31}{18}$ (i.e. $3c-2a\le 0$)
  exist, or can they be ruled out? Is there a stricter $a/c$ bound for $\mathcal{N}=1$
  SCFTs than $a/c<\tfrac32$?
- Analogous bounds from Cardy-like formulas in 3d, 5d, and especially 6d, where the Cardy
  formula is controlled by the anomaly combination $\alpha - 4\beta + 16\gamma$.
- Testing the conjecture with superpotential deformations (which can change IR physics
  dramatically) is left for future work; the classification of large-$N$ superconformal
  gauge theories is deferred to a companion paper — since published as
  [[2510.19136|Cho-Lee-Song 2025]], which completes the classification and validates
  the modified WGC across all of it with zero counterexamples.
- Why the $SO(10)$ theory with $1\,\mathbf{S}+1\,\mathbf{A}+4\,\Box$ *exactly saturates*
  the modified bound is noted as curious but unexplained.
