## TL;DR

The companion classification paper announced in [[2308.01717|Cho-Choi-Lee-Song 2023]] and
[[2408.02953|Cho-Maruyoshi-Nardoni-Song 2024]]: classifies all 4d $\mathcal{N}=1$ gauge
theories with simple classical gauge group ($SU(N)$, $SO(N)$, $Sp(N)$), zero
superpotential, and matter allowing a large-$N$ limit with fixed flavor symmetry, that
flow to interacting SCFTs — 20 $SU$, 6 $SO$, 9 $Sp$ families. All fall into three
universality classes Type I/II/III labeled by the effective number of rank-2 tensors
$N_{\text{rank-}2} = 1,2,3$, with universal large-$N$ R-charges and central charges
([[large-n-universality-classes]]): Type I is [[dense-spectrum-scft|dense-spectrum]] and
non-holographic; Types II and III have $a \simeq c$ and sparse spectra — holographic
candidates. Every theory at every finite $N$ satisfies the
[[modified-ads-wgc-3c-2a|modified AdS WGC]] $q^2/\Delta^2 \ge k_F/(12(3c-2a))$, while the
[[nakayama-nomura-wgc-bound|Nakayama-Nomura bound]] fails in numerous newly catalogued
cases.

## Motivation

Two threads. (1) **Emergence of gravity**: classify a tractable corner of
$\mathcal{N}=1$ theory space to ask when a CFT can have a weakly coupled Einstein-like
dual — requiring $a=c$ at large $N$ (Henningson-Skenderis) and a sparse spectrum. Prior
work found only [[dense-spectrum-scft|dense-spectrum]] (non-holographic) theories
(Agarwal-Song [1912.12881](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.12881), Agarwal-Lee-Song
[2007.16165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.16165)); this paper completes the classification
and finds sparse, putatively holographic classes too. (2) **Swampland**: test the AdS
[[weak-gravity-conjecture|WGC]] against a maximally broad, systematically generated set
of SCFTs — including deliberately non-holographic ones — to see whether the (modified)
WGC is a consistency condition of all CFTs, as modular invariance makes it in
AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$.

## Main results

1. **Classification.** Under the assumptions (i) interacting IR fixed point, (ii) simple
   classical gauge group with scalable $N$, (iii) $W=0$, (iv) flavor symmetry fixed at
   large $N$: **20 $SU(N)$, 6 $SO(N)$, 9 $Sp(N)$ families**, each with an extra
   parameter $N_f$. Asymptotic freedom restricts matter to fundamentals and rank-2
   tensors and forces
   $$N_{\text{rank-}2} \equiv N_{\mathbf{Adj}}
   + \tfrac12\left(N_S + N_{\bar S} + N_A + N_{\bar A}\right) \le 3 \quad (SU)\,,
   \qquad N_S + N_A \le 3 \quad (SO,\ Sp)\,.$$
   Fixed flavor symmetry requires $N_{\text{rank-}2} \in \{1,2,3\}$; classes with
   $N_{\text{rank-}2} = 0,\tfrac12,\tfrac32,\tfrac52$ exist only in the
   [[veneziano-limit|Veneziano limit]] (21 further $SU$ families tabulated, deferred to
   future work).
2. **Large-$N$ universality** ([[large-n-universality-classes]]): at leading order in
   $1/N$ (fixed $N_f$), $a$-maximization depends only on
   $\dim(\mathbf{R})/T(\mathbf{R})$, giving gauge-group-independent R-charges
   $$r_{\text{rank-}2} = 1 - \frac{1}{N_{\text{rank-}2}} + O(N^{-1})\,,\qquad
   r_{\text{fund}} = 1 - \sqrt{\frac{1}{18} + \frac{1}{2N_{\text{rank-}2}^2}}
   + O(N^{-1})\,.$$
   - **Type I** ($N_{\text{rank-}2}=1$): [[dense-spectrum-scft|dense spectrum]],
     $a, c = O(N)$ with $a \neq c$, non-holographic; universality restored in the
     double-scaling limit $N \gg N_f \gg 1$.
   - **Type II** ($N_{\text{rank-}2}=2$): $a \simeq c \simeq \tfrac{27}{128}\dim G$
     (Klebanov-Witten-like; $\tfrac{27}{128} = \tfrac{27}{32}\cdot\tfrac14$ with the
     Tachikawa-Wecht flow factor), sparse spectrum.
   - **Type III** ($N_{\text{rank-}2}=3$): $r \simeq \tfrac23$,
     $a \simeq c \simeq \tfrac14\dim G$ — $\mathcal{N}=4$-like, near-free.
3. **Veneziano-limit universality and phase transitions**: for $N_f = \alpha N$ the
   R-charges are universal functions of $(N_{\text{rank-}2}, \alpha)$; for Type I,
   successive decouplings of $qq'$, $qXq'$, $qX^2q'$, … induce an infinite sequence of
   second-order phase transitions in the R-charges at
   $\alpha = \tfrac{3-\sqrt7}{2} \simeq 0.177124$, $0.100506$, $0.0703675$, … — the
   $\alpha \to 0$ limit is not well defined for Type I.
4. **$a/c$ range** ([[n1-central-charge-bounds]]): within the classification,
   $a/c \le \tfrac{417}{368} \simeq 1.1332$ ($SO(5)$ + one symmetric tensor) and
   $a/c \ge \tfrac{70169619 - 220676\sqrt{3201}}{83242974} \simeq 0.6930$ ($SU(5)$,
   $1A+1\bar A$, $N_f=4$); a Veneziano-only $Sp(2)$ theory with 10 fundamentals reaches
   $a/c = \tfrac{339}{514} \simeq 0.6595$, close to the conjectured interacting lower
   bound $\tfrac35$. Empirically the windows $1.25 \lesssim a/c \lesssim 1.5$ and
   $0.5 \lesssim a/c \lesssim 0.6$ are unpopulated. Exact $a=c$ at finite $N$: the two-
   and three-adjoint theories (e.g. 3 $\mathbf{Adj}$: $r_\Phi = \tfrac23$,
   $a = c = \tfrac14(N^2-1)$).
5. **Conformal windows** per family ([[conformal-window]]): upper bound from asymptotic
   freedom, lower bound usually 0 but nonzero for adjoint-SQCD-like Type I families
   (e.g. $SU(N)$ 1 $\mathbf{Adj}$: $1 \le N_f \le 2N$; $Sp(N)$ $1A$:
   $4 \le N_f < 2N+4$).
6. **Holography diagnostics**: with $W=0$, exactly marginal operators are rare (integer
   condition on $p_\chi = \frac{2N_\chi T(\mathbf{R}_\chi)}{\sum_{\tilde\chi}
   N_{\tilde\chi}T(\mathbf{R}_{\tilde\chi}) - h^\vee_G}$), but for $N_f = 0,1$ in every
   Type II/III family a relevant (flip-assisted) superpotential deformation lands on a
   fixed point with a nontrivial conformal manifold, verified by the
   [[superconformal-index|index]]. (The paper's Introduction states this for all
   families, but for Type I it is not established — the paper itself reports failing to
   find such a deformation for $SU(N)$ adjoint SQCD at $N_f=1$ for general $N$.) Combined with $a=c$, sparse spectra, and a
   Hawking-Page-like transition of the large-$N$ index saddle (present for Type II/III,
   absent for Type I), Types II/III are argued to be good holographic candidates
   ([[holographic-duals-of-type-ii-iii]]).
7. **Comprehensive WGC test**: the [[nakayama-nomura-wgc-bound|Nakayama-Nomura bound]]
   $q^2/\Delta^2 \ge k_F/(12c)$ fails for most Type I/II $SU$ families at large
   Veneziano ratio, for sparse large-$N$ Type III theories at fixed $N_f$ (e.g. $SU$
   with $3A+3\bar A$ at $N_f = 3,4,5$ for *any* $N$), and at isolated finite
   $(N_f, N)$ points. The [[modified-ads-wgc-3c-2a|modified bound]]
   $$\frac{q^2}{\Delta^2} \;\ge\; \frac{k_F}{12(3c-2a)}$$
   **holds for every theory in the classification, at every finite $N$, including all
   Veneziano limits**, with some examples saturating it — no counterexamples, no further
   refinement needed (multiple $U(1)$'s via the [[convex-hull-condition]]).

## Methods

- **Enumeration** by anomaly and asymptotic-freedom constraints: cubic gauge anomaly
  $\sum_\chi \mathcal{A}(\mathbf{R}_\chi) = 0$ ($SU$ only), Witten anomaly ($Sp$: even
  number of fundamentals), $b_0 = 3h^\vee - \sum_\chi T(\mathbf{R}_\chi) \ge 0$.
- **[[a-maximization]]** with a Lagrange multiplier for the ABJ constraint
  $\mathrm{Tr}\,RGG = h^\vee + \sum_\chi (r_\chi - 1)T(\mathbf{R}_\chi) = 0$; the
  leading large-$N$ solution depends only on $\dim(\mathbf{R})/T(\mathbf{R})$ — the
  source of universality.
- **Iterative [[flip-deformation|flip-field]] decoupling** of unitarity-violating
  operators, flipping the operator farthest below the bound first (justified by the
  $a$-theorem), re-maximizing, and repeating — $O(N)$ times for Type I.
- **Double-scaling analysis** $N \gg N_f \gg 1$ for Type I: the flip contribution is
  dominated by generalized mesons $qX^nq$ over $\mathrm{Tr}X^m$, giving closed-form
  universal R-charges $r_{\text{rank-}2} = \frac{(4-\sqrt3)N_f}{3N}$,
  $r_{\text{fund}} = \frac{\sqrt3-1}{3}$.
- **[[superconformal-index|Index]]** positivity of the marginal-minus-fermionic
  coefficient to confirm conformal manifolds after deformation; large-$N$ index saddles
  for the Hawking-Page diagnostic.
- **WGC testing via [[convex-hull-condition|convex hull]]**: charge-to-dimension vectors
  of low-lying chiral-ring operators under all flavor $U(1)$'s (orthogonalized w.r.t.
  $\mathrm{Tr}\,RF_iF_j$), normalized by the extremal unit ball, per family and per
  $(N, N_f)$ or $(N, \alpha)$.

## Conventions

Same anomaly conventions as [[2308.01717|Cho-Choi-Lee-Song 2023]]:
$a = \tfrac{3}{32}(3\mathrm{Tr}R^3 - \mathrm{Tr}R)$, chiral operators
$\Delta = \tfrac32 R$, and two-point normalizations
$\langle JJ \rangle \propto \tfrac{3}{4\pi^4} k_F$,
$\langle TT \rangle \propto \tfrac{40}{\pi^4} c$ (so the WGC bounds take the
$k_F/12c$ form). Group-theory data: $T(\Box) = \tfrac12$ for $SU(N)$, $Sp(N)$;
$T(\text{vector}) = 1$ for $SO(N)$; $Sp(N)$ has rank $N$ ($2N$-dimensional
fundamental, $h^\vee = N+1$, adjoint = rank-2 symmetric); $SO(N)$ adjoint = rank-2
antisymmetric, symmetric tensor traceless. $N_f$ counts fundamental *pairs* for $SU$,
vectors for $SO$, and half the number of fundamentals for $Sp$. Veneziano ratio
$\alpha \equiv N_f/N$. The WGC bounds are written in the equivalent reciprocal form
$q^2/\Delta^2 \ge k_F/(12c)$ rather than $\Delta^2/q^2 \le 12c/k_F$ as on
[[2308.01717|Cho-Choi-Lee-Song 2023]].

## Key equations

- Effective rank-2 count: $N_{\text{rank-}2} = N_{\mathbf{Adj}} + \tfrac12(N_S + N_{\bar S} + N_A + N_{\bar A})$; asymptotic freedom forces
  $N_{\text{rank-}2} \le 3$.
- Universal R-charges (fixed $N_f$): $r_{\text{rank-}2} = 1 - \tfrac{1}{N_{\text{rank-}2}}$,
  $r_{\text{fund}} = 1 - \sqrt{\tfrac{1}{18} + \tfrac{1}{2N_{\text{rank-}2}^2}}$, up to
  $O(N^{-1})$.
- Veneziano universality:
  $$r_{\text{rank-}2} = \frac{6N_{\text{rank-}2}^2 - 6N_{\text{rank-}2} - 3\alpha^2
  + \alpha\sqrt{18 + 2N_{\text{rank-}2}^2 - \alpha^2}}{3(2N_{\text{rank-}2}^2 - \alpha^2)}
  + O(N^{-1})\,,$$
  and an analogous closed form for $r_{\text{fund}}$.
- Type II/III central charges: $a \simeq c \simeq \tfrac{27}{128}\dim G$ (Type II),
  $a \simeq c \simeq \tfrac14 \dim G$ (Type III).
- Modified WGC (validated): $\frac{q^2}{\Delta^2} \ge \frac{k_F}{12(3c-2a)}$.
- Marginality criterion ($W=0$): $\mathcal{O} = \prod_\chi \chi^{p_\chi}$ is marginal
  iff every $p_\chi = \frac{2N_\chi T(\mathbf{R}_\chi)}{\sum_{\tilde\chi} N_{\tilde\chi}
  T(\mathbf{R}_{\tilde\chi}) - h^\vee_G} \in \mathbb{Z}$.

## Open questions raised

- Find supergravity duals for the Type II and III families beyond the known cases
  ($\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM, Leigh-Strassler) — [[holographic-duals-of-type-ii-iii]].
- Bulk interpretation of Type I: matrix degrees of freedom with $a,c = O(N)$ and a
  dense spectrum — what is its "gravity" dual?
- Is the empirical $a/c$ gap a theorem? Is $\tfrac35$ a true lower bound for
  interacting SCFTs? ([[n1-central-charge-bounds]])
- Can the (modified) WGC be *proven* generic for 4d (S)CFTs — the analog of the 2d
  modular-invariance derivation? ([[higher-dimensional-modularity]],
  [[gravity-derivation-of-modified-wgc]])
- Compactness and size of the conformal manifolds of the classified strongly coupled
  SCFTs (CFT distance conjecture).
- The Veneziano-only classes ($N_{\text{rank-}2} = 0, \tfrac12, \tfrac32, \tfrac52$):
  future work; extensions to quivers and nonzero superpotential.
