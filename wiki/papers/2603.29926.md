## TL;DR

A recursive-algebraic method for solving the classical equation of motion for the
[[closed-string-tachyon-vacuum|closed string tachyon vacuum]], starting from the quadratic integral
equation~[[2603.29926|Kim 2026]] that the [[fvm-quadratic-integral-equation|FVM formulation]]
derives from the [[mirzakhani-topological-recursion|topological recursion]] of
[[hyperbolic-string-vertices|hyperbolic]] closed [[closed-string-field-theory|string field theory]].
Restricting to zero-momentum Lorentz-scalar states (closed under the EOM by Lorentz symmetry), a
[[seam-graded-expansion|seam-graded expansion]] — grading the string field by the number of
integrated internal geodesics — makes **every order purely algebraic**: the unknown at grade $n$
enters only through point-evaluation at the [[systolic-threshold|systolic length]] $L_*$, so each
grade reduces to a single matrix inversion with no Fredholm equation. Numerically the linearized
operator has a clean spectrum, but the tachyon-only expansion diverges by $\sim 10^{18}$,
confirming that [[ghost-dilaton-condensation-tachyon-vacuum|ghost-dilaton condensation is essential]].

## Motivation

The fate of the closed string tachyon is a central open problem. Unlike the open string tachyon —
whose condensation is the understood decay of an unstable D-brane, solved analytically by the $KBc$
subalgebra — the closed string tachyon couples to the metric and dilaton, so its condensation
restructures spacetime itself. The proposed endpoint in the bosonic string is a "nothing" state
with vanishing cosmological constant~[[2603.29926|Kim 2026]]. An analytic construction would give a
nonperturbative definition of the closed string landscape and a concrete realization of background
independence. The structural obstacle is that [[closed-string-field-theory|closed SFT]] is
intrinsically nonpolynomial (no star product, no associative algebra), so the $KBc$ technology has
no direct counterpart.

## Historical context

- Nonpolynomial closed SFT from minimal-area metrics, with a geometric BV master equation and a
  quantum $L_\infty$ algebra~[[2603.29926|Kim 2026]] (Zwiebach 1992); no consistent covariant cubic
  closed SFT exists, so quartic and higher vertices are mandatory (Sonoda–Zwiebach, Kugo–Suehiro).
- Level-truncation study of the bosonic closed string tachyon vacuum found that the
  [[ghost-dilaton-condensation-tachyon-vacuum|ghost dilaton must condense alongside the tachyon]]
  (Yang–Zwiebach 2005): $t_*\simeq0.416$ at cubic order, $t_*\simeq0.338$ with dilaton $d_*\simeq0.49$.
- [[hyperbolic-string-vertices|Hyperbolic string vertices]] with systole $\ge L$ and constant
  curvature $K=-1$ (Moosavian–Pius, Costello–Zwiebach 2019); explicit closed-form local coordinates
  for the cubic vertex from the Fuchsian equation and hypergeometric functions (Fırat 2021).
- Mirzakhani's Weil–Petersson [[weil-petersson-volumes|volume recursion]] (2006) and
  [[eynard-orantin-topological-recursion|Eynard–Orantin topological recursion]] (2007).
- The [[fvm-quadratic-integral-equation|FVM formulation]] (Fırat–Valdes-Meller): topological
  recursion for hyperbolic string vertices generating all $V_{0,n}$ from $V_{0,3}$, reducing the
  classical EOM to a single quadratic integral equation and solving the analogous stubbed scalar
  QFT exactly. This paper solves that equation for the string.

## Main results

1. **Closure of the scalar sector.** In the sector of zero-momentum Lorentz-scalar states, Lorentz
   symmetry guarantees closure under the EOM (scalar states cannot source non-scalars through the
   cubic vertex), and all spacetime-derivative terms drop, so the component vertex equation applies
   directly.
2. **Seam grading makes every grade algebraic.** Expanding $\Phi(L_1)=\sum_{n\ge0}\Phi_n(L_1)$ by
   [[seam-graded-expansion|seam grade]] (number of integrated internal geodesics), the A/B/C terms
   of the [[fvm-quadratic-integral-equation|quadratic equation]] carry intrinsic seam cost $0/1/2$,
   and grades add. Because the B- and C-terms open new seams, $\Phi_n$ can only appear via the
   A-term, which evaluates at the single point $L$. Hence at every grade $n\ge1$ the equation is
   $(I-M(L))\Phi_n(L)=S_n(L)$ — a matrix inversion, not a Fredholm equation.
3. **Grade-0 seed.** The grade-0 equation is a coupled quadratic system; in the tachyon-only
   projection the nontrivial root is $g_T^{(0)}(L)=1/(C_A V_T^{\mathrm{eff}}(L,L,L))\simeq
   8.10\times10^{-10}$ at $L_*$.
4. **Universal linearized operator $M=2JB$.** The linearization is grade-independent:
   $M(L)=2J\,B(L)$ with $J^{\alpha\beta}=C_A\sum_\gamma V^{\alpha\beta\gamma}(L,L,L)\tilde g_\gamma^{(0)}$
   symmetric and $B$ the $b$-ghost matrix. The dressed seed is an eigenvector of $M$ with eigenvalue
   $2$; in the tachyon sector $M_T(L)=2$ exactly (the linearization of $x=ax^2$ at its nontrivial
   root), so $(I-M_T)^{-1}=-1$ and $g_T^{(n)}(L)=-S_n^T(L)$ for all $n\ge1$.
5. **Seam iteration = genus-0 recursion.** Evaluated at the solution, the seam-graded iteration
   reproduces the genus-0 [[mirzakhani-topological-recursion|Mirzakhani/FVM recursion]]: B-terms give
   the R-channel building $V_{0,n+3}$ from $\langle R|\otimes V_{0,j+3}\otimes V_{0,k+3}$, C-terms
   the D-channel; the A-term back-reaction has no analog in the linear recursion and enforces
   nonlinear self-consistency.
6. **Multi-level structure.** At level $(0{+}2)$: 4 states ($T$, matter dilaton $S_1$, ghost states
   $D_+, D$), 11 vertices, with selection rules $V^{T,T,D}=V^{T,T,D_+}=V^{T,T,S_1}=0$. At level
   $(0{+}2{+}4)$: 15 states, 73 nonzero vertices; new $D_+$-dominated ($g_{D_+}=\pm0.283$) and
   $g_1$-dominated ($g_{g_1}=2.24$) solution branches appear that are absent at lower truncation.
7. **Clean spectrum, tachyon-only divergence.** At the tachyon-dominated seed the $14\times14$
   $M(L_*)$ has $\lambda_{\max}=+2.000000$ and next $|\lambda|=8.41\times10^{-5}$ — no eigenvalue
   near $1$, so the recursion is unobstructed. But the grade-1 convergence ratio
   $\epsilon_B=|S_1^T|/|g_T^{(0)}|\simeq2.6\times10^{18}$ (with $S_1^T(L_*)=(2.08\pm0.05)\times10^9$
   from three independent continuation methods) shows the tachyon-only expansion diverges —
   consistent with Yang–Zwiebach: [[ghost-dilaton-condensation-tachyon-vacuum|ghost-dilaton condensation is required]].

## Methods

- **[[hyperbolic-string-vertices|Fırat local coordinates]].** Vertex data reduced to explicit
  mapping radii $\rho_j$ from the Fuchsian/hypergeometric construction; the only vertex input is the
  cubic $V_{0,3}$, bypassing $V_{0,4}, V_{0,5},\dots$
- **[[twisted-mirzakhani-kernels|Twisted Mirzakhani kernels]]** $\hat R, \hat D$ (standard kernels
  minus Feynman-region subtractions) act as scalar multiplicative weights on $V_{0,3}$, collapsing
  the nonpolynomial EOM to the quadratic integral equation.
- **[[seam-graded-expansion|Seam grading and grade counting]].** Topological bookkeeping (seams =
  integrated geodesics) organizes the iteration and proves the point-evaluation structure at each
  grade.
- **Fock-space projection with $b$-ghost dressing.** Components projected with
  $B_{\beta\alpha}(L)=\langle\beta|c_0^-\,\mathrm{BG}(L)|\alpha\rangle$; level-matching makes $B$
  block-diagonal.
- **Newton solver / homotopy continuation** for the coupled-quadratic grade-0 solutions;
  eigenvalue computation of $M(L_*)$.
- **Analytic continuation of the divergent grade-1 source** via three independent methods —
  Padé–Borel resummation, conformal Padé extrapolation, contour deformation — cross-validated to
  $\simeq3\%$.
- **AI-assisted workflow.** Symbolic/numerical computation, adversarial review, and drafting done
  with Claude Code (Anthropic, Claude Opus 4.6) under the author's supervision; full repository and
  logs public at github.com/mk2427/csft-tachyon-vacuum.

## Conventions

- **$\alpha'=2$**, so the zero-momentum tachyon has weight $h=\bar h=-1$ and $m^2=-4/\alpha'=-2$.
- **String-field constraints** (not gauge conditions): $b_0^-|\Psi\rangle=0$, $L_0^-|\Psi\rangle=0$,
  with $b_0^\pm=b_0\pm\bar b_0$, $L_0^\pm=L_0\pm\bar L_0$.
- **BPZ inner product** $\langle A,B\rangle=\langle A|c_0^-|B\rangle$ with $c_0^-=\tfrac12(c_0-\bar c_0)$;
  nonzero when $\mathrm{gh}(A)+\mathrm{gh}(B)=5$ (sphere ghost-number anomaly $6$).
- **Asymmetric ghost zero-mode normalization** (Zwiebach): $b_0^+=b_0+\bar b_0$ carries no $\tfrac12$,
  $c_0^+=\tfrac12(c_0+\bar c_0)$, so $\{b_0^+,c_0^+\}=1$.
- **[[systolic-threshold|Systolic threshold]]** $L_*=2\,\mathrm{arcsinh}(1)=1.76274717\ldots$,
  reduced length $\lambda_*=L_*/(2\pi)=0.28055\ldots$; conformal weight at a puncture
  $\Delta_j=(1+\lambda_j^2)/2$.
- **$\kappa$ absorbed** by $\Phi\to(4/\kappa)\Phi$ (following FVM), giving $C_A=2$, $C_B=4$, $C_C=2$.
- **Gauge choice** $\beta=\mathrm{BG}$ (Fenchel–Nielsen $b$-ghost) with $\{Q_B,\beta(L)\}=-P$;
  leading coefficient $\mathrm{BG}(L_*)=1.126$.
- **Negative-length convention** (FVM): $L_i<0$ signals a $\mathrm{BG}$ insertion at puncture $i$
  with physical length $|L_i|$, folding integration from $(-\infty,\infty)$ to $[0,\infty)$.

This is the wiki's first [[closed-string-field-theory|string field theory]] page, so its conventions
become the local baseline. The [[weil-petersson-volumes|Weil–Petersson]] /
[[eynard-orantin-topological-recursion|topological-recursion]] machinery here is the same object used
in [[jt-gravity]], but applied to string vertices rather than JT amplitudes.

## Key equations

- Quadratic integral equation (gauge-fixed), schematically
  $$\Phi(L_1)=C_A\,A_{L_1,L,L}+C_B\!\int_0^\infty\!\!d\ell\,\hat R\,A_{L_1,L,\ell}+C_C\!\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty\!\!d\ell_1 d\ell_2\,\hat D\,A_{L_1,\ell_1,\ell_2}$$
  with $A$ quadratic in $\mathrm{BG}\,\Phi$ at the indicated border lengths.
- Grade counting: $\text{grade}=(\text{A/B/C seam cost }0/1/2)+j+k$.
- Grade-$n$ equation and solution: $(I-M(L))\Phi_n(L)=S_n(L)$, then
  $\Phi_n(L_1)=S_n(L_1)+M(L_1)(I-M(L))^{-1}S_n(L)$.
- Grade-0 seed: $g_T^{(0)}(L)=1/(C_A V_T^{\mathrm{eff}}(L,L,L))$ with
  $V_T^{\mathrm{eff}}=\rho_1^{-2}\rho_2^{-2}\rho_3^{-2}\,\mathrm{BG}(L_2)\mathrm{BG}(L_3)$.
- Universal linearization: $M(L)=2JB(L)$; $M_T(L)=2$; $g_T^{(n)}(L)=-S_n^T(L)$.
- Convergence ratio: $\epsilon_B=|S_1^T(L_*)|/|g_T^{(0)}(L_*)|\simeq2.6\times10^{18}$.

## Open questions raised

- Does the multi-level seam-graded expansion converge, or is it asymptotic (requiring
  Borel/other resummation)? The tachyon-only sector diverges; whether inter-level cancellations with
  the ghost dilaton push $\epsilon_B<1$ is the decisive next test. See
  [[closed-string-tachyon-vacuum-convergence]].
- Does the physical tachyon vacuum lie in the zero-momentum Lorentz-scalar sector at all, or does it
  require non-scalar condensation / nontrivial spacetime profiles?
- Is there a closed-string analog of the $KBc$ algebra adapted to hyperbolic vertices that would
  give the grade-0 seed analytically? See [[closed-string-kbc-algebra]].
- Do the new $D_+$-dominated and $g_1$-dominated grade-0 branches correspond to physical saddle
  points or truncation artifacts?
- What is the large-$n$ behavior of $\|S_n\|$ (governed by the moduli-space geometry of $V_{0,n+3}$)?
- Quantum (higher-genus) extension: the genus-1 recursion gives $V_{1,1}(L_1)$ and access to the
  mass spectrum of fluctuations around the tachyon vacuum.
