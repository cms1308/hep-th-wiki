## TL;DR

The infinite-dimensional soft-gluon [[s-algebra|$S$-algebra]] of conformally invariant
nonabelian gauge theory in flat space also exists in $AdS_4$: mapping both $M^4$ and $AdS_4$
into the [[einstein-cylinder]], the leading soft gluon operator on $\mathscr I^+$ becomes the
light transform of the conserved global-symmetry current $J^a_i$ of the boundary CFT$_3$, and
the $SO(3,2)$ descendants of that light transform generate the complete $S$-algebra
([[ads4-s-algebra-from-cft3-light-transforms]]). Along the way: $SO(4,2)$ invariance plus the
leading soft theorem alone already imply the full $S$-algebra, with the subleading tower
*defined* as conformal descendants.

## Motivation

Soft/celestial chiral algebras are the symmetry backbone of [[celestial-holography]] — any
holographic dual pair must share them, as Brown–Henneaux Virasoro underlies AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$.
Yet soft algebras had not been directly identified in AdS$_4$ at nonzero $\Lambda$ (only in
$\Lambda\to0$ limits). Ward's 1980 one-to-one correspondence between self-dual solutions with
$\Lambda<0$ and flat ones, and the deformed $w$-algebra found in AdS$_4$ self-dual gravity,
suggested they should exist. A bridge could also enable importing AdS/CFT technology —
especially [[light-ray-operators|CFT light-ray operator]] results — into flat-space
holography. The apparent obstruction from the AdS energy gap dissolves because "soft" refers
to boost weight, not global energy.

## Historical context

Builds on the soft-symmetry program of [[andrew-strominger|Strominger]] (leading soft gluon
as asymptotic symmetry, 2013; 2d Kac–Moody of 4d YM with He–Mitra, 2015) and the discovery of
the soft towers: the $S$-algebra and $w_{1+\infty}$ from soft theorems
(Guevara–Himwich–Pate–Strominger 2021; Strominger 2021 — see [[lw-infinity-algebra]]), their
twistor and twisted-holography realizations (Adamo–Mason–Sharma; Costello–Paquette). The
observation that the subleading soft gluon theorem is a special conformal transform of the
leading one (Larkoski 2014) seeds the descendant construction. On the CFT side the key input
is the commutator of light-transformed conserved currents (Córdova–Shao 2018), within the
light-ray operator technology of conformal collider physics (Hofman–Maldacena 2008;
Kravchuk–Simmons-Duffin 2018). The AdS$_4$ deformed $w$-algebra (Taylor–Zhu 2023;
Bittleston–Bogna–Heuveline–Kmec–Mason–Skinner 2024) is the anticipated gravity analogue —
realized in CFT$_3$ by the sequel [[2603.26459|Strominger–Wei 2026]].

## Main results

1. **Full $S$-algebra from $SO(4,2)$ + leading soft theorem.** Defining the subleading tower
   iteratively as special-conformal descendants,
   $$\bigl[K_{\bar r,r},\,S^{p,a}_{\bar m,m}\bigr]=(-)^{r-\bar r}\bigl((p-1)+2rm\bigr)\,S^{p+\frac12,a}_{\bar m+\bar r,\,m+r},$$
   and using the leading soft theorem
   $[S^{1,a}_{0,m},S^{p,b}_{\bar n,n}]=-if^{abc}S^{p,c}_{\bar n,m+n}$ to enter the wedge, the
   Jacobi identity implies
   $$[S^{p,a}_{\bar m,m},S^{q,b}_{\bar n,n}]=-if^{abc}S^{p+q-1,c}_{\bar m+\bar n,\,m+n}.$$
   Any theory with $SO(4,2)$ invariance and an undeformed leading nonabelian soft theorem has
   the full $S$-algebra. The descendant definition agrees with the standard Mellin/soft-pole
   one up to boundary terms on $\mathscr I^+$.
2. **Geometric embedding.** $M^4$ and $AdS_4$ are both conformal to the
   [[einstein-cylinder]] EC$^4$; choosing $i^0$ to lie *in* $\partial AdS_4$, a one-parameter
   family of null generators of $\mathscr I^+$ lies in both $\mathscr I^+$ and
   $\partial AdS_4$, running from $i^0$ to its antipode. The common preserved subgroup of the
   two embeddings is $ISO(2,1)\times D$.
3. **Boundary conditions select linear polarizations.** The Dirichlet condition
   $n\wedge F^a|_{\partial AdS_4}=0$ reflects positive into negative helicity, forbidding a
   purely self-dual soft operator at the boundary (only the CPT-violating imaginary
   $\theta=8\pi^2 i/g^2$ would allow one). The allowed combination
   $T^{1,a}_m=\tfrac12\bigl(S^{1,a}_{0,m}+\bar S^{1,a}_{-\bar m,0}\bigr)$ satisfies an
   isomorphic leading algebra.
4. **Leading soft gluon = light transform of the CFT$_3$ current.** With boundary current
   $J^a_i=-F^a_{i\psi}|_{\psi=\pi/2}$ (dimension 2, conserved) and after Weyl rescaling to the
   round EC$^3$,
   $$T^{1,a}(\phi)=2\pi\int_0^\pi dt^+\,\sin t^+\;J^a_+(t^+,t^-{=}0,\phi)\equiv 2\pi\,\mathcal{L}^a(\phi),$$
   the light transform of $J^a_i$ along a null geodesic between antipodal points of EC$^3$.
   Its commutator, computed purely in CFT$_3$ (Córdova–Shao),
   $[\mathcal{L}^a(\phi_1),\mathcal{L}^b(\phi_2)]=-if^{abc}\delta(\phi_{12})\mathcal{L}^c(\phi_2)$,
   reproduces the bulk $p=1$ algebra.
5. **Full $S$-algebra from light-ray descendants.** The tower
   $\mathcal{L}^{p,a}_{\bar n,m}=\tfrac{1}{4\pi}(S^{p,a}_{\bar n,m}+\bar S^{p,a}_{-\bar m,-n})$
   is a complete independent set of $SO(3,2)$ descendants of $\mathcal{L}^{1,a}_m$, closing on
   $$\bigl[\mathcal{L}^{p,a}_{\bar m,m},\mathcal{L}^{q,b}_{\bar n,n}\bigr]=-\frac{i}{2\pi}f^{abc}\,\mathcal{L}^{p+q-1,c}_{\bar m+\bar n,\,m+n}.$$

## Methods

Null-line soft operators on $\mathscr I^+$ with 2d radial-ordered commutators; conformal
representation theory ($(p,\bar m,m)$ as Cartan eigenvalues of $(D,\bar L_0,L_0)$;
commutators fixed by Jacobi identities); Mellin-transform cross-check via linearized
Bianchi identities; explicit Weyl
maps of $M^4$ and $AdS_4$ into EC$^4$ with conformal primary wavefunctions globally extended
by a CPT-fixed $i\epsilon$ prescription; the AdS/CFT dictionary for bulk gauge fields with
Dirichlet conditions; CFT$_3$ [[light-ray-operators|light-transform]] commutators
(Córdova–Shao).

## Conventions

Mostly-plus signature; flat metric $ds^2=-du\,dv+v^2dz\,d\bar z$ with $\mathscr I^\pm$ at
$v=\pm\infty$ ($v$ advanced, $u$ retarded). Gauge normalization
$\mathcal{L}=-\frac{1}{4g^2_{YM}}F^a_{\mu\nu}F^{a\mu\nu}$ with $g_{YM}=1$;
$F^a_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A^a_\nu-\partial_\nu A^a_\mu+f^{abc}A^b_\mu A^c_\nu$. Leading soft
operator $S^{1,a}(z)=-4\pi\int_{\mathscr I^+}du\,F^a_{uz}$; Mellin normalization
$N_p=4\pi i^{2p}/\Gamma(2p-1)$. Einstein cylinder at unit radius,
$ds^2_{EC_4}=-dt^2+d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta(d\psi^2+\sin^2\psi\,d\phi^2)$, Weyl factors
$\Omega_{M^4}=(\cos t-\cos\theta)^{-1}$, $\Omega_{AdS_4}=(\sin\theta\cos\psi)^{-1}$.
Boundary current normalized so $[Q^a,Q^b]=-if^{abc}Q^c$. No convention conversion needed —
the wiki has no canonical convention for this topic yet; wiki pages quote these directly.

## Key equations

- $S^{1,a}(z)=\sum_m S^{1,a}_{0,m}/z^{m+1}=-4\pi\int_{\mathscr I^+}du\,F^a_{uz}$ (eq. 3) —
  leading soft gluon generator.
- $[S^{1,a}_{0,m},S^{1,b}_{0,n}]=-if^{abc}S^{1,c}_{0,m+n}$ (eq. 5) — leading soft algebra.
- Eq. 16 (descendant definition), eq. 21 (full $S$-algebra) — quoted above.
- $X^{p,a}_z=N_p\int_{\mathscr I^+}du\,u^{2p-2}F^a_{uz}$ (eq. 22) — Mellin form of the tower.
- $n\wedge F^a|_{\partial AdS_4}=0$ (eq. 57) — Dirichlet boundary condition.
- Eqs. 74, 76, 80, 81 — light transform, its Córdova–Shao commutator, the CFT$_3$ tower and
  its $S$-algebra, quoted above.

## Open questions raised

- Gravity analogue: the deformed $w$-algebra from an $SO(3,2)$ multiplet of light-ray
  operators containing the [[anec-operator|ANEC operator]] — conjectured here, realized in
  CFT$_3$ by [[2603.26459|Strominger–Wei 2026]] ([[soft-algebras-for-gravity-in-ads4]]).
- Does the $S$-algebra act on IR-finite observables of $\mathcal N=4$ SYM in the interacting
  Coulomb phase? — taken up by [[2606.08582|Alday–Strominger 2026]].
- Non-conformal theories: Wilsonian corrections generically deform the soft algebra (minimal
  coupling to gravity mixes the $S$- and $w$-algebras).
- A gauge-invariant definition of the $S$-algebra on the full Einstein cylinder (the
  symplectic-product operator alone is insufficient).
- Quantum status of the light-ray $S$-algebras in AdS ("potentially well-defined at the
  quantum level"); whether the nonlocality of light-ray operators illuminates the
  nonlocalities of celestial holography.
