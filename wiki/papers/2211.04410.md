## TL;DR

Extended operators — lines, surfaces, and domain walls — live in the cohomology of the twisted
Schur supercharges $\mathbb T_\pm$ of a 4d $\mathcal N=2$ SCFT, built from the local
[[schur-operators]] by a Lorentzian version of Witten's topological descent. Their correlators
depend meromorphically only on where their world volumes cross a fixed spatial plane, so they
**enlarge the Beem–Rastelli et al. VOA of Schur operators into a larger
[[extended-vertex-algebra]]**. The construction is illustrated by explicit operator product
expansions in the $\mathcal O$–$\mathbf L^\pm$–$\mathbf S$ subalgebra of the free hypermultiplet.

## Motivation

The [[scft-voa-correspondence|4d N=2 SCFT/VOA correspondence]] gave analytic control over the
*local* protected operators of an $\mathcal N=2$ SCFT by mapping them to a 2d chiral algebra.
Supersymmetric *extended* operators (Wilson–'t Hooft lines, surface operators) in strongly
coupled, non-Lagrangian $\mathcal N=2$ SCFTs remain poorly understood. This paper asks whether
extended operators also sit inside the twisted Schur cohomology and what algebra they form — the
answer expands the protected sector from a VOA of local operators to a vertex algebra that
includes nonlocal ones.

## Historical context

The SCFT/VOA correspondence — associating a VOA to twist-translated Schur operators annihilated
by a nilpotent supercharge — is due to Beem–Lemos–Liendo–Peelaers–Rastelli–van Rees (2015).
**Topological descent**, building extended operators from local ones in twisted supersymmetric
theories, is due to Witten (1988); this paper generalizes it to a Lorentzian, non-topological
setting. Earlier surface operators in twisted-Schur cohomology appeared in Cordova–Gaiotto–Shao
(2017), Beem (2018), and Bianchi–Lemos (2019); the surfaces here are different. The line
operators $\mathbf L^\pm$ are close cousins of the light-ray operators of Kravchuk–Simmons-Duffin
(2018) — see [[light-ray-operators]]. Conventions follow Lemos (2020) and Córdova–Dumitrescu–
Intriligator (2016); marginal-coupling independence of the Schur VOA rests on the
Baggio–de Boer–Papadodimas (2012) non-renormalization theorem. A companion long paper with full
technical details is cited throughout as Argyres–Lotito–Weaver (2023, to appear).

## Main results

1. **Extended operators exist in twisted Schur cohomology.** From a local Schur operator
   $\mathcal O$, [[twisted-schur-descent]] by $\mathbb T_\pm$-exact bosonic generators produces
   a web of line, surface, and domain-wall operators, all annihilated by both $\mathbb T_+$ and
   $\mathbb T_-$.
2. **Simultaneous $\mathbb T_\pm$-closedness (non-topological).** Although $\mathbf D^{N_+}[\mathcal E]$
   is built from a $\mathbb T_+$-exact generator, it is *also* $\mathbb T_-$-closed. For local
   Schur operators this follows from unitarity; here it instead follows from the detailed
   superconformal algebra, and has no analog in topological descent. This is the identity that
   makes the extended operators meromorphic and hence a vertex algebra.
3. **The descent web is finite.** Using the twisted conformal group $\widehat{SO}(1,3)$ of the
   VOA plane, the $\mathbb{RP}^3$ of possible generators collapses to **10 inequivalent descent
   operations** per level; descent terminates at world-volume dimension 3 (domain walls) — no 4d
   volume operators arise. The classes are $\mathbf L^\pm$ (lines), $\mathbf S,\widehat{\mathbf S}_{L,R}$
   (surfaces), $\widehat{\mathbf W}^\pm$ (walls).
4. **Descent inherits Schur-like properties.** Each $\mathbf D^X$ raises world-volume dimension by
   one, flips statistics, shifts $U(1)_r$ charge by $\pm1$, preserves the chiral weight
   $h_{\mathcal O}$, and commutes with twist translations $\partial_z$. Correlators depend only on
   the holomorphic coordinate $z$ where world volumes meet the plane — the enlargement to a
   vertex algebra.
5. **"Hatted" operators are boundary-like.** $\widehat{\mathbf S}$ and $\widehat{\mathbf W}^\pm$,
   reachable only via the $M_{\pm3}$ (rotation) descent, have correlators with sign
   discontinuities ("boost" and VOA-plane "intersection" discontinuities), so they behave as
   boundary/line operators rather than genuine vertex operators.
6. **Regularization via weight functions.** Descent correlators generically diverge; a
   [[weight-function-regularization|weight-function regulator]] $F(\delta,\alpha)$ renders them
   absolutely convergent while preserving all $\mathbb T_\pm$ Ward identities. Regulator
   independence is conjectured with preliminary evidence, not proven.
7. **Free-hypermultiplet OPEs.** In the $\mathcal O$–$\mathbf L^\pm$–$\mathbf S$ subalgebra
   ([[free-hypermultiplet-extended-va-opes]]) all OPEs are meromorphic in $z$; defining
   $q_\pm \doteq \tfrac12(q\pm\mathbf S[q])$, $q_+$ is a free symplectic boson while $q_-$ has
   completely regular OPEs — motivating the conjecture $\mathbf S[q_I] =_{\mathbb T} q_I$.
8. **$T$ is Virasoro but not the algebra's stress tensor.** $T$ obeys the Virasoro OPE with
   central charge $-1$ but fails the primary condition on descent operators, so the extended
   vertex algebra need not be a conformal vertex algebra — see
   [[extended-va-conformal-vertex-algebra]].

## Methods

Works in the cohomology of the nilpotent supercharge pair $\mathbb T_\pm$. Extended operators
are produced by **generalized (Lorentzian) topological descent** — iterated integration of a
fermionic supercharge $\xi$ (with $\mathbb T\circ\xi = X$) against $e^{\alpha X}$ on a closed
operator. The even subalgebra is decomposed into $\mathbb T_\pm$-closed ($\mathfrak Z_\pm$) and
$\mathbb T_\pm$-exact ($\mathfrak B_\pm$) pieces to identify the descent-generating nilpotent
ideals $\mathfrak m_\pm$. The twisted conformal group action reduces the descent web to 10
classes. Light-cone singularities are handled with a causal $i\epsilon$ prescription and, for
$\langle\mathbf S\mathbf S\rangle$, Euclidean ($S^4$) continuation. Divergences are tamed by
weight-function regularization; free-hypermultiplet OPEs are computed by Wick contraction.

## Conventions

- **Signature/coordinates:** Minkowski $x^\mu$, $\mu=1,2,3,4$ with $x^1$ time. Lightcone
  $x^\pm \doteq \pm x^1 + x^2$; complex $z \doteq x^3 - i x^4$. The **VOA plane** is
  $\{x^\pm = 0\}$ with coordinates $(z,\bar z)$.
- **Twisted Schur supercharges:**
  $$\mathbb T_+ \doteq Q^1_{\ 2} + \tilde S^{2\dot2},\qquad
    \mathbb T_- \doteq S_1^{\ 2} - \tilde Q_{2\dot2}.$$
  These equal $\mathbb Q_1,\mathbb Q_2$ of Beem et al. (2015) and are the $\zeta=1$ case of
  Lemos (2020). Key relations: $\mathbb T_\pm\circ\mathbb T_\mp = \mathcal Z$,
  $\mathcal Z\circ N_\pm = \pm N_\pm$. Here $\mathcal Z$ is the **negative** of the $\mathcal Z$
  of Beem et al./Lemos.
- **R-symmetry:** $SU(2)_R$ generators normalized as in Córdova–Dumitrescu–Intriligator (2016),
  so $r(Q) = -1$, $R\in\mathbb Z$, and $r(\mathbb T_\pm) = \mp1$. Chiral weights $h_{\mathcal O}
  \in \mathbb N/2$; free hyper $h_q = 1/2$, $h_T = 2$.
- **Descent normalization** $N = (-2\pi i)^{-1/2}$ chosen to simplify the hyper OPEs;
  $\epsilon_{12} = -\epsilon^{12} = 1$. "$=_{\mathbb T}$" means equal in both $\mathbb T_+$ and
  $\mathbb T_-$ cohomology.

## Key equations

- Twisted Schur supercharges:
  $\mathbb T_+ \doteq Q^1_{\ 2} + \tilde S^{2\dot2}$, $\ \mathbb T_- \doteq S_1^{\ 2} - \tilde Q_{2\dot2}$.
- Generalized descent (build extended from local): with $X = \mathbb T\circ\xi$,
  $$\mathbf D^X[\mathcal E] \doteq \int d\alpha\; \xi\circ e^{\alpha X}\circ\mathcal E.$$
- Cohomology subalgebras:
  $\mathfrak Z_\pm = \mathfrak{sl}_2 \oplus \widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_2 \oplus \mathcal Z \niplus \mathfrak m_\pm$,
  with $\mathfrak m_\pm = \langle P_\pm, M_{\pm z}, M_{\pm\bar z}, K_\pm\rangle$.
- Simultaneous closedness:
  $$\mathbb T_-\circ\mathbf D^{N_+}[\mathcal E]
    = -\int d\alpha\;\nu_+\circ e^{\alpha N_+}\circ(\mathbb T_- + \alpha\nu_+)\circ\mathcal E = 0.$$
- The ten inequivalent descents:
  $\mathbf D^{P_\pm},\ \mathbf D^{M_{\pm3}},\ \mathbf D^{K_\pm},\ \mathbf D^{P_\pm+K_\pm},\ \mathbf D^{P_\pm-K_\pm}$.
- Free-hyper line/surface representatives:
  $$\mathbf L^+[q_I](z) = -iN\!\int_{L^+}\!\! dx^+\,\tilde\psi_{\dot1 I},\quad
    \mathbf L^-[q_I](z) = -iN\!\int_{L^-}\!\! dx^-\,\psi_{1 I},\quad
    \mathbf S[q_I](z) = -N^2\!\int_S\! dx^+dx^-\,\partial_z\rho^2_I.$$
- Diagonalization: $q_\pm \doteq \tfrac12(q\pm\mathbf S[q])$ — $q_+$ free symplectic boson,
  $q_-$ totally regular OPEs.
- Virasoro operator (central charge $-1$):
  $$T(z)\,T(0)\sim \frac{-1/2}{z^4} + \frac{2T(0)}{z^2} + \frac{\partial_z T(0)}{z}.$$

## Open questions raised

- [[extended-va-conformal-vertex-algebra]] — is the extended VA a conformal vertex algebra (does
  a genuine stress tensor exist)? $T$ is Virasoro but not primary on descent operators.
- Regulator-independence: are the descent cohomology classes independent of the weight function
  $F$? (see [[weight-function-regularization]].)
- Does $\mathbf S[q_I] =_{\mathbb T} q_I$ hold ($q_-$ trivial in cohomology)? What is a generating
  set of the extended VA even in the free theory?
- Do interacting descent OPEs carry information beyond 4d local-operator OPE data?
- Do the $\widehat{\mathbf S}_L, \widehat{\mathbf S}_R$ and wall operators satisfy the expected
  VA-type equivalences, and does marginal-coupling independence survive the extension?
- Extension to the free vector multiplet, conformal gauge theories, and genuine Wilson–'t Hooft
  line operators.
