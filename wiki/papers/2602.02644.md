## TL;DR

Invited review (Physics Reports style, ~200 pages, 443 refs) consolidating
[[carrollian-physics]] — the $c\to 0$ contraction of relativistic physics and the degenerate
geometry of null hypersurfaces — into the current framework of [[flat-space-holography]].
Two central claims: (1) the massless $\mathcal S$-matrix in 4d asymptotically flat space is
exactly encoded in correlators of a 3d [[carrollian-cft]] at null infinity
([[carrollian-amplitude|Carrollian amplitudes]]), equivalent to celestial amplitudes by an
invertible transform ([[carrollian-celestial-equivalence]]); (2) the bulk flat-space limit
$\ell\to\infty$ of AdS/CFT is a boundary Carrollian limit $c\to 0$
([[flat-carrollian-limit-correspondence]]), verified diagram-by-diagram for Witten diagrams
and realized top-down in ABJM/M-theory.

## Motivation

The [[ads-cft-correspondence]] gives holography only for $\Lambda<0$, while colliders,
gravitational waves, and astrophysical black holes live in asymptotically flat spacetimes,
whose *null* conformal boundary blocks a direct string construction. Of the two bottom-up
programs, [[celestial-holography]] (2d CFT on the celestial sphere) suffers distributional
low-point functions, a continuous complex spectrum, and non-associative OPEs; Carrollian
holography (3d Carrollian CFT at $\mathscr I$) builds in bulk translation invariance and the
ultra-locality that makes those "pathologies" natural. The review consolidates the Carrollian
program and argues flat-space holography can be *derived* as a controlled limit of AdS/CFT
rather than conjectured afresh.

## Historical context

Three converging streams:

- **Carroll structures**: Lévy-Leblond (1965) and Sen Gupta (1966) found the $c\to0$
  contraction of Poincaré; Henneaux (1979) and Geroch identified Carrollian geometry on null
  surfaces; Duval–Gibbons–Horvathy ([[1402.5894|DGH 2014]]) proved
  $\mathfrak{CCarr}_d \simeq \mathfrak{bms}_{d+1}$ ([[bms-symmetry]]).
- **Asymptotically flat gravity**: Bondi–van der Burg–Metzner–Sachs (1962), Penrose's
  conformal compactification, Newman–Penrose, Ashtekar's universal structure at
  [[null-infinity]]; extended BMS with superrotations (Barnich–Troessaert 2009–11);
  Damour's horizon equation and the [[membrane-paradigm]].
- **Holography**: [[hep-th-9711200|Maldacena 1997]] and GKP–Witten for AdS; early flat
  limits (Susskind, Polchinski, Giddings 1998–99, de Boer–Solodukhin 2003); Strominger's IR
  triangle and [[celestial-holography]] (Pasterski–Shao–Strominger Mellin amplitudes
  2016–17); Carrollian/celestial bridge ([[2212.12553|Donnay–Fiorucci–Herfray–Ruzziconi 2022]],
  [[2202.08438|Bagchi et al. 2022]]); [[2312.10138|Carrollian amplitudes 2023]];
  [[2406.19343|flat limit of Witten diagrams 2024]]; [[2504.10291|Carrollian ABJM 2025]].

## Main results

Organized by the review's sections:

1. **Carroll kinematics (§2)**: İnönü–Wigner contraction $x^0=cu$, $\vec\beta = c\vec v$,
   $c\to0$ gives the [[carroll-group]] — absolute space, relative time, light cones
   collapsed to lines (ultra-locality). Key isomorphism:
   $\mathfrak{CCarr}^{\text{glob}}_d \simeq \mathfrak{iso}(d,1)$ — conformal Carroll in $d$
   dimensions = Poincaré in $d{+}1$, the kinematic seed of the whole program.
2. **Carrollian geometry (§3)**: structure $(q_{ab}, n^a)$ with degenerate metric,
   $q_{ab}n^b=0$, plus Ehresmann connection $k_a$ defined up to Carroll boosts. The
   fundamental theorem of Riemannian geometry *fails*: torsion-free compatible connections
   exist iff $\theta_{ab}=\tfrac12\mathcal L_n q_{ab}=0$; generically torsion is
   geometrically forced. See [[carrollian-geometry]].
3. **Null hypersurfaces are Carrollian (§4)**: any null hypersurface inherits a Carroll
   structure; the null Brown–York stress tensor is a Carrollian stress tensor whose
   conservation = Raychaudhuri + Damour equations — the [[membrane-paradigm]] as a Carrollian
   limit of the stretched horizon. At $\mathscr I^+$: $\mathfrak{ebms}_4 \simeq
   \mathfrak{eCCarr}_3$, with the flat Carroll structure $q = 2dzd\bar z$, $n=\partial_u$.
4. **Electric vs magnetic limits (§5)**: every relativistic theory admits two inequivalent
   $c\to0$ contractions ([[electric-magnetic-carrollian-theories]]); electric keeps time
   derivatives only, magnetic is secretly a Euclidean CFT one dimension down. Quantization
   bottleneck: lattice-regularized electric theories become generalized free fields
   ([[2407.11971|Cotler et al. 2024]]) — no known Lagrangian quantization gives the
   non-Gaussian time-dependent correlators holography needs.
5. **Carrollian CFT without a Lagrangian (§6)**: conformal Carrollian primaries with weights
   $(k,\bar k)$; Ward identities solved by a time-independent **magnetic branch** (= 2d CFT
   correlators) plus distributional **electric branches** $\propto\delta^{(2)}(z_{ij})$ —
   symmetry does not fix low-point functions uniquely. Carrollian OPEs derived from symmetry
   alone. See [[carrollian-cft]].
6. **Holographic dictionary (§7)**: boundary values of massless spin-$J$ bulk fields are
   Carrollian primaries with $\Delta=1$, $k=\tfrac{1+\epsilon J}2$; the
   [[carrollian-amplitude]] transform maps $\mathcal S$-matrix elements to their correlators.
   Soft graviton theorems = large-$u$ limits of Carrollian stress-tensor Ward identities
   ([[2411.04902|Ruzziconi et al. 2024]]); collinear limits = Carrollian OPEs; position-space
   Feynman rules at $\mathscr I$ compute the amplitudes directly.
7. **Carrollian ⇄ celestial (§8)**: celestial primaries are an invertible $u$-integral
   transform of Carrollian ones ⇒ the two flat-holography proposals are equivalent for
   massless scattering ([[carrollian-celestial-equivalence]]). Celestial
   $\delta(\sum\Delta_i)$ structures, Kac–Moody/stress-tensor conformally-soft currents, and
   the celestial OPE are all rederived through the transform; [[lw-infinity-algebra]] and the
   $S$-algebra reviewed with their twistor interpretation.
8. **Flat limit of AdS/CFT (§9)**: in Bondi coordinates for AdS$_4$, identifying
   $c_{\text{boundary}} = \ell_{\text{bulk}}^{-1}$ makes bulk $\ell\to\infty$ induce boundary
   $c\to0$ ([[flat-carrollian-limit-correspondence]]). Verified two ways that agree diagram
   by diagram: AdS Witten diagrams limit to Carrollian Feynman diagrams
   ([[2406.19343|Alday–Nocchi–Ruzziconi–Yelleshpur Srikant 2024]],
   [[flat-space-limit-of-witten-diagrams]]), and intrinsically in the CFT the electric
   $\delta$-branch arises from the bulk-point singularity of $\bar D$-functions.
9. **Top-down example (§9.4)**: the Carrollian limit of bootstrap-derived ABJM half-BPS
   correlators matches Carrollian amplitudes of 11d supergravity gravitons with kinematics
   restricted to $\mathbb R^{1,3}$ ([[2504.10291|Lipstein et al. 2025]]) — the first concrete
   flat-space hologram from an explicit AdS/CFT pair.

## Methods

- İnönü–Wigner contractions of algebras, fields, and couplings (electric/magnetic scalings).
- Intrinsic degenerate-metric geometry: Carrollian frames, generalized Koszul formula,
  torsion classification.
- Rigging/projector techniques inducing Carroll structures on null hypersurfaces;
  Gauss–Codazzi → null Brown–York → Raychaudhuri/Damour.
- Bondi-gauge asymptotic analysis ($\mathfrak{ebms}_4$ from asymptotic Killing equations).
- Hamiltonian (ADM) contractions for electric/magnetic Carrollian field theories.
- Ward-identity bootstrap for Carrollian correlators and OPEs (no Lagrangian).
- Integral transforms between scattering bases: Fourier ($\omega\leftrightarrow u$,
  Carrollian), Mellin ($\omega\leftrightarrow\Delta$, celestial), and their composition.
- [[flat-space-limit-of-witten-diagrams|Position-space flat limits of Witten diagrams]] and
  bulk-point-singularity analysis of $\bar D$-functions.
- Superconformal bootstrap input: ABJM half-BPS correlators fed into the Carrollian limit.

## Conventions

No wiki-canonical convention exists yet for Carrollian/flat holography; this paper's
conventions are adopted as the working default for the [[carrollian-physics]] and
[[flat-space-holography]] topics:

- Lorentzian signature $(-,+,\cdots,+)$; Klein space $(-,+,-,+)$ for 3-point amplitudes.
  Carrollian degenerate metric signature $(0,+,\cdots,+)$.
- Carrollian contraction: $x^0 = cu$, $\vec\beta = c\vec v$, $c\to0$ (contrast Galilean
  $c\to\infty$); generators rescale $B_A\to B_A/c$, $H\to H/c$.
- Ehresmann connection $k_an^a = -1$; flat Carroll background $n=\partial_u$,
  $q = 2dzd\bar z$, $k=-du$. Conformal Carroll level $N=2$ (dynamical exponent $z=1$)
  throughout the holographic sections.
- *Rectangular* Bondi coordinates: $ds^2 = -2dudr + 2r^2 dz d\bar z$ with the boundary
  flattened to the complex plane ($\det q_{AB} = -1$), $\mathscr I^\pm$ at $r\to\pm\infty$ in
  a single chart (antipodal matching built into the geometry). AdS$_4$ version
  $ds^2 = -\tfrac{r^2}{\ell^2}du^2 - 2dudr + 2r^2dzd\bar z$.
- Null momenta $p^\mu = \epsilon\omega q^\mu(w,\bar w)$,
  $q^\mu = \tfrac1{\sqrt2}(1+w\bar w,\, w+\bar w,\, -i(w-\bar w),\, 1-w\bar w)$,
  $\epsilon=\pm1$ for out/in.
- Boundary operators: massless spin-$J$ fields give Carrollian primaries with
  $k=\tfrac{1+\epsilon J}2$, $\bar k=\tfrac{1-\epsilon J}2$ ($\Delta = 1$); higher-$\Delta$
  operators are $\partial_u$-descendants, not independent (nonstandard relative to the
  modified-Mellin literature, where $\Delta$ is kept general).
- Flat/Carroll dictionary: $c_{\text{boundary}} \equiv \ell^{-1}_{\text{bulk}}$; electric
  scaling $\Phi_\Delta \sim c^{\Delta-1}\mathcal O_\Delta$.
- Null Brown–York: $T^a{}_b = \tfrac{1}{8\pi G}(W^a{}_b - \delta^a_b W)$, $W^a{}_b = D_b n^a$.

## Key equations

Conformal Carroll = Poincaré one dimension up (and its infinite enhancement):
$$\mathfrak{CCarr}^{\text{glob}}_d \simeq \mathfrak{iso}(d,1), \qquad
\mathfrak{bms}_{d+1}\simeq\mathfrak{CCarr}_d .$$

Carrollian stress tensor on the flat Carroll background (conformal, boost-invariant):
$$\partial_a T^a{}_b = 0,\qquad T^A{}_u = 0 \;(\text{no energy transport: ultra-locality}),
\qquad T^a{}_a = 0 .$$

Carrollian amplitude (the dictionary entry — massless $\mathcal S$-matrix in position space
at $\mathscr I$):
$$\langle\Phi^{\epsilon_1}_{\Delta_1,J_1}\cdots\Phi^{\epsilon_n}_{\Delta_n,J_n}\rangle
= \frac{1}{(2\pi)^n}\prod_{k=1}^n\int_0^{+\infty}\! d\omega_k\,\omega_k^{\Delta_k-1}
e^{-i\epsilon_k\omega_k u_k}\,\mathcal A_n .$$

Carroll → celestial transform (invertible ⇒ equivalence):
$$\mathbb O^\epsilon_{(\Delta,J)}(z,\bar z) = 4\pi(i\epsilon)^{\Delta+1}\Gamma(\Delta)
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{du}{(u+i\epsilon\varepsilon)^\Delta}\,
\Phi^\epsilon_J(u,z,\bar z) .$$

Carrollian limit of a CFT 2-point function — the branch-splitting mechanism:
$$\frac{\mathcal N_2}{(-c^2u_{12}^2 + 2|z_{12}|^2 + i\varepsilon)^\Delta}
\;\xrightarrow{c\to0}\;
c^0\underbrace{\frac{\mathcal N_2}{2^\Delta|z_{12}|^{2\Delta}}}_{\text{magnetic}}
+ c^{2-2\Delta}\underbrace{\frac{\mathcal N_2}{2(\Delta-1)}
\frac{\delta^{(2)}(z_{12})}{(-u_{12}^2+i\varepsilon)^{\Delta-1}}}_{\text{electric}} .$$

Leading soft graviton theorem as a Carrollian stress-tensor Ward identity:
$$\langle iS^+_0(z,\bar z)\prod_i\Phi_i\rangle
= \sum_i\frac{\bar z - \bar z_i}{z - z_i}\,\partial_{u_i}\langle\textstyle\prod_i\Phi_i\rangle .$$

## Open questions raised

- **Intrinsic existence**: do the limiting Carrollian correlators define a standalone quantum
  theory, or is flat holography only a limiting procedure on AdS/CFT observables? →
  [[intrinsic-definition-of-carrollian-cft]] (named the central challenge).
- **Quantization**: find a regulator (possibly $c$ itself) giving electric Carrollian
  theories non-Gaussian, time-dependent correlators; lattices give only generalized free
  fields ([[2407.11971|Cotler et al. 2024]]).
- **Massive states**: encode massive scattering at null infinity; understand
  $\Delta\sim\ell$ scaling in the flat limit. → [[massive-states-in-carrollian-holography]]
- State-operator correspondence and conformal blocks for Carrollian CFTs; possible intrinsic
  non-locality of the flat-space dual.
- **Decompactification**: can a modified (R-symmetry-scaled) Carrollian limit of ABJM see
  unrestricted 11d kinematics / the $S^7$? → [[internal-space-decompactification]]
- IR sector as a magnetic Carrollian CFT coupled to the electric hard sector.
- Spacetime meaning of [[lw-infinity-algebra]] beyond the self-dual sector; extended objects
  (strings?) from $\mathfrak{ebms}_4$ representations; higher-dimensional supertranslations
  and relaxed falloffs.
