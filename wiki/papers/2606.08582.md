## TL;DR

Defines an all-orders soft/hard factorization
$\mathcal A_n=\mathcal A^{\rm soft}_n(\epsilon)\,\mathcal A^{\rm hard}_n$ for color-ordered
amplitudes of planar $\mathcal N=4$ SYM, with $\mathcal A^{\rm soft}_n=e^{G_n(\epsilon)}$
carrying all IR divergences (kinematically one-loop exact) and
$\mathcal A^{\rm hard}_n=\mathcal A^{\rm tree}_{n,\rm MHV}e^{R_n}\mathcal P_n$ IR finite.
Using the [[wilson-loop-ope|Wilson-loop OPE]], the hard amplitude is argued to obey the
uncorrected tree-level leading soft theorem and to have tree-exact half-collinear poles at
finite coupling, so its conformally soft gluon modes represent the undeformed tree-level
[[s-algebra]] ([[undeformed-s-algebra-planar-n4-sym]]) — evidence for a quantum-exact
soft algebra in a standard, IR-divergent flat-space 4d gauge theory.

## Motivation

In generic quantum gauge theories IR divergences mean there is no known $\mathcal S$-matrix
for the soft algebras to act on, and standard regulators deform the soft theorems beyond
leading order (loop-level logarithms of Bern–Davies–Nohle type); in QCD confinement kills
the algebra outright. It had been speculated that an uncorrected quantum $S$-algebra could
exist only in twistorial theories with no IR divergences. Planar $\mathcal N=4$ SYM — UV
finite, with amplitudes understood in extraordinary detail — is the natural testing ground.
An obstruction specific to it: the derivation of the full soft tower from $SO(4,2)$
invariance ([[2601.00096|Sheta et al. 2025]]) fails in flat space because special conformal
transformations are anomalous on amplitudes, necessitating the direct amplitude-level
analysis here.

## Historical context

Rests on two decades of planar $\mathcal N=4$ amplitude technology: IR exponentiation
(Magnea–Sterman 1990), the [[bds-ansatz|BDS ansatz]] (Bern–Dixon–Smirnov 2005),
[[amplitude-wilson-loop-duality|amplitude/Wilson-loop duality]] at strong coupling
(Alday–Maldacena 2007) and weak coupling (Drummond–Korchemsky–Sokatchev;
Brandhuber–Heslop–Travaglini 2007), the anomalous
[[dual-conformal-symmetry|dual conformal]] Ward identity defining the
[[remainder-function|remainder function]] (Drummond–Henn–Korchemsky–Sokatchev 2007), the
[[wilson-loop-ope|Wilson-loop/pentagon OPE]] in GKP flux-tube eigenstates
(Alday–Gaiotto–Maldacena–Sever–Vieira 2010; Basso–Sever–Vieira 2013), and Basso's
finite-coupling flux-tube spectrum. On the celestial side it continues the soft-symmetry
program: the $S$-algebra from celestial OPEs (Guevara–Himwich–Pate–Strominger 2021;
Strominger 2021), its loop-level deformations, twistorial quantum theories with exact soft
algebras (Costello–Paquette), and the AdS$_4$/CFT$_3$ realizations
([[2601.00096|Sheta et al. 2025]], [[2603.26459|Strominger–Wei 2026]]). The soft/hard
factorization template is the QED/gravity one of
Arkani-Hamed–Pate–Raclariu–Strominger 2020.

## Main results

1. **Soft/hard factorization** (eqs. 4.2–4.3):
   $$\mathcal A^{\rm soft}_n(\epsilon)\equiv e^{G_n(\epsilon)},\qquad
   \mathcal A^{\rm hard}_n\equiv\mathcal A^{\rm tree}_{n,\rm MHV}\,e^{R_n}\,\mathcal P_n,
   \qquad G_n\equiv\log M_n-R_n,$$
   with $R_n$ the dual-conformal remainder function ($h_0(a)$ fixed so $R_4=0$ exactly;
   $R_5=0$ then follows from collinear universality) and $\mathcal P_n$ the IR-finite
   ratio function of superamplitudes. Tree-level collinear
   divergences sit in $\mathcal A^{\rm hard}$; loop-level ones (collinear anomalous
   dimension $g(a)$) in $\mathcal A^{\rm soft}$.
2. **Smooth soft limit of the remainder** (eq. 2.20): under Assumptions 1–4 (Wilson-loop
   duality; universal collinear factorization; BDS exponentiation of the splitting
   function; uniform convergence of the Wilson-loop OPE in the Euclidean region),
   $R_n\xrightarrow{\rm soft}R_{n-1}$ with no additive constant, surviving continuation to
   the physical region; verified on the two-loop hexagon remainder
   (Goncharov–Spradlin–Vergu–Volovich).
3. **Half-collinear nonrenormalization at finite coupling**: in the half-collinear limit
   ($z_s\to z_2$, $\bar z_s,\omega_s$ fixed; complexified momenta or Klein space), the
   flux-tube saddle gives corrections weighted by $E_k(0)-|m|>0$ (strict at finite coupling
   by Basso's spectrum), so perturbative $\log(p_1\cdot p_2)$ terms essentially resum to
   $(p_1\cdot p_2)^c$ with $c$ a nonnegative function of the coupling: the leading
   half-collinear pole — the soft-algebra structure constant — is not renormalized.
4. **Uncorrected soft theorem for the hard amplitude** (eqs. 4.6–4.7):
   $\lim_{s\to0}\mathcal A^{\rm hard}_{n+1}=S^{(0)}\mathcal A^{\rm hard}_n+\cdots$ with the
   tree factor $S^{(0)}=\frac{\langle n1\rangle}{\langle ns\rangle\langle s1\rangle}$, and
   tree-exact adjacent positive-helicity splitting. $\mathcal A^{\rm hard}_n$ is
   dual-conformally covariant with **no anomaly**. Also
   $\mathcal P_n\to\mathcal P_{n-1}$ in the soft limit (checked at tree level and one loop
   for NMHV via momentum-supertwistor degenerations, and expected from the structure of
   the super-Wilson-loop OPE; the finite-coupling OPE argument is given for the
   half-collinear limit).
5. **Undeformed $S$-algebra** (eq. 5.6): gamma-normalized soft modes
   $\mathsf S^{p,a}_{\bar m,m}$ (residues at $\Delta=3-2p$, wedge $|\bar m|<p$) of the
   Mellin-transformed hard amplitudes obey
   $[\mathsf S^{p,a}_{\bar m,m},\mathsf S^{q,b}_{\bar n,n}]
   =-if^{ab}{}_c\,\mathsf S^{p+q-1,c}_{\bar m+\bar n,m+n}$ — the full tower, not just the
   $p=1$ current algebra. Loop corrections $\Delta\mathsf T$ to the soft-theorem
   *operators* must then satisfy $S$-algebra integrability conditions.
6. **Consistency with the Bern–Davies–Nohle one-loop soft anomaly**: reproduced exactly by
   conjugating the hard subleading soft operator with the soft factor,
   $S^{(1)}_{\rm full}=e^{G_n}S^{(1)}_{\rm hard}e^{-G_{n-1}}$ — the anomaly is no
   obstruction to $S$-covariance of IR-finite quantities.
7. **All-orders leading-log soft correction** (appendix): from the pentagon OPE, for
   $\ell=2,3,\dots$,
   $R^{(\ell)}_n=R^{(\ell)}_{n-1}+U\,P^{(\ell)}_{\rm LL}$ with
   $P^{(\ell)}_{\rm LL}(\sigma,\tau)
   =\frac{(-1)^\ell\sigma\tau^{\ell-1}\,{}_2F_1(1-\ell,2-\ell;2;\sigma/\tau)}{\Gamma(\ell)}$
   — proportional to Narayana polynomials; at one loop the Wilson-loop ratio
   $\log\hat{\mathcal R}$ agrees precisely with Brandhuber et al. 2015.

## Methods

BDS decomposition plus the anomalous dual-conformal Ward identity to isolate $R_n$;
[[wilson-loop-ope|Wilson-loop OPE]] in GKP flux-tube eigenstates with the kinematic
dictionary $e^{-2\tau}\sim\omega_s z_{s2}\bar z_{s2}$, $e^{-2\sigma}\sim\omega_s$ (soft
limit: $\tau,\sigma\to\infty$, $\tau-\sigma$ fixed; half-collinear: $\tau,\phi_e\to\infty$,
$\tau-\phi_e,\sigma$ fixed); finite-coupling gap argument from Basso's flux-tube spectrum;
[[momentum-twistors|momentum supertwistors]] and $R$-invariant identities for the ratio
function; celestial (Mellin) basis with soft generators as $\Delta$-plane residues;
multi-channel pentagon OPE for the leading-log appendix.

## Conventions

Dimensional regularization $d=4-2\epsilon$, IR scale $\mu_{IR}$; 't Hooft coupling
$a=\frac{g^2_{YM}N}{8\pi^2}$, [[cusp-anomalous-dimension|cusp anomalous dimension]]
$f(a)=2a+\cdots$, collinear anomalous dimension $g(a)$;
$\log M_n=-\frac14\sum_\ell a^\ell\bigl(\frac{f^{(\ell)}}{(\ell\epsilon)^2}
+\frac{g^{(\ell)}}{\ell\epsilon}\bigr)\sum_i(-s_{i,i+1}/\mu^2_{IR})^{-\ell\epsilon}
+F_n+\mathcal O(\epsilon)$. Parke–Taylor with explicit $i$:
$A^{MHV}_{n,\rm tree}=i\frac{\langle ij\rangle^4}{\langle12\rangle\cdots\langle n1\rangle}$.
Celestial parametrization
$p_i=\omega_i(1+|z_i|^2,\,z_i+\bar z_i,\,-i(z_i-\bar z_i),\,1-|z_i|^2)$,
$p_i\cdot p_j=-2\omega_i\omega_j z_{ij}\bar z_{ij}$; half-collinear limits require
complexified momenta or Klein space $(2,2)$. Dual variables $p_i=x_i-x_{i+1}$; "Euclidean
region" = non-consecutive $x_{ij}$ spacelike. Soft tower labels $p=\frac{3-\Delta}{2}$;
gamma-normalized modes
$\mathsf S^{p,a}_{\bar m,m}=\Gamma(p-\bar m)\Gamma(p+\bar m)\oint\frac{dz}{2\pi i}z^{m+1-p}
\oint\frac{d\bar z}{2\pi i}\bar z^{\bar m-p}\oint_{\Delta=3-2p}\frac{d\Delta}{2\pi i}\,
\mathcal O^a_{\Delta,+}$, wedge $|\bar m|<p$. The authors note their assignment of
loop-level collinear divergences to $\mathcal A^{\rm soft}$ differs from some other
discussions.

## Key equations

- Eq. 2.3 — exponentiated IR divergence of $\log M_n$ (quoted in Conventions).
- Eq. 2.7 — $F_n=\frac12 f(a)F^{(1)}_n+R_n+h_n(a)$, BDS + remainder.
- Eq. 2.20 — $R_n\xrightarrow{\rm soft}R_{n-1}$.
- Eqs. 4.2–4.3 — the soft/hard factorization (quoted above).
- Eqs. 4.6–4.7 — uncorrected soft theorem and tree-exact splitting for
  $\mathcal A^{\rm hard}$.
- Eq. 5.3 — tree-level celestial gluon OPE of the hard amplitude:
  $\mathcal O^a_{\Delta_1,+}\mathcal O^b_{\Delta_2,+}\sim-\frac{if^{ab}{}_c}{z_{12}}
  \sum_{r\ge0}B(\Delta_1-1+r,\Delta_2-1)\frac{\bar z_{12}^r}{r!}\bar\partial_2^r
  \mathcal O^c_{\Delta_1+\Delta_2-1,+}+O(z_{12}^0)$.
- Eq. 5.6 — the undeformed $S$-algebra of soft insertions.
- Eqs. A.8, A.19 — Narayana-polynomial leading-log soft corrections ($P^{(\ell)}_{\rm LL}$
  and the $R^{(\ell)}_n$ relation, $\ell\ge2$).

## Open questions raised

- Realize $\mathcal A^{\rm soft}$ as IR dressings / a soft-sector CFT$_2$ and
  $\mathcal A^{\rm hard}$ as amplitudes of Faddeev–Kulish-type dressed states, as done for
  QED/gravity — an IR-finite basis of scattering states for nonabelian gauge theory is
  unsolved.
- Connect the loop-level deformation of subleading soft theorems to the holomorphic
  anomaly of special conformal transformations in planar $\mathcal N=4$.
- $\log\omega$ terms as higher $\Delta$-plane poles in the celestial basis; undeformed
  closure directly from double-soft limits of $\mathcal A^{\rm hard}$; explicit checks of
  the integrability conditions on $\Delta\mathsf T$; systematic Wilson-loop-OPE soft
  corrections; the $\mathcal N=4$ supersymmetric extension of the $S$-algebra.
