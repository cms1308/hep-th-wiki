## TL;DR

The detailed long version of the [[2503.22987|companion letter]]: four-point functions of two light supergravitons and two heavy [[giant-graviton|giant gravitons]] in $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM are reformulated as two-point functions of the light operators in the presence of a zero-dimensional ($p=0$) [[giant-graviton-defect|conformal defect]], $\langle\!\langle\mathcal{O}\mathcal{O}\rangle\!\rangle=\langle\mathcal{O}\mathcal{O}\mathcal{D}\mathcal{D}\rangle/\langle\mathcal{D}\mathcal{D}\rangle$. Combining defect kinematics with the [[analytic-defect-bootstrap|holographic bootstrap]], all strong-coupling correlators are computed for maximal giant gravitons and arbitrary KK levels, packaged via a partially broken [[ten-dimensional-hidden-conformal-symmetry|10d hidden conformal symmetry]] into one generating function, and the complete tree-level [[giant-graviton-defect-anomalous-dimensions|anomalous-dimension spectrum]] of defect-channel double-particle operators is extracted. The defect picture is argued to be the natural nonperturbative description of any [[llhh-correlator|light-light-heavy-heavy correlator]], since four-point conformal blocks reduce to defect two-point blocks in the heavy limit.

## Motivation

The planar limit of $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM is well controlled, but genuinely non-planar physics — where the discrete "granular" nature of color matters — is largely unexplored. Maximal giant gravitons, dimension-$\Delta=N$ determinant operators, are ideal probes: their determinant structure invalidates naive ribbon-graph counting and their large dimension signals nonperturbative physics. Yet their correlators were essentially only known in free theory or at weak coupling to three loops, with no strong-coupling four-point results beyond integrated-correlator localization predictions. The goal is a computational framework that reaches strong coupling and reveals hidden structure.

## Historical context

The setting is 't Hooft's large-$N$ expansion (1974) and AdS/CFT~[[hep-th-9711200|Maldacena 1997]] (with Gubser–Klebanov–Polyakov and Witten 1998). Giant gravitons as $S^3\subset S^5$ D3-branes (McGreevy–Susskind–Toumbas 2000; Balasubramanian–Berkooz–Naqvi–Strassler 2001; Hashimoto–Hirano–Itzhaki 2000), with the Schur-polynomial/determinant combinatorics of Corley–Jevicki–Ramgoolam 2002, supply the operators. The method builds on the Rastelli–Zhou holographic bootstrap (2016–2017), the superconformal Ward identities of Eden et al. 2000 and Nirschl–Osborn 2004, the 10d hidden conformal symmetry of Caron-Huot–Trinh 2018 and its double-particle-unmixing consequences (Aprile–Drummond–Heslop–Paul), the defect conformal-block Casimir technology of Billò–Gonçalves–Lauria–Meineri 2016, and the defect bootstrap of Giménez-Grau 2023, Chen–Zhou 2023 (6d $(2,0)$), and Zhou 2024 (the $p=-1$/$\mathbb{RP}^4$ case). It is the long version of the letter~[[2503.22987|Chen-Jiang-Zhou 2025]]; a weak-coupling companion (Wu 2025) supplies loop integrands.

## Main results

**1. Zero-dimensional defect reformulation.** $\langle\!\langle\mathcal{O}\mathcal{O}\rangle\!\rangle=\langle\mathcal{O}\mathcal{O}\mathcal{D}\mathcal{D}\rangle/\langle\mathcal{D}\mathcal{D}\rangle$. The giant-graviton pair defines a $p=0$ defect breaking $SO(5,1)\to SO(1,1)\times SO(4)$ and $SO(6)\to SO(2)\times SO(4)$, preserving $SU(2|2)\times SU(2|2)\subset PSU(2,2|4)$. It is characterized by projectors $\mathbb{N},\mathbb{M}$ and their antisymmetric square roots $\mathbb{n},\mathbb{m}$ (only simultaneous $3\leftrightarrow4$ exchange is a symmetry).

**2. Parity-odd defect blocks.** A feature unique to $p=0$: defect-channel blocks come in $3\leftrightarrow4$ parity-even ($+$) and parity-odd ($-$) versions,
$$\widehat{g}_{\widehat{\Delta},s}^\pm=C_s^{(1)}\!\big(\tfrac{\chi}{2}\big)\times\begin{cases}V^{\widehat{\Delta}/2}, & 0<V<1,\\[2pt] \pm V^{-\widehat{\Delta}/2}, & V\ge1.\end{cases}$$
Parity-odd blocks exist only for zero-dimensional defects.

**3. All strong-coupling correlators via bootstrap.** An ansatz $\mathcal{G}^{\rm ansatz}_{k_1k_2}=\mathcal{G}^{\rm bulk}+\mathcal{G}^{\rm defect}+\mathcal{G}^{\rm contact}$ with non-extremality cutoffs ($\Delta_\mathcal{X}-\ell_\mathcal{X}<k_1+k_2$ bulk; $\widehat{\Delta}-s\le\min\{k_1,k_2\}$ defect), constrained by the superconformal Ward identities, uniquely fixes every $\langle\!\langle\mathcal{O}_{k_1}\mathcal{O}_{k_2}\rangle\!\rangle$ ($k_2-k_1$ even, any KK levels) up to one overall constant fixed by a protected one-point function.

**4. Partially broken 10d hidden conformal symmetry.** All reduced correlators $H_{k_1k_2}$ follow from a single seed $H_{22}$ by uplifting to a twelve-dimensional embedding $Z_i=(P_i,t_i)$ with $P_a\!\cdot\!\mathbb{N}\!\cdot\!P_b\to Z_a\!\cdot\!(\mathbb{N}+\mathbb{M})\!\cdot\!Z_b$ and $H_{k_1k_2}=\tfrac{\sqrt{k_1k_2}}{2}\mathbf{H}|_{\lambda_1^{k_1-2}\lambda_2^{k_2-2}}$. The novelty over Caron-Huot–Trinh is that the defect projector $\mathbb{N}+\mathbb{M}$ (onto the D3-brane worldvolume $\mathbb{R}\times S^3$) is promoted, not individual points $P_3,P_4$. The same structure organizes the weak-coupling loop integrands.

**5. Mellin space and flat-space limit.** The reduced correlators admit a Mellin representation; the seed gives $\mathcal{M}_{22}=2/[(\gamma-1)(\delta-1)]$, and in a 10d generalized-Mellin form the amplitude collapses to $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{M}}_{k_1k_2}=1/[(\boldsymbol\delta-1)(\boldsymbol\gamma-1)]$, independent of $k_1,k_2$. The high-energy $1/(\gamma\delta)$ behavior matches string–D-brane scattering.

**6. Complete defect anomalous-dimension spectrum.** Unmixing degenerate defect-channel double-particle operators $\widehat{[\mathcal{O}_p]}_{n,s}$ gives the closed form
$$\widehat{\gamma}^{(1)}_p=-\frac{2\,M_t^{(2)}M_{t+s+1}^{(2)}}{(s+p-r_1-1)_3},\qquad M_t^{(2)}=(t-1)(t+r_1),\quad t=\tfrac{\widehat{\tau}-r_1-r_2}{2},$$
mirroring bulk-channel double-trace anomalous dimensions from four-point unmixing.

**7. Hidden symmetry diagonalizes the unmixing.** Decomposing the 10d generating function into single-twist $p=4$, $q=6$ defect blocks produces mutually orthogonal projectors whose coefficients are the diagonalized anomalous dimensions — no matrix diagonalization needed.

**8. Fourth-order all-loop leading-log operator.** A fourth-order operator $\widehat{\mathbf{\Delta}}^{(4)}$ has the long defect superconformal blocks as eigenfunctions with eigenvalue $M_t^{(2)}M_{t+s+1}^{(2)}$, giving the $\log^\kappa V$ coefficient at any loop $\kappa$ as $(\widehat{\mathbf{\Delta}}^{(4)})^{\kappa-1}h^{(\kappa)}$ — the defect analogue of the Caron-Huot–Trinh eighth-order operator.

**9. Quantum vs. classical moduli averaging.** For two-point (and higher) correlators the moduli-space average must be done *before* computing (quantum superposition $|\Psi\rangle=\int d\Omega\,|\Omega\rangle$), not after (mixed state $\rho=\int d\Omega\,|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|$); only the former reproduces factorization and treats the giant graviton as a quantum eigenstate. The two agree only for one-point functions, which is why earlier work did not see the distinction.

**10. Defect emergence for generic LLHH.** For any $\langle\mathcal{O}_{\Delta_1}\mathcal{O}_{\Delta_2}\mathcal{O}_Q\mathcal{O}_Q\rangle$, in the heavy limit $Q\to\infty$ four-point blocks reduce to defect two-point blocks: the s-channel becomes the bulk channel and the *union* of t- and u-channels becomes the parity-even defect block $\widehat g^+_{\widehat\Delta,s}$ (background dimension $Q$ subtracted). Since blocks form a basis, this holds nonperturbatively and without supersymmetry; a nonperturbative analytic-functional program for LLHH is outlined.

## Methods

- **Embedding-space defect kinematics**: symmetric projectors $\mathbb{N},\mathbb{M}$ and antisymmetric square roots $\mathbb{n},\mathbb{m}$; defect cross ratios $\xi,\chi$ (spacetime) and $\Sigma,\bar\Sigma$ (R-symmetry).
- **Casimir-equation block construction**: one-particle quadratic Casimirs projected to $SO(1,1)\times SO(4)$ and $SO(2)\times SO(4)$, giving the $p=0$ specialization of Billò et al. blocks with new parity-odd branches.
- **[[analytic-defect-bootstrap|Holographic defect bootstrap]]**: bulk/defect exchange + contact Witten diagrams constrained by the superconformal Ward identities, with coefficients tied by shared single-particle data $\lambda_{k_1k_2k}=\sqrt{k_1k_2k}/N$.
- **D3-brane effective action**: DBI+WZ expanded to quadratic order for the defect fluctuation spectrum (with a "Doppler" shift $\Delta_{\rm static}=\Delta_{\rm CMF}+r_2$ from motion along $\Phi$) and one-point-function checks.
- **[[ten-dimensional-hidden-conformal-symmetry|Hidden-symmetry uplift]]**, **OPE unmixing** ($\mathbf{B}\mathbf{B}^T=\mathbf{N}$, $\mathbf{B}\mathbf{\Gamma}\mathbf{B}^T=\mathbf{\Omega}$), and **large-$Q$ saddle-point of Witten diagrams** (heavy propagators localize the AdS integral onto the boundary geodesic).

## Conventions

Euclidean CFT$_4$ uplifted to $\mathbb{R}^{1,5}$; null $P_A=\big(\tfrac{1+x^2}{2},\tfrac{1-x^2}{2},x^\mu\big)$. Supergravitons $\mathcal{O}_k=\mathrm{tr}(X^it^i)^k$ ($t\cdot t=0$, $\Delta=k$); giant gravitons are sub-determinants $\mathcal{D}_M$, $\Delta=M$, maximal at $M=N$; gauge group $SU(N)$ in the bootstrap ($U(N)$ in the free-theory reference — the origin of the even-$p$ discrepancy in the consistency check). Cross ratios as in the [[2503.22987|letter]], with defect combinations $\xi=U/\sqrt V$, $\chi=(1-U+V)/\sqrt V$, $\Sigma=1/\sqrt{\sigma\tau}$, $\bar\Sigma=(\sigma+\tau)/\sqrt{\sigma\tau}$. Large-$N$: $\widehat\Delta=\widehat\Delta^{(0)}+\tfrac{2}{N}\widehat\gamma^{(1)}+\dots$ (note the factor $2/N$). Mass–dimension on the $p=0$ defect uses $d=0$: $m^2=\Delta(\Delta-d)\to\Delta^2$.

## Key equations

- $\langle\!\langle\mathcal{O}\mathcal{O}\rangle\!\rangle=\langle\mathcal{O}\mathcal{O}\mathcal{D}\mathcal{D}\rangle/\langle\mathcal{D}\mathcal{D}\rangle$ — defining $p=0$ defect two-point function.
- $\mathbb{N}_{AB}=\tfrac{P_{3,A}P_{4,B}+P_{4,A}P_{3,B}}{P_3\cdot P_4}$, $\ \mathbb{n}_{AB}=\tfrac{P_{3,A}P_{4,B}-P_{4,A}P_{3,B}}{P_3\cdot P_4}$ (and R-symmetry $\mathbb{M},\mathbb{m}$).
- $(z\partial_z-\alpha\partial_\alpha)\mathcal{F}|_{\alpha=1/z}=0$ — superconformal Ward identity.
- $\widehat{\gamma}^{(1)}_p=-\dfrac{2M_t^{(2)}M_{t+s+1}^{(2)}}{(s+p-r_1-1)_3}$ — complete tree-level defect double-particle anomalous dimensions.
- $\widehat{\mathbf{\Delta}}^{(4)}=\dfrac{x\bar x\beta\bar\beta}{(x-\bar x)(\beta-\bar\beta)}(x\partial_x-\beta\partial_\beta)(x\partial_x-\bar\beta\partial_{\bar\beta})(\bar x\partial_{\bar x}-\beta\partial_\beta)(\bar x\partial_{\bar x}-\bar\beta\partial_{\bar\beta})\dfrac{(x-\bar x)(\beta-\bar\beta)}{x\bar x\beta\bar\beta}$, with $x=1-z$, $\beta=\alpha/(1-\alpha)$ — all-loop leading-log generator.
- $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{M}}_{k_1k_2}=1/[(\boldsymbol\delta-1)(\boldsymbol\gamma-1)]$, $\boldsymbol\delta=\delta+\tilde\delta$, $\boldsymbol\gamma=\gamma+\tilde\gamma+1$ — $k$-independent 10d Mellin amplitude.

## Open questions raised

- Compute $\mathcal{O}(1/N^2)$ (one-loop AdS) and string ($\alpha'$) corrections via the defect unitarity method and defect flat-space limits.
- Does the partially broken hidden symmetry survive for non-maximal and dual giant gravitons?
- Extend to five-point functions and [[four-giant-graviton-correlators|four-giant-graviton correlators]] (defect-defect interactions); to $AdS_7\times S^4$, $AdS_4\times S^7$.
- Rigorously construct the nonperturbative analytic-functional basis for general LLHH; connect to dispersion relations / Polyakov–Mellin bootstrap.
- Can integrability reproduce the extracted defect anomalous-dimension spectrum? Applications to black hole microstates and ETH; large-charge dual giants on the Coulomb branch.
