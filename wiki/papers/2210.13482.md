## TL;DR

Simulating a $2+1$D quantum Ising transition directly on $S^2\times\mathbb R$ — by projecting interacting electrons on a monopole sphere onto the lowest Landau level (the "[[fuzzy-sphere|fuzzy sphere]]") — makes the [[state-operator-correspondence]] of a 3D CFT visible in a tiny system. Exact diagonalization of only $N=8$–$16$ spins organizes the low-lying spectrum into complete 3D [[conformal-multiplet|conformal multiplets]], yielding scaling dimensions of 13 parity-even primaries (matching the [[conformal-bootstrap]] to $<1.6\%$) and 2 previously unknown parity-odd primaries. This is the first *direct* demonstration of the emergent conformal symmetry of the [[3d-ising-cft|3D Ising transition]] conjectured by Polyakov half a century ago, and it opens a general microscopic route to 3D CFTs.

## Motivation

In $d=2$, emergent conformal symmetry is confirmed by putting a critical quantum chain on $S^1\times\mathbb R$ (a periodic 1D lattice) and reading the [[state-operator-correspondence]] off its finite-size spectrum, $\delta E_n=(v/R)\Delta_n$. In $d\ge3$ this route is geometrically obstructed: no regular lattice tiles the curved sphere $S^{d-1\ge2}$. Consequently every prior piece of evidence for 3D Ising conformality was *indirect* — the numerical [[conformal-bootstrap]] *assumes* conformal symmetry as an input, and Monte Carlo simulations work on non-conformal manifolds ($T^3$, $T^2\times\mathbb R$). What was missing was a genuine quantum Hamiltonian realized on $S^2$, so that radial quantization could be tested from first principles. The [[lowest-landau-level-projection|Landau-level construction]] supplies exactly that.

## Historical context

- **Onsager 1944** — exact solution of the 2D Ising model.
- **Polyakov 1970** — conjectured emergent conformal symmetry at critical points; the birth of the CFT idea (see [[emergent-conformal-symmetry-3d-ising]]).
- **Belavin–Polyakov–Zamolodchikov 1984; Cardy 1984–85** — 2D CFT and the cylinder [[state-operator-correspondence]], $\delta E_n=(v/R)\Delta_n$ (see [[john-cardy]]).
- **Haldane 1983** — Landau levels on a monopole sphere and pseudopotentials, the technology reused here (see [[fdm-haldane]]).
- **Madore 1992** — the fuzzy (non-commutative) two-sphere.
- **Ippoliti–Mong–Assaad–Zaletel 2018** — half-filled-LLL regularization as a lattice-model design tool; the direct inspiration.
- **Rychkov, El-Showk, Kos–Poland–Simmons-Duffin 2009–2016** — the 3D Ising numerical [[conformal-bootstrap]] that supplies the benchmark spectrum.
- **Brower et al. 2013–2021** — radial-lattice discretizations of the sphere, which did *not* exhibit a clean state-operator correspondence.

## Main results

1. **A fuzzy-sphere Ising model.** Spinful electrons in the lowest spherical Landau level with $SO(3)$-invariant density-density interactions realize a continuous quantum phase transition from a quantum Hall Ising ferromagnet to a paramagnet, in the 3D Ising universality class. The critical point sits at $V_0=4.75$, $h_c\approx3.16$ (energy unit $V_1=1$).
2. **State-operator correspondence verified.** After fixing the single non-universal scale so the stress tensor $T_{\mu\nu}$ sits at $\Delta_T=3$, the lowest ~70 eigenstates ($\ell\le4$) organize into *complete* 3D [[conformal-multiplet|conformal multiplets]] — primaries plus their $\partial_\mu$, $\square$, and $\varepsilon$-tensor descendants — with no missing or extra states. This is direct, bootstrap-independent evidence of conformal symmetry.
3. **13 parity-even primaries.** Dimensions from $N=16$ exact diagonalization agree with bootstrap to $<1.6\%$: e.g. $\sigma$ at $\Delta=0.524$ (CB $0.518$), $\epsilon$ at $1.414$ (CB $1.413$), $\epsilon'$ at $3.838$, $\sigma'$ at $5.303$, $T'_{\mu\nu}$ at $5.583$.
4. **Two new parity-odd primaries.** Scalars $\epsilon^{P-}$ at $\Delta\approx10.01$ ($\mathbb Z_2{=}{+},P{=}{-},\ell{=}0$) and $\sigma^{P-}$ at $\Delta\approx11.19$ ($\mathbb Z_2{=}{-},P{=}{-},\ell{=}0$), not previously identified by the bootstrap or any other method.
5. **No low [[virial-current]].** No primary in the $(\mathbb Z_2{=}{+},P{=}{+},\text{odd }\ell)$ sector below $\Delta=7$; in particular the $\ell=1$ virial-current candidate lies above $\Delta=7$, structurally explaining why the transition is conformal, not merely scale-invariant.
6. **Tiny systems suffice.** Even $N=4$ spins yields 6 primaries and approximate conformal invariance; $N=16$ (16 GB, ~30 min on a laptop) gives the full table, with finite-size effects far smaller than in 3D classical Monte Carlo.

## Methods

- **[[lowest-landau-level-projection|Lowest Landau level projection]].** Electrons on a radius-$r$ sphere threaded by a $4\pi s$ monopole form spherical Landau levels; projecting onto the $2s+1$-fold degenerate LLL yields orbitals in an $SO(3)$ spin-$s$ irrep and makes the coordinates non-commuting — the [[fuzzy-sphere]].
- **Haldane pseudopotentials.** Ultra-local real-space interactions are converted, via monopole-harmonic expansion and Wigner $3j/6j$ symbols, into pseudopotentials $V_0,V_1$ that define the second-quantized Hamiltonian.
- **[[exact-diagonalization|Exact diagonalization]] and DMRG.** ED for $N\le16$; DMRG up to $N=36$ for finite-size scaling. Eigenstates carry exact quantum numbers $(\mathbb Z_2,P,\ell)$ from the model's Ising, particle-hole, and $SO(3)$ symmetries.
- **Finite-size scaling.** The order parameter obeys $\langle M^2\rangle\sim N^{2-\Delta_\sigma}$; rescaled curves for different $N$ cross at $h_c$, locating the transition.
- **Multiplet decomposition.** Iteratively identify the lowest state in each symmetry sector as a primary, enumerate its expected descendants from 3D conformal representation theory, remove that multiplet, and repeat until the whole low spectrum is matched.
- **Stress-tensor calibration.** The one non-universal energy-to-dimension factor is fixed by $\Delta_T\equiv3$, requiring no bootstrap input.

## Conventions

- Units $\hbar=e=c=1$; monopole strength $2s\in\mathbb Z$, LLL degeneracy $N=2s+1$ = number of electrons (half filling) = number of Ising spins. Energy unit $V_1=1$; tuning parameters $(V_0,h)$.
- Euclidean signature; conformal group $SO(d+1,1)$, rotations $SO(3)$ in $d=3$.
- **Symmetry dictionary (UV→IR):** Ising $\mathbb Z_2\to$ Ising $\mathbb Z_2$; $SO(3)$ sphere rotation $\to$ Lorentz $SO(3)$; microscopic particle-hole $\to$ spacetime **parity** $P$ of the CFT. This last identification is nonstandard and is verified in the paper by showing the relevant vector transforms under an improper $O(3)$ element.
- State-operator relation $\delta E_n=(v/R)\Delta_n$, with $v$ model-dependent and fixed by $\Delta_T\equiv3$. The crossing analysis for $h_c$ uses the bootstrap value $\Delta_\sigma=0.5181489$ as input.

## Key equations

- Sphere kinetic Hamiltonian and Landau levels: $H_0=\frac{1}{2M_er^2}\Lambda_\mu^2$, $E_n=\frac{n(n+1)+(2n+1)s}{2M_er^2}$.
- LLL orbitals (monopole harmonics): $\Phi_m(\theta,\varphi)=N_m\,e^{im\varphi}\cos^{s+m}\!\tfrac\theta2\,\sin^{s-m}\!\tfrac\theta2$, $m=-s,\dots,s$.
- Fuzzy-sphere non-commutativity: $[\tilde x_\mu,\tilde x_\nu]=i\frac{r}{s}\,\epsilon_{\mu\nu\rho}\tilde x_\rho$.
- Projected Ising Hamiltonian: $H=H_{00}+H_{zz}+H_t$, with $H_t=-h\sum_m\mathbf c^\dagger_m\sigma^x\mathbf c_m$ and $H_{zz}=-\tfrac12\sum V_{m_1m_2m_3m_4}(\mathbf c^\dagger_{m_1}\sigma^z\mathbf c_{m_4})(\mathbf c^\dagger_{m_2}\sigma^z\mathbf c_{m_3})\delta_{m_1+m_2,m_3+m_4}$.
- Order parameter and scaling: $M=\sum_m\mathbf c^\dagger_m\tfrac{\sigma^z}{2}\mathbf c_m$, $\langle M^2\rangle\sim N^{2-\Delta_\sigma}$.
- Cardy state-operator formula: $\delta E_n=\frac{v}{R}\Delta_n$.
- 3D descendant towers (scalar): $\partial_{\nu_1}\!\cdots\partial_{\nu_j}\square^n O$ has dimension/spin $(\Delta+2n+j,\,j)$; the $\varepsilon$-tensor operation $\varepsilon_{\mu\rho\tau}\partial_\rho(\cdots)$ raises $\Delta$ by 1 and flips parity, generating $P=-1$ descendants of $P=+1$ primaries.

## Open questions raised

- Precise dimensions and identity of the two new parity-odd primaries; can bootstrap of spinning correlators (e.g. $\langle TTTT\rangle$) confirm them?
- The critical Binder cumulant $U_4^c$ on $S^2\times\mathbb R$, and its computation from bootstrap four-point data.
- Extending the scheme to $O(N)$ Wilson–Fisher, deconfined criticality, and the conformal window of 3D gauge theories.
- Computing OPE coefficients, four-point/thermal correlators, and the $F$-quantity directly on the sphere.
- Whether fuzzy-sphere regularization can be recast in the language of non-commutative geometry and used for continuum QFTs without UV–IR mixing.
