# JT gravity as a matrix integral

## TL;DR

The genus expansion of [[jt-gravity|Jackiw–Teitelboim gravity]] — the sum over 2d hyperbolic
surfaces with $n$ asymptotic "Schwarzian" boundaries and arbitrary handles — is computed
exactly by a double-scaled Hermitian [[jt-gravity-matrix-integral-duality|matrix integral]]
whose leading eigenvalue density is $\rho_0(E)\propto\sinh(2\pi\sqrt{2\gamma E})$. The
identification works because [[weil-petersson-volumes|Weil–Petersson volumes]] (via
[[eynard-orantin-topological-recursion|Mirzakhani's recursion]]) obey the same loop equations
as a matrix model with [[jt-spectral-curve|spectral curve]] $y=\tfrac{1}{4\pi}\sin(2\pi z)$.
The matrix integral supplies a (non-unique) nonperturbative completion whose D-brane–type
effects explain the plateau of the [[spectral-form-factor|spectral form factor]] and predict
the large-genus growth of Weil–Petersson volumes.

## Motivation

The SYK model shows random-matrix energy-level statistics, and its low-energy dynamics is the
[[schwarzian-theory|Schwarzian theory]], dual to JT gravity on the disk. Earlier work traced
the linear "ramp" of the [[spectral-form-factor|spectral form factor]]
$\langle Z(\beta+iT)Z(\beta-iT)\rangle$ to a cylinder ("double cone") geometry, suggesting that
JT gravity on nontrivial topologies carries physical information. The paper asks: compute the
JT partition functions $\langle Z(\beta_1)\cdots Z(\beta_n)\rangle$ on surfaces of arbitrary
genus, identify the structure they form, and explain nonperturbative features — the plateau,
the discreteness of the energy spectrum — directly from the bulk.

## Historical context

- **JT gravity**: Jackiw (1985), Teitelboim (1983); revived near-$AdS_2$ by Almheiri–Polchinski
  (2014), connected to the Schwarzian by Maldacena–Stanford–Yang (2016), Engelsöy–Mertens–Verlinde.
- **Matrix models of 2d gravity / noncritical strings**: David, Kazakov, Ambjørn (1985);
  double-scaling by Brézin–Kazakov, Douglas–Shenker, Gross–Migdal (1990); minimal-string/Liouville
  duality by Moore–Seiberg–Staudacher and Seiberg–Shih.
- **Loop equations / topological recursion**: Migdal (1983); streamlined by Eynard (2004) and
  generalized to Eynard–Orantin topological recursion (2007).
- **Weil–Petersson volumes**: Wolpert (symplectic form); Mirzakhani's recursion (2007);
  Eynard–Orantin proved it equals topological recursion with $y=\tfrac{\sin 2\pi z}{4\pi}$.
- **D-brane nonperturbative effects**: Shenker (1990) on $(2g)!$ growth; Polchinski (1994)
  D-branes as $e^{-1/g_s}$; ZZ and FZZT branes (2000–2001).
- **SYK ramp**: Cotler et al. (2016); Saad–Shenker–Stanford (2018) "double cone"; Zograf (2008)
  large-genus volume conjecture.

## Main results

1. **JT genus expansion = matrix-integral genus expansion.** The connected correlators expand as
$$\langle Z(\beta_1)\cdots Z(\beta_n)\rangle_{\text{conn.}}\simeq\sum_{g=0}^\infty\frac{Z_{g,n}(\beta_1,\dots,\beta_n)}{(e^{S_0})^{2g+n-2}},$$
weighted by $(e^{S_0})^\chi$, $\chi=2-2g-n$, matching the $e^{-S_0}$ expansion of a
double-scaled Hermitian ensemble with $Z(\beta)=\mathrm{Tr}\,e^{-\beta H}$, $H$ random.

2. **Genus-$g$ integrand = trumpet × Weil–Petersson volume:**
$$Z_{g,n}(\beta_1,\dots,\beta_n)=\prod_{i=1}^n\int_0^\infty b_i\,db_i\,Z^{\text{tr}}_{\text{Sch}}(\beta_i,b_i)\,V_{g,n}(b_1,\dots,b_n),$$
with the Schwarzian disk and trumpet ($\gamma=\tfrac12$)
$$Z^{\text{disk}}_{\text{Sch}}(\beta)=\frac{\gamma^{3/2}e^{2\pi^2\gamma/\beta}}{(2\pi)^{1/2}\beta^{3/2}},\qquad Z^{\text{tr}}_{\text{Sch}}(\beta,b)=\frac{\gamma^{1/2}e^{-\gamma b^2/(2\beta)}}{(2\pi)^{1/2}\beta^{1/2}}.$$

3. **Spectral curve of JT gravity.** The disk fixes the leading density of states
$\rho_0(E)=\frac{\gamma}{2\pi^2}\sinh(2\pi\sqrt{2\gamma E})$, i.e. the
[[jt-spectral-curve|spectral curve]] $y=\frac{\sin 2\pi z}{4\pi}$ (for $\gamma=\tfrac12$, $z^2=-E$).
Feeding it into Eynard's recursion reproduces every $V_{g,n}$ — the precise sense in which
JT gravity *is* a matrix integral.

4. **Mirzakhani ⇔ topological recursion.** The Laplace transforms
$W_{g,n}(z_1,\dots,z_n)=\prod_i\int_0^\infty b_i\,db_i\,e^{-b_i z_i}V_{g,n}$ satisfy Eynard's
residue-at-origin recursion — Mirzakhani's volume recursion is the matrix-model loop equations.

5. **Cylinder = universal ramp.** The genus-zero double-trumpet gives
$Z_{0,2}(\beta_1,\beta_2)=\frac{\sqrt{\beta_1\beta_2}}{2\pi(\beta_1+\beta_2)}$, which under
$\beta_{1,2}\to\beta\pm it$ becomes the linear ramp $\frac{t}{4\pi\beta}$; its resolvent
$R_{0,2}=\frac{1}{4z_1z_2(z_1+z_2)^2}$ is the universal double-scaled RMT answer.

6. **BF-theory derivation of the measure.** Recasting JT as $SL(2,\mathbb{R})$ BF theory, one
symplectic form reduces to the Weil–Petersson form on bulk moduli and the Schwarzian
(Kirillov–Kostant–Souriau) form on boundary wiggles, fixing their relative normalization; all
$\alpha$-dependence is $\alpha^{-\frac32\chi}$, absorbable into $S_0$.

7. **JT = $p\to\infty$ limit of the $(2,p)$ minimal string.** The
[[minimal-string|minimal-string]] density $\rho_0\propto\sinh[\tfrac p2\,\mathrm{arccosh}(1+E/\kappa)]$
tends to $\sinh(2\pi\sqrt{E_{\rm JT}})$ as $p\to\infty$, so the two matrix models coincide.

8. **Nonperturbative density.** A brane/ghost-brane one-loop computation gives an oscillating
correction (eigenvalue discreteness → sine kernel → the [[spectral-form-factor|plateau]] at
$t\sim e^{S_0}$) plus an exponentially small tail in the classically forbidden region $E<0$.

9. **The completion is non-unique and unstable.** The effective potential
$V_{\rm eff}(E)=2e^{S_0}\int_0^{-E}dx\,y(\sqrt x)$ has a local max at $E=-\tfrac14$ — a
one-eigenvalue instanton (ZZ brane at $z_{\rm ZZ}=\tfrac12$) — then dips increasingly negative,
so the eigenvalue integral needs a complex contour: a
[[nonperturbative-completion-jt|metastable, non-unique completion]].

10. **Large-genus Weil–Petersson asymptotics.** Resurgence from the forbidden-region density
predicts $V_{g,0}\approx\frac{(4\pi^2)^{2g-5/2}}{\sqrt2\,\pi^{3/2}}\Gamma(2g-\tfrac52)$ and a
matching $V_{g,1}(b)$ formula — reproducing Zograf's conjecture and extending it, verified
against data to $g=20$. See [[large-genus-weil-petersson-asymptotics]].

11. **"D-brane logic applied to spacetime."** Nonperturbative effects arise from summing over
disconnected spacetimes glued to a common brane boundary;
$\mathrm{Tr}\frac1{E-H}=\partial_E\frac{\det(E-H)}{\det(E'-H)}\big|_{E'\to E}$ realizes the
resolvent as a brane–ghost-brane dipole that keeps nonperturbative pieces even as the branes
formally cancel.

## Methods

Matrix-model [[eynard-orantin-topological-recursion|loop equations]] and the
[[double-scaling-limit|double-scaling limit]] set the target structure. The JT path integral is
done in two steps: integrate out the dilaton (enforcing $R=-2$), then cut each surface along a
length-$b$ geodesic into a [[trumpet-geometry|trumpet]] glued to a bordered hyperbolic surface;
the [[schwarzian-theory|Schwarzian]] wiggle integral is one-loop exact and the moduli integral
gives [[weil-petersson-volumes|$V_{g,n}$]]. The measure normalization is fixed via the
$SL(2,\mathbb{R})$ BF (first-order) formulation and cross-checked in conformal gauge. Airy /
orthogonal-polynomial and Borel–resurgence techniques handle the nonperturbative sector.

## Conventions

- Euclidean signature; hyperbolic 2d metric with $R=-2$.
- JT action $I=-\frac{S_0}{2\pi}[\tfrac12\int\sqrt g R+\int\sqrt h K]-[\tfrac12\int\sqrt g\,\phi(R+2)+\int\sqrt h\,\phi(K-1)]$;
  the first bracket $=S_0\,\chi$, so topology is weighted by $(e^{S_0})^\chi$ and the string
  coupling is $g_s=e^{-S_0}$.
- Boundary conditions $g_{uu}|_{\rm bdy}=1/\epsilon^2$, $\phi|_{\rm bdy}=\gamma/\epsilon$,
  $\epsilon\to0$, boundary length $\beta/\epsilon$; usually $\gamma=\tfrac12$, ground state at $E=0$.
- Matrix integral $\mathcal Z=\int dH\,e^{-L\,\mathrm{Tr}V(H)}$ over $L\times L$ Hermitian $H$
  (orientable surfaces only; other ensembles are the subject of Stanford–Witten). Resolvent
  $R(E)=\mathrm{Tr}\frac1{E-H}$; density $\rho_0$ unit-normalized, $\rho_0^{\rm total}=e^{S_0}\rho_0$.
- Spectral-curve coordinate $z^2=-E$ (real $z$ = forbidden region $E<0$).

## Key equations

- $\rho_0(E)=\frac{\gamma}{2\pi^2}\sinh(2\pi\sqrt{2\gamma E})$ — genus-zero JT density of states.
- $y(z)=\frac{\sin 2\pi z}{4\pi}$ ($\gamma=\tfrac12$) — the JT spectral curve; the single input.
- $Z_{g,n}=\prod_i\int_0^\infty b_i\,db_i\,Z^{\rm tr}_{\rm Sch}(\beta_i,b_i)\,V_{g,n}$ — trumpet-glued volume integral.
- $W_{g,n}=\mathop{\mathrm{Res}}_{z\to0}\frac{1}{z_1^2-z^2}\frac1{4y(z)}\big[W_{g-1,n+1}(z,-z,J)+{\textstyle\sum'}W_{h,\cdot}(z,I)W_{h',\cdot}(-z,I')\big]$ — Eynard recursion.
- $Z_{0,2}=\frac{\sqrt{\beta_1\beta_2}}{2\pi(\beta_1+\beta_2)}$, $R_{0,2}=\frac{1}{4z_1z_2(z_1+z_2)^2}$ — double-trumpet = RMT ramp.
- $\mathrm{Tr}\frac1{E-H}=\partial_E\frac{\det(E-H)}{\det(E'-H)}\big|_{E'\to E}$ — resolvent as brane–ghost-brane dipole.
- $V_{\rm eff}(E)=2e^{S_0}\int_0^{-E}dx\,y(\sqrt x)$, extremum at $E=-\tfrac14$ — one-eigenvalue instanton / ZZ brane.

## Open questions raised

- Can the matrix / eigenvalue description be recovered from bulk JT gravity itself (what are the
  "constituents")? See [[nonperturbative-completion-jt]].
- For a fixed (non-averaged) boundary theory, what forbids connected wormhole geometries, and how
  is [[factorization-vs-wormholes|factorization]] restored?
- Are there higher-dimensional quantum gravities dual to *ensemble averages* of boundary theories?
- Can the plateau be obtained without summing over disconnected geometries (e.g. a two-replica
  $G,\Sigma$ SYK path integral)?
