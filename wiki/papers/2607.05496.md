## TL;DR

A systematic, purely effective-field-theory framework for building six-dimensional
$\mathcal N=(1,0)$ supergravity theories that respect every known consistency
constraint — anomaly cancellation and the non-perturbative $H$-string constraints of
[[tensor-charge-lattice|Kim-Vafa-Xu]]. The building objects are **supergravity blocks**:
minimal collections of tensor multiplets made of a single little string theory sharing the
distinguished $H$-string charge $f$ plus "external" BPS generators whose charges intersect
$f$ positively. Each block's Gram matrix has exactly one positive eigenvalue, so it
necessarily contains intrinsically **gravitational BPS strings** that can never all become
tensionless anywhere in tensor moduli space. As a first concrete step the authors
completely classify the **non-Higgsable gravity blocks** (blocks supporting only
non-Higgsable gauge algebras): 12,762 admissible $P$-type LST sectors and 9,630,606 blocks,
with tensor-base lists complete to $T=9$ that agree with F-theory geometric classifications
for $T\le 6$.

## Motivation

In six dimensions $\mathcal N=(1,0)$ supergravity is one of the most sharply constrained
arenas for the [[swampland-program|Swampland program]]: supersymmetry, unitarity and
anomaly cancellation are so restrictive that the set of consistent theories is nearly
pinned down — but perturbative consistency alone does not fully characterize which theories
UV-complete. The extra data come from the **tensionless BPS strings** that appear at
boundaries of tensor moduli space (6d SCFTs, little string theories, critical strings).
Recent work promoted this to an organizing principle — every moduli-space boundary hosts a
tensionless BPS string — and proved finiteness of the massless spectrum, but a systematic
*constructive* framework turning these constraints into landscape building blocks was
missing. This paper supplies one, deliberately phrased in pure EFT terms so it covers both
geometric ([[f-theory|F-theory]]) and intrinsically non-geometric theories.

## Historical context

- **6d gravitational-anomaly / finiteness relations** $H-V=273-29T$ and the broad
  landscape program (Vafa 2005; Acharya-Douglas 2006; Hamada-Vafa 2021).
- **Green-Schwarz-Sagnotti anomaly factorization** in 6d (Green-Schwarz 1984; Sagnotti
  1992; Erler 1993) — the [[green-schwarz-sagnotti-mechanism]] that fixes the anomaly
  vectors $b_0, b_i$ and the tensor pairing.
- **6d SCFT and LST classification via F-theory** (Heckman-Morrison-Vafa 2013/2015;
  Bhardwaj et al. 2015-2019), including endpoint classification and frozen singularities.
- **Non-Higgsable clusters** (Morrison-Taylor 2012): the complete list of minimal
  un-Higgsable gauge algebras on curves — the [[non-higgsable-cluster]] catalogue this
  paper builds blocks from.
- **Tensor-charge lattice** as a unimodular Lorentzian lattice of signature $(1,T)$
  (Seiberg-Taylor 2011; Monnier-Moore 2018) — the [[tensor-charge-lattice]].
- **Supergravity strings and central-charge bounds** (Kim-Tarazi-Vafa 2019; Lee-Weigand)
  and the **Emergent String Conjecture** (Lee-Lerche-Weigand 2019).
- **Load-bearing precursor** Kim-Vafa-Xu 2024 (`Kim:2024eoa`): refined tensor-intersection
  structure, existence of the $H$-string, the assumption that every moduli-space boundary
  hosts a tensionless BPS string, blowdown structure matching Kähler surfaces, and the
  finiteness proof with bounds $T\le 193$, $r(V)\le 480$; the improved central-charge bound
  $16$ for $T>1$ is from Baykara et al. 2025.

## Main results

1. **Supergravity-block framework.** Any consistent 6d $(1,0)$ supergravity with $T\ge 1$
   (except the $T=9$, $b_0=0$ case) is built by: (Step 1) constructing LSTs that share an
   $H$-string charge $f$; (Step 2) attaching external BPS generators (those with
   $\mathcal C_{\rm ext}\cdot f>0$) and their matter; (Step 3) placing gauge algebras and
   hypermultiplets on tensors of non-negative self-intersection. A [[supergravity-block]]
   bundles one LST sector's generators $\{\mathcal C_i^a\}$ (with $\mathcal C_i^a\cdot f=0$)
   with all external generators $\{\mathcal C_l^{\rm ext}\}$; generators of distinct LST
   sectors do not intersect, and all blocks in a theory share the same $b_0$ and the same
   external generators.

2. **One positive eigenvalue $\Rightarrow$ intrinsic gravity.** The Gram matrix
   $\mathcal G^a_{\alpha\beta}$ of each block has **exactly one positive eigenvalue**. Hence
   its BPS strings cannot all become simultaneously tensionless anywhere in moduli space
   (including at infinite distance): each block contains intrinsically gravitational tensor
   charges whose multiples give BPS **black strings**. This is what distinguishes a block
   from an arbitrary tensor subset or a local SCFT/LST sector (whose Gram matrix is negative
   (semi-)definite).

3. **Existence and role of the $H$-string.** Every 6d $(1,0)$ supergravity with $T\ge 1$
   (except $T=9,\,b_0=0$) contains a distinguished BPS string of charge $f$ with $f^2=0$,
   $b_0\cdot f=2$. For $T>1$ it is not itself a generator but is shared by $P$-type-endpoint
   LSTs, $f=\sum_i n_i\mathcal C_i$ with $n_i>0$ and $\mathcal C_i\cdot f=0$. Generators
   orthogonal to $f$ must belong to an LST carrying $f$ (a containment relation). See
   [[h-string]].

4. **Tensor intersection = Kähler-surface topology.** The intersection structure of tensor
   multiplets in any 6d supergravity matches that of $\mathbb P^2$ ($T=0$), $\mathbb F_n$
   ($T=1$), or their blowups ($T>1$); any pairing blows down to $\mathbb F_n$ or
   $\mathbb P^2$ (except $b_0=0$). Used as an EFT constraint without assuming a geometric
   realization.

5. **Classification of non-Higgsable gravity blocks.** Complete classification of blocks
   built solely from NHCs: **12,762** admissible $P$-type LST bases carrying the $H$-string;
   $N_{\rm tot}=\mathbf{9{,}630{,}606}$ non-Higgsable gravity blocks; 34,747 distinct
   external gauge-algebra patterns (1,293 distinct external gauge-algebra combinations). The
   dominant external generator is $\mathfrak{su}_2$ on a $-2$ curve (95.33% of blocks), most
   common combination $\mathfrak{su}_2^{\oplus 3}$. Complete tensor-base lists up to $T=9$;
   new features appear at $T=10$; agreement with geometric F-theory bases for $T\le 6$. See
   [[classification-non-higgsable-gravity-blocks]].

6. **$T=9$, $b_0=0$ exceptional case.** No $H$-string exists; the lattice is
   $\Gamma^{1,9}\simeq U\oplus E_8(-1)$ (even, unimodular, signature $(1,9)$ — the
   Enriques-surface divisor lattice). Only two primitive generator types survive:
   $\mathcal C^2=-2,\ b_0\cdot\mathcal C=0$ ($-2$ tensors / Weyl walls) and
   $\mathcal F^2=0,\ b_0\cdot\mathcal F=0$ (Type II critical string / genus-one fiber).
   Tensor bases are classified by chambers of the positive cone under the lattice
   automorphism (duality) group.

7. **Frozen-singularity supergravities.** The framework produces candidate non-geometric
   theories containing frozen NHCs
   $\{\overset{\mathfrak{su}_8}{\hat 1},\ \overset{\mathfrak{su}_8}{2}\,\overset{\mathfrak{su}_{16}}{\hat 1},\ \overset{\mathfrak{su}_8}{2}\!=\!\overset{\mathfrak{so}_{16}}{4}\}$.
   The middle cluster ($\mathfrak{su}_{16}$ on $\hat 1$) cannot be embedded in any
   supergravity. Explicit non-F-theory examples appear at $T=10$ (external $\hat 1$ with
   $\mathfrak{su}_8$ + symmetric hyper) and $T=11$ (with $2\!=\!4$); whether they admit a
   genuine UV completion is left open ([[uv-completion-frozen-nhc-supergravities]]).

## Methods

- **String/tensor charge lattice and Dirac pairing.** Charges live in a Lorentzian
  unimodular lattice $\Gamma$ of signature $(1,T)$ with pairing
  $v\cdot w=\Omega_{\alpha\beta}v^\alpha w^\beta$. Tensor moduli space is
  $SO(1,T)/SO(T)$ with $J\cdot J=1$, $J^0>0$; BPS string tension $\propto J\cdot Q\ge 0$
  defines the tensor cone, dual to the BPS cone generated by primitive charges
  $\mathcal C_i$ ($\mathcal C_i^2\le 0$).
- **Green-Schwarz-Sagnotti factorization.** $I_8=\tfrac12\Omega_{\alpha\beta}X_4^\alpha X_4^\beta$
  constrains $b_0,b_i$ and matter; the anomaly sublattice $\mathfrak L=\mathrm{span}(b_0,b_i)$
  has integral Gram matrix $\mathcal G_{IJ}=b_I\cdot b_J$ with $n_+^{\mathfrak L}\le 1$,
  $n_-^{\mathfrak L}\le T$ — the block "one positive eigenvalue" criterion is a special use.
- **Blowdown projection.** The iterated map $Q\to Q'=Q+(\mathcal C\cdot Q)\mathcal C$
  orthogonal to a $-1$ generator ($\mathcal C^2=-1$, $b_0\cdot\mathcal C=1$) reduces any base
  to $\mathbb F_n/\mathbb P^2$; used both to prove $H$-string existence and as a consistency
  test on candidate bases.
- **Worldsheet current-algebra bounds.** The $H$-string has $c_L=20$, $c_R=6$; gauge
  algebras $\mathfrak g_i$ with $f\cdot\mathcal C_i=k_i>0$ contribute
  $c_{\mathfrak g}=k\,\dim\mathfrak g/(k+h^\vee)$, bounded by
  $\sum_i c_{\mathfrak g_i}\le 20$ ($16$ for $T>1$). The E-string on a $-1$ generator gives
  the local bound $\sum_i c_{\mathfrak g_i}\le 8$.
- **Gluing rules and NHC attachment.** External generators attach to LST tensors via
  explicit gluing rules $p\overset{n}{-}[q]$, constrained by flavor-symmetry embedding,
  mixed-anomaly cancellation and the central-charge bounds. Enumerated attachment types:
  single-target, multi-target, mixed (core + $-1$ legs), and whole-NHC-cluster.
- **Equivalence relations / dualities.** Weyl reflections
  $s_\alpha(C)=C+(C\cdot\alpha)\alpha$ on gauge-less $-2$ tensors (M-strings) identify tensor
  bases; infinite duality (Cremona/Weyl) groups for many-blowup bases (e.g. $\mathrm{dP}_9$)
  bound otherwise-unbounded intersection numbers after quotienting.
- **Dummy LSTs and tensor counting.** $T=\sum_a T^H_a+1$; $A$-type and $D$-type dummy LSTs
  (only $-1,-2$ tensors, no gauge/matter) interpolate $T_{\rm min}\le T\le T_{\rm max}$.
- **Numerical scan.** A C++/OpenMP pipeline enumerated attachments over the $P$-type LST
  catalogue subject to anomaly, central-charge, Gram-signature and blowdown tests
  (~13,200 CPU-hours); data on Zenodo with a Python SUGRA toolkit.

## Conventions

- **Lattice signature:** tensor intersection form $\Omega_{\alpha\beta}$ is Lorentzian,
  signature $(+,(-)^T)$; charge lattice $\Gamma$ unimodular of signature $(1,T)$; all inner
  products $v\cdot w=\Omega_{\alpha\beta}v^\alpha w^\beta$.
- **Anomaly factorization:** $I_8=\tfrac12\Omega_{\alpha\beta}X_4^\alpha X_4^\beta$ with
  $X_4^\alpha=-\tfrac12 b_0^\alpha\,\mathrm{tr}R^2+\tfrac12\sum_i\frac{b_i^\alpha}{\lambda_i}\mathrm{tr}F_i^2$;
  unsubscripted $\mathrm{tr}$ in the fundamental/defining representation.
- **Normalization constants $\lambda_i$:** $SU(N){=}1$, $SO(N){=}2$, $Sp(N){=}1$,
  $G_2{=}2$, $F_4{=}6$, $E_6{=}6$, $E_7{=}12$, $E_8{=}60$.
- **Gravitational anomaly:** $H-V=273-29T$, $b_0\cdot b_0=9-T$.
- **$H$-string charge (defining):** $f^2=0$, $b_0\cdot f=2$.
- **Signature (block) constraint:** $n_+^{\mathfrak L}\le 1$, $n_-^{\mathfrak L}\le T$.
- **Curve notation:** $\overset{\mathfrak g}{n}$ is a tensor of self-intersection $-n$
  carrying algebra $\mathfrak g$; $p\!=\!q$ denotes mutual intersection number $2$; $\hat 1$
  is the generator with $\mathcal C^2=-1$, $b_0\cdot\mathcal C=-1$.
- **Supercharges:** $\mathcal N=(1,0)$ (eight supercharges); no Abelian gauge factors in the
  main analysis (Abelian sectors deferred).

## Key equations

- Anomaly factorization:
  $I_8=\tfrac12\Omega_{\alpha\beta}X_4^\alpha X_4^\beta$,
  $X_4^\alpha=-\tfrac12 b_0^\alpha\,\mathrm{tr}R^2+\tfrac12\sum_i\frac{b_i^\alpha}{\lambda_i}\mathrm{tr}F_i^2$.
- GS conditions: $H-V=273-29T$, $\ b_0\cdot b_0=9-T$,
  $\ b_0\cdot b_i=\tfrac{\lambda_i}{6}\big(\sum_{\mathbf r}n^i_{\mathbf r}A^i_{\mathbf r}-A^i_{\rm adj}\big)$,
  $\ b_i\cdot b_i=\tfrac{\lambda_i^2}{3}\big(\sum_{\mathbf r}n^i_{\mathbf r}C^i_{\mathbf r}-C^i_{\rm adj}\big)$.
- Eigenvalue constraint: $n_+^{\mathfrak L}\le 1$, $n_-^{\mathfrak L}\le T$.
- BPS tension positivity: $J\cdot Q_i\ge 0$ with $J\cdot J=1$; boundaries at
  $J_{\partial\mathcal M}\cdot Q=0$ (tensionless string).
- $H$-string charge: $f^2=0$, $b_0\cdot f=2$; decomposition $f=\sum_i n_i\mathcal C_i$,
  $\mathcal C_i\cdot f=0$.
- Blowdown map: $Q'=Q+(\mathcal C\cdot Q)\mathcal C$, $\mathcal C^2=-1$, $b_0\cdot\mathcal C=1$.
- Hirzebruch/$\mathbb P^2$ data:
  $\Omega=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&-n\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, $b_0=(2-n,2)$ for
  $\mathbb F_n$; $\Omega=1$, $b_0=3$ for $\mathbb P^2$.
- Current-algebra central charge: $c_{\mathfrak g}=\dfrac{k\,\dim\mathfrak g}{k+h^\vee}$,
  with $\sum_i c_{\mathfrak g_i}\le 20$ ($16$ for $T>1$) and $\le 8$ (E-string).
- Finiteness bounds (Kim-Vafa-Xu): $T\le 193$, $r(V)\le 480$; tensor counting
  $T=\sum_a T^H_a+1$.
- $T=9$, $b_0=0$ lattice: $\Gamma^{1,9}\simeq U\oplus E_8(-1)$.

## Open questions raised

- Extend beyond the non-Higgsable subclass: promote the block classification to a full
  classification of massless spectra with gauge enhancements, extra charged matter, and
  Abelian sectors on positive-self-intersection tensors.
- Whether the non-Higgsable tensor bases already give the complete list of tensor bases for
  general 6d supergravity with $T\le 9$ (conjectured; checked geometrically to $T\le 6$).
- Do the frozen-NHC / non-geometric candidate supergravities admit genuine quantum-gravity
  UV completions? ([[uv-completion-frozen-nhc-supergravities]])
- Replace the practical cutoffs on intersection numbers with sharp physically motivated
  bounds, likely via the Weyl/duality equivalence relations.
- Full enumeration for $T\ge 10$ and the general (non-NHC) classification.
