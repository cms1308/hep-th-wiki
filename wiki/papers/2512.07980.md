## TL;DR

The follow-up to [[2503.22789|Entropic Order]] strips [[entropic-order|entropic order]] down to its simplest realization: the **[[arithmetic-ising-model|Arithmetic Ising Model (AIM)]]** — Ising sites replaced by non-negative integers $n_x$ with only a nearest-neighbor repulsion, $H = \mu\sum_x n_x + U\sum_{\langle x,y\rangle} n_x n_y$. Via mean-field theory, a controlled large-flavor ($1/k$) expansion, and Monte Carlo, the AIM forms a checkerboard "solid" at *high* temperature whenever $U/\mu > 1/2$, a threshold that appears to become exact as $T\to\infty$. Quantum (bosonic-hopping) and classical continuum-gas analogues show the same high-$T$ order, so the effect is robust to quantum kinetics and does not need the earlier paper's contrived quartic interaction.

## Motivation

The predecessor paper realized high-$T$ order with an unnatural interaction $U\sum n_x^2 n_y^2$. This paper looks for *minimal, physically realizable* models — simple repulsive quadratic couplings of the kind appearing in Bose–Hubbard and Rydberg-atom systems — that still exhibit entropic order, and asks whether the effect survives when genuine quantum dynamics (hopping) is added.

## Historical context

- The direct predecessor and namesake: [[2503.22789|Entropic Order (Han et al. 2025)]], which introduced non-negative-integer spins and the checkerboard entropic solid.
- Rigorous no-go theorems forbidding high-$T$ order under finiteness/product-structure assumptions (Dobrushin 1968; Simon).
- High-$T$ symmetry breaking in QFT, where the Hilbert space is neither finite nor factorizable — the Chai–Komargodski program and its descendants; see [[high-temperature-order-in-qft]].
- Intermediate-$T$ entropic-ordering precedents: the [[pomeranchuk-effect|Pomeranchuk effect]] in $^3$He, order-by-disorder magnets (Villain), colloidal crystals.
- Baxter's exactly solvable hardcore square-lattice gas (1980, 1982), onto which the $U\to\infty$ AIM maps ($T_c \approx 4.27\,\mu$); hard-sphere freezing (Alder–Wainwright); cluster-crystal / penetrable-sphere phases; 2d $\phi^4$ critical-mass results (Rychkov–Vitale; Pelissetto–Vicari) used for the large-$k$ analysis.

## Main results

1. **Arithmetic Ising Model.** $H = \mu\sum_x n_x + U\sum_{\langle x,y\rangle} n_x n_y$, $n_x \in \{0,1,2,\dots\}$, $U,\mu \ge 0$, has a unique empty ground state (low-$T$ gas) but forms a checkerboard solid — broken sublattice translation, $\langle n_x\rangle \sim T$ on one sublattice, $\sim 0$ on the other — at high $T$.
2. **Mean-field threshold.** MFT predicts the high-$T$ solid whenever $U/\mu > 1/2$; this threshold appears to become *exact* as $T\to\infty$.
3. **Large-flavor ($1/k$) control.** With $k$ species per site ($n_x = \sum_\alpha n_{x,\alpha}$, $U \to U/k$), $k$ is a loop parameter making MFT exact at $k\to\infty$; the corrected line is $\tfrac{U}{\mu} > \tfrac12 + \tfrac{\sqrt{\beta\mu}}{2\sqrt2\,\pi k}\log\tfrac{1}{\beta\mu} + \cdots$, so $U = \mu/2$ survives to first order in $1/k$ as $T\to\infty$.
4. **Ising universality.** The gas/solid transition is second order and in the 2d Ising class; Monte Carlo susceptibility collapses with $\nu = 1$, $\gamma = 7/4$, and $U_c \approx 0.63$ at $k=1$.
5. **Emergent continuous symmetry at $T\to\infty$.** In the large-$k$ continuum description an exact staggered symmetry $\phi_x \to (-1)^{s(-1)^{x_1+x_2}}(\phi_x - \tfrac{\sqrt k}{2}) + \tfrac{\sqrt k}{2}$ emerges as $\beta\to0$, forbidding a mass term and protecting $U_c = \mu/2$. This gives a parametrically light $(\pi,\pi)$ mode and an anomalously *large* correlation length in the high-$T$ gas — the opposite of ordinary thermal screening.
6. **Quantum AIM.** Adding hopping $-t\sum_{\langle x,y\rangle}(b_x^\dagger b_y + \text{h.c.})$ does not destroy the solid: quantum fluctuations $\sim \sqrt T$ are subleading to $H_0 \sim T$. A large-$k$ Hubbard–Stratonovich treatment confirms the solid for $U > \mu/2$.
7. **Perturbations that melt it.** Capping occupation at $N$ restores disorder at very high $T$ (the solid lives only in $\mu \lesssim T \lesssim NU$); an on-site $K\sum n_x^2$ melts it at $T_c \sim U^2/K$ (arbitrarily high for small $K$).
8. **Continuum realizations.** A classical gas of extended "polymer" objects with $V_{ij} = \rho_i^2\rho_j^2\, v(|\bm x_i - \bm x_j|)$ becomes an effective hard-sphere solid at high $T$ (overlap weight $w_2/w_1^2 \to 0$); a grand-canonical gas with any repulsion having negative Fourier modes freezes into a cluster solid as $T$ rises.

## Methods

- **Two-sublattice mean-field theory:** energy density $e = 2U n_A n_B + \tfrac{\mu}{2}(n_A + n_B)$ with combinatorial entropy $e^S \approx e^{(n+1)\log(n+1) - n\log n}$; minimize $f = e - Ts$.
- **[[large-flavor-expansion-entropic-order|Large-flavor ($1/k$) expansion]]:** the binomial degeneracy $\binom{n_x + k - 1}{k-1}$ plus $n_x = k\rho_x$ turns $Z$ into $\int \prod d\rho\, e^{-k(\cdots)}$, so $k$ is a semiclassical parameter; MFT is exact at $k\to\infty$.
- **Perturbative 2d $\phi^4$ theory** around the gas saddle; the two-loop self-energy shows corrections to $U_c$ vanish as $\beta\to0$, using $m^2_{\rm crit} = \sqrt C\,\lambda$ with $C\approx 0.0025$.
- **Hubbard–Stratonovich + large-$k$ saddle (quantum):** decouple $n_x n_y$ with $\sigma_x = \sigma + (-1)^i\eta$; boson bands $E_k^\pm = \mu + \sigma \pm \sqrt{\eta^2 + \varepsilon_k^2}$ give gap equations with a solid ($\eta\ne0$) for $U > \mu/2$.
- **Density-functional / field-theory transform** of the classical grand-canonical gas; crystallization signaled where $\tilde U(k) < 0$.
- **Monte Carlo** with large occupation moves and finite-size-scaling collapse; **hardcore-gas mapping** for the $U\to\infty$ limit.

## Conventions

- Statistical-mechanics / lattice paper (no relativistic metric). Spins $n_x \in \{0,1,2,\dots\}$; nearest-neighbor pairs $\langle x,y\rangle$; energy constants $U,\mu \ge 0$; $\beta = 1/T$, $k_B = 1$; dimensionful quantities quoted in units of $\mu$.
- Gibbs ensemble $\mathbb{P}_\beta = Z^{-1} e^{-\beta H}$. Colored generalization: $k$ species with $U \to U/k$, $k=1$ recovering the AIM.
- Order parameter $O = \tfrac{1}{2L^2}(\sum_{x\in A} n_x - \sum_{x\in B} n_x)$, susceptibility $\chi = L^2(\langle O^2\rangle - \langle O\rangle^2)$.

## Key equations

- $H(n_x) = \mu\sum_x n_x + U\sum_{\langle x,y\rangle} n_x n_y$, $\ n_x \in \{0,1,\dots\}$ — the Arithmetic Ising Model.
- $U/\mu > 1/2$ — mean-field (apparently exact as $T\to\infty$) condition for the high-$T$ solid.
- $Z_k = \sum_{\{n_x\}} e^{-\beta H_1(n_x)} \prod_x \binom{n_x + k - 1}{k-1}$ — colored partition function; saddle $\bar\rho = \sqrt{T/4U}$.
- $\tfrac{U}{\mu} > \tfrac12 + \tfrac{\sqrt{\beta\mu}}{2\sqrt2\,\pi k}\log\tfrac{1}{\beta\mu} + \cdots$ — $1/k$-corrected transition line.
- $m_0^2 = \tfrac{1}{\beta U\rho(1+\rho)} - 4 = 2(\mu - 2U)\sqrt{\beta/U} + \cdots$ — critical-mode mass; vanishes at $U = \mu/2$ as $\beta\to0$.
- $E_k^\pm = \mu + \sigma \pm \sqrt{\eta^2 + \varepsilon_k^2}$, $\ \varepsilon_k = -2t(\cos k_x + \cos k_y)$ — quantum boson bands; $\eta\ne0$ (solid) for $U > \mu/2$.

## Open questions raised

- Whether the exact threshold $U_c = \mu/2$ at $T\to\infty$ survives at finite $k$ (in particular $k=1$) or receives non-perturbative corrections; the emergent continuous symmetry relies on continuous $\phi_x$, which fails at finite $k$ — see [[finite-k-corrections-entropic-transition]].
- The physical origin and consequences of the emergent $T\to\infty$ continuous symmetry and the light $(\pi,\pi)$ mode / large high-$T$ correlation length ("deferred to future work").
- Whether the transition's universality class depends on the microscopic lattice.
- Experimental realization in Rydberg-atom arrays ($n_x$ = principal quantum number).
