## TL;DR

In a [[ttbar-deformation|$T\bar T$-deformed CFT]] the conformal symmetry survives but becomes
nonlocal, and it does not act tractably on the natural local operators. Working in the
[[hamiltonian-flow-deformed-cft|Hamiltonian formalism]] on the plane, this paper constructs
in closed form the [[dressed-operator-deformed-cft|dressed operators]] (nonlocal primaries of
the flowed Virasoro algebra) and introduces a new class of
[[physical-operator-deformed-cft|physical operators]] that are local yet expressible as dressed
operators smeared against a field-dependent delta function. Computing the physical two-point
function in momentum space and resumming the most UV-sensitive terms reproduces both the
worldsheet [[tst-holography|TsT/$T\bar T$]] result and the field-theory large-momentum result,
giving [[deformed-cft-two-point-function|a two-point function of shifted weight]]
$h_\lambda=h+\tfrac{\lambda}{\pi}p\bar p$.

## Motivation

$T\bar T$-deformed CFTs retain conformal symmetry, but the Virasoro generators become nonlocal
and act intractably on the "undeformed" local operators, so correlators are hard to compute and
hard to compare across methods. Two nonperturbative two-point functions in the literature — a
worldsheet computation via TsT/$T\bar T$ and a field-theory large-momentum computation — agree
numerically and both satisfy Cardy's Callan–Symanzik equation, yet it was unclear whether the
operators used in the two calculations are the same object. The goal is to use the nonlocal
conformal symmetry itself to *define* operators, so correlators can be computed in a
symmetry-guided way and the competing operator definitions reconciled.

## Historical context

Builds on the $T\bar T$ deformation (Zamolodchikov 2004) and its solvable spectrum
([[ttbar-deformed-spectrum]]; Smirnov–Zamolodchikov and Cavaglià et al. 2016). The nonlocal
conformal symmetry was realized in the Lagrangian (Guica–Monten 2020), Hamiltonian
(Kruthoff–Parrikar 2020; Jorjadze–Theisen 2020; Guica 2021), gravity, and worldsheet pictures.
The two-point-function targets it reproduces are the worldsheet TsT/$T\bar T$ result
(Cui–Du–Song 2023) and the topological-gravity / large-momentum result (Aharony–Barel 2023),
both consistent with Cardy's Callan–Symanzik equation (Cardy 2019). The dressed operators are
the flowed operators of Kruthoff–Parrikar and Guica.

## Main results

1. **Dressed stress tensor and nonlocal Virasoro.** Solving the covariant-constancy flow
   equation for the stress tensor gives closed-form dressed components
   $$\tilde{\mathcal H}_L=\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{(-1)^n}{n!}\,\partial_y^n\!\big(\mathcal H_L\,(\delta\hat u)^n\big),\qquad
   \delta\hat u=2\lambda\,\bar\eta_0,$$
   and a barred copy, whose modes $\tilde L_m=i^{m+1}\!\int dy\,\hat u^{\,m+1}\mathcal H_L$ close
   into two commuting classical Witt/Virasoro algebras $i\{\tilde L_n,\tilde L_m\}=(n-m)\tilde L_{n+m}$.
2. **Nonlocal coordinates.** The generators define field-dependent coordinates
   $\hat u=u+2\lambda\bar\eta_0$, $\hat v=v+2\lambda\eta_0$, with
   $\tilde{\mathcal H}_L(\hat u)=\mathcal H_L/(1+2\lambda\mathcal H_R)$ — recovering the
   Lagrangian/holographic relation from the Hamiltonian side.
3. **Dressed primaries (closed form).** A primary of weight $(h,\bar h)$ dresses to
   $\tilde{\mathcal O}_{h,\bar h}(\hat u,\hat v)=\mathcal O_{h,\bar h}(u,v)/(1+\lambda^2\mathcal O_{T\bar T})$,
   a genuine $(h,\bar h)$ primary under $\tilde L_m$.
4. **Physical operators (new local class).** Defined by
   $$\mathcal O_{h,\bar h}\equiv\frac{1+\lambda^2\mathcal O_{T\bar T}}{(1+2\lambda\mathcal H_R)^h\,(1+2\lambda\mathcal H_L)^{\bar h}}\,\mathcal O^{(0)}_{h,\bar h},$$
   fixed uniquely by demanding the flow/Ward-identity remainder terms vanish. They are local but
   re-express as dressed operators smeared over a field-dependent delta function,
   $\mathcal O_{h,\bar h}(u,v)=\int d^2\sigma\,\delta\big(U-u,\,V-v\big)\,\tilde{\mathcal O}_{h,\bar h}(\sigma^+,\sigma^-)$.
5. **Equivalence with the Aharony–Barel operator.** Classically the physical operator equals the
   local operator of the topological-gravity formulation; checked explicitly for the deformed
   free scalar with $\mathcal O^{\rm CFT}_{1,0}=\partial_u\phi$.
6. **Nonperturbative two-point function.** Resumming the leading-log terms of the momentum-space
   correlator gives
   $$\langle\mathcal O_{h,\bar h}(p,\bar p)\,\mathcal O_{h,\bar h}(-p,-\bar p)\rangle\sim
   \frac{\pi\,\Gamma(1-2h_\lambda)}{\Gamma(2h_\lambda)}\Big(\tfrac{|p|}{2}\Big)^{4h_\lambda-2},\qquad
   h_\lambda=h+\frac{\lambda}{\pi}p\bar p,$$
   matching the TsT/$T\bar T$ and large-momentum results and Cardy's Callan–Symanzik equation.
7. **Total-derivative flow equation.** The physical two-point function obeys a flow equation with
   a *total* derivative, distinguishing physical operators from the undeformed operators of
   Kruthoff–Parrikar and Cardy.

## Methods

The deformation is treated as a canonical (Bogoliubov) transformation on the plane
([[hamiltonian-flow-deformed-cft]]): $\partial_\lambda H=i[\mathcal X,H]$, so dressed operators
are covariant constants $\mathcal D_\lambda\tilde{\mathcal O}=(\partial_\lambda-i[\mathcal X,\cdot])\tilde{\mathcal O}=0$,
solved by recursion. All algebra is classical (Poisson brackets, no classical central term). A
Noether construction gives the deformed stress tensor; a field-dependent coordinate change
$U,V$ smears dressed into physical operators. Quantum correlators of dressed operators are
computed order by order with CFT Ward identities and point-splitting, Fourier-transformed, and
the leading logs resummed.

## Conventions

Lorentzian 2d, lightcone $u=y+t$, $v=y-t$; $\mathcal H_L\equiv T_{ut}=(\mathcal H+\mathcal P)/2$,
$\mathcal H_R\equiv-T_{vt}=(\mathcal H-\mathcal P)/2$. Deformation parameter $\lambda$ with
$\partial_\lambda\mathcal H=-\mathcal O_{T\bar T}$, where
$\mathcal O_{T\bar T}=4\mathcal H_L\mathcal H_R/(1+2\lambda\mathcal H)$ — equal to $T\bar T$ only
infinitesimally near the CFT point. Wick rotation $u\to-ix$, $v\to i\bar x$, $p_L\to ip$,
$p_R\to i\bar p$, with $\tilde{\mathcal H}_L\to T/2\pi$, $\tilde{\mathcal H}_R\to\bar T/2\pi$ (the
$2\pi$ enters only at this identification). The construction is classical throughout; the
quantum central charge $c$ appears only in the Ward-identity correlator computations. This wiki
has no global $T\bar T$ convention yet — the page follows the source.

## Key equations

- $\mathcal D_\lambda\tilde{\mathcal O}=0$, $\ \mathcal D_\lambda\equiv\partial_\lambda-i[\mathcal X,\cdot]$ — flow as parallel transport.
- $\mathcal H=\tfrac{1}{2\lambda}\big(\sqrt{1+4\lambda\mathcal H^{(0)}+4\lambda^2\mathcal P^2}-1\big)$ — deformed energy density.
- $\mathcal H^{(0)}_L=(1+2\lambda\mathcal H_R)\,\mathcal H_L$ — deformed ↔ undeformed stress tensor.
- $\mathcal O_{h,\bar h}=\dfrac{1+\lambda^2\mathcal O_{T\bar T}}{(1+2\lambda\mathcal H_R)^h(1+2\lambda\mathcal H_L)^{\bar h}}\,\mathcal O^{(0)}_{h,\bar h}$ — physical operator.
- $h_\lambda=h+\tfrac{\lambda}{\pi}p\bar p$ — momentum-dependent effective weight of the two-point function.

## Open questions raised

- **Quantum uplift.** The construction is classical; a fully quantum definition of the physical
  operators (normal ordering, a proof that dressed correlators are exactly the undeformed CFT
  ones) is left open. See [[quantum-operators-deformed-cft]].
- **Full resummation.** Only the leading-log / most-UV-sensitive terms are resummed; the
  complete correlator including subleading logs is not obtained.
- **Classical central charge.** The stress-tensor brackets used have no classical central term;
  improved-stress-tensor / Liouville-type cases are flagged for future study.
- **Cylinder / finite size** and a promised companion worldsheet derivation from strings on the
  TsT-transformed AdS$_3$ background.
