## TL;DR

Every 3d CFT — including those dual to quantum gravity on AdS$_4$ — carries an action of
the one-parameter deformed soft algebra
[[lambda-deformed-w-algebra|$\mathcal L_\Lambda w_{1+\infty}$]] at $\Lambda=-1$, generated
by the [[anec-operator|ANEC operator]], its $SO(3,2)$ conformal descendants, and their
commutators ([[every-cft3-has-l-lambda-w-symmetry]]). The generators fill the wedge
$\bar m+p\ge1$, $m-p\ge-2$ and obey the deformed commutation relations exactly, extending
previous tree-level bulk AdS$_4$ results to the strongly-coupled quantum regime.

## Motivation

Flat-space gravity has the infinite-dimensional soft algebra
[[lw-infinity-algebra|$\mathcal Lw_{1+\infty}$]] generated by positive-helicity soft
gravitons. An AdS$_4$/CFT$_3$ analogue, as found for the gauge-theory [[s-algebra]]
([[2601.00096|Sheta–Strominger–Tropper–Wei 2025]]), looks problematic for gravity — which,
unlike 4d Yang–Mills, is not conformally invariant, and AdS has no soft theorems of the
usual type. But the Jacobi-consistent deformation $\mathcal L_\Lambda w_{1+\infty}$ with
global $SO(3,2)$ had been found acting on tree-level Einstein gravity in AdS$_4$/dS$_4$.
The paper asks whether the dual CFT$_3$ realizes it nonperturbatively; the answer is yes —
for every CFT$_3$, gravitational dual or not.

## Historical context

The $\Lambda$-deformation was discovered in "beautiful recent work": Taylor–Zhu 2023
(perturbing flat-space celestial OPEs with a cosmological-constant interaction),
Bittleston–Bogna–Heuveline–Kmec–Mason–Skinner 2024 (a twistor deformation of self-dual
gravity to negative curvature, where the same wedge subalgebra appeared),
Mago–Ren–Yelleshpur Srikant–Volovich 2023, and a 2023 talk of J. H. Schwarz. The direct precursor is the gauge-theory light-ray
realization of the $S$-algebra ([[2601.00096|Sheta et al. 2025]]); the technical input is
the universal commutator algebra of the light-ray operators
$\mathcal E,\mathcal K,\mathcal N$ (Córdova–Shao 2018) and the conformal transformation
rules of [[light-ray-operators]] from conformal collider physics (Hofman–Maldacena 2008).
The cited light-ray commutator literature includes Himwich–Pate, "Light-ray Operators and
the $w_{1+\infty}$ Algebra" (2512.18973).

## Main results

1. **$\mathcal L_\Lambda w_{1+\infty}$ in every CFT$_3$** (eq. 41, the $\Lambda=-1$ case of
   eq. 1):
   $$\bigl[w^p_{\bar m,m},w^q_{\bar n,n}\bigr]
   =\bigl(\bar m(q-1)-\bar n(p-1)\bigr)w^{p+q-2}_{\bar m+\bar n,m+n}
   +\bigl(m(q-2)-n(p-2)\bigr)w^{p+q-1}_{\bar m+\bar n,m+n},$$
   realized by the ANEC operator on EC$^3$, its $SO(3,2)$ descendants, and commutators.
2. **Generator dictionary** (eq. 37): Fourier modes of light transforms of the ten
   $SO(3,2)$ conformal-Killing-vector currents are the $w$-generators; in particular
   $\mathcal E_k=-i^k\,w^{\frac{3+k}{2}}_{-\frac{k+1}{2},\frac{k-1}{2}}$.
3. **ANEC is $SO(3,2)$ lowest weight**: annihilated by $Q(H_{-\frac12,-\frac12})$,
   $Q(L_{-1})$, $Q(\bar L_{-1})$; the constructed modes form a single $SO(3,2)$
   representation with quadratic Casimir 6; at fixed $p$, a lowest-weight $SO(2,2)$
   representation with $(\bar h,h)=(p,3-p)$.
4. **Wedge subalgebra** (eq. 43): exactly one generator for each $(p,\bar m,m)$ with
   $p\pm m,\,p\pm\bar m\in\mathbb Z$ in the wedge $\bar m+p\ge1$, $m-p\ge-2$, closed under
   commutation, ANEC modes saturating the boundary; a generator redefinition (eq. 55) maps
   it to the "plane-model" algebra (eq. 56) of Bittleston et al./Heuveline, whose $\hat w$
   fill the flat-space wedge (eq. 54). Constructive proof in
   Appendix C routes around lattice points with vanishing raising coefficients;
   uniqueness and the structure constants follow from lowering to ANEC modes plus Jacobi.
5. **Nonperturbative extension**: only stress-tensor light-ray commutators valid in any
   CFT$_3$ are used, so the tree-level bulk results extend to strongly interacting quantum
   systems. Operators outside the wedge may exist in a given CFT$_3$ but are not produced
   by this construction.
6. **Bulk expectation (conjectural)**: Dirichlet boundary conditions reflect helicity and
   are incompatible with self-duality; by analogy with the gauge case, the authors expect
   something similar here, "perhaps involving combinations of the form
   $w^p_{\bar m,m}\pm\bar w^{3-p}_{\bar m,m}$".

## Methods

Conformal mapping of the Córdova–Shao operators $\mathcal E,\mathcal K,\mathcal N$ from
$M^3$ to the [[einstein-cylinder]] EC$^3$ (Hofman–Maldacena transformation rules), where
all null lines run between antipodal points and $S^2_{x_i}$ is a null Cauchy surface;
Fourier decomposition on the $\phi$ circle; light transforms
$L(\zeta_A;\phi)=\int_0^\pi d\tau^+\sin\tau^+\,j_{A+}$ of the ten CKV currents
$j_{A\mu}=T_{\mu\nu}\zeta_A^\nu$; $SO(3,2)$ representation theory (ANEC as lowest weight,
lattice-filling via raising operators); the Jacobi identity fixing the structure
constants to the $\mathcal L_\Lambda w_{1+\infty}$ values.

## Conventions

Mostly-plus signature; $ds^2_{M^3}=-dy^+dy^-+dy^2$;
$ds^2_{EC^3}=-4d\tau^+d\tau^-+\sin^2(\tau^+-\tau^-)d\phi^2$, $\tau^\pm=(\tau\pm\theta)/2$.
Stress tensor Weyl weight in $D=3$:
$T_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu|_{M^3}=\Omega^{-1}T_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu|_{EC^3}$ with
$\Omega=[\sin(\tau^++\tau^-)+\sin(\tau^+-\tau^-)\cos\phi]^{-1}$. Charges
$Q(\zeta_A)=\int_{S^2}d^2\Sigma^\mu j_{A\mu}$ act as
$[Q(\zeta_A),T_{\mu\nu}]=-i(\mathcal L_{\zeta_A}T)_{\mu\nu}-\frac{i}{3}\nabla_\lambda\zeta_A^\lambda T_{\mu\nu}$.
$SO(3,2)$ basis $L_{0,\pm1},\bar L_{0,\pm1}$ ($SO(2,2)$) plus four $H_{\bar r,s}$
($\bar r,s=\pm\frac12$), with $[L_m,L_n]=-i(m-n)L_{m+n}$; $(\bar m,m)$ are
$(\bar L_0,L_0)$ eigenvalues. Deformation parameter: eq. 1 carries $-\Lambda$ on the
second term; the CFT$_3$ construction realizes $\Lambda=-1$ (the paper does not relate
this value to an AdS radius). Stress tensor normalization inherited from Córdova–Shao. No conversion needed against existing
wiki pages.

## Open questions raised

- De Sitter interpretation of $\mathcal L_\Lambda w_{1+\infty}$ at $\Lambda>0$.
- Bulk AdS$_4$ derivation incorporating Dirichlet boundary conditions, perhaps involving
  combinations of the form $w^p_{\bar m,m}\pm\bar w^{3-p}_{\bar m,m}$.
- Generators outside the wedge may exist in a given CFT$_3$ but are not produced by the
  construction.
