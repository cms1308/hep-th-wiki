## TL;DR

Systematically enumerates all interacting fixed points reachable by relevant superpotential
deformations and [[flip-deformation|flip deformations]] from ten rank-1/rank-2
$\mathcal{N}=1$ seed gauge theories ($SU(2)$, $SU(3)$, $Sp(2)\simeq SO(5)$, $G_2$ with small
matter), producing a database of **7,346 inequivalent 4d $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFT candidates**
(browsable at [qft.kaist.ac.kr/landscape](https://qft.kaist.ac.kr/landscape)) —
the [[4d-n1-scft-landscape|landscape program]]. The $a/c$ distribution is narrow
($0.7228 \lesssim a/c \lesssim 1.2100$, the maximum a new record for interacting
$\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFTs), and correlates with the lightest-operator gap and anti-correlates
with the number of relevant operators — both suggestively holographic. Highlights: a
Lagrangian for the [[minimal-n1-scft|minimal-known $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFT]]
($(a,c)=(\tfrac{633}{2000},\tfrac{683}{2000})$, the deformed $(A_1,A_4)$
[[argyres-douglas-theories|Argyres-Douglas theory]]), new Lagrangian duals for deformed AD
theories, [[non-commuting-rg-flows|non-commuting RG flows]], and a puzzling
[[candidate-rank-1-cb-dimension-5-4|candidate rank-1 $\mathcal{N}=2$ SCFT]] with Coulomb
branch dimension $5/4$ forbidden by the geometric classification.

## Motivation

Classifying all CFTs is intractable; string-theoretic constructions (6d $(1,0)$, class
$\mathcal{S}$, rank-1 Coulomb-branch geometry) cover only high-supersymmetry corners, and
for 4d $\mathcal{N}=1$ nothing systematic exists beyond the conformal-gauge-theory
classification. The authors continue the Lagrangian program of Maruyoshi-Nardoni-Song
([1806.08353](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08353)): start from gauge theories known to flow
to interacting SCFTs ("seed theories") and exhaustively deform. Secondary aims: find the
minimal 4d $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFT; gather $(a,c)$ statistics as input for AdS versions of
swampland conjectures (since $c-a$ controls $R^2$ higher-derivative corrections, enters
the [[supersymmetric-cardy-formula-4d|supersymmetric Cardy formula]], and constrains the
higher-spin gap); and hunt for new supersymmetry-enhancing RG flows to $\mathcal{N}=2$
non-Lagrangian theories.

## Historical context

- Directly continues the "Landscape of Simple Superconformal Field Theories in 4d"
  (Maruyoshi-Nardoni-Song, [1806.08353](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08353)), which found 34
  fixed points from $SU(2)$ $N_f=1$ adjoint SQCD; the refined procedure here (consistency
  checked at every step, not only at the end) finds 26.
- Seed theories from the classification of $\mathcal{N}=1$ gauge theories flowing to
  interacting fixed points: Agarwal-Lee-Song
  ([2007.16165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.16165)),
  [[2308.01717|Cho-Choi-Lee-Song 2023]], and the Cho-Lee-Song classification paper —
  in preparation at the time, since published as [[2510.19136|Cho-Lee-Song 2025]].

  [hep-th/0304128](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0304128)) with the
  Kutasov-Parnachev-Sahakyan correction for decoupled operators
  ([hep-th/0308071](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0308071)); flip deformations:
  Barnes-Intriligator-Wecht-Wright
  ([hep-th/0408156](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0408156)), Benvenuti-Giacomelli
  ([1706.02225](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.02225)).

  [1607.04281](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.04281)), Agarwal-Maruyoshi-Song
  ([1610.05311](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.05311)); index criteria for enhancement:
  Evtikhiev ([1708.08307](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.08307)).
- Frame for the statistics: the [[hofman-maldacena-bound|Hofman-Maldacena window]]
  $\tfrac12 \le a/c \le \tfrac32$; previous $a/c$ record $183/158 \simeq 1.158$ (ISS
  model, [hep-ph/9410203](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9410203)); minimal deformed-AD
  SCFT: Xie-Yan ([2109.04090](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.04090)).

## Main results

1. **Landscape of 7,346 fixed points.** All interacting fixed points reachable via chains
   of single relevant deformations $\delta W = \mathcal{O}$ ($R(\mathcal{O})<2$) and flip
   deformations $\delta W = M\mathcal{O}$ ($R(\mathcal{O})<\tfrac43$) from ten seeds:
   $SU(2)$ adj $+\,2\,\Box$ / adj $+\,4\,\Box$; $SU(3)$ adj $+(\Box+\overline\Box)$;
   $SO(5)$ $\mathbf{10}+\mathbf{5}$, $\mathbf{10}+2\cdot\mathbf{5}$,
   $\mathbf{14}+\mathbf{5}$, $\mathbf{14}+2\cdot\mathbf{5}$; $Sp(2)$
   $\mathbf{14}+2\cdot\mathbf{4}$, $\mathbf{10}+2\cdot\mathbf{4}$; $G_2$ adj
   $+\,\mathbf{7}$. Per-landscape counts: 26, 5894+$\alpha$ (truncated at 12
   deformations), 40, 23, 698, 8, 9, 10, 470, 34. Fixed points are distinguished by
   their unrefined [[superconformal-index|superconformal index]].
2. **Narrow $a/c$ distribution and a new record**:
   $0.7228 \simeq (a/c)_{\min} \le a/c \le (a/c)_{\max} \simeq 1.2100$, far inside the
   Hofman-Maldacena window. The maximum is $SO(5)$ with one traceless symmetric
   ($\mathbf{14}$) $S$ and one vector, $W = M_1 S^2 + M_1^2$:
   $(a,c) = (\tfrac{507}{256},\tfrac{419}{256})$, $a/c = \tfrac{507}{419} \simeq 1.21$ —
   exceeding the ISS record $183/158\simeq 1.158$, still below the $\mathcal{N}=2$
   bound $\tfrac54$. The authors write it is "tempting to conjecture that
   there is no interacting $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFT with $a/c \ge 5/4$"
   ([[n1-central-charge-bounds]]).
3. **Lagrangian for the minimal-known $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFT**
   ([[minimal-n1-scft]]): an $SU(2)$ $N_f=2$ adjoint SQCD fixed point with
   $(a,c) = (\tfrac{633}{2000},\tfrac{683}{2000})$, identical to the $\mathcal{N}=1$
   deformation of the $(A_1,A_4)$ AD theory by its dimension-$\tfrac{10}{7}$ Coulomb
   branch operator; a second dual description via $Sp(2)$ $N_f=1$ adjoint SQCD.
4. **Novel Lagrangian dualities**: two different Lagrangians for the $H_1=(A_1,A_3)$ AD
   theory that *differ in the number of flavors* (unlike Kutasov duality), verified by
   matching indices and chiral rings; and a Lagrangian for the $\mathcal{N}=1$-deformed
   $(A_2,A_3)$ theory ($(a,c)=(\tfrac{231}{256},\tfrac{239}{256})$) via $SU(3)$ $N_f=1$
   adjoint SQCD — notable since no $\mathcal{N}=1$ Lagrangian for undeformed
   $(A_{k-1},A_{p-1})$ with $\gcd(p,k)=1$ is known.
5. **Statistical correlations**: (a) as the lightest scalar chiral dimension approaches
   the unitarity bound 1, the $a/c$ distribution broadens; a larger gap forces
   $a/c \to 1$ (read via $c-a$ controlling non-Einstein bulk corrections); (b) $a/c$
   decreases as the number of relevant operators grows; (c) flip flows of flavor-neutral
   operators trace straight lines in the $(a,c)$ plane with slope
   $s = \frac{9R^2-18R+4}{9R^2-18R+6} \in (\tfrac53, 2)$.
6. **[[non-commuting-rg-flows|Non-commuting RG flows]]**: the IR endpoint depends on the
   *order* of superpotential deformations; nine cases tabulated in the $SU(2)$ $N_f=1$
   landscape alone.
7. **[[candidate-rank-1-cb-dimension-5-4|Candidate rank-1 $\mathcal{N}=2$ SCFT]]** with
   $(a,c)=(\tfrac{19}{48},\tfrac{5}{12})$: passes all index conditions for
   $\mathcal{N}=2$ enhancement with Coulomb branch dimension $\Delta = \tfrac54$ — not
   allowed by the geometric classification of rank-1 $\mathcal{N}=2$ SCFTs; its
   Hall-Littlewood index (Higgs branch $=\mathbb{C}$, not a cone) hints at an
   unidentified accidental symmetry. Fate left open.
8. **SUSY enhancement census**: confirmed $\mathcal{N}=2$ enhancements only reproduce
   known flows ($H_0$, $H_1$, $H_2$, $(A_1,A_4)$, $(A_1,A_5)$); no genuinely new
   $\mathcal{N}=2$ non-Lagrangian theory found.
9. **Miscellany**: 26 pairs of inequivalent fixed points (different indices) with
   identical $(a,c)$; $a=c$ fixed points in the $SO(5)$ and $Sp(2)$ landscapes; hundreds
   of fixed points with conformal manifolds or emergent flavor symmetry (sign of the
   $t^6$ index coefficient).

## Methods

- **Iterative deformation algorithm**: from a seed, list relevant ($R<2$) and
  super-relevant ($R<\tfrac43$) chiral operators; deform by each $\delta W=\mathcal{O}$
  or flip $\delta W = M\mathcal{O}$; flip any operator decoupling below the unitarity
  bound; recurse, checking consistency **at every step** (the refinement over
  [1806.08353](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08353)).
- **[[a-maximization]]** at each step, with the Kutasov-Parnachev-Sahakyan correction
  when an operator decouples. The stated central caveat: all accidental symmetries must
  be correctly identified.
- **[[superconformal-index|Index]] as consistency filter**: unitarity conditions on the
  coefficients of $t^R \chi_j(y)$ (Evtikhiev), higher-spin free-field detection,
  vanishing index ⇒ SUSY breaking; the $t^6$ coefficient $= \#(\text{marginal}) -
  \#(\text{conserved currents})$ diagnoses conformal manifolds and symmetry enhancement;
  a $+1$ coefficient of $t^7(y+1/y)$ (extra supercurrent) is the sufficient condition
  for $\mathcal{N}=2$ enhancement.
- **$\mathcal{N}=2$ index limits** (Macdonald, Hall-Littlewood, Schur, Coulomb) to
  diagnose the candidate rank-1 theory.
- **Computational pipeline**: Mathematica ($a$-maximization), FORM + LiE (symbolic index
  series and singlet counting), Python + MySQL, published as a website database.
- **Seed selection**: cubic anomaly cancellation $\sum_i \mathcal{A}(\mathbf{R}_i)=0$,
  asymptotic freedom $b_0 = 3h^\vee - \sum_i T(\mathbf{R}_i) \ge 0$, positive R-charges
  of charged chirals (no runaway superpotential), Witten anomaly.

## Conventions

- **Index**: $\mathcal{I}(t,y;x) = \mathrm{Tr}\,(-1)^F\, t^{3(R+2j_1)}\, y^{2j_2}\, x^f$,
  with $(j_1,j_2)$ the half-integer $SU(2)_1\times SU(2)_2$ Lorentz spins. **Reduced
  index**: $\mathcal{I}_{\rm red} = (1-t^3 y)(1-t^3/y)(\mathcal{I}-1)$; all indices in
  the paper are quoted reduced.
- **Dictionary to the $(\omega_1,\omega_2)$ convention** used on
  [[2308.01717|Cho-Choi-Lee-Song 2023]] and the [[superconformal-index]] page:
  $e^{-\omega_1} = t^3 y$, $e^{-\omega_2} = t^3 y^{-1}$, and $J_{1,2} = j_1 \pm j_2$
  (orthogonal-plane angular momenta vs. $SU(2)\times SU(2)$ spins). Check: the
  contributing states $\Delta - J_1 - J_2 - \tfrac32 R = 0$ become
  $\Delta - 2j_1 - \tfrac32 R = 0$, matching the $t$-exponent grading
  $t^{3(R+2j_1)}$.
- **Central charges**: $a = \tfrac{3}{32}(3\mathrm{Tr}R^3 - \mathrm{Tr}R)$,
  $c = \tfrac{1}{32}(9\mathrm{Tr}R^3 - 5\mathrm{Tr}R)$ — same as
  [[2308.01717|Cho-Choi-Lee-Song 2023]]. Free chiral: $a/c=\tfrac12$; free vector:
  $a/c=\tfrac32$.
- **Dimensions and thresholds**: $\Delta = \tfrac32 R$ for scalar chiral primaries;
  unitarity $R \ge \tfrac23$; relevant $\Leftrightarrow R<2$; "super-relevant"
  (flippable) $\Leftrightarrow R < \tfrac43$.
- **Multiplets**: Córdova-Dumitrescu-Intriligator notation $[j_1;j_2]^{(R)}_\Delta$, with
  half-integer $j_{1,2}$. $\mathcal{N}=1\subset\mathcal{N}=2$ embedding:
  $R = \tfrac13(r+4I_3)$, $J = \tfrac12(r-2I_3)$.
- **$\mathcal{N}=2$ fugacities**: $\mathfrak{p}=t^3y$, $\mathfrak{q}=t^3/y$,
  $\mathfrak{t}=t^4 v^{-1}$.
- Flip fields: $M_i$ are "active" flips, $X_i$ flips of decoupled operators; gauge
  groups' global form not distinguished.

## Key equations

- Trial R-symmetry: $R_{\rm IR} = R + \sum_{\bar i} \epsilon_{\bar i} J_{\bar i}$, fixed
  by maximizing $a$. For a deformation carrying a single $U(1)$:
  $\delta W=\mathcal{O}$ gives $\epsilon = \frac{2-R(\mathcal{O})}{J_1(\mathcal{O})}$;
  a flip $\delta W = M\mathcal{O}$ gives $\epsilon = \tfrac43 - R(\mathcal{O})$.
- Flip of a flavor-neutral operator of R-charge $R$:
  $$a_{\rm IR} = a + \tfrac{3}{32}(1-R)(3R^2-6R+2)\,,\qquad
  c_{\rm IR} = c + \tfrac{1}{32}(1-R)(9R^2-18R+4)\,,$$
  implying straight lines of slope $s = \frac{9R^2-18R+4}{9R^2-18R+6} \in (\tfrac53,2)$
  in the $(a,c)$ plane.
- Decoupling correction: $a_{\rm new} = a_{\rm old} + \tfrac{3}{32}\big(\tfrac29 -
  (3(R(\mathcal{O}_d)-1)^3 - (R(\mathcal{O}_d)-1))\big)$.
- Conformal-manifold diagnostic: $\mathcal{I}_{\rm red} \supset \alpha\, t^6$ with
  $\alpha = \#(\text{marginal}) - \#(\text{conserved currents})$.
- Minimal SCFT superpotential ($SU(2)$ $N_f=2$ adjoint, $\phi$ adjoint, $q_i,\tilde q_i$
  doublets):
  $$W = M_1 q_1 q_2 + M_2 q_1\tilde q_1 + \phi q_1 \tilde q_2 + M_2 \phi q_2^2
  + M_1 M_2 + \phi\tilde q_1^2 + X_1 \mathrm{Tr}\,\phi^2\,,\qquad
  (a,c) = \left(\tfrac{633}{2000},\tfrac{683}{2000}\right).$$
- Record-$a/c$ theory ($SO(5)$, $S$ in $\mathbf{14}$, one $\mathbf{5}$):
  $W = M_1 S^2 + M_1^2$, $(a,c) = (\tfrac{507}{256},\tfrac{419}{256})$,
  $a/c = \tfrac{507}{419} \simeq 1.2100$, with one exactly marginal operator
  $\mathrm{Tr}\,S^4$.

## Open questions raised

- Fate of the $\Delta_{\rm CB}=\tfrac54$ candidate: loophole in the rank-1 geometric
  classification, or unidentified accidental symmetry?
  ([[candidate-rank-1-cb-dimension-5-4]])
- Conjecture: no interacting $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFT with $a/c \ge \tfrac54$; more
  generally, rigorous lower bounds on $c$ for interacting $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFTs
  ([[n1-central-charge-bounds]]).
- Do the $a/c$–gap and $a/c$–#relevant-operators correlations persist for broader theory
  classes, and can they be made quantitative (AdS-swampland input)?
- Tools to detect accidental symmetries invisible to $a$-maximization + index — the
  validity caveat for the whole database.
- Systematic understanding of [[non-commuting-rg-flows|non-commuting RG flows]];
  completing the truncated landscapes; extending to more seeds and non-Lagrangian seeds.
- Why $\mathcal{N}=1$-deformed non-Lagrangian theories admit Lagrangians more readily
  than undeformed ones; an $\mathcal{N}=1$ Lagrangian for $(A_2,A_3)$ itself.
