## TL;DR

A unified complex-geometric **and** algebraic framework for symmetries and anomalies in
4d holomorphically twisted $\mathcal N\geq1$ SQFTs. The [[holomorphic-twist|minimal twist]]
produces a holomorphic field theory on $\mathbb C^2$ whose infinite-dimensional symmetry
algebras — higher analogues of Virasoro and Kac–Moody — are homotopy Lie ($L_\infty$)
algebras. The conformal anomalies $a,c$ and the 't Hooft anomalies appear as **ternary
brackets / 3-cocycles** (central extensions) of these algebras. Extended supersymmetry
enhances them to super-analogues $\mathfrak{svir}(2|\mathcal N-1)$, realized geometrically
as derived holomorphic (super)vector fields on $\mathbb C^{2|\mathcal N-1}\setminus\{0\}$.
A superconformal deformation by $\int z^2\widetilde{\mathbf G}$ localizes the theory to the
plane $z^2=0$ and reproduces the Beem-et-al [[scft-voa-correspondence|SCFT/VOA correspondence]],
with an explicit rule extracting the 2d VOA $\lambda$-brackets from the 4d holomorphic ones.

## Motivation

Holomorphic (minimal) twists isolate the protected sector counted by the full superconformal
index and organize it into a rich derived operator-product algebra. Two disconnected
approaches existed: a **purely algebraic** one (secondary products, [[holomorphic-lambda-bracket|$\lambda$-brackets]],
factorization algebras) concerned with local operators, and a **purely complex-geometric**
one (anomalous responses to curved backgrounds and holomorphic flavor bundles). A holistic
framework tying the anomalous coupling to background fields directly to the higher-bracket
structure of local operators was missing. This paper bridges that gap and resolves questions
left open in its predecessor (paper I ~[[2509.16737|Bomans-Garner-Williams-Wu 2023]]),
especially for theories with extended supersymmetry.

## Main results

1. **Reduced-superfield / semi-chiral framework.** Every $\mathcal N=1$ multiplet obeying an
   anti-chiral shortening condition contributes one $\mathbf Q$-closed operator — the bottom
   component of a [[semi-chiral-superfield|semi-chiral superfield]]. Semi-chiral superfields
   are in one-to-one correspondence with $\mathbf Q$-closed local operators.

2. **Symmetries as $L_\infty$ algebras.** Spacetime symmetry enhances to the
   [[two-virasoro-algebra|2-Witt algebra]] $\mathfrak{witt}(2)\simeq
   \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\mathbb C^2\setminus\{0\},\,T^{1,0})$; flavor symmetry to the
   [[higher-kac-moody-algebra|higher Kac–Moody algebra]]
   $\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\mathbb C^2\setminus\{0\})\otimes\mathfrak f$. These admit **no ordinary
   central extensions** but two, resp. one, $L_\infty$ central extensions.

3. **Anomalies = ternary brackets / 3-cocycles.** The $a,c$ conformal anomalies are the two
   central extensions of $\mathfrak{witt}(2)$, giving $\mathfrak{vir}(2)_{a,c}$; 't Hooft and
   ABJ anomalies appear as ternary $\lambda$-brackets of currents and as the failure of a
   current to be semi-chiral — see [[ac-anomalies-as-central-extensions]].

4. **Holomorphic $a$-maximization.** Spectral flow of the stress tensor plus the uniqueness of
   the $\langle SSJ\rangle$ tensor structure force $k_f=9k_{RRf}$, the $a$-extremization
   condition — see [[holomorphic-a-maximization]].

5. **Extended-SUSY super-Virasoro algebras.** For $\mathcal N=2,3,4$ the twisted stress-tensor
   multiplet generates $\mathfrak{svir}(2|\mathcal N-1)$, realized as derived holomorphic super
   vector fields; for $\mathcal N\geq3$ the uniqueness of the 3-cocycle **forces** $a=c$ — see
   [[higher-super-virasoro-algebra]].

6. **VOA from a superconformal deformation.** Deforming by $\int z^2\widetilde{\mathbf G}$
   localizes the theory to $z^2=0$ and reproduces the Beem-et-al VOA. The
   [[schur-superfield|Schur superfields]] $\mathbb O=\mathbf O+dz^2\,\Psi_{\mathbf O}$ generate
   it, and the 2d VOA $\lambda$-brackets are extracted from the 4d holomorphic ones by an
   explicit formula — see [[superconformal-deformation-to-voa]]. For the free hypermultiplet
   the result is the symplectic boson VOA, with $c_{2d}=-12c$ and flavor level $k_{2d}=-\tfrac12 k_F$.

## Methods

- **Twist via nilpotent supercharge + BV/BRST.** Add $\mathbf Q=\mathcal Q_-$ to the BV/BRST
  differential; $\{\mathbf Q,\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_{\dot\alpha}\}=\mathcal P_{-\dot\alpha}
  =-i\partial_{\bar z}$ makes anti-holomorphic translations exact, leaving a holomorphic theory
  on $\mathbb C^2$.
- **Free-field realization** as coupled $\mathbf{bc}$–$\boldsymbol{\beta\gamma}$ systems with
  BV action $S_{\rm BV}=\int_{\mathbb C^2}d^2z\,\mathrm{Tr}\,\mathbf b(\bar\partial\mathbf c
  +\tfrac12[\mathbf c,\mathbf c])+\boldsymbol\beta(\bar\partial\boldsymbol\gamma+\mathbf c\boldsymbol\gamma)+W(\boldsymbol\gamma)$.
- **Secondary products / [[holomorphic-lambda-bracket|$\lambda$-brackets]]** via $S^3$
  (configuration-space) integrals weighted by Dolbeault representatives $\rho_{m,n}$ of
  $H^{0,\bullet}(\mathbb C^2\setminus\{0\})$ and the Bochner–Martinelli kernel.
- **Equivariant BV master equation** for coupling to a background Beltrami differential $\mu$
  and connection $A$; the anomaly is the failure of the equivariant quantum master equation,
  computed from triangle/Feynman diagrams and via BV-bracket descent.
- **Anomaly-polynomial specialization** $p_1\mapsto c_1^2-2c_2$, $c_{1,r}\mapsto-\tfrac12 c_1$,
  translating untwisted 't Hooft anomalies into holomorphic ones.
- **Supermultiplet decomposition** of the $\mathcal N=2,3,4$ stress-tensor/flavor multiplets
  into $\mathcal N=1$ pieces, identifying $\mathfrak{svir}(2|\mathcal N-1)$ as derived super
  vector fields; spectral sequences + Cartan homotopy/descent for the VOA localization.

## Conventions

- **Space / signature:** Euclidean $\mathbb R^4\cong\mathbb C^2$; a choice of $\mathbf Q$ fixes
  the complex structure. After twisting the dotted indices are renamed $i=1,2$ with
  $z^1=z^{\dot+},\ z^2=z^{\dot-}$; holomorphic symplectic form $\omega=dz^1\wedge dz^2$.
- **Twisting supercharge:** $\mathbf Q=\mathcal Q_-$, the unique nilpotent supercharge up to
  complexified rotations/$R$-symmetry/parity; it is the BV/BRST differential of the twist.
- **Twisting homomorphism** $\phi:\mathrm{MU}(2,\mathbb C)\to\mathrm{Spin}(4)\times G_R$ (the
  metaplectic double cover of $U(2)$); the odd superspace coordinate $\theta$ is a section of
  $K^{1/3}$.
- **$2\pi$ factors:** secondary brackets use $\oint_{S^3}\frac{d^2z}{(2\pi i)^2}$; the
  Bochner–Martinelli kernel is $\omega_{\rm BM}=\frac{1}{(2\pi i)^2}\frac{\bar z^1 d\bar z^2-\bar z^2 d\bar z^1}{|z|^4}$.
- **VOA dictionary** (matching [[scft-voa-correspondence|Beem et al.]]): $c_{2d}=-12c$,
  flavor level $k_F=-2k_{2d}$. Holomorphic anomalies $c^{\rm hol}=\tfrac{3c-5a}{27}$,
  $a^{\rm hol}=\tfrac{2(a-c)}{3}$; equivalently $c^{\rm hol}=-\tfrac{k_{RRR}}{48}$,
  $a^{\rm hol}=\tfrac{k_R}{24}$.
- **$R$-charge maps** to $\mathcal N=1$: $r=\tfrac13 r_{\mathcal N=2}+\tfrac43 I_2$ ($\mathcal N=2$);
  $r=\tfrac19(r_{\mathcal N=3}+4I_3)$ ($\mathcal N=3$); $r=-\tfrac13 I_4$ ($\mathcal N=4$).

## Key equations

- **Semi-chiral condition & descent:** $D_-\mathbf O=(\mathbf Q-\bar\partial)\mathbf O=0$,
  $\ \mathbf Q\,\mathbf O^{(k)}=\bar\partial\,\mathbf O^{(k-1)}$.
- **$(n{+}1)$-ary $\lambda$-bracket:**
  $\{\mathbf O_1{}_{\lambda_1}\cdots{}_{\lambda_n}\mathbf O_{n+1}\}
  =\int_{\mathrm{Conf}^0_{n+1}}\prod_k\frac{d^2z_k}{(2\pi i)^2}\,e^{\lambda_k\cdot z_k}\,\mathbf Q[\mathbf O_1\cdots\mathbf O_{n+1}]$.
- **2-Virasoro binary bracket:** $\{\mathbf S_i{}_\lambda\mathbf S_j\}=\partial_i\mathbf S_j+\lambda_i\mathbf S_j+\lambda_j\mathbf S_i$.
- **$a,c$ ternary bracket:** $\{\!\{\mathbf S_i\,\mathbf S_j\,\mathbf S_k\}\!\}
  =16(a-c)\,\mathcal I^{\rm vec}_{ijk}+\tfrac43(3c-2a)\,\mathcal I^{\mathcal N=4}_{ijk}$.
- **ABJ non-conservation:** $\mathbf Q\,\mathbf J=\bar\partial\mathbf J-\tfrac{\hbar k}{2\pi^2}\partial\mathbf A\,\partial\mathbf A$
  (background) / $=\bar\partial\mathbf J+k_{FFf}\mathbf N$ with
  $\mathbf N=\tfrac{\hbar}{(2\pi i)^2}\epsilon^{jk}\mathrm{tr}_F\partial_j\mathbf c\,\partial_k\mathbf c$.
- **Holomorphic $a$-maximization:** $\{\!\{\mathbf S^{\rm sc}_i\,\mathbf S^{\rm sc}_j\,\mathbf J_f\}\!\}
  \propto k_f[\lambda_1\lambda_2](\lambda_{1i}\lambda_{2j}-3\lambda_{1j}\lambda_{2i})
  \ \Rightarrow\ k_f=9k_{RRf}$.
- **VOA extraction:** $\{\!\{\mathbb T{}_\lambda\mathbb T\}\!\}=\partial_1\mathbb T+2\lambda\mathbb T-c\,\lambda^3$
  (2d stress tensor with $c_{2d}=-12c$).

## Open questions raised

- [[intrinsic-holomorphic-a-maximization]] — can $a$-extremization + the unitarity condition
  $k_{Rfg}<0$ be derived **intrinsically** in the twist, via a notion of "graded unitarity"?
- [[supergeometric-3-cocycles-svir]] — explicit supergeometric representatives of the
  $\mathfrak{svir}(2|\mathcal N-1)$ 3-cocycles (known to be 2-dimensional for $\mathcal N=2$,
  corresponding to $(c_1^2,c_2)$, but no explicit formulas).
- Relation of exotic deformations by $(z^2)^m\widetilde{\mathbf G}$ to the R-twists of Cecotti et al.
- How the non-invertible (ABJ-salvaged) symmetries act on holomorphic surface operators, and
  the embedding of spacetime symmetries/twists into the SymTFT framework.
- Application to twisted Argyres–Douglas theories and the IR $\mathcal N=2$ enhancement of
  $\mathcal N=1$ Lagrangians (announced follow-up), extending
  ~[[2408.02953|Cho-Maruyoshi-Nardoni-Song 2024]].
