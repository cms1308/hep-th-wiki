## TL;DR

Gives the covariant generalization of the [[ryu-takayanagi-formula|Ryu-Takayanagi formula]] —
the [[hrt-formula|HRT proposal]]: in a general, possibly time-dependent asymptotically
AdS$_{d+1}$ spacetime the [[entanglement-entropy|entanglement entropy]] of a boundary region
$A$ is the area of a bulk codimension-two **extremal** surface anchored on $\partial A$,
divided by $4G_N$. The extremal surface (vanishing null expansions $\theta_+=\theta_-=0$) is
identified with a light-sheet construction from Bousso's [[bousso-bound|covariant entropy bound]], and the proposal is checked on rotating BTZ and on a collapsing Vaidya-AdS background
where $S_A$ grows monotonically.

## Motivation

The static RT prescription minimizes area on a constant-time slice, which relies on a Euclidean
/ preferred spatial slice. In a genuinely time-dependent bulk a minimal spacelike surface is
ill-defined — "wiggling a spacelike surface in the time direction makes its area arbitrarily
small" — and there is no canonical foliation extending the boundary time-slicing into the bulk.
Since the field-theory entanglement entropy is manifestly well-defined and time-dependent, its
holographic dual must be generally covariant.

## Historical context

Covariantizes [[hep-th-0603001|Ryu-Takayanagi 2006]] using Bousso's covariant entropy bound /
light-sheets (1999-2002). The 2d checks use the Calabrese-Cardy / Holzhey-Larsen-Wilczek
twist-operator method; the entanglement interpretation of Euclidean wormholes draws on
Maldacena's eternal black hole and Maldacena-Maoz. Uses [[hep-th-9711200|AdS/CFT]] and the
GKP-Witten dictionary for the variational derivation of the extremal-surface condition.

## Main results

1. **Covariant HEE (extremal form):** $S_A=\mathrm{Area}(\gamma^{\rm ext}_A)/4G_N^{(d+1)}$
   with $\theta_+=\theta_-=0$ on $\gamma^{\rm ext}_A$, $\partial\gamma^{\rm ext}_A=\partial A$;
   if several extremal surfaces exist, the minimum-area homologous one is selected.
2. **Light-sheet form:** $S_A=\min_\Sigma\mathrm{Area}(\Sigma_t)/4G_N$ over surfaces supporting
   future and past light-sheets ($\theta_{\hat\pm}\le0$), $\Sigma_t=L_t^+\cap L_t^-$; deforming
   toward the boundary drives it to the extremal surface, saturating the Bousso bound.
3. **Reduction to RT:** for static bulk the extremal surface lies on the constant-$t$ slice and
   $\theta_\pm=0$ becomes the minimal-surface condition.
4. **Rotating BTZ check:** the extremal geodesic is not confined to any constant-$t$ slice and
   gives $S_A=\frac{c}{6}\log\!\big[\frac{\beta_+\beta_-}{\pi^2\epsilon^2}\sinh\frac{\pi\Delta l}{\beta_+}\sinh\frac{\pi\Delta l}{\beta_-}\big]$,
   $\beta_\pm=\beta(1\pm\Omega)$, matching the CFT twist-operator result in
   $\rho=e^{-\beta H+\beta\Omega P}$ and factorizing into left/right movers.
5. **Vaidya-AdS (time-dependent):** $S_A(v)$ increases monotonically during collapse, proven
   perturbatively under the null energy condition (a second-law-type statement).
6. **Multi-boundary wormholes:** the cross-boundary correlations of Euclidean wormholes are
   reinterpreted as nonzero entanglement between the boundary CFTs.
7. An alternative "minimal surface on a maximal / totally-geodesic slice" candidate is shown to
   fail generically (rotating BTZ).

## Methods

Extremal-surface saddle of the Lorentzian area functional; null geodesic congruences with null
extrinsic curvatures $(\chi_\pm)_{\mu\nu}$ and expansions $\theta_\pm$, giving
$\delta(\mathrm{Area})\propto\int(\theta_+N_+^\mu+\theta_-N_-^\mu)\delta X_\mu$ so
$\theta_\pm=0\Leftrightarrow$ extremal; Bousso light-sheets to define the surface covariantly;
the 2d replica / twist-operator method for the CFT answers; a local coordinate map sending BTZ
to Poincaré AdS$_3$ for exact geodesic lengths; adiabatic + perturbative + numerical analysis
of the Vaidya geodesic length.

## Conventions

- **Signature:** mostly-plus Lorentzian, AdS radius set to $1$; e.g.
  $ds^2=z^{-2}(-dt^2+dz^2+\sum dx_i^2)$.
- **Extremal, not minimal:** the surface is a saddle of the area functional with
  $\theta_+=\theta_-=0$; **selection rule** when non-unique = minimum area among homologous
  extremal surfaces. (Contrast [[hep-th-0603001|RT]], which minimizes on a fixed-time slice —
  the two agree only in static spacetimes.)
- **Entropy:** $S=\mathrm{Area}/4G_N^{(d+1)}$; $\mathrm{Tr}\,\rho=1$.
- **Null normals** normalized $N_+\!\cdot N_-=-1$; light-sheet convergence $\theta\le0$.
- **BTZ:** $8G^{(3)}M=r_+^2+r_-^2$, $J=r_+r_-/4G^{(3)}$; ensemble
  $\rho=e^{-\beta H+\beta\Omega P}$, $\beta_\pm=\beta(1\pm\Omega)=2\pi l/(r_+\pm r_-)$; UV cutoff
  $\epsilon$, $r_\infty=1/\epsilon$.

## Key equations

- $S_A=\dfrac{\mathrm{Area}(\gamma^{\rm ext}_A)}{4G_N^{(d+1)}}$, $\theta_+=\theta_-=0$ —
  covariant HEE.
- $S_A=\dfrac{\min_\Sigma\mathrm{Area}(\Sigma_t)}{4G_N^{(d+1)}}$, $\Sigma_t=L_t^+\cap L_t^-$ —
  light-sheet form; and the Bousso bound $S_{L_\Sigma}\le\mathrm{Area}(\Sigma)/4G_N$.
- $S_A=\dfrac{c}{6}\log\!\big[\tfrac{\beta_+\beta_-}{\pi^2\epsilon^2}\sinh\tfrac{\pi\Delta l}{\beta_+}\sinh\tfrac{\pi\Delta l}{\beta_-}\big]$
  — rotating BTZ.
- $L_{\rm reg}(v)=\log\dfrac{\sinh^2(\sqrt{m(v)}\,h)}{m(v)}$, $\Delta S_A(v)\propto L_{\rm reg}(v)$
  increasing under the NEC — Vaidya time dependence.

## Open questions raised

- Rigorous proof that the extremal surface equals the light-sheet surface; uniqueness /
  selection among multiple extremal surfaces (later phase transitions and quantum extremal
  surfaces).
- Why entanglement entropy saturates the thermodynamic Bousso bound.
- Quantum / beyond-supergravity corrections; whether monotonicity survives Hawking-radiation
  backreaction; reconstructing the bulk metric from all subregion entropies.
