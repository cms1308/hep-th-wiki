## TL;DR

Type IIB string theory on $\mathrm{AdS}_3\times\mathrm{S}^3\times\mathcal{M}_4$ ($\mathcal{M}_4=\mathbb{T}^4$ or K3) is dual to the symmetric orbifold $\mathrm{Sym}^N(\mathcal{M}_4)$. The boundary Hilbert space of left–right [[chiral-primary-states|chiral primaries]] is **finite-dimensional** at finite $N$ — the [[stringy-exclusion-principle]] — but the bulk Kaluza–Klein spectrum is infinite. This paper gives a bulk prescription that reproduces the exact finite-$N$ spectrum: the chiral-primary partition function $Z_N$ is written as an infinite alternating sum of one-loop supersymmetric partition functions of $(\mathrm{AdS}_3\times\mathrm{S}^3)/\mathbb{Z}_k$ orbifold geometries and their spectral flows. The terms carry signs $(-1)^{k-1}$ coming from BPS twisted-sector modes of negative dimension and charge; the sum collapses by large cancellations to the known finite polynomial $Z_N$. This is the AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$ analog of the [[giant-graviton-expansion]], organized by the pole/[[stokes-sectors-partition-function|Stokes]] structure of the grand-canonical partition function — see [[ads3-giant-graviton-expansion]].

## Motivation

In any 2d $\mathcal{N}=(2,2)$ SCFT the [[chiral-primary-states|chiral primary]] charges obey $h,\bar h,j,\bar j\le c/6$. For the $\mathcal{N}=(4,4)$ [[symmetric-orbifold-cft|symmetric orbifold]] with $c=6N$ this makes the chiral-primary Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_{cc}$ finite-dimensional at finite $N$: the [[stringy-exclusion-principle]]. The bulk KK spectrum on $\mathrm{AdS}_3\times\mathrm{S}^3\times\mathcal{M}_4$ is infinite ($Z_\infty$), and it is not obvious what non-perturbative bulk mechanism truncates it.

The finite-$N$ deviation from $Z_\infty$ first appears at energy $E=h+\bar h\lesssim N/2$, of order the central charge $c\sim1/G_N^{(3)}$, well below the chiral-primary black-hole threshold at $E=N$. This leaves a "wedge" $N/2<E<N$ of states that are neither ordinary KK modes nor black holes. The paper asks what bulk contributions account for these states and what enforces the finite total count.

## Historical context

The D1–D5 system and AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$ go back to [[hep-th-9711200|Maldacena 1997]], with $N=Q_1Q_5$ ($\mathbb{T}^4$) or $Q_1Q_5+1$ (K3). Large-$N$ matching of chiral primaries with 6d supergravity KK modes was done by Maldacena–Strominger, de Boer, and others (1998); [[jan-de-boer|de Boer]] sharpened the finite-$N$ structure through grand-canonical residues. The [[stringy-exclusion-principle]] itself is due to Maldacena–Strominger (1998), resting on the $c/6$ unitarity bound (Boucher–Dean–Zumino 1986; Lerche–Vafa–Warner 1989). The [[dmvv-formula|DMVV formula]] (1996) organizes symmetric-orbifold partition functions as second-quantized strings.

The bulk side draws on the tensionless string on $\mathrm{AdS}_3\times\mathrm{S}^3\times\mathbb{T}^4$ at one unit of NS5 flux — the $\mathfrak{psu}(1,1|2)_1$ free-field description (Eberhardt–Gaberdiel–Gopakumar 2018) in the hybrid formalism (Berkovits–Vafa–Witten 1999) — and its ensemble/grand-canonical interpretation (Eberhardt 2020; Eberhardt–Gaberdiel 2021). The $\mathbb{Z}_k$ conical-defect geometries (Martinec; Maldacena–Maoz; Balasubramanian et al) had their tensionless worldsheet spectra computed recently (Gaberdiel et al 2023). The direct methodological template is the AdS$_5$/CFT$_4$ [[giant-graviton-expansion]] (Imamura; Gaiotto–Lee 2021) with alternating signs from negative-mode contour rotations ([[ji-hoon-lee|Lee]] 2023–24).

## Main results

1. **Sum-over-geometries formula.** For $\mathcal{M}_4=\mathbb{T}^4$ or K3,
$$Z_N(y,\bar y)=\sum_{\mu\in\mathcal{S}_{y,\bar y}}\ \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\widehat{Z}_k^{\,\mu}(y,\bar y),\qquad \mathcal{S}_{y,\bar y}\subset\{(0,0),(2,0),(0,2),(2,2)\},$$
where $\widehat{Z}_k^{\,\mu}$ is the one-loop supersymmetric partition function of IIB strings on $(\mathrm{AdS}_3\times\mathrm{S}^3)/\mathbb{Z}_k\times\mathcal{M}_4$ (for $\mu=(0,0)$) or its spectral flows / asymmetric orbifolds ($\mu=(2,0),(0,2),(2,2)$). See [[ads3-giant-graviton-expansion]].

2. **Grand-canonical residues = bulk saddles.** The $\widehat{Z}_k^{\,\mu}$ are (minus) the sums over $\mathbb{Z}_k$-image residues of the grand-canonical partition function $\mathcal{Z}(p,y,\bar y)=\sum_N p^N Z_N$ at four towers of simple poles accumulating on an essential-singularity wall $|p|=|y^{-1}\bar y^{-1}|$. This is the [[grand-canonical-residue-expansion]].

3. **Alternating signs from negative BPS modes.** The K3 orbifold determinant has bosonic negative modes ($n<k$ in the $\mathbb{Z}_k$-twisted sector) with negative $h,j$, producing an overall $(-1)^{k-1}$ via
$$\frac{1}{\prod_{n=1}^{k-1}\left(1-y^{\frac nk-1}\bar y^{\frac nk-1}\right)}=\frac{(-1)^{k-1}\,y^{\frac{k-1}{2}}\bar y^{\frac{k-1}{2}}}{\prod_{n=1}^{k-1}\left(1-y^{1-\frac nk}\bar y^{1-\frac nk}\right)}.$$
The alternating infinite sum collapses by large cancellations to the finite polynomial $Z_N$ — a bulk realization of the [[stringy-exclusion-principle]]. The subtracted ($k>1$) states are exactly the [[finite-n-null-states|finite-$N$ null states]] of $\mathrm{Sym}^N(\mathcal{M}_4)$ (checked operator-by-operator at $N=1$).

4. **One-loop staggering of the orbifold ground-state charge.** The classical charge of $(\mathrm{AdS}_3\times\mathrm{S}^3)/\mathbb{Z}_k$, $h=\bar h=j=\bar j=\tfrac N2(1-\tfrac1k)$, is quantum-corrected (K3) to
$$h=\bar h=j=\bar j=\frac N2\left(1-\frac1k\right)+\frac14(k-1),$$
resolving the $k\to\infty$ accumulation point and pushing orbifolds with $k\gtrsim\sqrt{2N}$ past the chiral-primary black-hole charge.

5. **Stokes-sector classification.** The set $\mathcal{S}_{y,\bar y}$ of contributing pole towers jumps discontinuously with the fugacities; e.g. $|\bar y|<|y|\le1$ gives $\mathcal{S}=\{(0,0),(2,0)\}$ while $|y|=|\bar y|<1$ gives $\mathcal{S}=\{(0,0)\}$. Different infinite subsets of spectrally-flowed orbifold saddles dominate the path integral in different fugacity regions — see [[stokes-sectors-partition-function]].

6. **Bulk duals of null states.** Path-integral quantization of BPS fluctuations of the flowed orbifold geometries (with Wick-rotated contours for the negative modes) yields bulk states dual to the chiral primaries that become null at finite $N$ — the AdS$_3$ analog of giant-graviton branes encoding trace relations. Matching requires an extra $\mathbb{Z}_k$ **Gauss constraint** beyond the worldsheet orbifold projection.

## Methods

- **DMVV / second-quantized string.** The grand-canonical partition function of $\mathrm{Sym}^N(\mathcal{M}_4)$ is an infinite product over seed degeneracies; poles and residues in $p$ are read off and matched to bulk saddles ([[dmvv-formula]]).
- **Grand-canonical contour analysis.** $Z_N=\oint\frac{dp}{2\pi i}\,p^{-N-1}\mathcal{Z}$; poles accumulate on a wall of essential singularities, so a naive residue theorem fails. A cutoff-$M$ regularization, then $M\to\infty$, fixes which towers contribute — this is what the Stokes sectors encode ([[grand-canonical-residue-expansion]]).
- **$\mathcal{N}=(4,4)$ spectral flow.** The automorphism $\sigma^\eta$: $L_0\to L_0+\eta J_0+\tfrac{c}{24}\eta^2$, $J_0\to J_0+\tfrac{c}{12}\eta$, used both in the CFT (building chiral primaries from R ground states, $\eta=\bar\eta=-1$) and in the bulk (large gauge transformations of $SU(1,1|2)^2$ Chern–Simons supergravity; only $(\eta,\bar\eta)\in\{(0,0),(2,0),(0,2),(2,2)\}$ survive $q,\bar q\to0$).
- **Tensionless worldsheet CFT.** The [[tensionless-string-ads3|$\mathfrak{psu}(1,1|2)_1$ free-field string]]; fractional worldsheet spectral flow $w=n/k$ reproduces the orbifold BPS spectra.
- **Chern–Simons supergravity / conical defects.** 3d reduction to asymptotically-AdS$_3$ metrics with deficit angle $2\pi(1-1/k)$ plus $SU(2)\times SU(2)$ gauge fields; Brown–Henneaux charges fix the NS chiral-primary condition.
- **Explicit state counting.** Finite-$N$ null states written as $S_N$-invariant combinations of chiral-primary oscillators and twist operators, matched term-by-term against $\sum_{k\ge2}\widehat Z_k^{(0,0)}$.

## Conventions

- Lorentzian, mostly-plus signature; $c=6N$, seed $c_L=c_R=6$; $G_N^{(3)}=1/(4N)$, $c=3/(2G_3)$ (Brown–Henneaux).
- $N=Q_1Q_5$ ($\mathbb{T}^4$), $N=Q_1Q_5+1$ (K3); K3 has 20 "middle" multiplets vs 4 for $\mathbb{T}^4$, plus corner Hodge numbers $h^{0,0}=h^{2,0}=h^{0,2}=h^{2,2}=1$.
- Fugacities $y,\bar y$ couple to R-charges via $y^{2J_0}\bar y^{2\bar J_0}$; grand-canonical fugacity $p$ ($p^N$); $q,\bar q$ couple to $L_0-J_0,\bar L_0-\bar J_0$; the chiral-primary projection is $q,\bar q\to0$.
- Chiral-primary partition function $Z_N=\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathcal{H}_{cc}}\!\big(y^{2J_0}\bar y^{2\bar J_0}\big)=\lim_{q,\bar q\to0}\mathrm{Tr}\big(q^{L_0-J_0}\bar q^{\bar L_0-\bar J_0}y^{2J_0}\bar y^{2\bar J_0}\big)$, with $J_0:=J^3_0$.
- $\varepsilon=+1$ partition function, $\varepsilon=-1$ index, $(-1)^F=e^{2\pi i(J_0+\bar J_0)}$. For $\mathbb{T}^4$, $\varepsilon$ is kept abstract to regularize fermionic negative modes.
- Worldsheet $\mathfrak{psu}(1,1|2)_{\mathsf k}$ at minimal level $\mathsf k=1$ (since $\ell^2_{\mathrm{AdS}_3}/\alpha'=Q_5=1$); charge map $\mathcal{J}^3_0=L_0$, $\mathcal{K}^3_0=J_0$.
- NS chiral-primary conditions $h-j=\bar h-\bar j=0$; black-hole/cosmic-censorship threshold $4N(L_0-J_0)-(2J_0-N)^2\ge0$ (and the right-moving analog).

## Key equations

- Large-$N$ (KK) spectrum, K3: $\displaystyle Z_\infty=\prod_{n\ge1}\Big[(1-y^{n+1}\bar y^{n-1})(1-y^{n-1}\bar y^{n+1})(1-y^{n+1}\bar y^{n+1})(1-y^n\bar y^n)^{21}\Big]^{-1}$.
- Unitarity/exclusion bound: $h,\bar h,j,\bar j\le c/6=N$.
- Finite-$N$ palindromes (K3): $Z_1=1+22y^2+y^4$, $Z_2=1+23y^2+276y^4+23y^6+y^8$.
- Main proposal: $\displaystyle Z_N(y,\bar y)=\sum_{\mu\in\mathcal{S}_{y,\bar y}}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\widehat Z_k^{\,\mu}(y,\bar y)$.
- NS grand-canonical partition function: $\displaystyle \mathcal{Z}^{\rm NS}(p;y,\bar y)=\prod_{n\ge1}\prod_{r,s=0}^{2}\Big(1-(-\varepsilon)^{r+s}p^n y^{n+r-1}\bar y^{n+s-1}\Big)^{-(-1)^{r+s}h^{r,s}}$.
- Pole towers: $(0/2,0/2)_k^m:\ p=e^{2\pi i m/k}\,y^{-(1\mp1/k)}\bar y^{-(1\mp1/k)}$.
- Quantum-corrected orbifold charge: $h=\bar h=j=\bar j=\tfrac N2(1-\tfrac1k)+\tfrac14(k-1)$.
- $\bar y=0$ collapse ($\mathbb{T}^4$): $Z_N(y,0)=\widehat Z_1^{(0,0)}(y,0)+\widehat Z_1^{(2,0)}(y,0)$ with $\widehat Z_1^{(2,0)}(y,0)=-y^{2N}\frac{(1+\varepsilon y)^2}{1-y^2}$.

## Open questions raised

1. Derive the $\widehat Z_k^{\,\mu}$ directly from the Euclidean gravitational path integral (one-loop exactness, contour treatment of negative modes, saddle origin of the Gauss constraint).
2. Extend to $\tfrac14$-BPS states across the black-hole threshold (elliptic genus of $\mathrm{Sym}^N$K3); connect to the Farey-tail / Rademacher expansion.
3. Relate the construction to "fortuity" in the D1–D5 system.
4. Understand the orbifold partition functions as branes in $\mathrm{AdS}_3\times\mathrm{S}^3$ (true giant gravitons).
5. Use the analytic structure of grand-canonical partition functions as a general organizing principle for sums over gravitational saddles.
6. Generalize to large $\mathcal{N}=4$, e.g. strings on $\mathrm{AdS}_3\times\mathrm{S}^3\times\mathrm{S}^3\times\mathrm{S}^1$.
7. Whether the IIB ensemble on $\mathrm{AdS}_3\times\mathcal{M}_4$ changes abruptly across moduli space.
