## TL;DR

Euclidean scalar wormholes exist only when the scalar is displaced by a fixed *imaginary*
amount in moduli space. Reading scalar VEVs as coupling constants, this fixes an absolute
limit — the **Imaginary Distance Bound (IDB)**, $\tau_{\rm IDB}=\tfrac{\pi}{2}\sqrt{2(D-1)/(D-2)}$
— on how far couplings can be analytically continued in the imaginary direction before the
low-energy gravity description must break down. In UV-complete string examples the theory
indeed fails at or before this bound (via $D(-1)$/worldsheet instantons whose action vanishes),
and the IDB is argued to be a single principle underlying both the
[[weak-gravity-conjecture|weak gravity conjecture]] and the
[[kontsevich-segal-witten-criterion|Kontsevich-Segal-Witten criterion]].

## Motivation

Spacetime wormholes drive much of recent quantum gravity, but their interpretation as an
average over couplings (Coleman; [[1903.11115|Saad-Shenker-Stanford 2019]]) clashes with the
absence of fundamental free couplings in string theory. The simplest scalar wormholes require
the scalar to take imaginary values, flipping the sign of its stress tensor. Taking these
imaginary scalars seriously — as complexified coupling constants — the paper asks how far a
consistent quantum-gravity theory can be continued in its couplings before it stops making
sense, and whether wormholes mark that boundary.

## Main results

1. **Scalar wormholes require a fixed imaginary displacement.** For $D$-dimensional Einstein
   gravity plus a canonically normalized massless scalar $\varphi=i\tau(a)$ with metric
   $ds^2=da^2/F(a)+a^2 d\Omega_{D-1}^2$, the flat-space (Giddings-Strominger) solution has
   $F=1-(a_0/a)^{2(D-2)}$, and the total imaginary displacement across the wormhole is
   size-independent: $\Delta\tau_\infty=\pi\sqrt{2(D-1)/(D-2)}$, with on-shell action $I=0$.

2. **The Imaginary Distance Bound (flat space).** From a real point in moduli space one can
   continue at most half the wormhole displacement, $\tau\le\tau_{\rm IDB}=\tfrac{\pi}{2}
   \sqrt{2(D-1)/(D-2)}$. Beyond it, $Z(p+i\tau)Z(p-i\tau)$ receives arbitrary-size
   zero-action wormhole contributions and diverges. See [[imaginary-distance-bound]].

3. **AdS IDB.** For AdS$_D$ with flat slicing, zero-action wormholes appear at a smaller
   displacement, $\tau\le\tau'_{\rm IDB}=\tfrac{\pi}{2}\sqrt{2(D-2)/(D-1)}$; the general
   spherical solution interpolates between the two.

4. **Central conjecture.** In a UV-complete quantum-gravity theory some effect always renders
   the low-energy approximation invalid at or before $\tau_{\rm IDB}$ as couplings are made
   imaginary — physically, corrections $c_n e^{\pm i n\varphi}$ that are small for real
   $\varphi$ but blow up when $\varphi=i\tau$.

5. **KK reduction = Schwarzschild = SSS double cone.** Reducing $(D+1)$-dimensional gravity on
   $S^1$ gives a non-compact scalar $\nu=\log\tilde r$ with $\hat\kappa^2=2(D-1)/(D-2)$; the
   scalar wormhole is the higher-dimensional Schwarzschild solution on a complex-$r$ contour,
   matching the [[spectral-form-factor|Saad-Shenker-Stanford double cone]]. The IDB becomes
   $|\mathrm{Im}\,\nu|\le\pi/2$, coinciding with the KSW criterion for the circle metric
   component.

6. **Torus compactification: IDB $\equiv$ KSW exactly.** For $T^N$ reduction, the longest
   KSW-allowed timelike geodesic in the internal-metric moduli space (eigenphases $\theta_i$,
   $\sum_i|\theta_i|<\pi$) gives $\tau^2<\tfrac{\pi^2}{4}\tfrac{2(D-1)}{D-2}=\tau_{\rm IDB}^2$,
   matching the flat-space bound. The IDB is thus a generalization of KSW to all moduli, not
   just the metric.

7. **IDB $\supset$ WGC.** Reducing Einstein-Maxwell on $S^1$ gives moduli $(\nu,\tilde\alpha)$
   on hyperbolic space of radius $\hat\kappa$; a wrapped charged particle's action vanishes on
   the extremal lines exactly when $m=\hat\kappa q$. Demanding a state with
   $|q|/m>\sqrt{(D-2)/2(D-1)}$ to violate the wormhole reproduces the WGC, and with several
   gauge fields the [[convex-hull-condition|convex hull condition]].

8. **String breakdown before the bound (type IIB).** On the axio-dilaton moduli space,
   $D(-1)$-instanton actions $I_\pm=1/g_s\pm iC_0$ vanish on null lines reached at imaginary
   distance $\pi/2<\tau_{\rm IDB}$, and the $R^4$ coefficient $\mathcal E_{3/2}$ (an
   $SL(2,\mathbb Z)$ Eisenstein series) diverges there.

9. **$T^4$ "safe" wormholes still fail before the bound.** The Arkani-Hamed-Orgera-Polchinski
   multi-field wormholes avoid the simple instanton lines, yet a specific worldsheet instanton
   has action $I=4\pi(1-\tan(\tau/2))$ vanishing at $\tau=\pi/2$; momentum/winding states become
   tachyonic ($M^2=4w^2\cos\tau$), and at the AdS$_3\times S^3\times T^4$ symmetric-orbifold
   point they correspond to CFT states crossing the unitarity bound.

## Methods

- **Euclidean (Dirichlet) wormhole construction:** fix scalar VEVs at both ends, solve the
  Einstein-scalar equations with an imaginary scalar profile; the trace of the Einstein
  equations plus scale invariance forces the flat on-shell action to vanish.
- **Moduli-space geodesics:** multi-field wormholes trace complexified geodesics through the
  sigma-model target; the Poincaré-patch geodesic length controls the bound.
- **Kaluza-Klein reduction** on $S^1$ and $T^N$, identifying lower-dimensional scalars with
  metric moduli to map wormholes to black holes and the double cone. See
  [[kaluza-klein-reduction-wormholes]].
- **KSW analysis:** diagonalize the complex internal metric, apply $\sum_i|\theta_i|<\pi$.
- **Legendre/Fourier transform** between Dirichlet (fixed VEV) and fixed-charge ensembles.
- **String instanton accounting:** $D(-1)$ instantons, $R^4$ Eisenstein series, worldsheet
  instantons, and one-loop Narain-lattice modular integrals.

## Conventions

$D$ = full spacetime dimension. Action (their eq. for $I$):
$$I=\frac{1}{16\pi G_N}\int d^Dx\sqrt{g}\Big[-(R-2\Lambda)+\tfrac12 h_{ab}(\varphi)\,\nabla\varphi^a\nabla\varphi^b\Big],$$
with $h_{ab}$ the Zamolodchikov / moduli-space metric on couplings. A canonically normalized
scalar has $h=1$; the wormhole scalar is $\varphi=i\tau$ with $\tau$ real. The KK reduction
uses $\hat\kappa^2=2(D-1)/(D-2)$ for the radion kinetic normalization. No translation to a
wiki-canonical convention is needed — these match standard swampland/KSW usage.

## Key equations

- Flat wormhole displacement: $\Delta\tau_\infty=\pi\sqrt{2(D-1)/(D-2)}$, on-shell $I=0$.
- Flat IDB: $\tau_{\rm IDB}=\tfrac{\pi}{2}\sqrt{2(D-1)/(D-2)}=\Delta\tau_\infty/2$.
- AdS IDB: $\tau'_{\rm IDB}=\tfrac{\pi}{2}\sqrt{2(D-2)/(D-1)}$.
- KSW criterion: $\sum_{i=1}^D\big|\mathrm{Arg}\,\lambda_i\big|<\pi$ for complex-metric
  eigenvalues $\lambda_i$; KSW $\to$ IDB match $\tau^2<\tfrac{\pi^2}{4}\tfrac{2(D-1)}{D-2}$.
- WGC coincidence: wrapped-particle action vanishes on the extremal lines when
  $m=\hat\kappa q$, giving the bound $|q|/m>\sqrt{(D-2)/2(D-1)}$.
- Type IIB: $I_\pm=1/g_s\pm iC_0$; $\mathcal E_{3/2}(\mathcal T,\bar{\mathcal T})=
  \sum_{(m,n)\neq0}\big[\mathcal T_2/((m+n\mathcal T)(m+n\bar{\mathcal T}))\big]^{3/2}$.
- $T^4$ worldsheet instanton: $I=2\pi\sqrt{(w\!\cdot\!w)(\tilde w\!\cdot\!\tilde w)-(w\!\cdot\!\tilde w)^2}+2\pi i\,B_{ij}w^i\tilde w^j$; special case $I=4\pi(1-\tan(\tau/2))$.
- Vafa-Witten K3: $Z(\mathrm{K3})=1/\eta(q)^{26}$, singular at $|q|=1$.

## Open questions raised

- Does the wormhole actually contribute to the gravitational path integral? Held et al. argue
  no for these imaginary-scalar wormholes, which would also threaten the double cone — the
  integration contour is unknown. See [[factorization-vs-wormholes]].
- Is there a single principle from which KSW, WGC and IDB all follow?
- A duality-invariant generalization of KSW including all fields (metric, form fields,
  non-geometric moduli).
- Precise relation between the imaginary-distance IDB and the real-distance
  [[swampland-program|swampland distance conjecture]].
- For Calabi-Yau with hundreds of moduli, does breakdown still occur before the IDB?
- Do IDB-type bounds hold for general non-holographic CFTs (e.g. QCD at imaginary $\theta$)?
