## TL;DR

At strong coupling, all four-point functions of two maximal [[giant-graviton|giant gravitons]] (heavy $\tfrac12$-BPS determinant operators, $\Delta=N$) and two arbitrary light $\tfrac12$-BPS single-trace operators $\mathcal{O}_k$ in 4d $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM are computed by reinterpreting them as two-point functions of the light operators in the presence of a zero-dimensional ($p=0$) conformal defect — the [[giant-graviton-defect|geodesic traced by the giant graviton]] in AdS$_5$. The correlators are fixed up to one overall constant by the [[analytic-defect-bootstrap|analytic defect bootstrap]] plus supersymmetry, and all infinitely many of them collapse into a single generating function via a defect version of the [[ten-dimensional-hidden-conformal-symmetry|10d hidden conformal symmetry]], which also appears to organize the weak-coupling loop integrands.

## Motivation

Correlators of heavy operators are far less understood than those of light operators. For two giant gravitons and one light operator, symmetry fixes the three-point function up to a constant, so nothing dynamical is learned; for two giant gravitons and two light operators the functional form is unconstrained and was essentially unknown at strong coupling (only an integrated-correlator localization prediction existed for the stress-tensor multiplet case). The key conceptual move — that these heavy-heavy-light-light four-point functions equal light-light two-point functions in the background of a $p=0$ defect — imports both the mature four-point superconformal kinematics and the defect-CFT/Witten-diagram bootstrap machinery, making the strong-coupling computation tractable.

## Historical context

Giant gravitons entered as heavy BPS D3-brane states~[[hep-th-9711200|Maldacena 1997]] with the determinant/sub-determinant dictionary developed shortly after (McGreevy–Schwarz–Verlinde 2000; Balasubramanian–Berkooz–Naqvi–Strassler 2001; Hashimoto–Hirano–Itzhaki 2000), and their vibration spectrum computed by Das–Jevicki–Mathur 2000. Determinant-operator correlators were known at weak coupling and via integrability: three-point functions (Jiang–Komatsu–Vescovi 2019) and four-point functions to three loops, with an integrated-correlator localization prediction from Brown–Wen–Xie 2024. On the technical side the work rests on the Rastelli–Zhou Mellin/analytic holographic-correlator bootstrap (2016–2017), the Witten-diagram bootstrap for holographic defects (Giménez-Grau et al., arXiv:2306.11896), the $p=-1$ ($\mathcal{N}=4$ on $\mathbb{RP}^4$) defect correlators of Zhou 2024, the four-point superconformal kinematics of Eden et al. 2000 and Nirschl–Osborn 2004, and the 10d hidden conformal symmetry of Caron-Huot–Trinh 2018.

## Main results

**1. Defect reformulation.** Dividing the four-point function by the giant-graviton two-point function recasts it as a defect two-point function of the light operators:
$$G_{k_1k_2}\equiv\langle\!\langle\mathcal{O}_{k_1}\mathcal{O}_{k_2}\rangle\!\rangle=\frac{\langle\mathcal{O}_{k_1}\mathcal{O}_{k_2}\mathcal{D}\mathcal{D}\rangle}{\langle\mathcal{D}\mathcal{D}\rangle},\qquad G_{k_1k_2}=G_{k_1k_2,\mathrm{free}}+\frac{t_{12}^2\,x_{13}^4x_{24}^4}{x_{34}^4}\,R\,H_{k_1k_2}.$$

**2. All tree-level correlators fixed.** A supergravity exchange spectrum plus the analytic defect bootstrap, with the superconformal Ward identity imposed, fixes every $G_{k_1k_2}$ up to one overall constant (set by the D3-brane action normalization). The lowest reduced correlator ($k_1=k_2=2$) is
$$H_{22}=\frac{2V\,(V^2-2V\log V-1)}{N\,(P_1\!\cdot\!\mathbb{N}\!\cdot\!P_1)^2(P_2\!\cdot\!\mathbb{N}\!\cdot\!P_2)^2\,U\,(1-V)^3}.$$

**3. Hidden higher-dimensional symmetry / generating function.** Promoting each light insertion to a twelve-component vector $Z_i=(P_i,t_i)$ and applying the replacement rules $P_1\!\cdot\!P_2\to Z_1\!\cdot\!Z_2$ and $P_a\!\cdot\!\mathbb{N}\!\cdot\!P_b\to Z_a\!\cdot\!(\mathbb{N}+\mathbb{M})\!\cdot\!Z_b$ (for $a,b=1,2$) turns the single seed $H_{22}$ into a generating function $\mathbf{H}$ for all others:
$$H_{k_1k_2}=\frac{\sqrt{k_1k_2}}{2}\,\mathbf{H}(x_i;t_i;\lambda_1,\lambda_2)\big|_{\lambda_1^{k_1-2}\lambda_2^{k_2-2}}.$$
This is a defect generalization of the Caron-Huot–Trinh 10d symmetry: the giant-graviton effect is encoded by uplifting the spacetime projector $\mathbb{N}$ into $\mathbb{N}+\mathbb{M}$, while the light points 1,2 alone are uplifted.

**4. Weak-coupling conjecture.** An enlarged replacement rule promotes the $k_i=2$ loop integrand into a generating function for all $I_{k_1k_2}^{(\ell)}$; verified explicitly at one and two loops against independent Feynman-diagram computations of $I_{pp}^{(\ell)}$.

**5. Consistency checks.** Free-theory results match prior determinant-operator computations (up to a $U(N)$-vs-$SU(N)$ one-point-function term); extracted bulk one-point functions match a direct supergravity computation; and the $k_1=k_2=2$ integrated correlator matches the Brown–Wen–Xie 2024 localization prediction.

## Methods

- **Defect-CFT reinterpretation**: dividing by $\langle\mathcal{D}\mathcal{D}\rangle$ recasts the four-point function as a defect two-point function; embedding-space projectors $\mathbb{N}$ (spacetime, breaking $SO(5,1)\times SO(1,1)\to SO(4)$) and $\mathbb{M}$ (R-symmetry, breaking $SO(6)_R\to SO(4)\times SO(2)$) organize the invariants and one-point functions.
- **D3-brane fluctuation analysis**: expanding the D3-brane DBI+WZ action about the maximal giant solution and integrating over the wrapped $S^3$ yields a 1d worldline Lagrangian of a probe particle minimally coupled to bulk supergravity fields, giving the defect spectrum and bulk-defect vertices.
- **[[analytic-defect-bootstrap|Analytic defect bootstrap]]**: an ansatz of exchange and contact Witten diagrams times R-symmetry polynomials, with exchanges reduced to finitely many contacts and coefficients fixed by the superconformal Ward identity ($\bar\alpha=1/\bar z$ independence).
- **Hidden-symmetry uplift** to package all correlators and (conjecturally) all weak-coupling loop integrands.

## Conventions

Euclidean CFT$_4$ uplifted to embedding space; null vectors $P_i$ (spacetime) and $t_i$ (R-symmetry, $t\cdot t=0$). Gauge group $SU(N)$ in the bootstrap; the free-theory reference uses $U(N)$ (source of the extra one-point-function term in the consistency check). Cross ratios $U=z\bar z=\frac{x_{12}^2x_{34}^2}{x_{13}^2x_{24}^2}$, $V=(1-z)(1-\bar z)=\frac{x_{14}^2x_{23}^2}{x_{13}^2x_{24}^2}$, $\sigma=\alpha\bar\alpha=\frac{t_{13}t_{24}}{t_{12}t_{34}}$, $\tau=(1-\alpha)(1-\bar\alpha)=\frac{t_{14}t_{23}}{t_{12}t_{34}}$. Superconformal factor $R=(1-z\alpha)(1-z\bar\alpha)(1-\bar z\alpha)(1-\bar z\bar\alpha)$. Common $AdS_5$/$S^5$ radius set to $1$.

## Key equations

- Operators: $\mathcal{D}(x,t)=\det(\Phi^I(x)t^I)$ (maximal giant, $\Delta=N$, rep $[0,N,0]$); $\mathcal{O}_k(x,t)=\mathcal{N}_k\,\mathrm{tr}(\Phi^I(x)t^I)^k$ ($\Delta=k$, rep $[0,k,0]$).
- Defect projectors: $\mathbb{N}_{AB}=\dfrac{P_{3,A}P_{4,B}+P_{4,A}P_{3,B}}{P_3\cdot P_4}$, $\quad\mathbb{M}_{IJ}=\delta_{IJ}-\dfrac{t_{3,I}t_{4,J}+t_{4,I}t_{3,J}}{t_3\cdot t_4}$.
- D3-brane action: $S_{\rm D3}=-\dfrac{N}{2\pi^2}\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}\times S^3}\!\big(\sqrt{\det g^{\rm P.B.}_{\alpha\beta}}-C^{\rm P.B.}_4\big)$.
- Seed reduced correlator $H_{22}$ (above) and hidden-symmetry replacement $P_a\!\cdot\!\mathbb{N}\!\cdot\!P_b\to Z_a\!\cdot\!(\mathbb{N}+\mathbb{M})\!\cdot\!Z_b$.
- Basic $p=0$ contact Witten diagram: $\mathcal{C}^{(0)}_{\Delta_1\Delta_2}=\dfrac{\pi^{1/2}\Gamma(\frac{\Delta_1+\Delta_2}{2})}{2^{\Delta_1+\Delta_2}\Gamma(\frac{\Delta_1+\Delta_2+1}{2})}\,{}_2F_1\!\Big(\Delta_1,\Delta_2,\tfrac{\Delta_1+\Delta_2+1}{2},-\tfrac{(1-\sqrt V)^2}{4\sqrt V}\Big)$ — the building block onto which all exchanges reduce.

## Open questions raised

- Extend to non-maximal (and dual) giant gravitons: does the hidden structure persist, and how do correlators depend on giant size?
- [[four-giant-graviton-correlators|Four giant-graviton correlators]] (two D3-branes exchanging supergravity), expected to exhibit phase transitions.
- Prove/extend the weak-coupling hidden-symmetry conjecture to general weights and higher loops; build AdS loop and stringy corrections.
- Analogues in AdS$_4$/CFT$_3$ (ABJM) and AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$; integrability interface (giant-graviton OPE coefficients as boundary-state overlaps / excited-state $g$-functions).
