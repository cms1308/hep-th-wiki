## TL;DR

Six lectures from Pre-Strings 2026 in three parts. **Day 1** develops neural networks through a
field-theoretic lens (expressivity, statistics, dynamics), with an ensemble of randomly initialized
networks read as a Euclidean field theory. **Day 2** defines a field theory directly from an
architecture $\phi_\theta$ and a parameter density $P(\theta)$ — the
[[neural-network-field-theory|NN-FT]] program — and surveys recent results: a
[[nn-ft-universality-theorem|universality theorem]], a neural-network realization of
[[liouville-from-neural-networks|Liouville theory]] matching DOZZ, the
[[string-amplitudes-from-neural-networks|Virasoro–Shapiro and Veneziano amplitudes]] from parameter
integrals, [[topological-sectors-nn-ft|topological sectors and the BKT transition]] via discrete
latents, a [[parameter-space-ward-identities|parameter-space Ward-identity/anomaly formalism]], and a
[[critical-dimension-from-mode-counting-nn-ft|new derivation of $D=26$]] from counting network modes.
**Day 3** turns the lens around to applied AI for string theory: agentic reasoning models, PINNs for
Calabi–Yau metrics, reinforcement learning and search in the landscape and in knot theory, and
interpretable ML for conjecture generation.

## Motivation

Machine-learning practice has outrun ML theory, and a theoretical physicist's toolkit — field
theory, ensemble statistics, symmetry, large-$N$ expansions, gradient dynamics — is proposed as the
natural language for an $O(1)$ mechanistic understanding. Reciprocally, the observation that a
network *ensemble* is literally a field theory (its moments are correlation functions) suggests a new
way to *define* field theories: not by an action, but by an architecture plus a parameter density.
The third thread is practical: in the author's estimate, agentic reasoning models have dropped the
implementation cost of ML-for-physics by roughly an order of magnitude, changing what a single
researcher can attempt — several Day 2 results were produced with AI-assisted workflows, humans
owning correctness. This is the Pre-Strings follow-on to the earlier
[[2408.00082|TASI lectures]], reusing the Day-1 field-theory foundations and extending into new NN-FT
physics and the applied-AI survey.

## Historical context

- **Approximation theory** — Cybenko's [[universal-approximation-theorem|universal approximation]]
  (1989) and Hornik (1991); the Kolmogorov–Arnold representation theorem (1957), revived as
  [[kolmogorov-arnold-network|KANs]] (Liu et al. 2024).
- **Transformers / reasoning** — attention (Vaswani et al. 2017); transformer universality
  (Yun et al. 2020); chain-of-thought (Wei et al. 2022) and reasoning models (OpenAI o1 2024,
  DeepSeek R1 2025).
- **Ensembles as field theory** — Neal's [[nngp-correspondence|NNGP correspondence]] (1990s); the
  [[neural-tangent-kernel|neural tangent kernel]] (Jacot et al. 2018; Lee et al. 2019);
  [[feature-learning|feature learning]] and [[maximal-update-parameterization|$\mu P$]]
  (Yang 2020; Bordelon–Pehlevan).
- **NN-FT lineage** — Halverson–Maiti–Stoner (2021), Demirtas et al. (2023), and the 2024–2026 wave
  (conformal symmetry, Virasoro, fermions/SUSY, RG flow, universality, Liouville, bosonic string,
  BKT, anomalies).
- **Constructive / probabilistic QFT** — Osterwalder–Schrader reconstruction (1973); the
  probabilistic construction of Liouville theory (David–Kupiainen–Rhodes–Vargas 2016) and Gaussian
  multiplicative chaos; DOZZ (Dorn–Otto 1994; Zamolodchikov–Zamolodchikov 1995).
- **Applied AI for strings** — opened by He, Krefl–Seong, Ruehle, Carifio–Halverson–Krioukov–Nelson
  (2017); numerical Calabi–Yau metrics by NN (Anderson et al.; Douglas et al.; Jejjala et al. 2020;
  Larfors et al. 2022, `cymetric`); RL in the landscape (Halverson–Nakajima–Ruehle 2019);
  RL/rigorous ML on knots (Gukov et al. 2020–2025); DeepMind's guided-intuition knot result
  (Davies et al. 2021).

## Main results

Organized by the lectures' three-day arc.

**Day 1 — Neural network essentials.**
- Networks studied through three pillars — expressivity, statistics, dynamics — with the *ensemble*
  the fundamental object; its mean and second moment are the one- and two-point functions
  $G^{(1)}(x)=\langle\phi(x)\rangle$, $G^{(2)}(x,y)=\langle\phi(x)\phi(y)\rangle$.
- Expressivity: [[universal-approximation-theorem|UAT]], Kolmogorov–Arnold representation, transformer
  universality, and "reasoning is expressivity" — chain-of-thought buys serial steps a fixed-depth
  transformer lacks.
- [[nngp-correspondence|NNGP correspondence]]: a width-$N$ network is a sum of i.i.d. functions, so by
  the CLT it tends to a Gaussian process (generalized free field); connected $2n$-point functions
  scale as $G^{(2n)}_c\propto N^{-(n-1)}$, so finite width $=$ interactions.
- Dynamics: gradient descent is governed by the [[neural-tangent-kernel|NTK]]; at $N\to\infty$ it
  freezes to a deterministic kernel and MSE training becomes exactly solvable kernel regression, with
  no [[feature-learning|feature learning]]. An $N$-scaling analysis fixes
  [[maximal-update-parameterization|$\mu P$]] ($c=1,d=\tfrac12$) as the unique feature-learning scaling.

**Day 2 — Neural network field theory.** (see [[neural-network-field-theory]])
- **[[nn-ft-universality-theorem|Universality]]**: any probability distribution over tempered
  distributions $\mathcal S'(\mathbb R^d)$ — hence any constructive Euclidean QFT — admits an NN-FT
  description with countably many parameters (Borel isomorphism theorem).
- **[[liouville-from-neural-networks|Liouville theory]]**: zero mode + real spherical harmonics on
  $S^2$; all interaction loaded onto the zero mode via a deformed conditional density involving
  Gaussian multiplicative chaos. Monte Carlo in parameter space at $L=30$ reproduces the DOZZ
  structure constant to the percent level.
- **[[string-amplitudes-from-neural-networks|Bosonic string]]**: the gauge-fixed Polyakov boson is a
  random-Fourier-feature ensemble with propagator $2\pi\alpha'\delta^{\mu\nu}/p^2$; Grassmann output
  weights supply the $bc$-ghost propagator. The four-tachyon amplitude is an analytic
  finite-dimensional parameter integral assembling the Koba–Nielsen factor into the Virasoro–Shapiro
  amplitude; an image trick gives open strings/Veneziano.
- **[[topological-sectors-nn-ft|Topological sectors / BKT]]**: enlarging the latent space with
  discrete parameters $Q$ reproduces the full BKT phenomenology of the 2d XY model
  ($\eta=b^2$, $b_c=\tfrac12$, $T_c=\tfrac\pi2$, essential-singularity $\xi$, vortex proliferation,
  Nelson–Kosterlitz stiffness jump).
- **[[parameter-space-ward-identities|Ward identities and anomalies]]**: a master identity
  $\langle\sum_a\xi^a\partial_a\mathcal O\rangle=-\langle B(\theta)\mathcal O\rangle$ with breaking
  function $B=\text{score}+\text{Jacobian}$; reproduces the $\phi^4$ one-loop $\beta=3\lambda^2/16\pi^2$.
- **[[critical-dimension-from-mode-counting-nn-ft|Critical dimension]]**: a Weyl flow on $S^2$ leaves
  only the Jacobian (a pure mode count); zeta-regularizing gives $B^{\rm reg}=-\tfrac13(D-26)$, so
  Weyl invariance of the ensemble forces $D=26$.

**Day 3 — Applied AI for strings.** (see [[machine-learning-in-string-theory]])
- **Agents** in a think–act–observe loop (Get Physics Done) lowered the implementation barrier for
  ML-for-strings by ~an order of magnitude, with warnings (plausible $\ne$ correct, verify results,
  hallucinated scholarship, "you are the physicist").
- **PINNs / Calabi–Yau metrics**: solve the complex Monge–Ampère equation via a Kähler-potential
  correction $J_{\rm CY}=J_{\rm ref}+\partial\bar\partial\phi_\theta$ (`cymetric`); no compact
  full-$SU(3)$-holonomy CY metric is known analytically. Ricci flow is a special infinite-width NN
  metric flow (frozen kernel + locality + Perelman functional as loss).
- **RL and search**: deep RL (A3C) on type IIA intersecting-D6-brane orientifolds finds consistent,
  SM-like vacua up to $O(200)\times$ faster than random, rediscovering and inventing strategies.
  UNKNOT recast as braid-word classification; TRPO agents produce human-checkable unknotting
  certificates; applied to SPC4, RL/Bayesian optimization eliminated all but two of 3375 candidate
  counterexample pairs.
- **Conjecture generation**: the data→model→interpret→conjecture→proof loop produced a proved
  conjecture for when an $E_6$ sector arises in the F-theory ensemble, and (DeepMind) the
  knot-signature/natural-slope relation; unknot networks spontaneously encode the Jones-polynomial
  degree.

## Methods

- **Field theory of ensembles**: moments = correlators; parameter-space partition function
  $Z[J]=\int d\theta\,P(\theta)\,e^{\int J\phi_\theta}$ dual to the function-space path integral.
- **CLT in function space + Williams' trick**: Gaussianity (NNGP) at $N\to\infty$; correlators
  computed directly in parameter space by factorizing i.i.d. expectations.
- **Density deformation**: $\tilde P(\theta)=P(\theta)e^{-\int V(\phi_\theta)}$ realizes $\phi^4$,
  Liouville (Gaussian multiplicative chaos on the zero mode).
- **Spectrum shaping with random Fourier features**: a Euclidean-invariant input layer tunes the
  propagator (free scalar, worldsheet boson, XY spin waves).
- **Grassmann-valued networks**: Berezin integration over anticommuting output weights realizes $bc$
  ghosts and SUSY.
- **Discrete latents**: $\sum_Q\int d\theta\,P(\theta,Q)$ realizes topological sectors; MCMC over
  vortex insertions/deletions.
- **Score / breaking-function calculus**: integration by parts in parameter space (score
  $s_a=\partial_a\log p$) yields Schwinger–Dyson equations, Ward identities, anomalies; zeta
  regularization of mode counts.
- **Applied ML**: PINNs (PDE-residual surrogate loss), reinforcement learning (policy gradient,
  TRPO/A3C/GRPO), interpretable ML (decision trees, saliency, KANs/symbolic regression, program
  search).

## Conventions

- **Euclidean throughout**: fields $\phi:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R^D$; Lorentzian/quantum content is
  imposed by the Osterwalder–Schrader axioms. Ensemble bracket $\langle\cdot\rangle$ = average over
  parameter draws $\theta\sim P(\theta)$; $t$ denotes training time.
- **Width normalization**: architectures $\phi=\tfrac1{\sqrt N}\sum_i w_i\varphi_i$ with output-weight
  variance $\sim1/N$; connected correlators $G^{(2n)}_c\propto N^{-(n-1)}$.
- **String normalization**: free-boson propagator $G^{(2)}(p)=2\pi\alpha'\delta^{\mu\nu}/p^2$ on the
  annulus, with $\alpha'=\sigma_a^2/(\Lambda^2-\epsilon^2)$ ($\Lambda,\epsilon$ UV/IR regulators,
  $\sigma_a$ the only physical dial). Liouville: $Q=b+1/b$, $c_L=1+6Q^2$, $\Delta_\alpha=\alpha(Q-\alpha)$.
- **Anomaly formalism**: score $s_a=\partial_a\log p$; breaking function $B=\text{score}+\text{Jacobian}$;
  the flat measure $d\theta$'s non-invariance is the finite-dimensional avatar of the Fujikawa anomaly.
- These are the same self-contained ML conventions as [[2408.00082|Halverson 2024]] — no overlap with
  the vault's holography conventions.
- Provenance note: the author states the notes were drafted with an AI assistant and human-polished.

## Key equations

- $Z[J]=\int d\theta\,P(\theta)\,e^{\int d^dx\,J(x)\phi_\theta(x)}$ — parameter-space partition
  function; the *definition* of an NN-FT.
- $(\phi_\theta)_*P=P_\phi$ with $\phi_\theta:\mathbb R^{\mathbb N}\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal S'(\mathbb R^d)$
  — the Borel isomorphism underlying [[nn-ft-universality-theorem|universality]].
- DOZZ target
  $C=\big(\pi\mu\gamma(b^2)b^{2-2b^2}\big)^{-s}\dfrac{\Upsilon_b'(0)\prod_i\Upsilon_b(2\alpha_i)}{\Upsilon_b(\sum_i\alpha_i-Q)\prod_i\Upsilon_b(\sum_j\alpha_j-2\alpha_i)}$,
  matched by conditioning the zero mode $P(t\mid a_{\ell m})\propto t^{s-1}e^{-M_X t}$,
  $s=(\sum_i\alpha_i-Q)/b$.
- $X^\mu(z)=\tfrac{C}{\sqrt N}\sum_i\tfrac{a_i^\mu}{|W_i|}\cos\!\big(\tfrac12(W_iz+\bar W_i\bar z)+c_i\big)$,
  giving $\langle X^\mu X^\nu\rangle=\alpha'\delta^{\mu\nu}[-\log r+\cdots]$; $\alpha'=\sigma_a^2/(\Lambda^2-\epsilon^2)$.
- $\mathcal A^{(4)}=\dfrac{g_s^2\,\delta^D(\sum_r p_r)}{\mathrm{Vol}(SL(2,\mathbb C))}\int\big(\prod_\alpha d^2z_\alpha\big)\prod_{r<s}|z_r-z_s|^{\alpha'p_r\cdot p_s}$
  — Virasoro–Shapiro as a parameter-space correlator.
- $\langle e^{ib\theta_{\rm sw}(x)}e^{-ib\theta_{\rm sw}(0)}\rangle\sim|x|^{-\eta}$, $\eta=b^2$;
  $\eta_c=\tfrac14\Rightarrow b_c=\tfrac12,\ T_c=\tfrac\pi2$; jump $\Upsilon_R(T_c^-)=\tfrac2\pi T_c$.
- $\langle\sum_a\xi^a\partial_a\mathcal O\rangle=-\langle B(\theta)\mathcal O\rangle$,
  $B=\sum_a\xi^a s_a+\sum_a\partial_a\xi^a$; $B=0\iff\partial_a(\xi^a p)=0$.
- $B^{\rm reg}_{\rm anom}=-D[\zeta_X(0)+1]+2\zeta_{bc}(0)+12=-\tfrac13(D-26)\Rightarrow D=26$.
- $\dfrac{dg_{ij}}{dt}=-\int_X d\mu(x')\,\Theta_{ijkl}(x,x')\dfrac{\delta\ell(x')}{\delta g_{kl}(x')}$
  — NN metric flow; Ricci flow is the frozen-kernel + local + Perelman-loss corner.
- $|2\sigma(K)-\mathrm{slope}(K)|\le c\,\mathrm{vol}(K)/\mathrm{inj}(K)^3$ — the DeepMind knot theorem
  from [[machine-learning-in-string-theory|conjecture generation]].

## Open questions raised

- Systematic constructions of reflection positivity / cluster decomposition (genuinely quantum
  NN-FTs) beyond examples remain open.
- Whether finite-width interacting NN-FTs give controlled $1/N$ non-Gaussian corrections to string
  amplitudes.
- No compact full-$SU(3)$-holonomy Calabi–Yau metric is known in closed form; numerical metrics
  remain approximate, verified-numerics certificates only partial.
- PINN failure modes (loss balancing, wrong local minima) and pairing with rigorous existence proofs.
- Whether program-search agents can rediscover (or generalize) hand-found landscape structures.
- How reliably agentic pipelines can be made to self-verify, given "plausible $\ne$ correct."
- SPC4 remains open; RL/Bayesian optimization narrowed but did not resolve the candidate
  counterexamples.
