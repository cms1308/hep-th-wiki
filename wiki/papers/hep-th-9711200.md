## TL;DR

Conjectures that the large-$N$ limit of certain superconformal field theories is exactly dual
to M/string theory on anti-de Sitter backgrounds. Flagship example: $\mathcal{N}=4$ $U(N)$
super-Yang-Mills in $3{+}1$d is dual to type IIB string theory on $AdS_5\times S^5$ with $N$
units of five-form flux. Founded the [[ads-cft-correspondence]].

## Motivation

Two tensions converge: (1) since [[gerard-t-hooft|'t Hooft]] it was suspected the large-$N$
limit of gauge theory is a string theory, but the string was never identified; (2) string/M-theory
lacked a nonperturbative definition ([[matrix-theory|Matrix theory]] being a partial answer).
Meanwhile D-brane calculations — [[strominger-vafa-entropy|black hole entropy counting]],
absorption cross-sections/greybody factors — kept agreeing with supergravity *beyond* their
naive regime of validity. The paper explains the agreements as symptoms of an exact duality.

## Historical context

Builds on: 't Hooft's large-$N$ expansion (1974); D-branes (Polchinski 1995); black $p$-brane
solutions (Horowitz–Strominger 1991); [[strominger-vafa-entropy|Strominger–Vafa 1996]] entropy
counting via the [[d1-d5-system]]; greybody/absorption calculations (Klebanov, Gubser,
Tseytlin, Das–Mathur, Maldacena–Strominger); [[near-horizon-supersymmetry-enhancement]]
(Gibbons, Kallosh–Peet, Gibbons–Townsend); 1980s gauged supergravity/KK spectra on
$AdS_5\times S^5$, $AdS_7\times S^4$, $AdS_4\times S^7$ (Günaydin, Duff, Nicolai–Sezgin–Tanii);
[[matrix-theory|BFSS Matrix theory]] (1996); Polyakov's proposal that the YM string has an
extra Liouville dimension.

## Main results

1. **[[maldacena-conjecture|The conjecture]]**: IIB string theory on $(AdS_5\times S^5)_N$ is
   dual to $\mathcal{N}=4$ $U(N)$ SYM at its superconformal point.
2. **Parameter dictionary**: $\frac{1}{g^2_{YM}} + i\frac{\theta}{8\pi^2} =
   \frac{1}{2\pi}\left(\frac{1}{g} + i\frac{\chi}{2\pi}\right)$ and
   $R^4/\alpha'^2 = 4\pi g N$; supergravity trustworthy for $gN\gg1$ — a strong/weak duality.
3. **'t Hooft string identified**: in the [[large-n-thooft-limit]] the SYM spectrum contains
   free IIB strings on $(AdS_5\times S^5)_{gN}$; the large-$N$ master field is the AdS
   supergravity solution.
4. **Symmetry matching**: AdS super-isometries $=$ superconformal group
   ($SO(2,4)\times SO(6)$, 32 supercharges), via [[near-horizon-supersymmetry-enhancement]];
   IIB $SL(2,\mathbb{Z})$ from Montonen–Olive duality.
5. **Other examples**: (0,2)$_6$ theory $\leftrightarrow$ $AdS_7\times S^4$; M2-brane CFT
   $\leftrightarrow$ $AdS_4\times S^7$; [[d1-d5-system]] (4,4) CFT $\leftrightarrow$
   $(AdS_3\times S^3)_{Q_1Q_5}\times M^4$, subsuming entropy/greybody results
   ([[btz-black-hole]] = thermal states); MSW (0,4) CFT $\leftrightarrow$ $AdS_3\times S^2\times M^6_p$.
6. **New nonperturbative definition of M/string theory**: the dual field theory defines
   quantum gravity on these backgrounds; $N$ measures spacetime size in Planck units, so
   quantum-gravity effects are $1/N$ effects (contrast [[matrix-theory]], where $N$ is
   light-cone momentum).

## Methods

- [[brane-decoupling-limit]]: $\alpha'\to0$ with $U\equiv r/\alpha'$ fixed — decouples the
  brane field theory from bulk gravity while keeping the near-horizon region; defines both
  sides of the duality at once.
- [[near-horizon-limit|Near-horizon geometry]]: the harmonic function's "1" drops, black brane
  → $AdS_{p+2}\times$ sphere with radius fixed in string/Planck units.
- Supergravity validity analysis: curvature $\sim$ negative power of $N$ ⇒ gravity reliable at
  large $gN$, exactly where gauge perturbation theory fails.
- [[brane-probe-action|Brane probe / Born–Infeld]]: separated D3-brane ($U(N)\to
  U(N-1)\times U(1)$) whose effective action is fixed by broken superconformal symmetry +
  non-renormalization, cross-checking $\tilde R^4 = 4\pi gN$.
- Thermodynamic checks: finite-temperature states $\leftrightarrow$ near-extremal branes /
  BTZ-type black holes.

## Conventions

- String coupling normalized so that $g \to 1/g$ under S-duality (stated in §2).
- Curvature radius convention: $R^4 = 4\pi g N \alpha'^2$ (equivalently
  $R^4/\alpha'^2 = 4\pi g_s N$; in 't Hooft language $\lambda = g^2_{YM} N \sim 4\pi g_s N$
  up to the $g^2_{YM} = 2\pi g_s$-type normalization — fix per topic when comparing papers).
- Signature mostly-plus in the appendix hyperboloid construction.

## Key equations

Decoupling limit: $\alpha'\to 0,\ U \equiv r/\alpha' \text{ fixed}$.

D3-brane solution: $ds^2 = f^{-1/2}dx_{||}^2 + f^{1/2}(dr^2 + r^2 d\Omega_5^2),\quad
f = 1 + \frac{4\pi g N \alpha'^2}{r^4}$.

Near-horizon geometry ($AdS_5\times S^5$):
$$ds^2 = \alpha'\left[\frac{U^2}{\sqrt{4\pi gN}}dx_{||}^2 + \sqrt{4\pi gN}\,\frac{dU^2}{U^2}
+ \sqrt{4\pi gN}\,d\Omega_5^2\right]$$

Coupling dictionary: $\frac{1}{g^2_{YM}} + i\frac{\theta}{8\pi^2} =
\frac{1}{2\pi}\left(\frac{1}{g} + i\frac{\chi}{2\pi}\right)$.

Redshift relation ($U$ as [[holographic-radial-coordinate|energy scale]]):
$E_{proper} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha'}}\frac{\omega\,(4\pi gN)^{1/4}}{U}$.

AdS as hyperboloid: $-X_{-1}^2 - X_0^2 + \sum_{i=1}^{p+1} X_i^2 = -R^2$, Poincaré patch
$ds^2 = \frac{U^2}{R^2}dx^2 + R^2\frac{dU^2}{U^2}$ (see [[anti-de-sitter-space]]).

## Open questions raised

- What boundary conditions/observables at AdS infinity complete the duality? →
  [[ads-boundary-dictionary]] (resolved by [[gkp-witten|GKP & Witten 1998]]).
- Meaning of the horizon/interior and global AdS beyond the Poincaré patch in gauge-theory terms.
- The $3/4$ near-extremal entropy factor at large $gN$.
- The $AdS_2$/Reissner–Nordström puzzle: extremal RN gives quantum mechanics, not a 2d chiral
  CFT — would define M-theory with 4 non-compact dimensions if solved.
- Extensions to non-conformal and QCD-like theories; de Sitter analogues.
