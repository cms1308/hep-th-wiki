## TL;DR

A unified construction of **genuine multipartite entanglement (GME) signals** — local-unitary
invariants that vanish on all separable (or layerwise-separable) states — from *compatible
families* of lower-partite symmetric LU-invariants. The organizing device is [[mobius-inversion]]
on the [[partition-lattice]] $\Pi_X$: the signal space is freely spanned by **Möbius vectors**
$M_\rho[f]=\sum_{\pi\le\rho}\mu(\pi,\rho)\,f_\pi$. Almost every multipartite entanglement signal in
the recent literature — genuine multi-entropy, $q$-information, residual information, correlation
signals from entanglement of purification — is a special case, and a non-symmetric version is
built directly from permutation/edge-colored-graph [[local-unitary-invariant|multi-invariants]].

## Motivation

Diagnosing *genuinely* multipartite entanglement — entanglement not reducible to bipartite
entanglement across some cut — needs LU-invariant quantities that vanish precisely when the state
is separable in *any* way. Several such quantities had been proposed independently and ad hoc.
The paper asks whether a single principle generates all of them, explains why they vanish on
separable states, and parametrizes the full space of such quantities. The answer: multipartite
separability is bookkept by the [[partition-lattice]], and [[mobius-inversion]] isolates exactly
the combinations that vanish on every nontrivially separable state.

## Historical context

- Möbius inversion on the partition lattice is classical combinatorics — Rota (1964), Stanley
  (*Enumerative Combinatorics*); the moments-to-cumulants reading is Speed (1983).
- Multi-entropy and its permutation/edge-colored-graph ([[local-unitary-invariant|multi-invariant]])
  formulation come from the author's earlier program~[[2603.07680|Gadde 2026]] (Gadde and
  collaborators, 2022–2025; not yet ingested).
- "Genuine multi-entropy" $\mathrm{GM}^{(n)}$ as a holographic GME diagnostic: Iizuka–Nishida and
  Iizuka–Lin–Nishida (2025).
- $q$-information and residual information: Balasubramanian–Kang–Murdia–Ross (2024).

  Del Zotto–Gadde–Putrov (2026).

## Main results

1. **Signal / pre-signal hierarchy.** A [[entanglement-signal|pre-signal]] is an LU-invariant that
   vanishes on all separable states; a [[entanglement-signal|signal]] vanishes on all
   *layerwise-separable* states (states factorizing into layers each of which is separable). An
   additive pre-signal is automatically a signal. **Key Lemma:** an additive LU-invariant that
   vanishes on $\pi$-separable states of type $(1,q-1)$ is a signal — every layer factorizes into
   $(1,q-1)$-type pieces.

2. **Compatible families.** A family $\{f_m\}_{m=1}^q$ of $m$-partite symmetric LU-invariants is
   *compatible* if $f_\pi(|\psi\rangle_\kappa)=f_{\pi\wedge\kappa}(|\psi\rangle_\kappa)$ on every
   $\kappa$-separable state. The restrictions $\{f|_m\}$ of any symmetric **additive** $q$-partite
   LU-invariant form a compatible family (Lemma 2), and $\{g(f_m^{(i)})\}$ is compatible for any
   $g:\mathbb C^r\to\mathbb C$ — giving an infinite supply, including non-additive ones.

3. **Meet-vanishing solved by Möbius vectors.** Encoding coefficients as $V=\sum_\pi c_\pi\,\pi$ in
   the group algebra $\mathbb C[\Pi_X]$ with $\wedge$ extended bilinearly, the constraints
   $V\wedge\kappa=0\ (\forall\kappa\in\mathcal K)$ have most general solution
   $V=\sum_{\rho\in\mathcal N}a_\rho M_\rho$ with $M_\rho=\sum_{\pi\le\rho}\mu(\pi,\rho)\,\pi$ and
   $\mathcal N=\Pi_X\setminus\downarrow\!\mathcal K$; the $M_\rho$ are linearly independent. The
   engine is the identity $M_\rho\wedge\kappa=M_\rho$ if $\rho\le\kappa$, else $0$.

4. **Signal theorem.** For an additive compatible family, $\sum_{\rho\in\mathcal N}a_\rho M_\rho[f]$
   with $M_\rho[f]=\sum_{\pi\le\rho}\mu(\pi,\rho)f_\pi$ is an additive signal, where $\mathcal N$ =
   partitions with no size-1 block; these span the whole space of additive signals.

5. **Pre-signal theorem.**
   $M_{\mathbf 1}[\tilde f]=\sum_{\pi\neq\mathbf 1}(-1)^{|\pi|-1}(|\pi|-1)!\,\tilde f_\pi$ is a
   pre-signal for any compatible family, and the *unique* one (up to scale) for a generic
   non-additive family.

6. **No-go for GME measures.** [[no-signal-is-gme-measure|No signal can be a GME measure]]:
   LOCC monotonicity is incompatible with positivity, because a local operation can map a
   layerwise-separable state to a GHZ-type non-layerwise-separable state. Pre-signals are the
   natural resource-theoretic objects — a GME measure need only be a positive, LOCC-monotone
   pre-signal.

7. **Unification of literature signals.** Choosing the seed $f$:
   - $f_1=\sum_a S^{(n)}_{A_a}$ (sum of single-party Rényi entropies) $\Rightarrow$
     **$q$-information**, $M_{\mathbf 1}[f_1]=\sum_{\varnothing\neq A\subsetneq X}(-1)^{q-|A|}S^{(n)}_A$;
     [[q-information-odd-q-vanishing|vanishes for odd $q$]] on pure states.
   - $f_2=\sum_{a=1}^{q-1}(\tfrac12 S_{A_aA_a^*}-S_{A_a})$ (reflected-entropy seed after tracing one
     party and canonically purifying) $\Rightarrow$ **residual information**, non-vanishing at odd $q$.
   - $f_3=S^{(n)}_q$ (Rényi [[multi-entropy]]) $\Rightarrow$ **genuine multi-entropy** $\mathrm{GM}^{(n)}$,
     now with a free basis $\{M_\rho[f_3]\}$.
   - $f_4=E_p^{(q)}$ (multipartite entanglement of purification) $\Rightarrow$ **correlation signals**
     $\Delta_p^{(q)}$.

8. **Non-symmetric signals from multi-invariants.** For a positive multi-invariant
   $\mathcal Z(\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_q;|\psi\rangle)$ with $\mathcal E=-\tfrac1n\log\mathcal Z$
   (additive), any rank-$q$ tensor $T$ with vanishing marginals $\sum_{i_k}T_{i_1\dots i_q}=0$ for
   each $k$ gives a signal $\sum T_{i_1\dots i_q}\,\mathcal E(\sigma_{i_1},\dots,\sigma_{i_q})$. The
   minimal one is $\mathcal E(\sigma_1-\sigma_2,\sigma_2-\sigma_1,\sigma_3-\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_q-\sigma_1;\cdot)$.

## Methods

- **[[partition-lattice|Partition-lattice bookkeeping]]**: separability types indexed by
  $\pi\in\Pi_X$ under refinement; $\pi$-separability = factorization across blocks of $\pi$.
- **[[local-unitary-invariant|Coarse-graining, restriction, $\pi$-extension]]**:
  $\mathrm{grp}_\pi$ merges each block into one effective party; $f_\pi=f\circ\mathrm{grp}_\pi$;
  $f|_m(|\psi\rangle)=f(|\psi\rangle\otimes|0\rangle^{\otimes(q-m)})$. Symmetric invariants require
  embedding all local spaces into a common $\mathbb C^d$.
- **Additivity via multiplicativity**: multi-invariants multiply under layer decomposition, so
  their logarithms are additive; additivity + Key Lemma reduces the signal condition to $(1,q-1)$ cuts.
- **[[mobius-inversion|Möbius inversion]]** on the meet-vanishing linear system, with the identity
  $M_\rho\wedge\kappa\in\{M_\rho,0\}$ diagonalizing the constraints.
- **Combinatorial identities**: interval inclusion–exclusion for $M_\rho\wedge\kappa$; the Stirling
  identity $\sum_r(-1)^r r!\,S(m,r)=(-1)^m$ (with purity $S_A=S_{A^c}$) proves the odd-$q$ vanishing.
- **Kraus-operator counterexample** for the GME no-go.

## Conventions

Pure $q$-partite states $|\psi\rangle\in\mathcal H_{A_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathcal H_{A_q}$;
parties $A_1,\dots,A_q$ or $A,B,C,\dots$

- **Lattice order (combinatorics convention).** $\kappa\le\pi$ means $\kappa$ is *finer* (a
  refinement of) $\pi$. Bottom $\mathbf 0$ = all singletons ($|\pi|=|X|$); top $\mathbf 1$ = single
  block ($|\pi|=1$). "Separable" = $|\pi|\ge2$; "completely separable" = $\mathbf 0$; GME = not
  separable across any partition.
- **Möbius function.** $\mu(\kappa,\pi)=\prod_{B\in\pi}(-1)^{k_B-1}(k_B-1)!$, $k_B$ = number of
  $\kappa$-blocks inside block $B$; $\mu(\kappa,\mathbf 1)=(-1)^{|\kappa|-1}(|\kappa|-1)!$.
- **Rényi entropy** $S^{(n)}_A=\frac{1}{1-n}\log\mathrm{Tr}\,\rho_A^n$.
- **Multi-invariant** $\mathcal Z(\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_q;|\psi\rangle)=\langle\psi|^{\otimes n}\,
  \sigma_1\otimes\cdots\otimes\sigma_q\,|\psi\rangle^{\otimes n}$, $|\mathcal Z|\le1$ (Cauchy–Schwarz),
  with relabeling redundancy $\mathcal Z(\sigma_i)=\mathcal Z(g\sigma_i)=\mathcal Z(\sigma_i h)$,
  $g,h\in S_n$; logarithmic version $\mathcal E=-\tfrac1n\log\mathcal Z$.

No spacetime metric enters — this is a quantum-information paper. The preamble macros
`\tq,\tX,\tGX` are defined but unused. No topic-canonical convention conflict: this is the wiki's
first entry in multipartite entanglement, so its conventions become the local baseline.

## Key equations

- Compatibility: $f_{\pi}(|\psi\rangle_\kappa)=f_{\pi\wedge\kappa}(|\psi\rangle_\kappa)$.
- Möbius inversion: $g(\pi)=\sum_{\kappa\le\pi}f(\kappa)\iff f(\pi)=\sum_{\kappa\le\pi}\mu(\kappa,\pi)g(\kappa)$.
- Signal ansatz: $s=\sum_{\pi}c_\pi f_\pi$; vanishing $\sum_\pi c_\pi f_{\pi\wedge\kappa}(|\psi\rangle_\kappa)=0$.
- Möbius vector basis: $M_\rho=\sum_{\pi\le\rho}\mu(\pi,\rho)\,\pi$; solution $V=\sum_{\rho\in\mathcal N}a_\rho M_\rho$.
- Eigen-identity: $M_\rho\wedge\kappa=M_\rho\ (\rho\le\kappa)$ else $0$.
- Pre-signal: $M_{\mathbf 1}[\tilde f]=\sum_{\pi\neq\mathbf 1}(-1)^{|\pi|-1}(|\pi|-1)!\,\tilde f_\pi$.
- $q$-information: $M_{\mathbf 1}[f_1]=\sum_{\varnothing\neq A\subsetneq X}(-1)^{q-|A|}S^{(n)}_A$.
- Non-symmetric signal: $\sum T_{i_1\dots i_q}\mathcal E(\sigma_{i_1},\dots,\sigma_{i_q})$,
  $\sum_{i_k}T=0\ \forall k$.

## Open questions raised

- Can pre-signals be upgraded to genuine **GME measures** (non-negative, LOCC-monotone), perhaps
  via the edge-convexity of the graph technology, and extended to mixed states by convex roof of
  the polynomial multi-invariants? See [[pre-signals-to-gme-measures]].
- A **structural characterization of compatibility** — identify *all* compatible LU-invariant
  families, beyond those built from additive invariants (the explicit condition is cumbersome). See
  [[structural-characterization-compatibility]].
- Relate this partition-lattice signal space to the hypergraph/Sperner-state construction of
  Ju–Sun–Zhao and find when they coincide.
- Non-additive signals (e.g. the fidelity-type $1-\sup_{|\phi\rangle\in\mathcal L}|\langle\phi|\psi\rangle|^2$)
  are left unexplored.
