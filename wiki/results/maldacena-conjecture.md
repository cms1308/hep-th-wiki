## Statement

Type IIB string theory on $AdS_5\times S^5$ with $N$ units of self-dual five-form flux is
exactly dual to $\mathcal{N}=4$ $U(N)$ super-Yang-Mills theory in $3{+}1$ dimensions at its
superconformal point. Parameter dictionary:

$$\frac{1}{g^2_{YM}} + i\frac{\theta}{8\pi^2} = \frac{1}{2\pi}\left(\frac{1}{g_s} +
i\frac{\chi}{2\pi}\right), \qquad \frac{R^4}{\alpha'^2} = 4\pi g_s N$$

so $1/N$ counts string loops and the 't Hooft coupling $\lambda = g^2_{YM}N$ controls the
curvature: $\lambda \gg 1$ ⇔ weakly curved supergravity.

## Derivation sketch

Apply the [[brane-decoupling-limit]] to $N$ coincident D3-branes: the open-string description
gives $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM; the closed-string description gives the near-horizon
$AdS_5\times S^5$ geometry. Both survive the same limit ⇒ duality
([[hep-th-9711200|Maldacena 1997]]).

Full derivation of the geometry side: [[d3-near-horizon-limit]].

## Assumptions

Decoupling of the throat region from the asymptotic bulk; boundary conditions at AdS infinity
(made precise later by [[gkp-witten|GKP–Witten]]). The strong form (all $N$, all $g_s$) goes
beyond the supergravity derivation.

## Status

Conjectured — never proven, but passes every known check: symmetry matching
($SO(2,4)\times SO(6)$, 32 supercharges, $SL(2,\mathbb{Z})$), KK spectra, protected
correlators, localization, integrability, entropy matches. De facto definition of IIB strings
on $AdS_5\times S^5$.

## Implications

Nonperturbative definition of string theory on AdS; realization of the
[[holographic-principle]]; identification of the [[large-n-thooft-limit|'t Hooft string]];
foundation of the whole [[ads-cft-correspondence]] program.
