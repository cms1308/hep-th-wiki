## Statement

For a $2+1$-dimensional topological phase $\mathcal T$ and a chosen Abelian anyon $a$ of order $n$ that becomes light, the most general relativistic (Lorentz-invariant) continuum theory of the [[proliferation-transition|proliferation transition]] built from a single complex scalar $\Phi$ depends — beyond the fixed data $(\mathcal T,a)$ — on **only one additional integer** $p$. Although for a given $(\mathcal T,a)$ there are infinitely many such field theories, they are all labelled by this single integer, the integer lift of the anomaly of the [[one-form-global-symmetry|$\mathbb Z_n^{(1)}$]] generated by $a$ (via $h(a)=\frac{p}{2n}\bmod 1$).

## Derivation sketch

The transition theory is $\mathcal L=\mathcal L_{\mathcal T'}+\frac1{2\pi}\mathfrak b\,dc+|D_c\Phi|^2-\mu^2|\Phi|^2+\cdots$ (Eq. 3.1 of [[2603.00245|Cheng–Seiberg 2026]]), with $\Phi$ creating $a\simeq e^{i\int c}$ and $D_c\Phi=(d-ic)\Phi$. The scalar couples to $\mathcal T$ only through the Abelian field $c$; a Lagrange-multiplier coupling $\frac1{2\pi}(c-C(\beta))\,d\gamma$ (Sec. 7) forces this to be the unique structure. The apparent freedom (choice of auxiliary Chern–Simons level, dressing) collapses because the local dynamics depends only on the rational $n/p$: e.g. $-\frac{np}{4\pi}\check b\,d\check b+\frac n{2\pi}\check b\,dc\to\frac n{4\pi p}c\,dc$ (Eqs. 3.3–3.4). The far side is $\mathcal T'=\frac{\mathcal T\boxtimes\mathrm U(1)_{-pn}}{(a,p)}$ for $p\neq0$, and gapless for $p=0$.

## Assumptions

- Single light Abelian anyon $a$ (not several mutually non-local ones, not a non-Abelian anyon).
- Relativistic Lorentz invariance; a single complex scalar $\Phi$.
- Bosonic TQFT ($pn\in2\mathbb Z$).
- The integer $p$ is kept as an integer lift, not reduced mod $2n$; the transition theory (and $L=\gcd(n,p)$) genuinely depends on this lift.

## Status

Established within the stated assumptions in [[2603.00245|Cheng–Seiberg 2026]]: the classification of transition theories by $p$, together with their phases, global symmetries, and responses, is worked out in full.

## Implications

- Gives a finite, one-integer parameterization of an a priori infinite family of critical theories.
- Separates two regimes: $p\neq0$ (far side is a TQFT via the [[abelian-hierarchy-construction]]) and $p=0$ (transition is a gauging, [[p-zero-transition-implements-gauging]]).
- The parameter $p$ carries physical response data: the emergent-$\mathrm U(1)_{\mathcal B}$ Hall-conductivity jump is $\ell^2/(np)$.
