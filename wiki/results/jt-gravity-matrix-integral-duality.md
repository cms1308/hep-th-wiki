## Statement

The full genus expansion of orientable [[jt-gravity|JT gravity]] equals the genus expansion of a
double-scaled Hermitian matrix integral $\mathcal Z=\int dH\,e^{-L\,\mathrm{Tr}V(H)}$, under the
dictionary $Z(\beta)\leftrightarrow\mathrm{Tr}\,e^{-\beta H}$ and $e^{-S_0}\leftrightarrow$ the
genus-counting (string) coupling. Concretely, for every $g,n$
$$\langle Z(\beta_1)\cdots Z(\beta_n)\rangle_{\text{conn.}}\simeq\sum_{g}\frac{Z_{g,n}}{(e^{S_0})^{2g+n-2}},\qquad
Z_{g,n}=\prod_i\int_0^\infty b_i\,db_i\,Z^{\rm tr}_{\rm Sch}(\beta_i,b_i)\,V_{g,n}(b_1,\dots,b_n),$$
and both sides are generated by the
[[eynard-orantin-topological-recursion|Eynard–Orantin topological recursion]] applied to the single
[[jt-spectral-curve|spectral curve]]
$y(z)=\tfrac{1}{4\pi}\sin(2\pi z)$ ($\gamma=\tfrac12$), equivalently the leading density of states
$\rho_0(E)=\frac{\gamma}{2\pi^2}\sinh(2\pi\sqrt{2\gamma E})$ read off from the disk.
Established by [[1903.11115|Saad–Shenker–Stanford 2019]].

## Derivation sketch

1. Integrate out the dilaton in the JT path integral: $R=-2$, so each surface is hyperbolic and
   the only data are its topology $(g,n)$ and moduli.
2. Cut along each asymptotic boundary's neighboring geodesic (length $b_i$) into a
   [[trumpet-geometry|trumpet]] glued to a bordered genus-$g$ surface. The Schwarzian wiggle
   integral is one-loop exact, giving $Z^{\rm disk}_{\rm Sch}$, $Z^{\rm tr}_{\rm Sch}$; the moduli
   integral gives the [[weil-petersson-volumes|Weil–Petersson volume]] $V_{g,n}(b)$.
3. Laplace-transform in $b$: $W_{g,n}(z)=\prod_i\int b_i\,db_i\,e^{-b_iz_i}V_{g,n}$. Mirzakhani's
   recursion for $V_{g,n}$ becomes Eynard's residue-at-origin recursion — the matrix-model loop
   equations — with input $y=\tfrac{\sin 2\pi z}{4\pi}$.
4. The disk fixes $\rho_0$; the double-trumpet reproduces the universal RMT resolvent
   $R_{0,2}=\frac1{4z_1z_2(z_1+z_2)^2}$ (the linear ramp). By the Eynard–Orantin theorem the same
   spectral curve then generates *all* higher $W_{g,n}$, i.e. all JT amplitudes.

## Assumptions

- Orientable surfaces, Hermitian ensemble (the $\beta=2$ / class A case). Time-reversal-invariant
  and fermionic variants need the other Altland–Zirnbauer ensembles (Stanford–Witten).
- Double-scaling: one edge of the eigenvalue support is pushed to infinity with the edge density
  held fixed as $e^{S_0}$; $\rho_0$ is non-normalizable.
- The matching is order-by-order in the genus (asymptotic) expansion; the sum is divergent and
  requires a [[nonperturbative-completion-jt|nonperturbative completion]].

## Status

**Conjectured / established perturbatively.** The equality holds to all orders in the genus
expansion by the Mirzakhani ⇔ topological-recursion theorem. It is a *matrix integral*, not a
single matrix: JT gravity computes ensemble-averaged quantities, which is exact perturbatively but
leaves the nonperturbative completion non-unique.

## Implications

- Gravitational path integrals over connected topologies (wormholes) encode random-matrix
  statistics — the origin of the [[spectral-form-factor|ramp and plateau]].
- Holography for JT is a duality to an *ensemble* of boundary theories, sharpening the
  [[factorization-vs-wormholes|factorization puzzle]].
- Bulk gravity predicts
  [[large-genus-weil-petersson-asymptotics|large-genus Weil–Petersson volume asymptotics]], a
  statement in pure mathematics.
