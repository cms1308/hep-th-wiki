## What it computes

A way to run the [[ttbar-deformation|$T\bar T$]] / [[jt-bar-deformation|$J\bar T$]] deformation
on the plane as a **canonical (Bogoliubov) transformation** on phase space, and to classify
operators by how they behave under that flow. It produces the deformed stress tensor / currents,
the [[nonlocal-conformal-symmetry-deformed-cft|nonlocal symmetry generators]], and the
[[dressed-operator-deformed-cft|dressed]] and [[physical-operator-deformed-cft|physical]]
operators, all in closed form.

## Prerequisites

A 2d CFT with a well-defined stress tensor (and a chiral $U(1)$ current for $J\bar T$); the
Poisson-bracket / canonical structure of the seed theory; the plane ($R\to\infty$) so that the
spectrum-deforming boundary term drops.

## How it works

The deformation is written as
$$\partial_\lambda H=i[\mathcal X,H]+\mathcal Y,$$
with $\mathcal X$ a bilocal generator. On the plane the term $\mathcal Y\to0$, so the flow is a
**spectrum-preserving canonical transformation** generated by $\mathcal X$ — for $T\bar T$ the
bilocal $\mathcal X_{T\bar T}$, for $J\bar T$ the bilocal
$\mathcal X_{J\bar T}=\int dy\,dy'\,G(y-y')\,\tilde{\mathcal J}_+(y)\mathcal H_R(y')$ with $G$ the
plane Green's function.

Operators are then classified by the **covariant derivative** (flow equation)
$\mathcal D_\lambda\equiv\partial_\lambda-i[\mathcal X,\cdot]$:

- **Undeformed** operators — evolved by the deformed Hamiltonian (local, not symmetry-organized);
- **Dressed** operators — covariant constants, $\mathcal D_\lambda\tilde{\mathcal O}=0$ (nonlocal
  primaries), solved by recursion into closed sums;
- **Physical** operators — local combinations engineered so the field-dependent weight-shift
  terms in the Ward identity cancel.

Everything is done classically (Poisson brackets replacing commutators, $[F,G]\to i\{F,G\}$),
with the deformed stress tensor from a Noether construction; quantum correlators are then
computed with CFT Ward identities and point-splitting, and the leading logs resummed.

## Regime of validity

- **Plane only** in the clean form: on the cylinder the $\mathcal Y$ term reintroduces spectrum
  deformation and rescaled-vs-unrescaled generators must be tracked.
- **No classical central charge**: the Noether stress tensor used has no classical central term,
  so improved-stress-tensor / Liouville-type theories fall outside the current treatment.
- The operator construction is **classical**; a full quantum uplift (normal ordering) is assumed
  but not proven — see [[quantum-operators-deformed-cft]].
