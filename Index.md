## Methods

- [[brane-decoupling-limit]] — $\alpha'\to0$, $U$ fixed: derive gauge/gravity duals from brane configurations.
- [[flat-space-limit-of-witten-diagrams]] — position-space $\ell\to\infty$ limit of AdS diagrams → Carrollian amplitudes; bulk-point singularity mechanism.
- [[a-maximization]] — maximize trial $a(R_t)$ over flavor mixings to find the exact superconformal R-symmetry and central charges.
- [[convex-hull-condition]] — WGC with multiple $U(1)$'s: convex hull of charge-to-mass vectors must contain the extremal-black-hole unit ball.
- [[flip-deformation]] — $\delta W = M\mathcal O$ removes $\mathcal O$ from the chiral ring; drives SUSY-enhancing flows and the landscape program.
- [[hamiltonian-flow-deformed-cft]] — $T\bar T/J\bar T$ as a canonical transformation on the plane; the covariant-constancy flow equation classifying operators.
- [[eynard-orantin-topological-recursion]] — residue recursion generating all matrix-model correlators from a spectral curve; equals Mirzakhani's volume recursion for JT.
- [[double-scaling-limit]] — edge-focused $L\to\infty$ limit turning the $1/L$ matrix expansion into the $e^{-S_0}$ genus expansion of 2d gravity.
- [[kaluza-klein-reduction-wormholes]] — $S^1/T^N$ reduction mapping higher-dim black holes/Schwarzschild to lower-dim scalar wormholes; reads off IDB = KSW = WGC.
- [[grand-canonical-residue-expansion]] — finite-$N$ partition function as a contour integral over grand-canonical poles = sum over bulk saddles.
- [[mobius-inversion]] — invert poset sums $g(\pi)=\sum_{\kappa\le\pi}f(\kappa)$; solves meet-vanishing constraints on the partition lattice, engine of the signal construction.
- [[seam-graded-expansion]] — grade CSFT solutions by number of integrated internal geodesics; makes each order a matrix inversion, not a Fredholm equation.
- [[mirzakhani-topological-recursion]] — Weil–Petersson volume recursion generating all $V_{g,n}$ (and all string vertices $V_{0,n}$) from $V_{0,3}$.
- [[large-flavor-expansion-entropic-order]] — $k$-species colored generalization ($U\to U/k$) making mean-field theory exact at $k\to\infty$; controls the entropic-order threshold.
- [[lowest-landau-level-projection]] — monopole-sphere Landau levels + Haldane pseudopotentials → a finite $SO(3)$-invariant many-body Hamiltonian on $S^2$; the fuzzy-sphere model builder.
- [[exact-diagonalization]] — direct Lanczos diagonalization of a finite many-body Hamiltonian; the numerical engine turning fuzzy-sphere energies into scaling dimensions.
- [[conformal-bootstrap]] — CFT data from crossing + unitarity alone; supplies the 3D Ising benchmark spectrum the fuzzy sphere is checked against.
- [[gauging-one-form-symmetry]] — three-step Moore–Seiberg gauging of an anomaly-free $\mathbb Z_n^{(1)}$ in a $2+1$d TQFT; $\mathcal T\to\mathcal T/\mathbb Z_n$; twisted gauge fields.
- [[analytic-defect-bootstrap]] — Witten-diagram ansatz (bulk/defect exchange + contact) fixed by the superconformal Ward identity; solves holographic defect two-point functions up to one constant.
- [[weight-function-regularization]] — regulator $F(\delta,\alpha)$ making divergent twisted-Schur-descent integrals convergent while preserving the $\mathbb T_\pm$ Ward identities.
- [[green-schwarz-sagnotti-mechanism]] — 6d anomaly-polynomial factorization $I_8=\tfrac12\Omega X_4 X_4$; fixes the anomaly vectors $b_0,b_i$ and the tensor-lattice signature bound.
- [[superconformal-deformation-to-voa]] — deform the holomorphic twist by $\int z^2\widetilde{\mathbf G}$ to localize to $z^2=0$ and recover the Beem-et-al VOA; extract 2d $\lambda$-brackets from 4d ones.
- [[holomorphic-a-maximization]] — spectral flow + uniqueness of the $\langle SSJ\rangle$ tensor structure gives $k_f=9k_{RRf}$; $a$-extremization inside the holomorphic twist.

## Results

- [[maldacena-conjecture]] — $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM ↔ IIB on $AdS_5\times S^5$, with parameter dictionary (conjectured).
- [[flat-carrollian-limit-correspondence]] — bulk $\ell\to\infty$ ⇔ boundary $c\to0$, $c=\ell^{-1}$; flat holography as a limit of AdS/CFT (established at tree level).
- [[carrollian-celestial-equivalence]] — Carrollian and celestial holography equivalent for massless scattering via an invertible $u$-transform (established).
- [[modified-ads-wgc-3c-2a]] — every 4d $\mathcal N=1$ SCFT has an operator with $\Delta^2/q^2\le 12(3c-2a)/k_F$ (conjectured; passes all known tests).
- [[nakayama-nomura-wgc-bound]] — original AdS WGC translation $\Delta^2/q^2\le 12c/k_F$; fails for SQCD at $N_f/N\gtrsim2.1$ (disputed as universal).
- [[supersymmetric-cardy-formula-4d]] — Cardy-like limit of the 4d index: $\log\mathcal I$ fixed by anomalies, $\mathrm{Re}(S)\propto(3c-2a)^{1/3}J^{2/3}$ (proven).
- [[hofman-maldacena-bound]] — collider positivity bounds on $a/c$; guarantees $3c-2a>0$ for interacting $\mathcal N=1$ SCFTs (proven).
- [[minimal-n1-scft]] — smallest-known interacting $\mathcal N=1$ SCFT, $(a,c)=(\tfrac{633}{2000},\tfrac{683}{2000})$ = deformed $(A_1,A_4)$, with two dual Lagrangians (established).
- [[large-n-universality-classes]] — all simple-gauge-group large-$N$ $\mathcal N=1$ SCFTs ($W=0$) fall into Type I/II/III keyed to $N_{\text{rank-}2}=1,2,3$, with universal R-charges (established).
- [[susskind-uglum-low-energy-theorem]] — Rindler entropy per unit area equals $1/4G$ with the fully renormalized Newton constant (derivation paper not yet ingested).
- [[ads4-s-algebra-from-cft3-light-transforms]] — the full $S$-algebra exists in AdS$_4$/CFT$_3$: light transform of the boundary current + $SO(3,2)$ descendants (established).
- [[every-cft3-has-l-lambda-w-symmetry]] — ANEC descendants generate a wedge of $\mathcal L_\Lambda w_{1+\infty}$ ($\Lambda=-1$) in any CFT$_3$ (established).
- [[undeformed-s-algebra-planar-n4-sym]] — IR-finite hard amplitudes of planar $\mathcal N=4$ SYM represent the undeformed tree-level $S$-algebra at finite coupling (argued under standard assumptions).
- [[ttbar-deformed-spectrum]] — Burgers-equation √ formula for the deformed finite-size energy levels (proven; Zamolodchikov, Smirnov–Zamolodchikov).
- [[deformed-cft-two-point-function]] — nonperturbative UV two-point function of physical operators with momentum-dependent weight $h_\lambda$; matches TsT string (with a $J\bar T$ instanton).
